# Prescription extinctive

> **Type**: Extinction of obligations by time  
> **General period**: 30 years (art. 2262)  
> **Commercial**: 10 years (art. 189 C.com)

---

## General Periods

| Period | Application | Article |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **30 years** | Personal actions (droit commun) | 2262 |
| **10 years** | Commercial obligations | 189 C.com |
| **5 years** | Periodic payments, salaries | 2277 |
| **5 years** | Vices du consentement | 1304 |
| **1 year** | Lésion (art. 1118) | 1118 al. 3 |

---

## Starting Point

### General Rule

Prescription runs from when right **could be exercised**.

### Special Rules

| Action | Starting Point |
|--------|---------------|
| **Erreur, Dol** | Discovery |
| **Violence** | Cessation |
| **Lésion** | Contract conclusion |
| **Nullité relative** | Per art. 1304 |

---

## Commercial Prescription (art. 189 C.com)

> **Poelmans §564**: Applies to all obligations "nées à l'occasion de leur commerce entre commerçants ou entre commerçants et non-commerçants."

### Scope

- **Mixed acts**: Either party commercial → 10 years applies
- **Contractual and delictual**: Both covered
- **Applies regardless** of which party invokes

---

## Suspension (arts. 2251-2259)

Prescription clock **stops** temporarily.

### Grounds

| Ground | Article | Effect |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Minors/interdits** | 2252 | Suspended during incapacity |
| **Between spouses** | 2253 | Suspended during marriage |
| **Héritier bénéficiaire** | 2258 | For claims vs. succession |
| **Contra non valentem** | Jurisprudence | Impossibility to act |

---

## Interruption

Prescription clock **resets** to zero.

### Grounds

| Ground | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Citation en justice** | New period starts |
| **Commandement/saisie** | New period starts |
| **Acknowledgment by debtor** | New period starts |

---

## Exception de Nullité

> **CRITICAL**: The right to invoke nullity **as a defense** is **imprescriptible**.

Even after action is time-barred:
- Cannot sue to annul
- **But** can defend against enforcement

Applies to **both** absolute and relative nullity.

---

## Intentional/Dolous Fault

> **Poelmans §563**: Proof of **intentional or dolous fault** makes shorter prescriptions inapplicable → return to 30-year period.

This is a **sanction** against bad faith debtor.

---

## Banking Application

### Loan Claims

- Commercial: 10 years
- Consumer: Check specific rules

### Guarantee Claims

- Follow main obligation
- But guarantee may have own prescription rules

### Running of Time

- Each installment: Separate prescription
- Acceleration: Affects calculation
