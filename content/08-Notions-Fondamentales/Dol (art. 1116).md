# Dol (art. 1116)

> **Type**: Vice du consentement  
> **Article**: 1116 Code civil  
> **Sanction**: Nullité relative  
> **Prescription**: 5 years from discovery

---

## Definition

> **Dol** = maneuvers (*manœuvres*) used to deceive co-contractor into consenting.

Creates an induced error through deception.

---

## Requirements

### 1. Manœuvres

**Active fraud**: Lies, falsified documents, staging, misrepresentation.

**Réticence dolosive** (silence): Deliberate concealment of material fact.

### 2. Déterminant

Without the deception, party would **not have contracted**.

### 3. Emanates from Co-contractor

> **Critical**: Dol must come from the **co-contractor**, not a third party.

This distinguishes dol from [[Violence (arts. 1111-1114)]] (which can come from anyone).

---

## Réticence Dolosive (Silence as Fraud)

### Condition Nécessaire: Obligation de Parler

> **Poelmans**: For silence to constitute dol, there must be a **duty to speak** (*obligation de parler*).

This duty may arise from:
- Law (disclosure requirements)
- Contract (representations)
- Professional relationship (duty to inform)
- Circumstances creating reasonable expectation

### Employment Case (Poelmans)

**Facts**: Employee did not disclose prior incarceration when hired.

**Held**: **No dol** because:
- Employer had no right to ask about criminal record for that position
- No *obligation de parler* existed
- Employee entitled to protection of private life

### Contrasting Example

Real estate agent must disclose known defects → *obligation de parler* exists.

---

## Dol vs. Erreur

| Aspect | Dol | Erreur |
|--------|-----|--------|
| **Source** | Induced by other party | Party's own mistake |
| **Proof** | Must prove manœuvres | Must prove error |
| **Damages** | Available (fault element) | Rarely available |
| **On motive** | May suffice | Generally excluded |

---

## Dol Incident vs. Dol Principal

| Type | Effect | Remedy |
|------|--------|--------|
| **Dol principal** | Would not have contracted at all | Nullité |
| **Dol incident** | Would have contracted but on different terms | Damages only (controversy) |

---

## Sanction

### Nullité Relative

- Only deceived party may invoke
- Confirmable (art. 1115)
- Judge cannot raise ex officio

### Damages

Possible in addition to nullity — fault-based liability.

### Prescription (art. 1304)

**5 years** from **discovery** (*du jour où ils ont été découverts*).

---

## Banking Application

### Duty to Inform

Banks have extensive information duties:
- Product risks
- Conflicts of interest
- Fees and charges

Breach may constitute *réticence dolosive* if material and deliberate.

### Client Representations

Client misrepresentations in loan applications:
- Bank can invoke dol
- Financial statements, purpose of loan

### KYC/AML

Deliberate concealment of beneficial ownership:
- May constitute dol
- Affects contract validity
