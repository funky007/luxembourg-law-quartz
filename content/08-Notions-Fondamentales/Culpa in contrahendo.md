# Culpa in Contrahendo (Pre-contractual Liability)

> **Type**: Extra-contractual liability in pre-contractual phase
> **Legal Basis**: Arts. 1382-1383 Code civil (by analogy)
> **Source**: Poelmans §§20-29

---

## Definition

> Liability arising from **fault during negotiations** (*pourparlers*), before contract formation.

**Nature**: Extra-contractual (quasi-delictual) — parties not yet bound by contract.

**CA, 13 février 2008, Pas. 34, p. 155**: Duty of loyalty exists in pre-contractual phase; breach triggers quasi-delictual liability.

---

## The Pre-contractual Phase

### No Binding Effect

**Poelmans §20**: Preliminary negotiations, proposals, and counter-proposals are not contractual commitments. Parties may always break off negotiations.

**Maxim**: "Pas de contrat vaut mieux qu'un mauvais contrat."

**CA, 5 mars 2008, n° 19272**: Negotiations remained preliminary when price not agreed; rupture after 18 months not abusive given lack of progress.

### Duty of Good Faith

Despite no contract, parties owe duties sanctioned by extra-contractual liability:
- **Continue negotiating in good faith** (*loyalement*)
- **Rupture loyale** — break-off must be legitimate

---

## When Rupture is Abusive

### Fault Standard

**No intentional fault required** — no need to prove *intention de nuire*.

**Test**: Did rupture breach **legitimate trust** (*confiance légitime trompée*)?

### Fault Factors (Poelmans §21)

| Factor | Analysis |
|--------|----------|
| **State of advancement** | More advanced = higher duty |
| **Nature of negotiations** | Complex/singular contract = more care |
| **Professional status** | Higher duty on professionals |
| **Publicity given** | Public statements increase reliance |
| **Existence of offer** | Firm offer creates expectation |
| **Precision of terms** | Detailed terms = closer to agreement |
| **Duration of negotiations** | Length may increase trust |

### Abusive Conduct Examples

**Trib. Lux., 24 janvier 2002, n° 68830** (confirmed CA, 11 juin 2003, n° 26817): Theatre broke off advanced negotiations without serious reason — liable for *culpa in contrahendo*.

**Trib. Lux., 15 mai 2008, n° 102925**: Vendor broke off land sale negotiations after failing to secure co-owner's agreement — breach of good faith duty.

**CA, 13 février 2008, Pas. 34, p. 155**: Pre-contractual agreements not respected; seller failed to prove competing offer met agreed conditions.

---

## Damages for Culpa in Contrahendo

### Traditional Rule

> Recovery limited to **negotiation costs** and **preparatory studies** — NOT lost profits.

**CA, 5 mars 2008, n° 19272**: Confirmed restrictive approach to damages.

**CA, 11 juin 2003, n° 26817**: Restrictive view of damages affirmed on appeal.

### Extended Recovery (Minority View)

Some Luxembourg decisions have allowed:
- **Lost gains** (*gains espérés*)
- **Loss of chance** (*perte de chance*) of deal completion

**Trib. Lux., 24 janvier 2002, n° 68830**: Awarded expected performance loss (receipts minus costs).

**Trib. Lux., 15 mai 2008, n° 102925**: Awarded 20% of expected profit (loss of chance analysis).

---

## Pre-contractual Agreements

### Effect

Pre-contractual agreements (*accords préparatoires*) may be binding even before final contract.

**CA, 13 février 2008, Pas. 34, p. 155**: Seller's breach of pre-contractual engagement (to sell unless better offer received) was actionable fault.

### Types

| Agreement | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| **Lettre d'intention** | May bind depending on language |
| **Vereinbarung** | Partial agreement on some terms |
| **Heads of terms** | Generally non-binding unless stated |
| **Exclusivity agreement** | Binding commitment |

---

## Employment Context

**Trib. Trav. Esch, 4 juillet 2000**: Testing candidates does not create employment contract; no binding effect until tests concluded.

---

## Banking Application

### Loan Negotiations

If bank:
- Advanced negotiations significantly
- Then withdrew without legitimate reason
- May face culpa in contrahendo liability

### Due Diligence

| Stage | Implication |
|-------|-------------|
| Initial discussions | No duty; withdrawal free |
| Term sheet signed | Potential reliance; withdrawal risky |
| Credit committee approval | High expectation created |
| Conditions precedent stage | Near-contractual; withdrawal costly |

### Mitigation

Clear disclaimers in preliminary documents may limit liability.
