# Interprétation du contrat (arts. 1156-1164)

> **Type**: Judicial methodology  
> **Articles**: 1156-1164 Code civil  
> **Nature**: Question of fact (generally no cassation)

---

## Fundamental Principle (art. 1156)

> "On doit dans les conventions rechercher quelle a été la commune intention des parties contractantes, plutôt que de s'arrêter au sens littéral des termes."

**Search for real intent**, not just literal meaning.

---

## Question of Fact

> **Poelmans §180**: The interpretive precepts of arts. 1157-1164 are merely **advice** (*conseils*) to the judge, not absolute rules.

**Consequence**: Violation does not give rise to cassation.

**Exception**: Dénaturation of clear terms → cassation possible.

---

## Interpretive Precepts

### Art. 1157 — Effective Interpretation

If two readings possible → choose one that **gives effect** to the contract.

### Art. 1158 — Contextual Meaning

Ambiguous terms → meaning fitting **subject matter** of contract.

### Art. 1159 — Usage

Interpret according to **local usage**.

### Art. 1160 — Implied Clauses

Supply **customary clauses** not expressed.

### Art. 1161 — Unity

Interpret clauses **together**, not in isolation.

### Art. 1162 — Contra Proferentem

Doubt → against **drafter** of contract.

### Art. 1163 — No Extension

Don't extend beyond what parties **had in mind**.

### Art. 1164 — Inclusive Interpretation

Specific case mentioned doesn't exclude others if **intent was broader**.

---

## Evolution of Art. 1162

**Original rule**: In doubt, favor party undertaking obligation.

**Modern evolution** (Poelmans §182):
> Art. 1162 has become instrument for interpreting **contrats d'adhésion**.

**New meaning**: Interpret **against the drafter** who bears responsibility for obscurities.

---

## Interpretation Even if Terms Clear

> **Poelmans §178**: Even if terms appear clear, interpretation not precluded when parties make **divergent readings** giving rise to litigation.

---

## Restrictive Interpretation

### Exorbitant Clauses

Clauses derogating from common law → **strictly interpreted**.

**Examples**:
- Non-warranty clauses
- Liability limitations
- Penalty clauses

### Transactions

> Settlements cover only disputes **expressly included**. In doubt → element stayed outside.

---

## Consumer Contracts

> **Art. L.211-2(2) Code conso.**: In case of doubt, interpretation **most favorable to consumer** prevails.

---

## Qualification

### Judge's Power

Judge may **qualify or requalify** contract.

Not bound by parties' denomination, but must act prudently when changing it.

### Method

Examine characteristic elements:
- Nature of prestations
- Economic purpose
- Parties' conduct
