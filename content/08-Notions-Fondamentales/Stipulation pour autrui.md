# Stipulation pour autrui

> **Type**: Exception to privity of contract  
> **Article**: 1121 Code civil  
> **Effect**: Third party acquires rights under contract

---

## Definition

> Contract clause by which one party (*stipulant*) obtains from another (*promettant*) a commitment to perform in favor of a **third party** (*tiers bénéficiaire*).

**Exception** to *effet relatif* — contract normally only binds parties (art. 1165).

---

## Parties

| Party | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Stipulant** | Contracting party who creates the benefit |
| **Promettant** | Contracting party who promises to perform |
| **Tiers bénéficiaire** | Third party who receives the benefit |

---

## Conditions (art. 1121)

### 1. Valid Contract

Must be embedded in valid underlying contract between stipulant and promettant.

### 2. Benefit to Third Party

Clear intention to confer **benefit** on identified or identifiable third party.

### 3. Stipulant's Interest

Stipulant must have **personal interest** in the stipulation:
- Material interest, OR
- Moral interest

> **Art. 1121**: "...lorsque telle est la condition d'une stipulation que l'on fait pour soi-même ou d'une donation que l'on fait à un autre."

---

## Effects

### Third Party's Rights

#### Direct Right

Beneficiary acquires **direct right** against promettant:
- Not derived through stipulant
- Arises from moment of contract (or acceptance)

#### No Obligations

Third party acquires **rights only** — not bound by contract obligations.

### Acceptance

| Timing | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Before acceptance | Stipulant can revoke |
| After acceptance | Right becomes irrevocable |

Acceptance can be **tacit** — no formality required.

### Revocation

Stipulant may revoke benefit **before** third party accepts.

After acceptance → benefit locked in.

---

## Promettant's Defenses

Against beneficiary, promettant may raise:
- Defenses from underlying contract
- Nullity of stipulation
- Non-performance by stipulant (exception d'inexécution)

---

## Common Applications

| Context | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **Life insurance** | Insured (stipulant) designates beneficiary |
| **Transport** | Sender stipulates delivery to recipient |
| **Construction** | Owner stipulates in favor of future purchaser |
| **Trust-like arrangements** | Settler creates benefit for beneficiary |

---

## Distinction from Related Concepts

| Concept | Key Difference |
|---------|----------------|
| **Porte-fort** | Promising third party will do something (art. 1120) |
| **Cession de créance** | Transfer of existing right |
| **Mandate** | Acting on behalf of, not creating rights for |

---

## Banking Application

### Guarantee Structures

Stipulation in favor of lenders:
- Parent company contract with subsidiary
- Benefit to financing banks

### Insurance Products

Life insurance policies:
- Classic stipulation pour autrui
- Bank as beneficiary for collateral purposes

### Payment Instructions

Standing orders benefiting third parties:
- Regular payments to beneficiaries
- Direct debit authorizations

### Trust-Adjacent Structures

Luxembourg fiduciary arrangements:
- Contractual "trust" using stipulation
- Beneficiary rights against fiduciary
