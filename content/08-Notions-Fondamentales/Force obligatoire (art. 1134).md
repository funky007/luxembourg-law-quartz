# Force obligatoire (art. 1134)

> **Type**: Fundamental contractual principle  
> **Article**: 1134 Code civil  
> **Maxim**: Pacta sunt servanda

---

## The Principle — Convention-Loi

> **Art. 1134 al. 1**: "Les conventions légalement formées tiennent lieu de loi à ceux qui les ont faites."

Contracts legally formed have **force of law** for the parties.

---

## Three Alinéas of Art. 1134

| Alinéa | Content | Principle |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **Al. 1** | Conventions = law for parties | Force obligatoire |
| **Al. 2** | Revocation only by mutual consent or law | Irrévocabilité |
| **Al. 3** | Must be executed in good faith | [[Bonne foi (art. 1134 al. 3)]] |

---

## Consequences of Force Obligatoire

### 1. Binding on Parties

Parties **must perform** their obligations:
- Cannot unilaterally escape
- Must execute what was promised

### 2. Binding on Judge

Judge **cannot**:
- Dispense party from obligations
- Modify contract content
- Add or remove clauses

**Exception**: When law permits (e.g., [[Clause pénale (art. 1152)]] modification).

### 3. Irrevocability (al. 2)

Contract can only be revoked by:
- **Mutual consent** (*mutuus dissensus*)
- **Causes authorized by law** (resolution, nullity, etc.)

---

## Limits

### Ordre Public

Cannot contract against:
- Public policy
- Good morals (*bonnes mœurs*)
- Mandatory rules (*lois impératives*)

### Imprévision (Hardship)

> **Luxembourg position**: No general doctrine of *imprévision*.

Changed circumstances making performance more difficult (but not impossible) do **not** excuse performance.

**Contrast**: [[Force majeure (arts. 1147-1148)]] requires **impossibility**.

### Good Faith Modulation

[[Bonne foi (art. 1134 al. 3)]] may modulate strict application:
- Devoir de modération
- Cannot exploit technical breaches abusively

---

## Autonomie de la Volonté

Art. 1134 reflects **party autonomy**:
- Freedom to contract
- Freedom to choose terms
- Freedom to choose counterparty
- Freedom to choose applicable law (within limits)

---

## Contractual vs. Statutory Obligations

| Source | Binding Force |
|--------|---------------|
| **Contract** | Art. 1134 — as between parties |
| **Law** | General application |

Contract binds **inter partes** only — see effet relatif (art. 1165).

---

## Banking Application

### Facility Agreements

Once signed:
- Bank must make funds available (per terms)
- Borrower must repay
- Neither can unilaterally modify

### Amendment Requirements

Changes require:
- Written agreement (typically)
- All party signatures
- Sometimes lender consent for waivers

### MAC Clauses

Material Adverse Change clauses:
- Contractually agreed escape mechanism
- Application of convention-loi principle
