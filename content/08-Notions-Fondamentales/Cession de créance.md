# Cession de créance

> **Type**: Transfer of obligations (creditor side)  
> **Articles**: 1689-1701 Code civil  
> **Effect**: Transfer of claim from cedant to cessionnaire

---

## Definition

> Contract by which creditor (*cédant*) transfers their claim to a third party (*cessionnaire*) against the debtor (*débiteur cédé*).

---

## Parties

| Party | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Cédant** | Original creditor, transfers claim |
| **Cessionnaire** | New creditor, acquires claim |
| **Débiteur cédé** | Debtor, now owes cessionnaire |

---

## Formalities

### Between Parties (Cédant ↔ Cessionnaire)

> **Art. 1689**: Transfer effective by **mere agreement**.

No specific form required — consensual.

### Against Debtor and Third Parties

> **Art. 1690**: Transfer only opposable after:
> - **Signification** (formal notification by huissier), OR
> - **Acceptation** by debtor in authentic act

**Critical**: Until art. 1690 formality completed:
- Debtor can validly pay cédant
- Other creditors can seize the claim
- Competing cessionnaires may have priority

---

## What Transfers

### The Claim Itself

Principal amount owed.

### Accessories (art. 1692)

> Transfer includes all **accessory rights**:
> - Sureties (*cautionnements*)
> - Privileges
> - Mortgages (*hypothèques*)
> - Interest claims

### Limits

Cannot transfer:
- Claims attached to person (*intuitu personae*)
- Claims declared non-transferable by contract
- Certain statutory restrictions

---

## Debtor's Position

### Defenses Preserved

Debtor may raise against cessionnaire **all defenses** they could have raised against cédant:
- Nullity of underlying obligation
- Compensation (if conditions met before notification)
- Payment already made (before notification)

### No Consent Required

Debtor's consent **not needed** for valid transfer — but must be notified.

---

## Warranty by Cédant

### Existence of Claim (art. 1693)

Cédant warrants that:
- Claim **exists**
- Cédant has **right to transfer**

### Solvency of Debtor

Cédant does **NOT** warrant debtor's solvency unless expressly agreed.

If agreed — only covers solvency **at time of transfer**, not future.

---

## Special Regime: Loi du 22 mars 2004

### Simplified Transfer

For certain financial claims, Luxembourg law allows transfer by:
- Simple written agreement
- Without art. 1690 formalities

### Scope

Applies to securitization and certain financial transactions.

---

## Banking Application

### Loan Transfers

Banks regularly transfer loan portfolios:
- Art. 1690 compliance essential
- Often use simplified regime where applicable

### Factoring

Purchase of receivables:
- Cession de créance structure
- Notification to debtors critical

### Security Assignments

Claims assigned as security:
- Accessory rights follow
- Formalities determine priority

### Syndicated Loans

Transfer provisions in facility agreements:
- Contractual mechanisms
- Often require borrower notification/consent
