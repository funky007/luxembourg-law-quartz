# Obligations à terme (Term Obligations)

> **Type**: Deferred obligation
> **Articles**: 1185-1188, 1244 Code civil
> **Source**: Poelmans §§291-305

---

## Definition

**Poelmans §292**: The *terme* is a future and certain event upon which depends either the **exigibility** or the **extinction** of a right.

| Type | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Terme suspensif** | Defers exigibility until event occurs |
| **Terme extinctif** | Ends obligation upon event occurrence |

**Certitude requirement**: Event must be certain to occur (unlike condition).

---

## Term vs. Condition

| Feature | Terme | Condition |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| **Event** | Certain to occur | Uncertain |
| **Effect** | Defers exigibility | Affects existence |
| **Wording** | "dès l'arrivée" (when it arrives) | "s'il arrive" (if it arrives) |

**Trib. Lux., 7 juillet 2009, n° 114132**: When parties promise to perform "dès l'arrivée" of event, treating it as certain, this is terme not condition.

---

## Effects of Terme Suspensif

### Before Term Arrives (art. 1185-1186)

The right **exists** but is not yet exigible:
- No payment can be demanded
- No compensation operates (art. 1291)
- Prescription suspended (art. 2257)
- Creditor may take conservatory measures
- Creditor may sue for declaratory judgment

**CA, 15 mai 2002, n° 25788**: In a *vente à terme*, contract is formed and ownership transfers immediately — only **execution** (delivery and payment) is deferred.

**Art. 1186**: Payment before term cannot be recovered (paid voluntarily).

### In Whose Favor (art. 1187)

**Presumption**: Term established in favor of **debtor**.

**Effect**: Debtor may renounce and perform early.

**Rebuttal**: By clause, proof, or law that term benefits creditor or both parties.

**CSSF Rapport 2005, p. 154**: Right to prepay mortgage depends on contract terms.

**Trib. Lux., 20 décembre 2006, n° 86063**: Without contrary clause, debtor may prepay without penalty.

### At Term Arrival (art. 1186)

Obligation becomes **immediately exigible** — no retroactivity.

**Nature of debtor's obligation**: Obligation de résultat — debtor presumed at fault if non-performance, unless force majeure proven.

**Trib. Lux., 24 octobre 2008, n° 114091**: Contractor failed to prove force majeure for delay.

## Imperative vs. Indicative Terms

**Issue**: When is a delivery date truly a binding term?

**Poelmans §296**: Due to severe consequences of non-respect, **precise and formal indications** required to establish imperative term.

**Presumption**: In doubt, term is merely **indicative**.

**Factors considered**:
- Nature of thing sold
- Intended use
- Professional context
- Express contract language

---

## Forfeiture of Term (Déchéance du terme)

**Art. 1188**: Debtor loses benefit of term when:
- Declared bankrupt (*faillite*)
- Diminishes securities given to creditor

**Effect**: Entire obligation becomes immediately due.

---

## Term and Pacte sur Succession Future

**CA, 4 juin 2003, n° 26058**: Contract with term at death of party is NOT prohibited *pacte sur succession future*, but valid contract with suspensive term.

**Distinction**: Contract creates immediate right of creditor, exercisable against succession — not wagering on inheritance.

## Banking Application

### Loan Agreements

| Issue | Analysis |
|-------|----------|
| **Prepayment right** | Depends on who benefits from term |
| **Prepayment penalty** | Only if contractually agreed |
| **Acceleration clause** | Converts term to immediate exigibility |

### Bonds/Obligations

Term obligations commonly used in structured products — term may be at issuer's discretion.
