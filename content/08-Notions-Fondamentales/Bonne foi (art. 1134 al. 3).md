# Bonne foi (art. 1134 al. 3)

> **Type**: Fundamental contractual principle  
> **Article**: 1134 alinéa 3 Code civil  
> **Effect**: Creates supplementary duties beyond express terms

---

## The Principle

> **Art. 1134 al. 3**: Contracts "doivent être exécutées de bonne foi."

Good faith is not merely absence of bad faith — it imposes **positive duties**.

---

## Three Duties

### 1. Devoir de Loyauté (Duty of Loyalty)

| Requirement | Application |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Honest dealing** | No exploitation of loopholes |
| **Information** | Provide necessary information |
| **No frustration** | Don't deprive other party of contract's normal benefits |

**Case**: Real estate agent found buyer at 8M LUF but didn't inform seller of other buyer offering 9M LUF → breach of loyalty, contractual liability.

### 2. Devoir de Modération (Duty of Moderation)

| Requirement | Application |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Proportionate enforcement** | No overly scrupulous demands |
| **Reasonable exercise of rights** | Consider other party's interests |
| **No abuse** | Avoid technical traps |

### 3. Devoir de Collaboration (Duty of Cooperation)

| Requirement | Application |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Facilitate performance** | Help other party perform |
| **Technical assistance** | Share necessary expertise |
| **Problem-solving** | Work together on difficulties |

**Case**: Subcontractor followed improper instructions → still liable because duty to advise and refuse manifestly inappropriate orders.

---

## Pre-contractual Application

Good faith applies even before contract formation:
- **Culpa in contrahendo**: Pre-contractual liability
- **Devoir de loyauté**: Cannot break negotiations abruptly without reason
- See [[02 - Formation of Contracts]] for details

---

## Condition Accomplishment (art. 1178)

> Condition is deemed fulfilled if debtor **prevented its accomplishment**.

**Poelmans**: This is an application of bonne foi — creates **positive obligation** to facilitate condition.

**Case**: Buyer under condition of obtaining credit who didn't request full amount from bank → condition deemed fulfilled, buyer bound.

---

## Employment Application

Before specific harassment legislation, good faith (art. 1134 al. 3) sanctioned **harcèlement moral**:
- Degrading or humiliating repeated acts
- Excessive overtime affecting health
- Abusive use of disciplinary power

---

## Interpretation Role

> Good faith informs **interpretation** of contracts (art. 1156).

Judge considers bonne foi when determining parties' intentions.

---

## Limits

Good faith cannot:
- Override express clear terms
- Create obligations parties explicitly excluded
- Rewrite bad bargains

---

## Banking Application

### Information Duties

Banks owe extensive information duties:
- Suitability of products
- Risk warnings
- Fee disclosures

Breach may engage contractual liability via art. 1134 al. 3.

### Enforcement of Security

Must exercise rights in good faith:
- Reasonable notice before enforcement
- Proportionate enforcement actions
- Consider debtor's situation (within limits)

### Termination of Facilities

Even if contractually permitted:
- Good faith modulates exercise
- Reasonable notice may be required
- Brutale rupture may be abusive
