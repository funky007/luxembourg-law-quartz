# Gestion d'affaires

> **Type**: Quasi-contract  
> **Articles**: 1372-1375 Code civil  
> **Nature**: Voluntary management of another's affairs without mandate

---

## Definition

> Voluntary intervention in another's affairs **without mandate**, creating obligations for both parties.

The *gérant* (manager) acts for the *maître* (principal) without being asked.

---

## Conditions

### 1. Intervention in Another's Affairs

Act must concern **another person's** interests:
- Property management
- Legal acts on their behalf
- Material acts for their benefit

### 2. Without Mandate

No **authorization** — express or implied:
- No contract
- No legal duty to act
- Purely voluntary

### 3. Useful Intervention

Act must be **utile** (useful/beneficial):
- Objectively necessary or advantageous
- Judged at time of intervention (not outcome)

### 4. Intention to Manage for Another

*Animus gerendi* — intention to act for another's benefit, not one's own.

---

## Obligations of the Gérant

### Standard of Care

> **Art. 1374**: Must bring to management the care of a *bon père de famille*.

### Complete the Management

Once begun, must see it through — cannot abandon mid-way.

### Account to the Maître

Must render accounts of actions taken and funds handled.

---

## Obligations of the Maître

### Reimburse Expenses

> **Art. 1375**: Must reimburse gérant for all **useful and necessary** expenses.

### Indemnify for Losses

Must compensate gérant for losses incurred in management.

### Perform Engagements

Bound by contracts gérant made on their behalf (if within scope).

---

## Effects

### Ratification

If maître **ratifies** → converts to mandate retroactively.

### No Ratification

Obligations still arise from quasi-contract itself.

---

## Distinction from Related Concepts

| Concept | Key Difference |
|---------|----------------|
| **Mandat** | Authorization exists |
| **Enrichissement sans cause** | Subsidiary; no voluntary act required |
| **Negotiorum gestio** | Latin term for same concept |

---

## Banking Application

### Emergency Actions

Bank acting to protect client's interests without specific instruction:
- Preserving collateral value
- Emergency payments to prevent loss

### Limits

Banks typically have contractual authority — true gestion d'affaires rare.
