# Compensation

> **Type**: Extinction by set-off  
> **Articles**: 1289-1299 Code civil  
> **Effect**: Automatic extinction of reciprocal debts

---

## Definition

> **Compensation** = automatic extinction of two reciprocal debts up to the **concurrent amount**.

---

## Automatic Operation

> **Art. 1290**: "La compensation s'opère de plein droit par la seule force de la loi, même à l'insu des débiteurs."

Operates **automatically** — even unknown to parties.

---

## Conditions for Legal Compensation

### Five Cumulative Conditions

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **1. Réciprocité** | Same parties, in same capacities |
| **2. Fongibilité** | Same kind (usually money) |
| **3. Liquidité** | Amount determined/determinable |
| **4. Exigibilité** | Both debts due |
| **5. Saisissabilité** | Both debts seizable |

---

## Detailed Conditions

### 1. Réciprocité

Each party is **both creditor and debtor** of the other.

**Same capacity required**: Cannot set off personal debt against representative capacity debt.

### 2. Fongibilité

Both debts must be of **same nature**:
- Money against money ✓
- Grain against grain ✓
- Money against goods ✗

### 3. Liquidité

Debt is **liquid** when amount is:
- Fixed, or
- Determinable by simple calculation

**Disputed debt** = not liquid → no compensation.

### 4. Exigibilité

Both debts must be **currently due**:
- Not subject to term
- Not conditional

### 5. Saisissabilité

Cannot set off against **unseizable** debts:
- Alimony
- Minimum wage portion
- Specific protected amounts

---

## Types of Compensation

| Type | Basis | Conditions |
|------|-------|------------|
| **Légale** | Art. 1289 | All 5 conditions met |
| **Conventionnelle** | Agreement | Parties can modify conditions |
| **Judiciaire** | Court order | When one debt not liquid |

---

## Effects

### Extinction

Both debts extinguished to extent of **lesser amount**.

### Accessory Rights

Sureties and other accessories extinguished pro tanto.

### Retroactivity

Operates from moment conditions were met — even if invoked later.

---

## Exclusions

Compensation **cannot** occur for:
- Claims arising from **fraud** (art. 1293)
- Claims for **restitution** of deposited/loaned thing
- **Alimony** claims
- Claims **after saisie-arrêt** by third party

---

## Invoking Compensation

### Automatic but Must Be Raised

Though automatic, party must **invoke** it:
- As defense in litigation
- By declaration to other party

### Not Raised = Waived?

Failure to invoke → may be deemed waiver (debate).

---

## Banking Application

### Close-Out Netting

**Contractual compensation** — agreements to net mutual exposures:
- ISDA Master Agreements
- GMRA (repos)
- Banking facility set-off clauses

### Importance in Insolvency

Properly documented netting:
- Reduces credit exposure
- May survive insolvency (if valid)
- Regulated by Financial Collateral Directive (transposed)

### Current Account

Mutual debts and credits automatically compensated in current account — special regime.
