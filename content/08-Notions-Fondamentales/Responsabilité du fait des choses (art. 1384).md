# Responsabilité du fait des choses (art. 1384)

> **Type**: Extra-contractual (strict) liability  
> **Article**: 1384 alinéa 1 Code civil  
> **Nature**: Presumption of fault against guardian

---

## The Principle

> **Art. 1384 al. 1**: "On est responsable non seulement du dommage que l'on cause par son propre fait, mais encore de celui qui est causé par le fait des personnes dont on doit répondre, ou **des choses que l'on a sous sa garde**."

Liability for damage caused by **things** under one's custody.

---

## Three Conditions

### 1. Chose (Thing)

**Any corporeal thing**:
- Movable or immovable
- Animate or inanimate
- Dangerous or not

**No distinction** based on inherent danger — all things covered.

### 2. Garde (Custody)

The **gardien** is person with:
- **Use** (*usage*)
- **Direction** (*direction*)
- **Control** (*contrôle*)

of the thing.

**Not necessarily owner** — custody is factual, not legal.

### 3. Fait de la Chose (Active Role)

Thing must have played **active role** in causing damage:
- Intervention of the thing
- Causal connection to damage

---

## Presumption Against Gardien

### Nature

**Presumption of fault** (not strict liability in pure sense).

### Rebuttal

Gardien can escape only by proving:
- [[Force majeure (arts. 1147-1148)]]
- **Fault of victim** (*fait de la victime*)
- **Fault of third party** (*fait d'un tiers*)

**Cannot escape** by proving:
- Own absence of fault
- Thing was not defective
- All precautions taken

---

## Transfer of Garde

Custody transfers with **effective control**:
- Lending thing → garde may transfer to borrower
- Theft → garde transfers to thief
- Professional custody → professional becomes gardien

---

## Structure of Art. 1384

| Alinéa | Liability For |
|--------|---------------|
| **Al. 1** | Things under custody |
| **Al. 3** | Servants (*préposés*) |
| **Al. 4** | Minors (parents) |
| **Al. 5** | Pupils (teachers) |

---

## Relationship with Contractual Liability

### Non-Cumul Applies

If contract exists → [[Non-cumul des responsabilités]] prevents using art. 1384 between parties.

### Third Party Claims

Third parties (not in contract) may invoke art. 1384 against gardien.

---

## Banking Application

### Limited Direct Application

Banks rarely "guardians" of things in traditional sense.

### Potential Scenarios

- Bank's premises causing injury (building as thing)
- ATM malfunctions
- Safe deposit boxes

### More Relevant

- Art. 1384 al. 3: Bank liable for employees' acts
- General delictual liability (art. 1382)
