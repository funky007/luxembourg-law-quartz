# Exception d'inexécution

> **Type**: Defensive remedy for non-performance  
> **Nature**: Temporary suspension, not termination  
> **Requirement**: Synallagmatic contract with reciprocal obligations

---

## Definition

> The right of a party to **withhold own performance** until the other party performs their obligation.

A **defensive** mechanism — not an offensive remedy like resolution.

---

## Conditions

### 1. Synallagmatic Contract

Must be a contract with **reciprocal obligations**.

### 2. Reciprocal Obligations

The obligations must be **connected** — arising from the same contract.

### 3. Simultaneous Performance Due

Both obligations must be **due at the same time**.

If creditor's obligation not yet due → cannot invoke exception.

### 4. Proportionality

The exception must be **proportionate** to the breach:
- Cannot withhold major performance for minor breach
- Must be reasonable response

### 5. Good Faith

Exercise must respect [[Bonne foi (art. 1134 al. 3)]]:
- Not used as pretext
- Not abusive

---

## Effects

### Temporary Suspension

- Own obligation **suspended**, not extinguished
- When other party performs → must perform own obligation
- No damages during legitimate suspension

### Not Termination

Does **not** end the contract — contrast with [[Résolution judiciaire (art. 1184)]].

### No Judicial Intervention Required

Can be invoked **unilaterally** — no need to go to court first.

But: Subject to judicial review if disputed.

---

## Comparison with Other Remedies

| Remedy | Nature | Effect |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Exception d'inexécution** | Defensive | Temporary suspension |
| **Résolution** | Offensive | Termination |
| **Droit de rétention** | Defensive | Retain thing until paid |

---

## Practical Application

### Sequence

1. Other party fails to perform
2. Own obligation becomes due
3. Invoke exception (notify other party)
4. Suspend own performance
5. Wait for other party to perform

### Risk

If exception found **unjustified**:
- Own non-performance becomes breach
- Liable for damages

---

## Banking Application

### Payment vs. Delivery

Bank may withhold:
- Loan disbursement until conditions precedent satisfied
- Documents until payment received

### Letters of Credit

Strict documentary compliance:
- Discrepancies = right to refuse payment
- Related to exception principle
