# Responsabilité contractuelle (art. 1147)

> **Type**: Liability for contract breach  
> **Article**: 1147 Code civil  
> **Scope**: Between contracting parties only

---

## The Principle

> **Art. 1147**: "Le débiteur est condamné, s'il y a lieu, au paiement de dommages et intérêts, soit à raison de l'inexécution de l'obligation, soit à raison du retard dans l'exécution, toutes les fois qu'il ne justifie pas que l'inexécution provient d'une cause étrangère qui ne peut lui être imputée, encore qu'il n'y ait aucune mauvaise foi de sa part."

---

## Three Conditions

```
RESPONSABILITÉ CONTRACTUELLE
        │
        ├─→ 1. INEXÉCUTION (breach)
        │
        ├─→ 2. DOMMAGE (damage)
        │
        └─→ 3. LIEN DE CAUSALITÉ (causation)
```

---

## Proof of Breach

### Depends on Obligation Type

| Type | Standard | Proof |
|------|----------|-------|
| **Obligation de résultat** | Achieve specific result | Failure = breach (fault presumed) |
| **Obligation de moyens** | Best efforts | Creditor must prove fault |
| **Obligation de moyens renforcée** | Best efforts + presumed fault | Luxembourg creation |

### Obligation de Résultat

- **Non-achievement = breach** (no need to prove fault)
- Debtor can only escape by proving [[Force majeure (arts. 1147-1148)]]
- Example: Deliver goods, pay money

### Obligation de Moyens

- **Creditor must prove fault** (lack of diligence)
- Standard: *bonus pater familias* in same circumstances
- Example: Medical treatment, legal advice

---

## Damages

### Compensatory Principle

Damages aim to **compensate** prejudice, not punish.

### Limitation to Foreseeable Damage

> **Art. 1150**: Debtor only liable for damage that was **foreseeable** at contract formation.

**Exception**: In case of **dol** (fraud) → liable for all direct damage.

### Types of Damage

| Type | Recoverable |
|------|-------------|
| **Damnum emergens** | Actual loss suffered — Yes |
| **Lucrum cessans** | Lost profits — Yes, if proven |
| **Moral damage** | Non-pecuniary — Yes, in principle |

---

## Exoneration

### Force Majeure

Three conditions (see [[Force majeure (arts. 1147-1148)]]):
- Extériorité
- Imprévisibilité  
- Irrésistibilité

### Fait du Créancier

Creditor's own fault contributed to damage.

### Fait d'un Tiers

Third party caused the damage (if conditions of force majeure met).

---

## Contractual Modifications

### Clause Limitative

Caps maximum liability — generally valid unless:
- Contrary to ordre public
- Excludes dol or faute lourde

### Clause Exonératoire

Excludes liability entirely — stricter scrutiny:
- Cannot exclude dol
- Cannot exclude faute lourde
- Consumer protection limits

### Clause Pénale

See [[Clause pénale (art. 1152)]] — pre-agreed damages.

---

## Non-Cumul

> **Fundamental**: Cannot combine with delictual liability.

See [[Non-cumul des responsabilités]] — if contract exists, liability is necessarily contractual.

---

## Banking Application

### Nature of Bank's Obligations

| Service | Obligation Type |
|---------|-----------------|
| Payment execution | Résultat |
| Investment advice | Moyens |
| Custody | Résultat (preservation) |
| Information | Moyens (generally) |

### Duty of Care

Professional standard — higher than ordinary *bonus pater familias*.

### Limitation Clauses

Common in banking contracts — subject to:
- Consumer protection rules
- Cannot exclude gross negligence
- Must be clear and accepted
