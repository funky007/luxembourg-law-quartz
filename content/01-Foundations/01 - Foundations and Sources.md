## 1. Notion of Obligation

An **obligation** is a *lien de droit* (legal bond) by which one or more persons may compel one or more others to give, do, or refrain from doing something.

### Key Characteristics

1. **Lien de droit** — juridically enforceable bond
2. **Pouvoir de contrainte** — power of compulsion through courts
3. **Patrimonial character** — relates to economic value

---

## 2. Obligation Juridique vs. Obligation Morale

| Juridical Obligation    | Moral Obligation   |
| ----------------------- | ------------------ |
| Enforceable by courts   | No legal sanction  |
| Creates rights & duties | Conscience only    |
| *Lien de droit* present | No *lien de droit* |

### Examples of Moral Obligations

Per Poelmans:
- Accords à caractère mondain (social arrangements)
- Arrangements familiaux
- Religious agreements  
- **Gentlemen's agreements** — manifestations of intent without legal force
- **Debt of gambling** (art. 1965 C.civ.) — legislatively designated as moral

> **Case**: Political engagements by government officials are moral obligations only. The Administration cannot contractually bind itself in areas of unilateral decision-making power.

## 4. Classification of Obligations

### 4.1 By Object

| Type             | Definition           | Example            |
| ---------------- | -------------------- | ------------------ |
| **Donner**       | Transfer ownership   | Sale of goods      |
| **Faire**        | Positive performance | Construction work  |
| **Ne pas faire** | Abstention           | Non-compete clause |

### 4.2 By Intensity (Critical for Banking)

| Type                               | Standard                      | Proof of Breach                   |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Obligation de résultat**         | Achieve specific result       | Failure = breach (presumed fault) |
| **Obligation de moyens**           | Best efforts only             | Creditor must prove fault         |
| **Obligation de moyens renforcée** | Best efforts + presumed fault | Luxembourg creation               |

> **Luxembourg Specificity**: The *obligation de moyen renforcée* is a Luxembourg jurisprudential creation, intermediate between moyens and résultat.

### 4.3 By Source

- **Obligations conventionnelles** — from contract
- **Obligations légales** — from law (art. 1370)
- **Obligations naturelles** — moral duties given legal effect upon voluntary execution

---

## 5. Banking & Finance Application

### Banker's Obligations

Typically classified as:
- **Obligation de moyens** for advice and information
- **Obligation de résultat** for execution of payment orders
- **Obligation de moyens renforcée** for due diligence (AML/KYC)

### Corporate Benefit Doctrine

Related to [[Cause (arts. 1131-1133)]] — upstream guarantees require demonstrable corporate benefit.
