# Gage sur métaux précieux fongibles (Pledge on Fungible Precious Metals)

> **Type**: Special Pledge (*gage spécial*)
> **Source**: Westendorf, T.1, §§695-698
> **Key Articles**: R.G.D. 18 décembre 1981; Loi 5 août 2005

---

## Overview

A specialized form of pledge for fungible precious metals, now governed by the [[13 - Contrat de garantie financière|financial collateral regime]].

> **Legal Basis**: Règlement grand-ducal modifié du 18 décembre 1981 concernant les dépôts fongibles de métaux précieux

---

## Scope of Application (§695)

### Definition of Fungible Precious Metals

Per Article 1 R.G.D. 18 December 1981:
> "Sont réputés fongibles les métaux précieux de même nature, de même forme et de qualité usuelle, déposés sans indication d'éléments d'identification, auprès des établissements de crédit et d'autres dépositaires professionnels de métaux précieux autorisés au Grand-Duché de Luxembourg"

**Requirements**:
- Same nature (*même nature*)
- Same form (*même forme*)
- Usual quality (*qualité usuelle*)
- Deposited **without** identification elements
- With credit institutions or authorized professional precious metals depositaries

### Form of Deposits (Art. 2 R.G.D.)

Deposits may be represented by:
- Account entries (*remises en compte*)
- Bearer titles (*titres au porteur*)
- Registered titles (*titres nominatifs*)

### Key Distinction

The **object** of the pledge is NOT the precious metals themselves, but the **title** (*titre*) giving right to restitution - the account entries or securities.

### Restitution (Art. 3 R.G.D.)

Depositaries validly discharge their restitution obligations by delivering precious metal of:
- Same nature and form as indicated in account/title
- Usual quality

Differences in weight/quality are compensated in cash at market rate on day of restitution demand.

---

## Applicable Regime (§696)

### Reference to Financial Collateral Law

Per Article 6(2) R.G.D. (as modified by loi du 20 juillet 2022):
> "Les gages sur métaux précieux fongibles sont régis par la loi modifiée du 5 août 2005 sur les contrats de garantie financière"

**Result**: Full application of [[13 - Contrat de garantie financière|CGF regime]] including:
- Simplified formation requirements
- Protection against insolvency
- Accelerated enforcement methods

---

## Constitution of Pledge (Art. 6(1) R.G.D.)

The Grand-Ducal Regulation provides **non-exhaustive** rules on formal constitution (inspired by banking practices for cash pledges and securities pledges).

### Three Modes of Dispossession

| Type of Metal | Mode of Dispossession |
|---------------|----------------------|
| **Account entries** | Delivery without identification to special account with depositary (as pledgee or third holder) |
| **Non-endorsable registered title** | Transfer as guarantee (*transfert à titre de garantie*) inscribed in depositary's registers |
| **Endorsable registered/bearer title** | Delivery of title (*remise du titre*) to pledgee or third holder |

### Supplementary Rules

For all other matters (including enforcement/realization): refer to loi 5 août 2005 as *droit commun* in this context.

---

## Practical Considerations

### Banking Application

**Common use cases**:
- Loans secured by gold/silver holdings
- Margin for derivatives transactions
- Collateral for trading facilities

**Advantages**:
- Fungibility allows efficient management
- CGF regime provides strong creditor protection
- Simplified enforcement in case of default

### Due Diligence Points

1. Verify depositary is authorized in Luxembourg
2. Ensure proper account segregation
3. Confirm pledge registration/inscription completed
4. Document quality and quantity clearly

## Common Law Comparison

### vs. English Financial Collateral Arrangements

| Feature | Gage sur métaux précieux (Lux) | FCA Regulations (UK) |
|---------|-------------------------------|---------------------|
| **Governing Law** | Loi 5 août 2005 (CGF) | Financial Collateral Arrangements (No.2) Regulations 2003 |
| **Scope** | Fungible precious metals only | Cash, securities, credit claims |
| **Fungibility** | Required (same nature/form/quality) | Implied for cash/securities |
| **Dispossession** | Account delivery or title transfer | Possession or control |
| **Enforcement** | Accelerated (CGF regime) | Self-help appropriation permitted |
| **Insolvency protection** | Strong (Art. 20 CGF) | Strong (disapplication of insolvency rules) |

### vs. UCC Article 8 (Investment Property)

| Feature | Luxembourg Regime | UCC Article 8 |
|---------|------------------|---------------|
| **Concept** | Security over fungible deposited metals | Security interest in investment property |
| **Perfection** | Account segregation/title transfer | Control agreement or possession |
| **Priority** | CGF rules apply | Control beats filing |
| **Commingling** | Permitted (fungible by definition) | Permitted for securities accounts |
| **Restitution** | Same nature/form, usual quality | Equivalent securities |

### Key Distinctions

**1. Asset Type Specificity**
- Luxembourg: Specific regime for precious metals as distinct asset class
- Common law: Precious metals typically treated as commodities or goods, not as financial collateral

**2. Depositary Framework**
- Luxembourg: Must be with credit institution or authorized professional depositary
- UK/US: Broader range of intermediaries possible

**3. Title vs. Security Interest**
- Luxembourg: Object is the *titre* (account entry/certificate), not the metal itself
- Common law: Security interest attaches to the underlying commodity or its representation
