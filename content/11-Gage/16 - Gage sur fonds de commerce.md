# Gage sur fonds de commerce (Pledge on Business Assets)

> **Type**: Special Pledge (*gage sans dépossession*)
> **Source**: Westendorf, T.1, §§700-774
> **Key Articles**: Arrêté grand-ducal du 27 mai 1937

---

## Overview

A specialized non-possessory pledge (*gage sans dépossession*) enabling merchants to secure loans using their business assets (*fonds de commerce*) without losing physical possession.

---

## Purpose and Nature (§700-701)

### Rationale

**Westendorf, T.1, §700**:
The 1937 Grand-Ducal Decree aimed to enable small merchants (*petits commerçants*) to obtain credit more easily. Often these merchants:
- Had no real property to hypothecate
- Could not grant a possessory pledge over their movables (needed for business operations)
- Required security over their "sole instrument of credit" — the *fonds de commerce*

### Legal Sources (§701)

**Hierarchy of applicable rules**:
1. **Arrêté grand-ducal 27 mai 1937** — special regime
2. **Code de commerce** — default rules (Art. 1 of arrêté)
3. **Code civil** — residual application where not contrary to spirit of the pledge

## Section 1: Formation

### A. Substantive Conditions (*Conditions de fond*)

#### I. Secured Claim (*Créance garantie*) (§705-706)

**Any claim may be secured** (§705):
- Including future claims
- Must specify amount in the registration bordereau
- Term must be indicated

**Interest limitation** (§706):
Per Art. 9 of the Loi du 18 avril 1910 (applicable via Art. 10 of arrêté):
> Privilege only covers interest/arrears for **three years**

To secure additional interest, separate inscription required.

#### II. Object of Pledge — Fonds de Commerce (§707-714)

**Elements that may be included** (Art. 2 arrêté) (§707):
- Clientele (*clientèle*)
- Trade name (*enseigne*)
- Concession
- Commercial organization
- Trademarks (*marques*)
- Patents (*brevets d'invention*)
- Right to lease (*droit au bail*)
- Furniture used in business (*mobilier servant à l'exploitation*)
- Equipment and materials (*outillage et matériel*), including vehicles
- **Merchandise in stock** — up to 50% of value only

> **Westendorf, T.1, §707**: In practice, creditors want the pledge to cover as many elements as possible, so exhaustive enumeration in the pledge agreement is recommended.

**Stock limitations**:
- Merchandise only included if **expressly mentioned** in bordereau (Art. 4(3)(3°))
- Limited to **50% of value** — protecting chirographic creditors
- Raw materials (*matières premières*): May be included at **100%** of value (Belgian doctrine, §708)

**Cabaretage licenses** (§708):
- Fixed licenses (*privilèges de cabaretage*): Cannot be pledged
- Floating licenses (*concessions volantes*): Can be included (with exceptions)

**Universality concept** (§709):

**Trib. Luxembourg, 13 décembre 1985, n° 641/85**:
> "Les éléments du fonds de commerce constituent une universalité mobilière qui possède une individualité propre, son existence juridique demeure indépendante de celle de ses éléments qui concourent à sa formation."

The pledge never covers individual elements in isolation — always the universality as a global value.

**Immovables by destination** (§710):
Belgian jurisprudence (Cass. belge, 26 mai 1972) admits these may be included. This avoids different treatment between:
- Grantor who is tenant (more assets available)
- Grantor who is owner (assets become immovable by destination)

**Subrogation into indemnity claims** (§711):
Per Art. 70 of Loi du 27 juillet 1997 sur le contrat d'assurance:
> Insurance proceeds covering loss/deterioration of pledged assets are allocated to privileged creditors according to rank.

**Pledge on isolated elements** (§712):
Cannot constitute a *gage sur fonds de commerce* on only certain isolated elements.

**Trib. Luxembourg, 13 décembre 1985, n° 641/85**:
> Objects not constituting a fonds de commerce (lacking incorporeal elements, not serving commercial activity) cannot be subject to this pledge.

**Exception**: Art. 28 arrêté permits pledge on stocks/raw materials alone (introduced by loi 21 décembre 1994).

**Future object** (§713):
French jurisprudence admits pledge on a *fonds de commerce* in formation, provided it can be sufficiently identified.

#### III. The Parties (§715-717)

**A. Beneficiary — Limited Circle** (§716)

**Art. 12 arrêté**: Pledge may only be granted to:
- Credit institutions (*établissements de crédit*) approved by Government
- Breweries (*brasseries*) approved by Government

**Cour d'appel, 22 novembre 1995, n° 17.677**:
> "L'article 12 de l'arrêté grand-ducal du 27 mai 1937 [...] réserve le bénéfice du gage sur fonds de commerce aux seules personnes, banques, établissements de crédit [...] ou brasseries agréées par le Gouvernement."

**Sanction**: Absolute nullity (Cass. belge, 4 février 1966)

**Exception**: Vendor of fonds de commerce may stipulate pledge for sale price even without approval.

**Cession of secured claims**: Only to approved persons/institutions, otherwise pledge is null.

**B. Grantor** (§717)

Must be **owner** of the fonds de commerce.

**Liège, 22 décembre 1992**:
> "Le débiteur doit, pour l'engager, être propriétaire de son fonds de commerce ; qu'à défaut, la sûreté est nulle."

**Art. 2279 C.civ. protection**: Generally considered inapplicable to incorporeal universalities like fonds de commerce. However, Luxembourg jurisprudence tends to admit possession rules for incorporeal movables (Cour d'appel, 16 novembre 2011, n° 31.289).

---

### B. Formal Conditions (*La forme*) (§718-729)

#### I. Absence of Physical Dispossession (§719-720)

**Gage sans dépossession matérielle** (§719):
If dispossession were required, grantor would be deprived of means to operate business — contrary to the security's purpose.

**Trib. Luxembourg, 28 avril 2014, n° 466/2014, Pas., 37, p. 206**:
> "Dans le cadre des travaux parlementaires ayant abouti à la loi du 21 décembre 1994 il avait été envisagé d'ajouter dans l'arrêté grand-ducal du 27 mai 1937 une disposition en vertu de laquelle l'inscription valablement faite du gage réaliserait la dépossession du débiteur. Mais cette modification n'a pas été retenue à cause d'une certaine hostilité à l'introduction de la notion de dépossession fictive du débiteur sur fonds de commerce donné en gage."

**Debtor as guardian** (§720):
Art. 13 arrêté constitutes debtor as guardian (*gardien*) of pledge elements — deemed holder for creditor's account.

**Criminal sanctions** (Art. 13(2) arrêté):
Fraudulent alienation or displacement punishable under Art. 491 Code pénal (breach of trust).

#### II. Written Instrument (§722)

**Art. 3 arrêté**: Pledge constituted by:
- Authentic act (*acte authentique*), OR
- Private deed (*acte sous seing privé*)

**Cour d'appel, 28 octobre 1969, Pas., 21, p. 232**:
> "(résumé) En disposant à l'article 3 de l'arrêté grand-ducal du 27 mai 1937 [...] que le gage est constitué par acte authentique ou sous seing privé, l'auteur dudit texte a manifesté sa volonté de faire du gage du fonds de commerce un acte solennel requérant pour sa validité un acte écrit. Il s'ensuit qu'un accord purement verbal est insuffisant à réaliser la mise en gage d'un fonds de commerce."

**This is a solemn act** — writing required for validity, not merely proof.

#### III. Registration at Conservation des Hypothèques (§723-729)

**Location** (§723):
Bureau of Conservation des hypothèques of the judicial district where fonds de commerce is established.

**Multiple locations**: Strongly recommended to register in all relevant districts. Otherwise, only elements in registered district are covered.

**Effect of registration** (§724):
- Renders pledge **opposable to third parties**
- Conserves pledge for **10 years** (renewable before expiration — Art. 19)
- Priority among creditors determined by registration date (Art. 20)

**Sanction for formal defects** (Art. 9 arrêté) (§726):
> Omission of formalities only causes nullity **when prejudicial to a third party**.

Result: Pledge remains valid between parties even without proper formalities.

**Bankruptcy rules** (§729):
Arts. 445-448 Code de commerce apply. Inscription during suspect period or 10 days prior must occur within 15 days of constitution, otherwise may be declared null.

## Section 2: Effects

### A. Effects Between Parties (§731-742)

#### I. Grantor's Obligations (§732-733)

**Exploitation obligation** (§732):
Grantor must exploit the fonds de commerce and not let it perish or drain its substance through alienations or displacements.

**Sanctions** (§733):

1. **Civil**: Art. 1188 C.civ. — loss of benefit of term if debtor diminishes securities by his own act

2. **Criminal**: Art. 13(2) arrêté — fraudulent alienation/displacement punishable as breach of trust (Art. 491 Code pénal). Requires fraudulent intent.

#### II. Creditor's Rights (§734-742)

**A. No Right of Retention** (§736)

**Trib. Luxembourg, 1er juillet 1986, n° 280/86**:
> "les décisions citées par le curateur avaient toutes trait à des gages sur fonds de commerce, nantissements qui [...] diffère de la situation du créancier gagiste ordinaire en ce sens que le créancier bénéficiant d'un nantissement sur fonds de commerce n'est pas mis en possession de la chose donnée en gage, la jurisprudence refusant au créancier nanti sur fonds de commerce le droit de rétention."

**Consequence** (§737): Creditor cannot oppose seizure by third parties. But privilege must be respected in distribution of proceeds.

**B. Limited Right of Pursuit (*Droit de suite*)** (§738-742)

**On the fonds de commerce as whole** (§738):
If entire fonds is alienated, registered pledge is **maintained** — true *droit de suite* exists, provided fonds retains its identity.

**Cass. belge, 21 octobre 1999**:
Pledge subsists on transferred fonds de commerce.

**On individual elements** (§739-741):
Art. 15 arrêté: Creditor may seize raw materials, equipment, tools displaced without consent — within **40 days** of displacement.

**BUT**: Good faith acquirer may invoke **Art. 2279 C.civ.** — no true *droit de suite* on individual elements.

**Trib. Luxembourg, 28 avril 2014**:
> "le créancier qui ne détient pas le fonds est privé de son droit de rétention."

---

### B. Realization (§743-752)

#### Preliminary: Seizure (§744A)

**Art. 15 arrêté**: If debtor doesn't pay at term, creditor may seize without prior *mise en demeure*.

Seizure is **prerequisite** to realization (unlike possessory pledge).

#### Mode 1: Public Sale (§745-748)

**Procedure** (Art. 16 arrêté):
1. Seizure must be declared valid
2. President of Tribunal de commerce designates: location, conditions, public officer
3. Debtor summoned with 3 clear days' notice
4. Sale may be *en bloc* or *en détail*
5. Minimum 8 days between notification and sale

**Distribution** (Art. 18 arrêté):
- Public officer receives price
- 15-day waiting period
- If no opposition and no other inscriptions: pay creditor, return surplus to debtor
- If multiple inscriptions/oppositions: deposit at Caisse de consignation, distribution by contribution

#### Mode 2: Judicial Attribution (§749-750)

**Controversy**: Some authorities held attribution impossible for this pledge.

**Trib. Luxembourg, 22 février 1984**:
> Arrêté "ne prévoit pas l'attribution en propriété du fonds de commerce."

**Better view** (§750):
Art. 16(4) only prohibits **contractual** appropriation (*pacte commissoire*). Judicial attribution under Art. 117 Code de commerce should be available — involves court supervision, no danger to grantor.

Art. 14 arrêté (lease assignment clause) contemplates creditor becoming owner via *dation en paiement*.

#### Radiation (§751)

Per Art. 22 arrêté, registrations cancelled conforming to Arts. 2157+ C.civ. and Art. 7 Loi 18 avril 1910:
- By consent (authentic act)
- By final judgment

## Conflicts with Other Securities (§754-765)

### A. Pledge on Fonds de Commerce vs. Landlord's Privilege (§754)

**Art. 21 arrêté**: Pledge creditor's privilege **primes** landlord's if inscription is **anterior** to lease commencement.

For renewed/extended leases: landlord may rely on original lease date.

### B. Pledge on Fonds de Commerce vs. Pledge on Stocks (§756-762)

**Without dispossession** (§757):
If both under arrêté regime, pledge on fonds de commerce (covering 50% of stocks) is opposable. Stock pledgee has first rank on remaining 50%.

**With dispossession** (§758-762):

**Belgian position** (Cass. belge, 19 novembre 1992):
> Registration renders pledge opposable to third parties and determines preference by date — *prior tempore potior jure*.

**French position** (§761):
Right of retention is **absolutely opposable**. Possessory pledgee may oppose retention right even to prior registered pledge.

### C. Pledge on Fonds de Commerce vs. Retention of Title (§763-764)

**Art. 2279 C.civ. inapplicable** to non-possessory pledge.

**Cass. fr. com., 5 avril 1994**: Seller with retention of title prevails over non-possessory pledgee.

### D. Pledge on Fonds de Commerce vs. Hypothèque (§765)

If immovables by destination enter the pledge's scope, conflict resolved by **prior tempore** — both securities are registered.

## Banking Practice

### Advantages
- Grantor retains business assets for operations
- Credit facility for commercial sector
- Clear publicity through registration

### Risks
- No right of retention
- Limited *droit de suite* on individual elements (Art. 2279 defense)
- 50% cap on merchandise value
- Restricted circle of beneficiaries

### Due Diligence Checklist

1. Verify beneficiary has government approval
2. Search Conservation des hypothèques for existing pledges
3. Enumerate all elements clearly in pledge agreement
4. Ensure fonds de commerce has essential incorporeal elements
5. Register promptly (15-day rule during suspect period)
6. Renew registration before 10-year expiry
