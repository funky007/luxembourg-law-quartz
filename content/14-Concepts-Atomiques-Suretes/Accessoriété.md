# Accessory Character (Accessoriété)

> **Type**: Fundamental principle / Characteristic of security interests
> **Source**: Westendorf, T.1, §50-51; T.3, §5 (note 54)
> **Key Articles**: Arts. 2012-2013 C.civ. (cautionnement); Art. 2082 C.civ. (gage)
> **Related**: [[10 - Principes généraux des sûretés]]

---

## Definition

The accessory character (*accessoriété*) means that a security interest is dependent on and subordinate to the principal obligation it secures. The security cannot exist independently of a valid secured claim.

**Westendorf, T.1, §51**:
> "Le gage est un accessoire de la créance garantie, le caractère accessoire supposant une dépendance unilatérale. Cette dernière est le 'support' du gage, qui ne peut pas exister en l'absence de créance garantie."

*Translation*: "The pledge is an accessory of the secured claim, the accessory character presupposing a unilateral dependence. The latter is the 'support' of the pledge, which cannot exist in the absence of a secured claim."

---

## Legal Basis

### For Pledge (Gage)

The Code civil does not expressly state the accessory character of pledge, but it is implicit throughout:

> **Art. 2082 C.civ.**: "Le gage est indivisible nonobstant la divisibilité de la dette entre les héritiers du débiteur ou ceux du créancier."
>
> *Translation*: "The pledge is indivisible notwithstanding the divisibility of the debt between the heirs of the debtor or those of the creditor."

### For Suretyship (Cautionnement)

> **Art. 2012 C.civ.**: "Le cautionnement ne peut exister que sur une obligation valable."
>
> *Translation*: "A suretyship can only exist on a valid obligation."

> **Art. 2013 C.civ.**: "Le cautionnement ne peut excéder ce qui est dû par le débiteur, ni être contracté sous des conditions plus onéreuses."
>
> *Translation*: "A suretyship cannot exceed what is owed by the debtor, nor be contracted under more onerous conditions."

---

## Degrees of Accessoriété

Westendorf identifies different degrees of the accessory relationship (T.3, §5, note 54):

| Degree | Description | Consequence | Examples |
|--------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| **Structural** (*structurellement accessoire*) | Existence depends on claim throughout life | Security extinguishes with claim | Cautionnement, gage, hypothèque |
| **Functional** (*fonctionnellement accessoire*) | Claim must exist at constitution only | May survive after claim extinguished | Garantie indemnitaire |
| **Autonomous** | Independent of underlying claim | Survives defenses to principal claim | Garantie à première demande |

---

## Consequences of the Accessory Character

### At Constitution (Westendorf, T.1, §51, refs 148-149)

1. **Valid claim required**: The security is void if the principal obligation is void
2. **Future claims**: Security can be granted for future, contingent, or hypothetical claims (Art. 2075 C.civ. as amended 1994)
3. **Scope limitation**: Security cannot exceed the principal obligation

### During Life of Security

1. **Following the claim**: Security follows transfer of the secured claim (cession de créance)
2. **Reduction**: If claim is reduced, security is proportionally reduced
3. **Defenses**: Guarantor may invoke defenses available to principal debtor (for accessory securities)

### At Extinction

1. **Principal claim paid**: Security automatically extinguishes
2. **Claim prescribed**: Security prescribes with the claim (subject to specific rules)
3. **Claim nullified**: Security is nullified with the principal obligation

**Trib. Luxembourg, 3 mars 2010, n° 71/2010**:
> "Comme toute sûreté, le gage repose sur une créance qu'il garantit. Lorsque la créance disparaît, à la suite d'un paiement ou d'une action en résolution, le gage s'éteint."

---

## Exceptions and Limitations

### Security for Obligations Naturelles

Traditional view held that pledge cannot secure a natural obligation (*obligation naturelle*) since these cannot be forcibly executed. However, some authorities accept this.

**Contra**: Trib. Luxembourg, 3 mars 2010, n° 71/2010, nos 111.132 et 111.178:
> "Le gage ne peut garantir une obligation naturelle, puisque celle-ci, par définition, ne peut donner lieu à aucune action en exécution forcée."

**For**: G. Baudry-Lacantinerie et P. de Loynes (cited in Westendorf, T.1, §61, note 191)

⚠️ **To Be Independently Verified**: The current state of Luxembourg law on pledge for natural obligations is unsettled.

### Non-Accessory Securities

Modern practice has developed securities that depart from strict accessoriété:

- **Garantie à première demande**: Guarantor cannot invoke defenses from principal contract
- **Garantie indemnitaire**: Only functionally accessory

## Distinction from Indivisibilité

| Accessoriété | Indivisibilité |
|--------------|----------------|
| Relates to dependence on principal claim | Relates to internal structure of security |
| Of the **essence** of the security | Of the **nature** (can be waived by parties) |
| Security follows claim | Each part of asset secures whole debt |

**Westendorf, T.1, §50** (citing E. Becqué):
> "Le caractère accessoire est de son essence tandis que le caractère indivisible n'est que de sa nature, et peut être écarté par les stipulations des parties."

---

> **Common Law Comparison**
>
> English law recognizes the accessory nature of guarantees (true guarantees follow the principal debt). However, English law also permits:
> - **Indemnities**: Primary obligations, not dependent on validity of principal debt
> - **Demand guarantees**: Autonomous undertakings common in trade finance
>
> The distinction between "guarantee" and "indemnity" in English law roughly parallels the Luxembourg distinction between *cautionnement* (accessory) and *garantie indépendante* (autonomous).

## Banking Application

### Drafting Considerations

When drafting security documents:

1. **Identify secured obligations precisely**: Future/revolving facilities require careful drafting
2. **Consider scope clauses**: "All monies" clauses vs. specific facility
3. **Address prescription**: Security may prescribe with underlying claim
4. **Transfer provisions**: Ensure security transfers with assignment of claim

### Due Diligence

When taking security:
- Verify validity of principal obligation
- Check no grounds for nullity of underlying contract
- Confirm security scope matches intended coverage
