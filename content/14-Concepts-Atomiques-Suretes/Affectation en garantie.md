# Affectation en garantie (Dedication as Security)

> **Concept**: The act of allocating or dedicating specific assets to secure an obligation
> **Source**: Westendorf, T.1, §§100-115, 1100-1110
> **Related**: All property-based securities (*propriétés-sûretés*)

---

## Definition

*Affectation en garantie* refers to the juridical act by which a debtor (or third party) dedicates specific assets to secure the performance of an obligation. This dedication may take different forms depending on the security mechanism employed.

**Westendorf, T.1, §100**:
> "L'affectation de biens en garantie constitue le mécanisme fondamental par lequel un débiteur renforce la position de son créancier."

*Translation*: The dedication of assets as security constitutes the fundamental mechanism by which a debtor strengthens the position of his creditor.

---

## Forms of Dedication

### 1. Traditional Securities (*Sûretés traditionnelles*)

| Security Type | Dedication Mechanism | Ownership |
|---------------|---------------------|-----------|
| **Gage (Pledge)** | Delivery or control | Remains with debtor |
| **Nantissement** | Registration/control | Remains with debtor |
| **Hypothèque** | Registration | Remains with debtor |

The debtor retains ownership; the creditor acquires a *droit réel accessoire* (accessory real right).

### 2. Property-Based Securities (*Propriétés-sûretés*)

| Security Type | Dedication Mechanism | Ownership |
|---------------|---------------------|-----------|
| **Réserve de propriété** | Retention of title | Stays with seller |
| **Fiducie-sûreté** | Transfer to fiduciary patrimony | Transferred |
| **Cession à titre de garantie** | Assignment of ownership | Transferred |

Ownership itself serves the security function.

---

## Key Characteristics

### A. Specificity Principle (*Principe de spécialité*)

Assets must be **identifiable** either:
- Individually (specific assets)
- By category (future receivables, inventory)

**Art. 13, al. 2 Loi 5 août 2005**:
> "[L]es opérations visées [...] sont celles qui consistent dans le transfert de la propriété d'avoirs appartenant ou venant à appartenir au cédant, **sans qu'il soit besoin de les spécifier**."

*Note*: Financial collateral law permits generic description - a significant facilitation for banking practice.

### B. Voluntariness

Dedication requires the **consent** of the asset owner (debtor or third-party security provider).

**Exception**: Judicial attachment (*saisie conservatoire*) - involuntary dedication by court order.

### C. Accessory Character

The dedication follows the secured claim:
- Extinction of claim → release of security
- Transfer of claim → transfer of security (generally)

## Dedication Methods by Asset Type

### Receivables (*Créances*)

| Method | Legal Basis | Key Feature |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Pledge | Loi 5 août 2005, Art. 5 | Debtor retains ownership |
| Assignment | Loi 5 août 2005, Art. 13 | Ownership transfers |
| Fiduciary transfer | Loi 27 juillet 2003 | Separate patrimony |

### Financial Instruments

| Method | Legal Basis | Key Feature |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Pledge | Loi 5 août 2005, Art. 5 | Book-entry or certificated |
| Title transfer | Loi 5 août 2005, Art. 13 | Full ownership to creditor |

### Cash (*Espèces*)

| Method | Legal Basis | Key Feature |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Cash pledge | Loi 5 août 2005, Art. 5 | Blocked account |
| Cash deposit | Art. 2082 C.civ. | Traditional civil pledge |

## Banking Application

### Facility Documentation

Standard dedication clauses identify:
1. **Secured obligations** - all amounts due under facility
2. **Collateral assets** - present and future, by category
3. **Security mechanism** - pledge vs. transfer

**Example clause**:
> "Le Constituant affecte en garantie au profit du Bénéficiaire l'intégralité des Créances Nanties, présentes et futures, telles que décrites à l'Annexe 1."

### Due Diligence Points

1. **Ownership verification** - can grantor dedicate these assets?
2. **Prior encumbrances** - are assets already dedicated elsewhere?
3. **Restrictions** - contractual limitations on dedication?
4. **Future assets** - adequate description for after-acquired property?
