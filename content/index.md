## Key Warnings

> **1-Year Prescription for Lésion**: Art. 1118 al. 3 — much shorter than other vices (5 years)

> **Luxembourg Retains Cause**: Unlike France (2016 reform), Luxembourg still uses *cause* as validity requirement

> **Non-Cumul**: Contractual and delictual liability cannot be combined between contracting parties
