# ⚖️ Luxembourg Law Vault

A structured, cross-referenced reference on Luxembourg civil and commercial law, with a focus on **banking & finance** and **fund finance** practice.

> [!warning] Disclaimer
> Provided for **educational and reference purposes only**. Luxembourg law is subject to ongoing reform (Code civil modernization in progress). Verify current law for specific transactions. This is **not** a substitute for professional legal advice. French doctrine is cited for context only — Luxembourg has **not** adopted all French reforms (e.g. the 2016 contract law reform); the *cause* doctrine remains in force.

## ⚠️ Key Warnings

- **Lésion** — action must be brought within **1 year** of contract conclusion (art. 1118 al. 3), far shorter than other vices (5 years).
- **Cause** — Luxembourg **retains** the *cause* doctrine as a validity requirement (unlike France post-2016).
- **Non-cumul** — contractual and delictual liability **cannot be combined** between contracting parties.
