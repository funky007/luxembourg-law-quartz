# SARL - Gerance et associes

> **Type**: Limited Liability Company Management / Direction de la SARL
> **Source**: Steichen, *Precis de droit des societes*, Chapter 1, Part III - SARL, Sections 2-3 (pp. 586-624)
> **Key Legislation**: Loi du 10 aout 1915, arts. 710-14 et seq.

---

## 1. Overview of SARL Governance

### 1.1 Management Structure

**Steichen, n 780**: The SARL enjoys significant management flexibility compared to the SA:

| Element | SARL | SA |
|---------|------|-----|
| **Management Organ** | Gerant(s) | Board of Directors |
| **Number** | One or multiple | Minimum 3 |
| **Collegiality** | Optional | Mandatory |
| **Legal Person Manager** | Permitted | Permitted (with permanent rep.) |
| **Revocation** | For legitimate cause | *Ad nutum* |

---

## 2. The Manager (*Gerant*)

### 2.1 Who May Be Manager

**Steichen, n 780-782**:

| Category | Eligibility |
|----------|-------------|
| **Associates** | Yes |
| **Non-Associates** | Yes (unless statutes restrict) |
| **Minors** | Yes (mandataire capacity suffices) |
| **Incapacitated Adults** | Yes |
| **Spouses** | Yes (separately or together) |
| **Foreigners** | Yes (EU or non-EU) |
| **Legal Persons** | Yes (no permanent rep. required unlike SA) |

**Steichen, n 782**: The manager is a "*mandataire*" (agent) under Art. 710-14 L1915, although functionally an *organ* of the company. Thus anyone capable of being a mandataire under Art. 1990 C.civ. may be manager.

### 2.2 Excluded Persons

**Steichen, n 783**:

| Category | Reason |
|----------|--------|
| **Fonctionnaires** | Public function incompatibility |
| **Judges** | Judicial function |
| **Avocats/Notaires** | Professional rules |
| **Deputes/Government** | Political office |
| **Military** | Service rules |
| **Bankrupt Directors** | If court-imposed ban (up to 20 years) |

### 2.3 Commercial Status

**Steichen, n 783**: The SARL manager is NOT a merchant (*commercant*) by virtue of their functions:
- Commercial capacity not required
- Commercial prohibitions do not apply
- Exceptions only for regulated activities (accountants, auditors, lawyers)

---

## 3. Appointment

### 3.1 Methods of Appointment

**Steichen, n 785**:

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Statutory Manager** (*gerant statutaire*) | Named in constitutive deed |
| **Ordinary Manager** (*gerant ordinaire*) | Named by subsequent act |

**Steichen, n 785**: If no manager designated in statutes or by assembly, all associates are deemed managers by operation of law (Art. 1859 al. 1er C.civ.).

### 3.2 Appointment Conditions

**Steichen, n 785-786**:

| Decision | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| **First Assembly** | Must designate (for publication) |
| **Subsequent Appointments** | Majority of voting capital |
| **Second Convocation** | Majority of votes cast (regardless of quorum) |
| **Statutory Override** | May require higher majority |

### 3.3 Duration

**Steichen, n 787**: In absence of statutory provision:
- May be limited or unlimited
- Default: duration of the company
- Acceptance takes effect upon (informal) acceptance

### 3.4 Substitute Manager (*gerant suppleant*)

**Steichen, n 785**: Family SARLs sometimes provide for a substitute manager to take over upon death of principal manager (e.g., son succeeds father).

---

## 4. Remuneration

### 4.1 General Principles

**Steichen, n 788-789**:

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Default** | Functions may be gratuitous |
| **Burden of Proof** | Manager must prove remuneration agreed |
| **Determination** | By statutes or associate decision |
| **Self-attribution** | Prohibited (abuse of corporate assets) |

### 4.2 Forms of Remuneration

**Steichen, n 789**:
- Fixed compensation
- Percentage of turnover or profits
- Mixed (fixed + variable)
- Additional: bonuses, expense reimbursement, benefits in kind

### 4.3 Tax Treatment

**Steichen, n 789**: Majority managers taxed as employees (no distinction with minority managers or non-associate managers).

### 4.4 Social Security

**Steichen, n 790-791**:

| Manager Type | Social Security Regime |
|--------------|------------------------|
| **Non-Associate** | General employee regime |
| **Minority Associate** | General employee regime (if remunerated) |
| **Majority Associate** | Self-employed regime |

## 5. Cessation of Functions

### 5.1 Termination Events

**Steichen, n 793-794**:

| Event | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Term Expiry** | Functions end |
| **Death** | Functions end |
| **Incapacity** | Functions end |
| **Management Ban** | Functions end |
| **Personal Bankruptcy** | Functions end |
| **Resignation** | Permitted (statutory manager included) |
| **Revocation** | Per assembly decision |

**Key Difference from SNC**: Manager cessation does NOT dissolve the SARL (unlike SNC).

### 5.2 Revocation

**Steichen, n 795-796**: Both statutory and ordinary managers revocable only for "*cause legitime*" (legitimate cause) - Art. 710-14 al. 2 L1915.

| Legitimate Cause | Not Legitimate Cause |
|------------------|---------------------|
| Incompetence | Simple majority change |
| Management faults | Private life matters (unless affecting society) |
| Disinterest in business | Arbitrary will of associates |
| Personal bankruptcy | Loss of confidence alone |
| Legal/statutory violations | Personal faults unrelated to functions |
| Behavior against group interest | |

### 5.3 Revocation Procedure

**Steichen, n 800-802**:

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Competent Organ** | General assembly only (Art. 710-17 L1915) |
| **Majority** | >50% of voting capital |
| **Manager Vote** | Manager-associate may vote on own revocation |
| **Judicial Revocation** | Available if majority manager blocks assembly vote |
| **Effect** | Immediate (even if contested) |

### 5.4 Revocation Without Cause

**Steichen, n 797-798**: If revoked without legitimate cause:
- May contest judicially
- Entitled to damages
- Reinstatement theoretically possible (but impractical)
- Amount based on lost remuneration

### 5.5 *Ad Nutum* Revocation

**Steichen, n 800**: Statutes may provide for *ad nutum* revocation (at will, like SA directors) - not public order.

## 6. Manager Powers

### 6.1 Single vs. Multiple Managers

**Steichen, n 803-804**:

| Structure | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Single Manager** | Most common; full powers |
| **Multiple Managers** | Concurrent powers (each may act alone) |
| **Board of Managers** (*conseil de gerance*) | Collegial; SA-style rules |

### 6.2 Internal Powers (vis-a-vis Associates)

**Steichen, n 806-808**:

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Determination** | Freely by statutes |
| **Default** | All acts necessary or useful for corporate purpose |
| **Limitations** | May require prior associate approval for certain acts |
| **Prohibited** | Cannot strip manager of all initiative |
| **Delegation** | Permitted for daily management; special powers for specific acts |

**Steichen, n 807**: Unlike SA, there are no "*conventions reglementees*" (regulated agreements) in SARL - no special approval procedure for manager-company contracts (except for SARL-U).

### 6.3 External Powers (vis-a-vis Third Parties)

**Steichen, n 809-811**:

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Full Representation** | Each manager represents company externally |
| **Statutory Limits** | NOT opposable to third parties |
| **Internal Limits** | NOT opposable to third parties |
| **Beyond Object** | Company bound unless third party knew/should have known |

**Exception**: Statutes may require joint signatures (*signature conjointe*) - opposable to third parties if published at RESA.

### 6.4 Daily Management Delegate

**Steichen, n 811**: Statutes may provide for *delegue a la gestion journaliere* (daily management delegate) - same concept as in SA.

---

## 7. Manager Liability

### 7.1 Civil Liability

**Steichen, n 812-815**: Managers liable for:

| Liability Type | Basis |
|----------------|-------|
| **To Company** | Breach of mandate; management faults |
| **To Associates** | Direct damage from violations |
| **To Third Parties** | Tortious acts; Art. 1382-1383 C.civ. |

### 7.2 Criminal Liability

**Steichen, n 816**: Managers may face criminal sanctions for:
- Abuse of corporate assets
- False declarations
- Fraudulent bankruptcy acts

---

## 8. Associates

### 8.1 Rights of Associates

**Steichen, n 820-821**:

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Voting** | On all matters reserved to assembly |
| **Information** | Right to inspect company records |
| **Dividend** | Share in profits (per statutes or contribution) |
| **Preemption** | On share transfers (if provided in statutes) |
| **Convocation** | May convene if >50% capital (Art. 710-21 L1915) |

### 8.2 Share Transfers (*Cession de parts*)

**Steichen, n 822-826**:

| Transfer Type | Requirement |
|---------------|-------------|
| **To Associates** | Free unless statutes restrict |
| **To Spouses/Heirs** | Free unless statutes restrict |
| **To Third Parties** | Requires approval by associates holding 3/4 of capital |
| **Formalities** | Written deed (notarial or private); signification or acceptance |
| **RCS Filing** | Amendment filed at RCS |

**Steichen, n 824**: The 3/4 approval requirement is *suppletive* (statutes may modify).

### 8.3 Transfer Procedure

**Steichen, n 825-826**:

1. Written cession agreement
2. Notification to company (or acceptance in deed)
3. Update of associate register
4. Publication at RCS

### 8.4 Redemption Right (*Rachat*)

**Steichen, n 827**: If transfer refused, associate may demand company redemption of shares (to avoid lock-in).

## 9. Assembly of Associates

### 9.1 Competences

**Steichen, n 830-835**:

| Matter | Assembly Competence |
|--------|---------------------|
| **Accounts Approval** | Annual (within 6 months) |
| **Dividend Distribution** | Annual or interim |
| **Manager Appointment/Revocation** | Per rules above |
| **Statutory Amendments** | Extraordinary assembly |
| **Capital Operations** | Extraordinary assembly |
| **Dissolution** | Extraordinary assembly |

### 9.2 Ordinary Decisions

**Steichen, n 837**:

| Requirement | Rule |
|-------------|------|
| **First Convocation** | Majority of voting capital |
| **Second Convocation** | Majority of votes cast |

### 9.3 Extraordinary Decisions

**Steichen, n 838**:

| Requirement | Rule |
|-------------|------|
| **Quorum** | Majority of capital present/represented |
| **Vote** | 3/4 of voting capital |
| **If Quorum Not Met** | Second meeting: same majority rule |

### 9.4 Written Resolutions

**Steichen, n 836**: Resolutions may be adopted by written consultation without meeting, unless statutes prohibit.

## 10. Special Forms

### 10.1 Single-Member SARL (SARL-U)

**Steichen, n 841-850**:

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Creation** | Formation or subsequent reunion of shares |
| **Decisions** | Sole associate decisions recorded in minutes |
| **Contracts** | Manager-company contracts recorded in special register |
| **Publication** | Single-member status published |

### 10.2 Simplified SARL (SARL-S)

**Steichen, n 851-857**:

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Capital** | EUR 1 - EUR 12,000 |
| **Associates** | Natural persons only; max 100 |
| **Manager** | Must be natural person; must be associate |
| **Reserve** | 5% of profits until reserve equals EUR 12,000 |
| **Object** | Artisanal, commercial, industrial, or liberal |

## 11. Banking & Finance Application

### 11.1 Governance in Practice

In banking/finance structures, SARL governance typically features:

1. **Professional Directors**
   - Non-resident managers common
   - Legal person managers (e.g., management company)
   - Multiple signature requirements for control

2. **Board of Managers**
   - SA-style collegial governance
   - Formal meeting procedures
   - Delegation to daily management

3. **Reserved Matters**
   - Associate approval for major decisions
   - Borrowing limits
   - Related-party transactions

### 11.2 Key Structuring Points

| Consideration | Impact |
|---------------|--------|
| **Transfer Restrictions** | Standard 3/4 approval or modify |
| **Preemption Rights** | Important for joint ventures |
| **Tag-Along/Drag-Along** | May be included in statutes |
| **Deadlock Resolution** | Mechanisms important with 50/50 structures |
| **Minority Protection** | Considered via blocking minorities |

### 11.3 Regulatory Implications

When SARL used for regulated activities:
- Manager fit and proper requirements
- Central administration requirements
- Substance requirements
- Notification obligations for manager changes
