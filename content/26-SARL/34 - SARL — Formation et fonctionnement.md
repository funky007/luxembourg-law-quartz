# SARL - Formation et fonctionnement

> **Type**: Limited Liability Company / Societe a responsabilite limitee
> **Source**: Steichen, *Precis de droit des societes*, Chapter 1, Part III - SARL (pp. 577-624)
> **Key Legislation**: Loi du 10 aout 1915, arts. 710-1 et seq.

---

## 1. Overview & Characteristics

### 1.1 Definition

**Steichen, n 751**: Art. 710-1 L1915 defines the SARL as: "la societe ou des associes en nombre limite n'engagent qu'une mise determinee et dont les parts sociales representees exclusivement par des titres non negociables ne peuvent etre cedees que conformement aux modes et conditions prescrits par la presente section."

The SARL is defined by two criteria:
1. **Limited liability** of associates to their contribution
2. **Non-negotiable shares** that are only transferable with restrictions

### 1.2 Historical Background

**Steichen, n 751**: Created in Luxembourg by the law of September 18, 1933:
- French origin (1925 law)
- German inspiration (1898 *GmbH* law via Alsace-Lorraine)
- Also influenced by Belgian draft law

### 1.3 Hybrid Nature

**Steichen, n 752**: The SARL is a "*societe hybride*" (hybrid company):

| SA Characteristics | Partnership Characteristics |
|--------------------|----------------------------|
| Limited liability | Non-negotiable shares |
| Not dissolved by death/incapacity | Partially based on *intuitu personae* |
| Capital company features | Transfer restrictions |

### 1.4 Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Liability** | Limited to contribution (*mise determinee*) |
| **Minimum Capital** | EUR 12,000 (or EUR 1 for SARL-S) |
| **Associates** | 1-100 |
| **Shares** | Non-negotiable (*parts sociales*) |
| **Legal Personality** | Yes |
| **Commercial Status** | Commercial by form |

## 2. Advantages

### 2.1 General Advantages

**Steichen, n 753-754**:

| Advantage | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Limited Liability** | Associates not personally liable for company debts |
| **Lower Capital** | EUR 12,000 vs. EUR 30,000 for SA |
| **Unipersonal Form** | Single-member SARL permitted (SARL-U) |
| **Simpler Governance** | Less complex than SA |
| **Flexibility** | Less regulated than SA |
| **No Auditor Mandatory** | Unless thresholds exceeded |

### 2.2 Tax Advantages

**Steichen, n 754**:

| Advantage | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Opaque Treatment** | Corporate tax treatment like SA |
| **Treaty Benefits** | DTT and EU directives applicable |
| **Check-the-Box** | US tax: may elect pass-through treatment |
| **Contributions in Kind** | No auditor report required |
| **No Financial Assistance Rules** | Art. 430-19 L1915 not applicable |

## 3. Formation - Conditions of Substance

### 3.1 Associate Requirements

**Steichen, n 756-757**:

| Requirement | Rule |
|-------------|------|
| **Natural/Legal Persons** | Both permitted |
| **Commercial Capacity** | Not required (civil capacity suffices) |
| **Minors** | Permitted (emancipated or with representation) |
| **Incapacitated Adults** | Permitted with appropriate authorization |
| **Spouses** | May both participate; may be sole associates |

### 3.2 Number of Associates

**Steichen, n 757-758**:

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Minimum** | 1 (SARL-U: unipersonal SARL) |
| **Maximum** | 100 (increased from 40 by 2016 law) |
| **Exceeding Maximum** | 1-year regularization period |

### 3.3 Capital Requirements

**Steichen, n 759**:

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Minimum** | EUR 12,000 (Art. 710-5 L1915) |
| **Currency** | May be in any currency |
| **Subscription** | Must be fully subscribed |
| **Release** | Fully released at subscription |
| **Authorized Capital** | Permitted since 2016 law |

### 3.4 Shares (*Parts sociales*)

**Steichen, n 760-762**:

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Value** | With or without nominal value |
| **Minimum Value** | 1 cent or fraction thereof |
| **Categories** | Privileged or priority shares permitted |
| **Voting Rights** | All shares have voting rights (no non-voting shares) |
| **Form** | Non-negotiable; cannot be bearer or order |
| **Full Release** | Must be fully paid at subscription |

### 3.5 Contributions

**Steichen, n 764-766**:

| Type | Rule |
|------|------|
| **Cash** (*numeraire*) | Deposited in special account; blocked until deed |
| **In Kind** (*nature*) | Permitted; valued in deed; NO auditor required |
| **Industry** (*industrie*) | Permitted but outside capital; special shares |

**Steichen, n 764**: Industry contribution shares are non-transferable and non-transmissible (extinguish on death).

### 3.6 Founder Liability

**Steichen, n 762-763**:

| Liability | Scope |
|-----------|-------|
| **Subscriptions** | Solidarily liable for unsubscribed capital |
| **Release** | Solidarily liable for unpaid amounts |
| **Damages** | Liable for defects in statutes |
| **Criminal** | False declarations punishable (Art. 1500-2 n 2 L1915) |

### 3.7 Corporate Name and Purpose

**Steichen, n 767**:

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Name** | Free choice; must include "SARL" or full form |
| **Associate Name** | May be incorporated; requires change if associate leaves |
| **Mentions** | Capital, seat, RCS number on all documents |
| **Object** | Civil or commercial; commercial form regardless |

### 3.8 Prohibited Objects

**Steichen, n 768**:

| Activity | Prohibition |
|----------|-------------|
| **Insurance** | Prohibited |
| **Capitalization** | Prohibited |
| **Savings** | Prohibited |
| **Banking** | Effectively prohibited (capital requirements) |

## 4. Formation - Formal Requirements

### 4.1 Notarial Deed Requirement

**Steichen, n 769-770**:

| Requirement | Consequence |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Form** | Notarial deed required (Art. 100-4 al. 2 L1915) |
| **Sanction** | Nullity if formed by private agreement |
| **Appearance** | All associates (personally or by proxy) |

### 4.2 Mandatory Statutory Content

**Steichen, n 771**:

| Element | Reference |
|---------|-----------|
| Identity of founders | Art. 710-7 L1915 |
| Legal form | Art. 710-7 L1915 |
| Duration | Art. 710-7 L1915 |
| Corporate name | Art. 710-7 L1915 |
| Registered office | Art. 710-7 L1915 |
| Corporate purpose | Art. 710-7 L1915 |
| Capital amount | Art. 710-7 L1915 |
| Share categories and rights | Art. 710-7 L1915 |
| Contributions in kind | Art. 710-7 L1915 |

### 4.3 Publication and Registration

**Steichen, n 772**:

| Formality | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **RESA Publication** | Required (Art. 100-16 L1915) |
| **RCS Registration** | Company must register |
| **Content** | Same as statutory content |

### 4.4 Register of Associates

**Steichen, n 773-774**:

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Location** | At registered office |
| **Content** | Constitutive deed, amendments, names/residence, transfers |
| **Access** | Associates only (not creditors) |
| **Legal Effect** | Proof, not title of ownership |
| **Certificates** | Company may issue participation certificates |

### 4.5 Document Requirements

**Steichen, n 775**: All documents must contain:
- Corporate name
- "Societe a responsabilite limitee" or "SARL"
- Registered office address
- RCS number

**Sanction**: Manager may be personally liable for commitments (Art. 710-10 al. 2 L1915).

## 5. Nullities

### 5.1 Grounds for Nullity

**Steichen, n 776**: Nullity limited to specific grounds (Art. 100-18 L1915):

| Ground | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **No Notarial Deed** | Formation by private agreement |
| **Illicit Object** | Object contrary to public order |
| **No Valid Founder** | None validly committed |
| **Missing Mentions** | Complete absence (not mere inaccuracy) of name, object, contributions, or capital |

### 5.2 Prescription

**Steichen, n 776**: Nullity actions prescribe after 5 years from publication of constitutive deed.

### 5.3 Contractual Vices

**Steichen, n 776**: Classic contractual vices (e.g., *clause leonine*) do not cause company nullity but may affect individual subscriptions.

## 6. Capital Operations

### 6.1 Increases and Reductions

**Steichen, n 760**:

| Operation | Requirements |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Increase** | Extraordinary assembly decision |
| **Reduction** | Extraordinary assembly decision |
| **Auditor** | Not required (unlike SA) |
| **Creditor Opposition** | Not available (unlike SA) |
| **Authorized Capital** | Permitted (Art. 710-26 al. 3 L1915) |

### 6.2 Authorized Capital

**Steichen, n 760**: Since 2016:
- Shares must be issued to existing associates or approved persons
- Follows SA authorized capital principles (Art. 32 L1915)

## 7. Dissolution

### 7.1 Non-Dissolution Causes

**Steichen, n 777**:

| Event | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Death of Associate** | No dissolution (unless statutes provide) |
| **Incapacity** | No dissolution |
| **Bankruptcy** | No dissolution |
| **Reunion in Single Hand** | No dissolution (becomes SARL-U) |

### 7.2 Dissolution Causes

**Steichen, n 777-778**:

| Cause | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Term Expiry** | As per statutes |
| **Object Exhaustion/Loss** | Standard company law |
| **Contract Annulment** | If nullity pronounced |
| **Judicial Dissolution** | For just cause |
| **Voluntary Dissolution** | Associate decision |
| **Excess Associates** | >100 for more than 1 year |

### 7.3 Loss of Capital

**Steichen, n 779**: Unlike SA:
- No mandatory consultation of associates on heavy losses
- No manager liability for failure to convene
- Reflects international holding company use

## 8. Banking & Finance Application

### 8.1 Primary Uses

The SARL is the **most popular company form** in Luxembourg:

1. **Holding Companies**
   - International holding structures
   - Check-the-box US tax planning
   - EU directive benefits (parent-subsidiary)

2. **Operating Subsidiaries**
   - Group structures
   - Joint ventures
   - Management companies

3. **Private Equity**
   - GP entities (often as commandite of SCS)
   - Carried interest vehicles
   - Management participation

4. **Real Estate**
   - Property holding
   - Development vehicles

### 8.2 Key Advantages in Finance

| Advantage | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **No Auditor for Contributions** | Fast formation with in-kind contributions |
| **Check-the-Box** | US tax transparency election |
| **Treaty Network** | Full access to Luxembourg DTTs |
| **EU Directives** | Parent-Subsidiary, Interest/Royalties |
| **Flexibility** | Less formal than SA |
| **Lower Capital** | EUR 12,000 entry point |

### 8.3 Limitations

| Limitation | Impact |
|------------|--------|
| **Max 100 Associates** | Not suitable for widely-held structures |
| **Non-Negotiable Shares** | Transfer restrictions |
| **No Public Offering** | Cannot access capital markets |
| **Banking Prohibition** | Cannot conduct banking activity |

### 8.4 Regulatory Considerations

When advising on SARLs:
- Common GP vehicle for investment funds
- AIFM entities may be SARLs
- Management companies may use SARL form
- Consider SA if wider shareholding needed
