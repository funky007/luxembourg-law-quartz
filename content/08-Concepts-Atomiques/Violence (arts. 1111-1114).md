# Violence (arts. 1111-1114)

> **Type**: Vice du consentement  
> **Articles**: 1111-1114 Code civil  
> **Sanction**: Nullité relative  
> **Prescription**: 5 years from cessation of violence

---

## Definition (Poelmans §66)

Violence exists when a person contracts under a **threat** (*menace*) that weighs on their consent.

> "Il y a violence lorsqu'une personne s'engage dans les liens d'un contrat sous l'emprise d'une menace qui va peser sur son consentement."

---

## Requirements

### 1. Threat of Considerable Harm

The threat must concern a **mal considérable et présent** — considerable and present harm.

The harm must be sufficiently grave to have a **determining character** on consent.

### 2. Target of Threat

| Article       | Target                        |
| ------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Art. 1112** | Person or goods of contractor |
| **Art. 1113** | Close relatives (*proches*)   |

### 3. Source of Violence

> **Art. 1111**: Violence may come from **co-contractor or third party**.

This distinguishes violence from [[Dol (art. 1116)]], which must emanate from co-contractor.

### 4. Illegitimate Character

> **Art. 1114**: Violence must be **illégitime** — not motivated by normal exercise of a right or legitimate relationship.

**Not violence**:
- Threat of lawful lawsuit
- Normal economic or subordination relationships (unless abusive)

### 5. Reasonable Person Standard

> **Art. 1112**: Violence must be "de nature à faire impression sur une personne raisonnable."

Assessed against standard of **reasonable person**, not hypersensitive individual.

---

## Key Case (Poelmans §67)

**Facts**: Blind person signed credit contract for another's benefit. Alleged violence based on threat of abandonment.

**First instance**: Found violence — threat to physical integrity through abandonment of dependent person.

**Cour d'appel**: **Reversed**. No violence because:
- Person was employed (communal employee)
- Could have recourse to public services for dependents
- Mere expectation of losing services ≠ danger to person or goods

---

## Sanction

### Nullité Relative

- Only victim may invoke
- Contract not void *de plein droit* — must be requested in court (art. 1117)
- Can be confirmed after violence ceases (art. 1115)

### Prescription (art. 1304)

> **5 years** from the day **violence ceased** (*du jour où elle a cessé*).

This differs from erreur/dol (from discovery).

## Practical Points

### Proof

Victim must prove:
- Existence of threat
- Gravity sufficient for determining effect
- Illegitimate character
- Causation (would not have contracted otherwise)

### Economic Duress

Pure economic pressure generally insufficient unless:
- Exploits vulnerability
- Combined with illegitimate conduct
- See also [[Lésion (art. 1118)]] for disproportion cases
