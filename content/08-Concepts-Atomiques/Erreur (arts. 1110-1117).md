# Erreur (arts. 1110-1117)

> **Type**: Vice du consentement  
> **Articles**: 1110-1117 Code civil  
> **Sanction**: Nullité relative  
> **Prescription**: 5 years from discovery

---

## Definition

> **Erreur** = distortion between declared will and real will.

The party's understanding does not match reality.

---

## Types of Error

### 1. Erreur-Obstacle

**Definition**: Error so profound it prevents meeting of minds entirely.

**Examples**:
- One thinks sale, other thinks lease (*error in negotio*)
- Error on object itself — buying house A thinking it's house B
- Error on price currency — francs vs. euros

> **Poelmans**: Both parties may invoke nullité relative for erreur-obstacle.

### 2. Erreur Vice du Consentement

Error that vitiates consent without destroying it.

**Requirements**:
1. **Déterminante** — would not have contracted without error
2. **Qualités substantielles** — essential qualities of object
3. **Excusable** — not due to party's own negligence

---

## Erreur Déterminante

> Must have "provoked, at moment of consent, an alteration or disappearance of the cause of the obligation."

**Test**: Would the party have contracted knowing the truth?

---

## Qualités Substantielles (art. 1110)

**Definition**: Qualities that determined the party to contract.

### Objective vs. Subjective

- **Objective**: Qualities any reasonable person would consider essential
- **Subjective**: Qualities this particular party considered essential (if known to other party)

---

## Erreur Inexcusable (Poelmans §62)

> **Duty to self-inform**: Error is not excusable when party could have informed themselves.

### Examples from Jurisprudence

| Case                                             | Held                   |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| **Caution** who didn't verify debtor's finances  | Inexcusable            |
| **Buyer** who didn't read copropriété rules      | Inexcusable            |
| **Language** — contract in unknown language      | NOT ground for error   |
| **Value error** — shares worth less than thought | NOT error on substance |

---

## Excluded Errors

### Error on Value (art. 1110)

> Error on value alone ≠ vice du consentement.

**Case**: Buyer thought shares worth more based on incorrect assessment of company situation — not error on *qualités substantielles*.

### Error on Person (art. 1110 al. 2)

Generally not a vice **unless** person's identity was determinant (intuitu personae contracts).

### Error on Motives

Personal motives for contracting generally not relevant.

---

## Error and Vices Cachés

**Controversy** (Poelmans §65):

| View 1                             | View 2                                    |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Must use garantie des vices cachés | May choose between error and vices cachés |

> **Poelmans favors View 2**: If error conditions are met, no reason to exclude this basis just because vices cachés might also apply.

---

## Sanction

### Nullité Relative

- Only erring party may invoke
- Cannot be raised ex officio
- Confirmable

### Prescription (art. 1304)

**5 years** from **discovery** of error (*du jour où ils ont été découverts*).
