# Enrichissement sans cause

> **Type**: Quasi-contract (jurisprudential creation)  
> **Nature**: Unjust enrichment remedy  
> **Key Feature**: SUBSIDIARY — only when no other action available

---

## Definition

> Action allowing person who has **enriched another without cause** to recover, limited to the **lesser** of enrichment and impoverishment.

Not codified — created by jurisprudence based on equity.

---

## Five Conditions (Poelmans)

### 1. Enrichissement (Enrichment)

Defendant must have received a **benefit**:
- Positive gain (asset received)
- Negative gain (expense avoided)
- Can be material or immaterial

### 2. Appauvrissement (Impoverishment)

Claimant must have suffered **corresponding loss**:
- Expenditure made
- Service rendered
- Asset transferred

### 3. Correlation

**Link** between enrichment and impoverishment:
- Need not be direct
- But must be connected

### 4. Absence de Cause (No Legal Basis)

No **legal justification** for the enrichment:
- No contract
- No legal obligation
- No gift intention

### 5. Subsidiarité (Subsidiarity)

> **CRITICAL**: Action only available if claimant has **no other remedy**.

If contract claim exists → use contract.
If delict claim exists → use delict.
Enrichissement sans cause is **last resort**.

---

## The Subsidiarity Requirement

### Absolute Bar

Not merely "preferred" to use other actions — **must** use them if available.

### Lost Other Actions

If claimant **had** another action but lost it (prescription, procedural failure):
- Enrichissement sans cause **NOT available**
- Cannot use as backup for negligent litigation

### Never Had Other Action

Only available if no other action **ever existed**.

---

## Measure of Recovery

> Recovery limited to **lesser of**:
> - Defendant's enrichment, OR
> - Claimant's impoverishment

**Not** full compensation — only reversal of unjust transfer.

---

## Common Applications

| Scenario | Application |
|----------|-------------|
| Improvements to another's property | Builder may recover value added |
| Payments made in error | If répétition de l'indu unavailable |
| Services rendered without contract | If no implied contract found |

---

## Distinction from Paiement de l'Indu

| Aspect | Enrichissement sans cause | Paiement de l'indu |
|--------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| **Basis** | General equity | Specific: mistaken payment |
| **Codified** | No | Yes (arts. 1376-1381) |
| **Subsidiarity** | Yes — strict | No |

---

## Banking Application

### Mistaken Transfers

If répétition de l'indu fails → possible fallback (but subsidiarity applies).

### Unpaid Services

Services rendered without valid fee agreement — but rare given typical documentation.

### Practical Limits

Banks usually have contractual claims → enrichissement sans cause rarely needed.
