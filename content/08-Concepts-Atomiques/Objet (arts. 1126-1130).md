# Objet (arts. 1126-1130)

> **Type**: Condition de validité (art. 1108)  
> **Articles**: 1126-1130 Code civil  
> **Sanction**: Nullité absolue (illicit/impossible) or relative (indeterminate)

---

## Definition (art. 1126)

> "L'objet de l'obligation est la prestation que le cocontractant s'engage à fournir à l'autre."

The **object** is what the party undertakes to provide — the *prestation*.

---

## Requirements

### 1. Dans le Commerce (art. 1128)

Only things **in commerce** can be object of contract.

**Things Outside Commerce** (Poelmans §91):

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Human body** | Except regulated donations (blood, organs, tissues) |
| **Narcotics** | Unless under loi 19 février 1973 |
| **Dangerous products** | Per loi 31 juillet 2006 |
| **Family personal relations** | Art. 1388 (evolving) |

### 2. Licéité (art. 1128)

Object must not be contrary to:
- Law
- Public policy (*ordre public*)
- Good morals (*bonnes mœurs*)

### 3. Possibility

> **Poelmans §86**: "Le contrat n'existe que si la prestation promise est possible."

**Absolute impossibility** = nullity (e.g., "I promise to touch the sky")

**Non-existent thing** = nullity (selling house you don't own)

**BUT** — Future things permitted (art. 1130): Can sell next wine vintage.

### 4. Déterminé ou Déterminable (art. 1129)

| Status | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **Déterminé** | Nature and extent apparent from contract |
| **Déterminable** | Contract contains elements for determination |

---

## Determination of Object (Poelmans §87)

### Third-Party Determination

Parties may delegate determination to third party (arts. 1592-1593 for price).

**Effect if third party cannot/refuses**: Contract becomes *caduc* (lapses).

### Key Cases

**Undetermined parcel**: Contract for sale of "parcel from garden" without specifying area or location → **null** (indeterminable).

**Fonds de commerce**: Sale of business without inventory → **valid**. Designation of "the business" sufficiently individualized it; parties waived inventory clause by conduct.

**Insurance exclusions**: Exclusions for "normally foreseeable" damage → potentially abusive if professional alone judges foreseeability.

---

## Price Determination

### General Rule

Price must be determined or determinable at formation.

### Consumer Protection (art. L.211-3.10 Code conso.)

Abusive: Clauses allowing price increase without consumer's right to terminate if price becomes excessive.

---

## Sanction

### Nullité Absolue

For:
- Object **hors commerce**
- **Illicit** object
- **Impossible** object (absolute)

**30 years** prescription, any party may invoke, judge ex officio.

### Nullité Relative

For:
- **Indeterminate** object (arguably)

---

## Banking Application

### Financial Instruments

- Must be sufficiently determined/determinable
- Reference to market price = determinable
- ISDA definitions = determination mechanism

### Guarantees

- Scope of guarantee must be determinable
- "All sums" clauses: Valid if limit ascertainable
- Future obligations: Permitted if determinable

### Regulatory Objects

- Certain financial products restricted by regulation
- Derivatives for retail: consumer protection limits
