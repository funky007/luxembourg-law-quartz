# Solidarité (art. 1202)

> **Type**: Plurality of obligations  
> **Article**: 1202 Code civil  
> **Key Rule**: Never presumed — must be express or legal

---

## Definition

**Solidarité passive** (debtor side): Each debtor liable for **entire** debt.

**Solidarité active** (creditor side): Each creditor can claim **entire** debt.

---

## The Non-Presumption Rule

> **Art. 1202**: "La solidarité ne se présume point; il faut qu'elle soit expressément stipulée."

This is **fundamental**: Solidarity must be:
- **Expressly stipulated** in contract, or
- **Imposed by law**

---

## Sources of Solidarity

### 1. Conventionnelle (Contractual)

Express clause in contract:
> "Les emprunteurs sont solidairement tenus..."

Must be **clear and unambiguous**.

### 2. Légale (Statutory)

Law imposes solidarity:
- Co-managers of SARL (liability)
- Co-insurers
- Spouses for household debts
- Co-authors of crime/délit

### 3. In Solidum (Jurisprudential)

**Different from true solidarity** but similar effect.

When multiple persons cause same damage through **independent faults**:
- Each liable for whole
- Recourse against co-liable parties

---

## Effects of Passive Solidarity

### Primary Effects (Between Creditor and Debtors)

| Effect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Full liability** | Creditor can claim whole from any debtor |
| **Single payment** | Payment by one releases all |
| **Choice of debtor** | Creditor chooses which debtor to pursue |
| **No division** | Debtor cannot require creditor to divide claim |

### Secondary Effects (Representative)

| Effect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Mise en demeure** | Against one = against all |
| **Interruption of prescription** | Against one = against all |
| **Recognition** | By one debtor benefits creditor vs. all |
| **Judgment** | Against one binds all (res judicata) |

---

## Defenses

### Personal Defenses

Each debtor may raise defenses **personal** to them:
- Their own incapacity
- Vice affecting their consent

Cannot oppose defenses personal to co-debtor.

### Common Defenses

Any debtor may raise defenses affecting **whole obligation**:
- Payment
- Prescription
- Nullity affecting all
- Force majeure

---

## Recourse Between Debtors

### Division of Debt

Among co-debtors, debt divides:
- Per agreement if any
- Per shares of interest
- Equally if no other basis

### Subrogation

Paying debtor subrogated to creditor's rights → can pursue co-debtors.

---

## Banking Application

### Loan Agreements

Joint and several liability clause essential for:
- Multiple borrowers
- Guarantors
- Credit facilities with multiple obligors

### Standard Clause

> "The Borrowers are jointly and severally (*solidairement*) liable for all amounts due under this Agreement."

### Co-Guarantors

Without solidarity:
- Each guarantor liable only for their share
- Creditor must pursue each separately

With solidarity:
- Any guarantor can be pursued for whole
- Much stronger protection for bank

---

## Distinction: Solidarité vs. In Solidum

| Aspect | Solidarité | In Solidum |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| **Source** | Contract or law | Jurisprudence |
| **Representative effects** | Yes | Limited/debated |
| **Contribution** | Per agreement/shares | Per fault contribution |
