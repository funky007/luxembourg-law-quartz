# Capacité (arts. 1123-1125)

> **Type**: Condition de validité (art. 1108)  
> **Articles**: 1123-1125 Code civil  
> **Sanction**: Nullité relative  
> **Prescription**: 5 years

---

## Definition (Poelmans §79)

> "La capacité est l'aptitude d'une personne à faire par soi-même un acte juridique créateur de droits nouveaux et de situations juridiques nouvelles."

Two types:
- **Capacité de jouissance**: Ability to hold rights
- **Capacité d'exercice**: Ability to exercise rights oneself

---

## Principle (art. 1123)

> "Toute personne peut contracter, si elle n'en est pas déclarée incapable par la loi."

**Presumption**: Everyone has capacity unless law provides otherwise.

---

## Categories of Incapacity

### 1. Mineurs Non Émancipés (art. 388)

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Age** | Under 18 |
| **Representation** | Parents or tuteur |
| **Acts void** | Acts exceeding daily administration |
| **Protection** | Lésion rescission for any prejudice (art. 1305) |

### 2. Majeurs Protégés (art. 488)

Persons under:
- **Curatelle** — partial incapacity
- **Tutelle** — full incapacity

### 3. Failli (art. 444 C.com)

> **Poelmans §84**: Bankrupt loses all power of disposition over assets from judgment declaring bankruptcy.

- Payments to/by bankrupt after judgment: void *de droit*
- Good faith irrelevant
- Foreign bankruptcy judgment has same effect in Luxembourg

---

## Legal Persons (Poelmans §80-83)

### Principle

Legal persons have capacity, but more limited than natural persons.

### Limits

- **Objet social**: Can only act within corporate purpose
- **Attribution légale**: Personality depends on legislative grant
- **Public entities**: Limited to statutory purpose

### Key Case — Commune

**Facts**: Commune contracted for wind farm project.

**Held**: Contract void — outside *intérêt communal* because:
- Electricity supply already assured by Cegedel
- No direct benefit to commune inhabitants
- Activity not among normal municipal obligations

---

## Specific Incapacities

### Spouses in Community (arts. 1421-1 et s.)

- Cannot dispose of common property without consent
- Cannot dispose of family home (even if separate property)
- **Sanction**: Nullité (not mere inopposability)

> **Poelmans §85**: Action is for nullity, not inopposability. Nullity is for excess of power — no mandate or ratification can legitimate.

### Contractual Restrictions

- **Competition clauses**: Limit capacity to contract in certain areas
- **Ultra vires**: Acts beyond corporate powers

---

## Sanction

### Nullité Relative (art. 1125)

> Only the **incapable person** (or protected party) may invoke nullity.

- Cannot be invoked by capable co-contractor
- Judge cannot raise ex officio

### Prescription

**5 years** from:
- Majority (for minors)
- End of incapacity measure (for protected adults)
- End of bankruptcy (for failli)

### Corporate Capacity

Bank should verify:
- Authority of representatives
- Corporate purpose compatibility
- Board/shareholder approvals where required

- May be challenged if no corporate benefit
