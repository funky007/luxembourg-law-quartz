# Subrogation

> **Type**: Transfer of obligations (by operation of law or agreement)  
> **Articles**: 1249-1252 Code civil  
> **Effect**: Third party who pays acquires creditor's rights

---

## Definition

> Legal mechanism by which person who **pays another's debt** is substituted into the creditor's rights against the debtor.

**Key difference from cession**: Subrogation follows **payment**, not sale of claim.

---

## Two Types

### 1. Subrogation Légale (art. 1251)

Operates **automatically by law** in specified cases:

| Case | Example |
|------|---------|
| Creditor paying senior creditor | Second-ranking mortgagee pays first |
| Purchaser paying creditor with security | Buyer pays off mortgage |
| Co-debtor or surety who pays | Guarantor pays lender |
| Heir paying estate debts | To preserve assets |

### 2. Subrogation Conventionnelle

By **agreement** — two forms:

**Ex parte creditoris (art. 1250-1°)**:
- Creditor receiving payment subrogate payer
- Must be **express** and **contemporaneous** with payment

**Ex parte debitoris (art. 1250-2°)**:
- Debtor borrows to pay debt
- Subrogate new lender into old creditor's rights
- Requires authentic act or specific formalities

---

## Effects

### Rights Transferred

Subrogee acquires:
- Original claim (to extent of payment)
- All **accessories**: sureties, privileges, mortgages
- Right to sue debtor

### Limits

> Subrogation operates only **to extent of actual payment**.

Paid €80k of €100k debt → subrogated for €80k only.

### Defenses

Debtor may raise against subrogee all defenses available against original creditor.

---

## Comparison: Subrogation vs. Cession

| Aspect | Subrogation | Cession de créance |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Trigger** | Payment | Agreement |
| **Consideration** | Payment of debt | Purchase price |
| **Formalities** | None (légale) or specific | Art. 1690 notification |
| **Extent** | Limited to payment | Full claim |
| **Warranty** | None by creditor | Existence warranty |

---

## Subrogation and Sureties

### Surety's Right

When surety pays:
- **Art. 1251-3°**: Automatic subrogation
- Acquires creditor's rights against principal debtor
- Includes other sureties (co-cautions)

### Bank's Concern

If bank impairs rights surety would acquire through subrogation:
- Surety may be discharged pro tanto
- Must preserve collateral and other securities

---

## Banking Application

### Guarantor Payment

When guarantor pays bank:
- Subrogated to bank's rights
- Acquires mortgage, pledge, other securities
- Can pursue borrower

### Inter-Creditor Dynamics

Senior/junior creditor relationships:
- Junior paying senior → subrogated to senior position
- Critical for priority disputes

### Insurance Subrogation

Insurer paying claim:
- Subrogated to insured's rights
- Can pursue liable third parties

### Refinancing

New lender paying old debt:
- Subrogation ex parte debitoris possible
- Preserves existing security interests
