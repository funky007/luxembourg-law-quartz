# Paiement

> **Type**: Primary mode of extinction  
> **Articles**: 1235-1270 Code civil  
> **Effect**: Extinguishes obligation by performance

---

## Definition

> **Paiement** = voluntary execution of the obligation.

Not limited to money — any performance that satisfies the obligation.

---

## Who May Pay (arts. 1236-1238)

### General Rule

**Anyone** may pay:
- Debtor (natural payer)
- Third party (art. 1236)

### Exception: Intuitu Personae

Creditor may refuse third party payment if:
- Obligation is **personal** to debtor
- Creditor has **interest** in debtor's personal performance

### Third Party Payment Effects

| Scenario | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| With subrogation | Third party acquires creditor's rights |
| Without subrogation | Presumption of liberality (rebuttable) |

---

## To Whom (arts. 1239-1242)

### Valid Recipients

- **Creditor** or their **mandataire**
- **Possessor of debt** — if paid in good faith (art. 1240)

### Invalid Recipients

- **Incapable person** (minor, bankrupt) — unless proven profitable (art. 1241)
- **After saisie** — payment to creditor invalid against seizing creditor (art. 1242)

> **Poelmans §492**: Payment to bankrupt or minor only liberates if debtor proves creditor profited.

---

## Object of Payment (arts. 1243-1248)

### Three Requirements

| Requirement | Rule |
|-------------|------|
| **Identité** | Exact thing owed (not substitute) |
| **Intégrité** | Full amount (no partial) |
| **Indivisibilité** | Cannot force partial acceptance |

### Dation en Paiement

Creditor may **agree** to accept different thing — but cannot be forced.

---

## Place of Payment (art. 1247)

### Default Rules

| Type | Place |
|------|-------|
| **Corps certain** | Where thing was at contract formation |
| **Other** | Debtor's domicile (*dette quérable*) |

Contract may specify otherwise (*dette portable*).

---

## Time of Payment

### When Due

Per contract terms or law.

### Anticipation

Debtor may pay early **unless** term benefits creditor.

### Délai de Grâce

Judge may grant reasonable delay (art. 1244) — but limited discretion.

---

## Imputation (arts. 1253-1256)

When debtor owes **multiple debts** to same creditor:

### 1. Debtor's Choice (art. 1253)

Debtor designates at time of payment.

### 2. Default Rules (if silent)

| Priority | Rule |
|----------|------|
| First | Debt **due** over debt not yet due |
| Then | Most **onerous** debt (highest interest, etc.) |
| Then | **Oldest** debt |
| Finally | **Proportional** if equal |

### Interest Rule (art. 1254)

Payment imputed first to **interest**, then to **principal**.

---

## Proof of Payment

### Burden

**Debtor** must prove payment was made.

### Means

- Receipt (*quittance*)
- Bank records
- Witnesses (limited by art. 1341)

---

## Banking Application

### Wire Transfers

- Payment complete when credited to beneficiary's account
- Intermediary bank issues

### Loan Repayments

- Imputation rules apply to multiple facilities
- Contract typically specifies order
- Interest before principal (art. 1254)

### Payment Instructions

- Bank must follow instructions
- Payment to wrong party = not valid payment
