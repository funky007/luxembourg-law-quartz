# Part 05 — Non-performance and Remedies

> **Poelmans Reference**: Chapter VII (L'inexécution des contrats)  
> **Navigation**: [[🧭 Droit des obligations — Index|← Back to Index]]

---

## 1. Typology of Non-performance

### 1.1 Types of Breach

| Type | French | Description |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Total non-performance** | Inexécution totale | No performance at all |
| **Partial non-performance** | Inexécution partielle | Only part performed |
| **Defective performance** | Exécution défectueuse | Performance below standard |
| **Late performance** | Retard | Performance after due date |

### 1.2 Imputability

Non-performance may be:
- **Imputable** to debtor (fault)
- **Non-imputable** — force majeure, fait du créancier, fait du tiers

---

## 2. Force Majeure (arts. 1147-1148)

**See full note**: [[Force majeure (arts. 1147-1148)]]

### 2.1 Definition

Event that prevents performance and exonerates debtor from liability.

### 2.2 Three Cumulative Conditions (Strict Conception)

| Condition | Test |
|-----------|------|
| **Extériorité** | External to debtor |
| **Imprévisibilité** | Unforeseeable at contract formation |
| **Irrésistibilité** | Insurmountable, prevents performance |

> **Poelmans**: Luxembourg applies the **strict conception** — all three conditions must be met.

### 2.3 Effects

- **Temporary**: Suspends obligation
- **Permanent**: Extinguishes obligation
- Risk allocation depends on contract type

---

## 3. Mise en Demeure

### 3.1 Principle

Before claiming breach, creditor must generally put debtor in default.

### 3.2 Forms

- **Sommation** by huissier
- **Acte équivalent** (registered letter, clear demand)
- **Contract may dispense** with mise en demeure

### 3.3 Effects

- Triggers running of interest
- Shifts risk of loss (*res perit debitori* after mise en demeure)
- Prerequisite for damages claim

## 4. Remedies for Non-performance

### 4.1 Overview

```
REMEDIES FOR BREACH
        │
        ├─→ DEFENSIVE
        │       └─ Exception d'inexécution
        │
        ├─→ ENFORCEMENT
        │       ├─ Exécution forcée en nature
        │       └─ Exécution par équivalent (D&I)
        │
        └─→ TERMINATION
                ├─ Résolution judiciaire (art. 1184)
                ├─ Résolution unilatérale (urgence)
                └─ Clause résolutoire
```

### 4.2 Exception d'Inexécution

**See full note**: [[Exception d'inexécution]]

**Definition**: Right to withhold own performance until other party performs.

**Conditions**:
- Synallagmatic contract
- Reciprocal obligations
- Simultaneous performance due
- Proportionality

**Effects**:
- Temporary suspension, not termination
- Defensive mechanism only

## 5. Résolution pour Inexécution (art. 1184)

**See full note**: [[Résolution judiciaire (art. 1184)]]

### 5.1 Principle

> **Art. 1184**: In synallagmatic contracts, resolution is always implied for case of non-performance. The innocent party may choose: (1) force performance, or (2) request resolution with damages.

### 5.2 Conditions

1. **Synallagmatic contract** (with exceptions — see below)
2. **Demandeur is creditor** of unperformed obligation
3. **Mise en demeure** (generally required)
4. **Judicial pronouncement** (principle)
5. **Sufficiently grave** breach

### 5.3 Excluded Contracts

Per Poelmans §211:
- **Partage** (partition) — declaratory effect precludes resolution
- **Rente viagère** (art. 1978) — aleatory character
- But **transaction** can be resolved for breach

### 5.4 Gravity Requirement

> **Poelmans §215**: Judge only resolves if alteration of contractual bond is such that victim would not have contracted had they foreseen it.

Even partial breach may suffice if essential.

---

## 6. Résolution Unilatérale

### 6.1 Exception to Judicial Requirement

Despite art. 1184 requiring judicial resolution, jurisprudence admits unilateral resolution in certain cases.

### 6.2 Conditions (Poelmans §214)

**Standard case**:
1. Put debtor in demeure
2. Allow reasonable time to perform
3. If still no performance → notify resolution

**Urgency case**:
- Resolution without mise en demeure or delay
- When waiting would cause **constant worsening** of prejudice
- Relationship of trust gravely breached

### 6.3 Safeguards

> Creditor must take measures to preserve debtor's defense rights for effective *a posteriori* judicial control.

**Risk**: If resolution found unjustified, the resolving party bears liability.

---

## 7. Clause Résolutoire

**See full note**: [[Clause résolutoire]]

### 7.1 Definition

Contractual clause providing for automatic resolution upon specified breach.

### 7.2 Operation (Poelmans §218)

Even with clause résolutoire:
- **Mise en demeure** still required (unless clause dispenses)
- Clause must be **strictly interpreted** (protects debtor)
- Party must **scrupulously comply** with clause conditions

### 7.3 Key Case (Bank Loan)

> **Poelmans**: Bank's clause résolutoire required mise en demeure. Bank sent demand for full balance without first demanding arrears. Court held: no valid resolution; bank retained right to seek judicial resolution under art. 1184.

### 7.4 Bail (Lease) Exception

> **Art. 1762-2 C.civ.**: Clause résolutoire in lease remains subject to judicial appreciation. Resolution of lease can only occur judicially.

### 7.5 Waiver of Resolution Right

Not *ordre public* — parties may contractually waive right to resolution (Poelmans §220).

---

## 8. Effects of Resolution

### 8.1 Retroactive Effect

Resolution operates *ab initio* — contract deemed never to have existed.

### 8.2 Restitutions

Parties must return what they received:
- In kind if possible
- By equivalent if not

### 8.3 Exception: Successive Contracts

For ongoing performance contracts, resolution operates **ex nunc** (prospective only) — called *résiliation*.

### 8.4 Surviving Clauses

Some clauses survive resolution:
- Jurisdiction clauses
- Arbitration clauses  
- **Clause pénale compensatoire** — survives (Poelmans §217)
- *Clause pénale moratoire* — does NOT survive

---

## 9. Droit de Rétention

### 9.1 Definition (Poelmans §196)

Right of unpaid creditor to retain thing they should deliver until connected debt is paid.

### 9.2 Conditions

- Corporeal object, in commerce, alienable
- Debt: certain, liquid, exigible
- **Connexité** between thing and debt
- Thing must belong to debtor
