# 21 - Constitution de la société

> **Type**: Main Part / Formation
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 2, Chapitres 1-4
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 420-1 to 420-23 L1915 (SA); Arts. 710-1 to 710-13 L1915 (SARL)

---

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Formation Process

**Steichen, Titre 2**: Company formation involves:

| Stage | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Pre-formation** | Negotiations, promoter acts |
| **Constitution** | Execution of founding documents |
| **Registration** | RCS filing, legal personality |
| **Post-formation** | First board meeting, commencement |

### 1.2 Applicable Rules

| Company Type | Primary Rules |
|--------------|---------------|
| **SA** | Arts. 420-1 to 420-23 L1915 |
| **SARL** | Arts. 710-1 to 710-13 L1915 |
| **SNC/SCS** | Arts. 200-1, 310-1 L1915 |
| **SC** | Arts. 1832-1873 C.civ. |

## 2. Conditions of Substance

### 2.1 General Contract Conditions

**Art. 1108 C.civ.**: Valid contract requires:

| Condition | Application to Companies |
|-----------|-------------------------|
| **Consent** | Associates' agreement |
| **Capacity** | Legal capacity of founders |
| **Object** | Corporate purpose |
| **Cause** | Legitimate purpose |

### 2.2 Number of Associates

| Company | Minimum | Maximum |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **SA** | 1 | Unlimited |
| **SARL** | 1 | 100 |
| **SNC** | 2 | Unlimited |
| **SCS** | 2 | Unlimited |
| **SC** | 2 | Unlimited |

### 2.3 Capacity of Founders

**Steichen**: Requirements vary:

| Person | Can Found Company? |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Natural persons** | Yes (with legal capacity) |
| **Legal persons** | Yes |
| **Minors** | Generally no (except emancipated) |
| **Protected adults** | Subject to representation rules |

### 2.4 Corporate Purpose (*Objet social*)

See [[Objet social]]:
- Must be lawful
- Must be possible
- Must be determinate or determinable
- Commercial for commercial companies

## 3. Conditions of Form

### 3.1 Notarial Deed

| Company | Form Required |
|---------|---------------|
| **SA** | Notarial deed mandatory |
| **SARL** | Notarial deed mandatory |
| **SAS** | Notarial deed mandatory |
| **SNC** | Private deed permitted |
| **SC** | Private deed permitted |

### 3.2 Contents of Articles

**Arts. 420-5, 710-3 L1915**: Mandatory contents:

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Name** | Corporate name (*dénomination sociale*) |
| **Registered office** | Address in Luxembourg |
| **Purpose** | Corporate purpose |
| **Capital** | Amount, division, payment |
| **Shares** | Number, form, rights |
| **Duration** | Limited or unlimited |
| **Financial year** | Start and end dates |
| **Governance** | Management structure |
| **Distributions** | Profit allocation rules |

## 4. Capital Requirements

### 4.1 Minimum Capital

| Company | Minimum | Paid at Formation |
|---------|---------|-------------------|
| **SA** | €30,000 | 25% |
| **SARL** | €12,000 | 100% |
| **SARL-S** | €1 | 100% |
| **SAS** | None | As agreed |
| **SE** | €120,000 | 25% |

### 4.2 Contributions

See [[Apports]]:

| Type | Permitted In |
|------|--------------|
| **Cash** | All companies |
| **In kind** | All (with valuation) |
| **Services** | Only partnerships |

### 4.3 Valuation (In Kind)

**Art. 420-6 L1915**: For SA/SARL:
- Independent *réviseur d'entreprises agréé*
- Valuation report
- Filed with RCS

## 5. Registration and Publicity

### 5.1 RCS Filing

**Art. 3 LRCS**: Must file:
- Notarial deed / articles
- List of managers/directors
- Declaration of beneficial owners

### 5.2 Publication

**Art. 9 LRCS**: Published at RESA:
- Extract of articles
- Appointments
- Legal notices

### 5.3 Effect of Registration

| Aspect | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Legal personality** | Acquired upon RCS deposit |
| **Third-party effect** | From publication |
| **Capacity to act** | From registration |

---

## 6. Pre-Formation Acts

### 6.1 Promoters (*Fondateurs*)

**Steichen**: Promoters act before company exists:
- Company not yet legal person
- Cannot bind non-existent company
- Personal liability of promoters

### 6.2 Ratification

**Art. 420-4 L1915** (SA): Company may ratify pre-formation acts:
- By express decision
- Acts then deemed company's from origin
- Promoters discharged

### 6.3 Promoter Liability

If not ratified:
- Promoters personally liable
- Jointly and severally
- To third parties who dealt with them

---

## 7. Nullity of Company

### 7.1 Grounds for Nullity

**Art. 420-8 L1915** (SA): Limited grounds:

| Ground | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **No notarial deed** | Form requirement violated |
| **Illegal purpose** | Purpose contrary to law |
| **No subscribers** | Capital not subscribed |
| **Incapacity** | All founders incapable |

### 7.2 Consequences

**Art. 420-9 L1915**:
- Nullity operates prospectively
- Does not affect past acts
- Leads to liquidation
- Third-party rights protected

### 7.3 Ratification

Defects may be remedied:
- Before court declares nullity
- By proper procedure
- Saves company from dissolution

---

## 8. Founders' Liability

### 8.1 Joint and Several Liability

**Art. 420-10 L1915** (SA): Founders liable for:

| Matter | Liability |
|--------|-----------|
| **Unpaid capital** | Difference if capital not fully subscribed |
| **Overvalued contributions** | Difference in value |
| **Formation defects** | Damages to company/third parties |

### 8.2 Duration

Liability continues until:
- Company properly constituted
- Defects remedied
- Prescription (5 years)

## Banking & Finance Application

### Formation Due Diligence

| Check | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **RCS extract** | Verify registration |
| **Notarial deed** | Proper formation |
| **Capital certificate** | Payment verified |
| **Valuation report** | If contributions in kind |

### Common Formation Issues

| Issue | Risk |
|-------|------|
| **Capital not paid** | Founder liability |
| **Missing notarial deed** | Potential nullity |
| **Improper valuation** | Founder liability |
| **Pre-formation acts** | Personal liability |

### Financing Newly-Formed Companies

- Verify capital actually paid
- Check founder liability exposure
- Consider guarantee from founders
- Monitor for formation defect claims
