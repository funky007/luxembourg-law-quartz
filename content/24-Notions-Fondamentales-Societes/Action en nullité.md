# Action en nullité

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 450-10 L1915 (SA); General principles

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Action en nullité* (nullity action) is a legal action to have a corporate decision (resolution of general meeting or board) declared null and void due to procedural or substantive defects.

---

## Grounds for Nullity

### Procedural Defects

| Defect | Example |
|--------|---------|
| **Convocation** | Improper notice |
| **Quorum** | Insufficient attendance |
| **Majority** | Not achieved |
| **Formalities** | Notarial deed missing |

### Substantive Defects

| Defect | Example |
|--------|---------|
| **Illegal object** | Resolution contrary to law |
| **Articles violation** | Beyond corporate purpose |
| **Abuse of majority** | Oppression of minority |
| **Fraud** | Fraudulent manipulation |

---

## Who May Bring Action

**Art. 450-10 L1915** (SA):

| Person | Standing |
|--------|----------|
| **Shareholders** | Generally yes |
| **Directors** | Yes |
| **Third parties** | If legitimate interest |
| **Creditors** | In limited cases |

---

## Time Limits

**Art. 450-10 L1915**:

| Period | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Standard** | 6 months from resolution/publication |
| **Starting point** | Date of meeting or publication |
| **Forfeiture** | Action barred after period |

---

## Procedure

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Court action** | Before tribunal d'arrondissement |
| **Burden of proof** | On plaintiff |
| **Defense** | Company may defend validity |
| **Interim measures** | May suspend execution |

---

## Effects of Nullity

**Steichen**: If resolution annulled:

| Effect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Retroactive** | Resolution void ab initio |
| **Third parties** | May be protected (good faith) |
| **New resolution** | May be adopted properly |
| **Damages** | May be sought additionally |

---

## Regularization

**Steichen**: Defects may be cured:
- By subsequent proper resolution
- Before court declares nullity
- Minor defects may be overlooked

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Challenge period for corporate actions
- Verify 6-month period elapsed for comfort
- Proper procedures reduce nullity risk
- Third-party protection for good faith
