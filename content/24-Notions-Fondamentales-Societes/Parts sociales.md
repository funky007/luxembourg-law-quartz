# Parts sociales (Membership Interests)

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 2-2, Chapitre 1
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 710-1 to 710-13 L1915 (SARL)

---

## Definition

> *Parts sociales* (membership interests or quota) are the ownership units in companies where shares (*actions*) are not used — primarily SARL, SNC, SCS, and société civile. Unlike shares, parts sociales are not freely negotiable and transfer is restricted.

**Steichen, Titre 2-2, Ch. 1**: Parts sociales represent the associate's proportional interest in the company, combining property rights and membership rights.

---

## 1. Distinction from Actions

### 1.1 Key Differences

| Feature | Parts sociales | Actions |
|---------|---------------|---------|
| **Company types** | SARL, SNC, SCS, SC | SA, SCA, SAS |
| **Negotiability** | NOT negotiable | Negotiable |
| **Transfer** | Requires formalities | Simplified |
| **Form** | Registered only | Registered/dematerialized |
| **Representation** | No certificates (principle) | Certificates possible |
| **Character** | Intuitu personae | Anonymous investment |

### 1.2 Legal Nature

**Steichen**: Parts sociales are:
- **Movable property** (*bien meuble incorporel*)
- **Not negotiable instruments** (*non négociables*)
- **Subject to civil law transfer rules** (not commercial law)

---

## 2. Characteristics

### 2.1 Non-Negotiability

**Fundamental principle**: Parts sociales cannot be transferred by mere delivery or endorsement.

**Steichen**: Transfer requires:
- Formal assignment (*cession de créance*)
- Compliance with statutory restrictions
- Company approval (in many cases)

### 2.2 Intuitu Personae Character

**Steichen**: Ownership of parts sociales is personal:
- Identity of associates matters
- Consent of co-associates often required
- Exit mechanisms limited

### 2.3 Indivisibility

**Art. 710-5 L1915** (SARL): Each part sociale is indivisible:
- Co-owners must appoint single representative
- Cannot be split into fractions

---

## 3. SARL Parts Sociales

### 3.1 Capital Structure

**Art. 710-5 L1915**:

| Requirement | Rule |
|-------------|------|
| **Minimum capital** | EUR 12,000 |
| **Minimum par value** | EUR 1 (since 2016) |
| **Payment** | Fully paid at formation |
| **Contributions** | Cash or in kind (no services) |

### 3.2 Register of Associates

**Art. 710-8 L1915**: SARL must maintain register showing:
- Names and addresses of associates
- Number of parts held by each
- Transfers and dates
- Amounts paid up

### 3.3 Rights Attached

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Profit share** | Proportional to parts held |
| **Voting** | One vote per part (principle) |
| **Information** | Access to accounts |
| **Exit** | Subject to restrictions |

---

## 4. Transfer of Parts Sociales

### 4.1 SARL Transfer Rules

**Art. 710-12 L1915**:

| Transfer to | Requirement |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Existing associates** | Free (unless articles restrict) |
| **Spouse** | Free (unless articles restrict) |
| **Heirs in direct line** | Free (unless articles restrict) |
| **Third parties** | Requires approval |

### 4.2 Approval Procedure (Third Parties)

**Art. 710-12 L1915**:

1. **Request**: Transferor notifies company by bailiff (*huissier*)
2. **Meeting**: Associates decide in meeting
3. **Majority**: More than 50% of capital must approve
4. **Silence**: If no response in 3 months, approval deemed given
5. **Refusal**: Associates must purchase or arrange purchase

### 4.3 Formalities

**Art. 1690 C.civ.**: Transfer effective against third parties by:
- Notification to company (*signification*), OR
- Company acceptance in authentic act

**Steichen**: In practice:
- Notarial deed recommended
- Registration with RCS required for opposability

---

## 5. Transfer in Other Company Types

### 5.1 SNC (General Partnership)

**Art. 200-3 L1915**: Transfer requires:
- **Unanimous consent** of all associates
- Reflects strong intuitu personae character

### 5.2 SCS (Limited Partnership)

| Associate type | Rule |
|----------------|------|
| **Commandités** (general partners) | Unanimous consent |
| **Commanditaires** (limited partners) | Per articles (may be more flexible) |

### 5.3 Société Civile

**Art. 1861 C.civ.**: Transfer requires consent of all associates unless articles provide otherwise.

---

## 6. Encumbrances

### 6.1 Pledge of Parts Sociales

**Art. 2075 C.civ.** and pledge law requirements:

| Step | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Creation** | Written pledge agreement |
| **Perfection** | Notification to company |
| **Registration** | RCS filing for opposability |
| **Enforcement** | Judicial sale or appropriation |

### 6.2 Usufruct

**Steichen**: Parts sociales may be subject to usufruct (*démembrement*):
- **Usufructuary**: Voting rights on profit distribution
- **Bare owner** (*nu-propriétaire*): Other decisions
- Articles may vary allocation

---

## 7. Inheritance and Succession

### 7.1 Death of Associate

**SARL default**: Parts sociales pass to heirs, but:
- Articles may require heir approval by other associates
- Articles may provide for mandatory redemption

### 7.2 SNC

**Art. 200-4 L1915**: Death causes dissolution unless:
- Articles provide for continuation
- With or without heirs

### 7.3 Planning Considerations

**Steichen**: Consider:
- Approval clauses for heirs
- Redemption mechanisms
- Valuation methods for buyout
- Life insurance funding

---

## 8. Valuation

### 8.1 Statutory Valuation

**Art. 710-12 L1915** (SARL): If parties cannot agree on price:
- Expert valuation
- Appointed by mutual agreement or president of tribunal

### 8.2 Common Methods

| Method | Application |
|--------|-------------|
| **Net asset value** | Asset-heavy companies |
| **Earnings multiple** | Trading companies |
| **DCF** | Growing businesses |
| **Contractual formula** | Per shareholders' agreement |

## Banking & Finance Application

### Security over Parts Sociales

**Key considerations**:

| Issue | Analysis |
|-------|----------|
| **Perfection** | Notification to company; RCS filing |
| **Transfer restrictions** | May impede enforcement |
| **Approval requirements** | Pledgee may not be approved |
| **Valuation** | Often opaque (private company) |

### Due Diligence Checklist

| Check | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Register of associates** | Verify ownership |
| **Articles of association** | Transfer restrictions |
| **RCS extracts** | Pledges and encumbrances |
| **Shareholders' agreements** | Additional restrictions |
| **Prior notifications** | Existing pledges |

### Enforcement Challenges

1. **Approval required**: Pledgee or buyer may need associate approval
2. **Pre-emption**: Other associates may have first refusal
3. **Valuation disputes**: No market price available
4. **Illiquidity**: Limited market for minority stakes

### Structuring Considerations

For financing secured by parts sociales:
- Obtain waiver of approval requirements in articles
- Secure associate consent to pledge
- Agree valuation methodology in advance
- Consider call option as alternative to pledge
