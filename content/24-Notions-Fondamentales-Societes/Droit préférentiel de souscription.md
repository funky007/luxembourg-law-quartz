# Droit préférentiel de souscription

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Ch. 1
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 430-9 to 430-11 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: The *droit préférentiel de souscription* (pre-emption right / preferential subscription right) is the right of existing shareholders to subscribe to new shares in proportion to their existing holdings, protecting them against dilution.

---

## Purpose

| Purpose | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Anti-dilution** | Maintain proportional ownership |
| **Value protection** | Subscribe at advantageous price |
| **Control protection** | Preserve voting power |
| **Minority protection** | Statutory right |

---

## Application

**Art. 430-9 L1915**: Right applies in SA to:
- Cash capital increases
- Convertible bonds (conversion rights)
- Bonds with subscription rights

---

## Exercise

**Art. 430-10 L1915**:

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Exercise period** | Minimum 15 days |
| **Notice** | Publication in RESA |
| **Proportionality** | Pro rata to existing holdings |
| **Transferability** | May be sold/assigned |

---

## Suppression or Limitation

**Art. 430-11 L1915**: May be suppressed by EGM:

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Board report** | Justifying suppression |
| **EGM approval** | 2/3 majority |
| **In interest of company** | Must be justified |
| **Price floor** | Cannot be below fair value |

---

## Consequences of Waiver

**Steichen**: If rights not exercised:
- Lapse at end of exercise period
- May be offered to third parties
- Shareholder's stake diluted

---

## SARL Position

**Art. 710-18 L1915**: No statutory DPS in SARL:
- Subject to articles provisions
- Practice often includes similar rights
- Approval clause provides different protection

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- DPS affects share issuance timing
- Structuring around exercise periods
- Rights offerings to existing shareholders
- Suppression requires proper process
