# Parts d'intérêts (Partnership Units)

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Sources**: D. Boone & A. Trotska, JurisNews 2012-2013; C. Duro, ACE 2013
> **Key Legislation**: L1915, Arts. 310-1, 310-6, 320-1, 320-7

---

## Definition

**Parts d'intérêts** represent a partner's interest in an SCS or SCSp. Per Art. 310-1(1) and 320-1(1) L1915:

> "...un ou plusieurs associés commanditaires qui n'engagent qu'une mise déterminée, constitutive de parts d'intérêts, représentées ou non par des titres..."

The Doc. Parl. 6471 explains:
> "Les intérêts d'un associé dans la société seront représentés par des parts d'intérêts (partnership interests), subdivision de l'ensemble des mises en fonds."

**Source**: D. Boone & A. Trotska, "Le renouveau de la commandite (3ème Partie)", JurisNews Droit des sociétés Vol. 6 No 1-2/2013, p. 212-213

---

## 1. Distinction from Other Instruments

### Not Shares (Actions)

Parts d'intérêts differ from actions:
- No minimum nominal value requirement
- No equal value requirement between units
- Transfer rules entirely contractual
- No mandatory stock exchange listing restrictions

### Not Parts Sociales

Unlike Sàrl parts sociales:
- No statutory transfer restrictions (Art. 710-12 L1915 not applicable)
- No capital maintenance rules
- No mandatory notarial formalities

### Comparison Table

| Instrument | Vehicle | Nature |
|------------|---------|--------|
| **Actions** | SA, SCA | Capital shares, freely transferable |
| **Parts sociales** | Sàrl | Capital shares, transfer restricted |
| **Parts d'intérêts** | SCS, SCSp | Partnership interests, contractually defined |
| **Units** | FCP | Co-ownership interests |

## 2. Relationship to Capital

### SCS (With Legal Personality)

In an SCS, parts d'intérêts constitute capital:
> "Toute société dotée de la personnalité morale doit disposer d'un capital social."

**Source**: D. Boone & A. Trotska, op. cit., p. 213

### SCSp (Without Legal Personality)

The question is more complex. Traditional doctrine holds that partnerships without legal personality have no capital. However, the functional definition of capital (values contributed in exchange for social rights) suggests parts d'intérêts form a type of capital.

**Key difference**: The principle of capital fixity (*fixité du capital social*) is excluded:
- Free distributions and reimbursements permitted
- No capital maintenance requirements
- No legal reserve requirement

**Source**: D. Boone & A. Trotska, op. cit., p. 213; C. Duro, ACE 2013/9, p. 13

## 3. Creation and Structure

### Issuance Modalities

**Art. 310-1(2) and 320-1(3) L1915** provide that issuance is governed by the contrat social:
> "Les modalités d'émission des parts d'intérêts sont laissées à la libre discrétion des associés, qui les définissent dans le contrat social."

Issuance may result from:
- Unanimous or majority partner decision
- Delegation to gérants

**Source**: D. Boone & A. Trotska, op. cit., pp. 213-214

### Form Options

Parts d'intérêts may be:
1. **Represented by titres** (certificates) — facilitates transfers and pledges
2. **Not represented by titres** — recorded in partner accounts only

**Source**: C. Duro, ACE 2013/9, p. 12

### Structural Features to Define in Contrat Social

- Proof of ownership (e.g., register inscription)
- Nominal value (if any)
- Classes with differentiated rights:
  - Voting rights (including non-voting units)
  - Multiple voting rights
  - Financial rights variations
  - Tracking shares (*parts d'intérêts traçantes*)

### Apports and Parts d'Intérêts

Parts d'intérêts are issued in exchange for:
- **Apports en numéraire** (cash contributions)
- **Apports en nature** (in-kind contributions)
- **Apports en industrie** (services/expertise contributions)

**Innovation**: Apports en industrie now expressly permitted for both commandités and commanditaires, not entering fixed capital.

**Source**: C. Duro, ACE 2013/9, p. 12

## 4. Transfer Mechanisms

### General Principle (Art. 310-6 and 320-7 L1915)

Transfers of parts d'intérêts are governed primarily by the contrat social, subject to:
- Form requirements specified in the agreement
- Consent requirements specified in the agreement

> "La règle de l'intuitu personae présidant à toute société de personnes doit être réconciliée avec le souhait de réserver une grande part à l'autonomie de volonté des associés."

**Source**: D. Boone & A. Trotska, op. cit., p. 213

### Default Rules (Suppletive)

**For Commanditaire parts d'intérêts**:
- Default: Unanimous consent of commandités required
- Consent of other commanditaires not required
- Rationale: Commandités may have legitimate interest in controlling commanditaire identity

**For Commandité parts d'intérêts**:
- Default: Consent of partners voting as for contrat social modification
- Rationale: Stronger intuitu personae due to unlimited liability

**Source**: C. Duro, ACE 2013/9, pp. 20-21

### Opposability

Transfers become opposable:
- **To the partnership**: By notification or acceptance
- **To third parties**: By publication (for commandité transfers only, given confidentiality of commanditaire identity)

**Source**: D. Boone & A. Trotska, op. cit., pp. 213-214

### Dismemberment (Démembrement)

**Art. 310-6 and 320-7 L1915** expressly permit:
- Usufruct/bare ownership separation
- Fractioning of parts d'intérêts

The contrat social should specify:
- Exercise of voting rights (usufruitier vs. nu-propriétaire)
- Financial rights allocation

**Conseil d'État concern**: Dismemberment of commandité parts d'intérêts raises intuitu personae issues, but the Commission des Finances confirmed it does not change the commandité status, which attaches to the person, not the parts.

**Source**: C. Duro, ACE 2013/9, p. 20

### Pledging (Nantissement)

Parts d'intérêts may be pledged under:
- Contrat social provisions
- Law of 5 August 2005 on financial collateral arrangements

Pledge contracts typically specify register inscription requirements.

**Source**: D. Boone & A. Trotska, op. cit., p. 214

## 5. Redemption Procedures

### Legal Framework

**Art. 310-6(4) and 320-7(4) L1915**:
> "Le contrat social peut autoriser la gérance ou les associés à réduire ou à racheter, en tout ou partie, le cas échéant sur demande d'un ou plusieurs associés, les parts d'intérêts..."

### Redemption Scenarios

| Scenario | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **At partner request** (withdrawal/retrait) | Partner requests redemption; entitled to value at redemption date; contrat social should specify valuation methodology |
| **At partnership initiative** (exclusion) | Partnership initiates redemption; effectively excludes the partner; requires precise contractual conditions |

**Source**: C. Duro, ACE 2013/9, p. 21

### Valuation Considerations

The contrat social should address:
- Calculation methodology for redemption price
- Independent valuation procedure
- Expert appointment mechanism

**Warning**: Conditions should not deprive the partner of all rights in partnership assets.

**Source**: C. Duro, ACE 2013/9, p. 21 (note 51-52)

## 6. Carried Interest Implications

### Carried Interest via Parts d'Intérêts

Fund managers typically receive carried interest through special classes of parts d'intérêts:
- Subordinated to return of investor capital
- Entitled to share of profits above hurdle rate
- Often issued for nominal consideration or apports en industrie

### Tax Treatment

The Loi AIFM introduced a favorable tax regime for carried interest (*intéressement aux plus-values*):
- Subject to specific conditions regarding the manager's activities
- The parts d'intérêts structure facilitates implementation

**Source**: D. Boone & A. Trotska, op. cit., p. 212

### Structuring Considerations

For carried interest parts d'intérêts:
- Define priority/waterfall in contrat social
- Specify vesting conditions
- Address forfeiture provisions (good leaver/bad leaver)
- Consider clawback mechanisms

## 7. Register Requirements

### Mandatory Register (Art. 310-1(6) and 320-1(6) L1915)

The partnership must maintain a register containing:
- Partner names, surnames, professions, addresses
- For legal entities: name, form, address, registration number
- Parts d'intérêts held by each partner
- Transfers and dates of notification/acceptance

**Location**: Register kept at registered office.

**Access**: Partners may consult, subject to contrat social limitations.

**Source**: C. Duro, ACE 2013/9, p. 15

---

## 8. Listing Possibility

### Exchange Listing

The question of listing parts d'intérêts was extensively debated during the legislative process.

**Conclusion**: Listing of commanditaire parts d'intérêts appears possible:
- Luxembourg law does not prohibit admission to trading
- Simple admission is not a public offer
- Public offers of parts d'intérêts remain prohibited

**Source**: C. Duro, ACE 2013/9, p. 13

## Banking & Finance Application

### Security Over Parts d'Intérêts

| Issue | Analysis |
|-------|----------|
| **Perfection** | Notification to partnership; register inscription |
| **Transfer restrictions** | May impede enforcement; obtain waivers |
| **Consent requirements** | Pledgee may not be approved; include consent in pledge |
| **Valuation** | Often opaque; agree methodology in advance |

### Due Diligence Checklist

- [ ] Review contrat social transfer provisions
- [ ] Identify any consent requirements for pledge/transfer
- [ ] Verify register of partners
- [ ] Check for existing pledges or encumbrances
- [ ] Confirm valuation methodology for enforcement

### Subscription Line Security

Parts d'intérêts can serve as collateral alongside:
- Uncalled capital commitments
- Rights under subscription agreements
- Distributions receivable
