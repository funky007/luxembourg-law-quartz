# Actionnaire

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 430-1 to 430-3 L1915; Various L1915 provisions

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: An *actionnaire* (shareholder) is a person who holds shares (*actions*) in a company, specifically in capital companies like SA, SCA, or SAS, giving them ownership rights in the company.

---

## Distinguished from Associé

| Term | Applies To |
|------|-----------|
| **Actionnaire** | SA, SCA, SAS (share-based) |
| **Associé** | Generic term for any company member |
| **Porteur de parts** | SARL, SNC (parts sociales) |

---

## Rights of Shareholders

### Financial Rights

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Dividends** | Share in distributed profits |
| **Liquidation surplus** | Share on dissolution |
| **Pre-emption (DPS)** | Priority on new issues |
| **Capital return** | On reduction for shareholders |

### Political Rights

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Voting** | At general meetings |
| **Information** | Access to documents |
| **Question** | At AGM |
| **Challenge** | Contest invalid resolutions |

### Individual Rights

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Transfer shares** | Subject to any restrictions |
| **Equality** | Equal treatment within class |
| **Minority protection** | Against abuse |

See [[23 - Les associés]] for details.

---

## Obligations

| Obligation | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Pay for shares** | Honor subscription |
| **Good faith** | Act loyally |
| **Share losses** | Participate in losses |

---

## Types of Shareholders

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Majority** | >50% voting rights |
| **Minority** | <50% voting rights |
| **Controlling** | De facto control |
| **Institutional** | Funds, pension funds |
| **Individual** | Natural persons |

---

## Proof of Status

**Steichen**: Shareholder status evidenced by:

| Evidence | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Share register** | Company's register of shareholders |
| **Share certificate** | If issued |
| **Account statement** | For dematerialized shares |

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Identify shareholders for due diligence
- Shareholder analysis for credit decisions
- Share register is primary evidence
- Beneficial ownership may differ from legal
