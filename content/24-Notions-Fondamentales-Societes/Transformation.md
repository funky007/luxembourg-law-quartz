# Transformation

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 1010-1 to 1010-9 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Transformation* is the change of a company's legal form (e.g., SARL to SA) without dissolution, the company continuing with its new form while maintaining legal personality.

---

## Key Principle

**Art. 1010-1 L1915**: Transformation does NOT:
- Create a new legal person
- Dissolve the existing company
- Interrupt legal personality
- Affect existing contracts

---

## Types of Transformation

| Direction | Common Examples |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Upgrade** | SARL → SA, SNC → SARL |
| **Downgrade** | SA → SARL |
| **Specialized** | SA → SAS, SA → SE |
| **Partnership** | SNC → SARL, SC → SARL |

---

## Procedure

**Art. 1010-3 L1915**:

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Board proposal** | Report on transformation |
| **Financial statements** | Not older than 3 months |
| **Expert report** | If capital verification needed |
| **EGM resolution** | Required majority |
| **Notarial deed** | New articles in new form |
| **RCS filing** | Publication |

---

## Majority Requirements

| Transformation | Required Majority |
|----------------|-------------------|
| **SA → SARL** | 2/3 majority |
| **SARL → SA** | 2/3 of capital |
| **To unlimited** | Generally unanimity |
| **From unlimited** | Per articles |

---

## Effects

| Effect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Continuity** | Same legal person |
| **Contracts** | Continue unchanged |
| **Assets/liabilities** | Remain with company |
| **New rules** | Governed by new form's rules |

---

## Restrictions

**Steichen**: Certain transformations restricted:
- Minimum capital must be met for new form
- Cannot transform below minimum requirements
- SE transformation has special rules

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- No change of counterparty (same legal person)
- Verify new form compliance with loan terms
- Update documentation for new legal form
- Check governance changes affect controls
