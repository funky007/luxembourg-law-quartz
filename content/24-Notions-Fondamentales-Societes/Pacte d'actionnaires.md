# Pacte d'actionnaires

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 1134 C.civ. (contractual freedom)

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: A *pacte d'actionnaires* (shareholders' agreement) is a private contract between some or all shareholders governing their relationships and exercise of rights, separate from and supplementary to the articles of association.

---

## Nature

| Characteristic | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| **Contractual** | Governed by contract law |
| **Inter partes** | Binds only signatories |
| **Confidential** | Not published (unlike articles) |
| **Supplementary** | Complements articles |
| **Cannot contradict** | Must respect mandatory law |

---

## Common Provisions

### Governance

| Clause | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Board composition** | Reserved seats, nomination rights |
| **Voting agreements** | How to vote on matters |
| **Reserved matters** | Unanimity/veto rights |
| **Information rights** | Additional reporting |

### Transfer Restrictions

| Clause | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Pre-emption (ROFR)** | Right of first refusal |
| **Tag-along** | Join in sale by majority |
| **Drag-along** | Force sale by minority |
| **Lock-up** | Prohibition on transfer |

### Exit Provisions

| Clause | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Put options** | Right to sell to others |
| **Call options** | Right to buy from others |
| **Exit events** | IPO, trade sale triggers |
| **Valuation** | How to determine price |

---

## Enforceability

**Steichen**: Shareholders' agreements are enforceable:

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Between parties** | Fully binding |
| **Against company** | Not directly (separate person) |
| **Against third parties** | No effect (privity) |
| **Specific performance** | Courts may order |
| **Damages** | For breach |

---

## Relationship with Articles

| Aspect | Articles | Shareholders' Agreement |
|--------|----------|------------------------|
| **Publication** | Published at RCS | Private |
| **Binding on** | Company and all shareholders | Only signatories |
| **Amendment** | EGM procedure | Contract amendment |
| **Third-party effect** | Yes | No |

---

## Validity Limits

**Steichen**: Certain clauses may be invalid:
- Contrary to mandatory company law
- Contrary to public policy
- Unduly restricting voting rights (debated)
- Perpetual commitments (may need term)

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Always request copy in due diligence
- May contain change of control provisions
- Drag-along affects exit strategy
- Lock-ups restrict share pledges/enforcement
