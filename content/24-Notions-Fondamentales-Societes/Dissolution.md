# Dissolution (Winding Up)

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Chapitre 4
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 1100-1 to 1100-13 L1915

---

## Definition

> *Dissolution* (winding up) is the legal event that ends the company's active existence and triggers the liquidation process. The company continues to exist only for purposes of its liquidation until final cancellation from the RCS.

**Steichen, Titre 4, Ch. 4**: Dissolution is not the same as cessation of existence — the company survives for liquidation purposes (*la société subsiste pour les besoins de la liquidation*).

---

## 1. Causes of Dissolution

### 1.1 Common Causes (All Companies)

**Art. 1100-1 L1915**:

| Cause | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Expiry of term** | Duration fixed in articles expires |
| **Realization of object** | Corporate purpose achieved |
| **Impossibility** | Corporate purpose becomes impossible |
| **Shareholder decision** | Voluntary dissolution |
| **Court order** | Judicial dissolution |
| **Other statutory causes** | Merger, bankruptcy, etc. |

### 1.2 Causes Specific to Company Types

| Company Type | Special Causes |
|--------------|----------------|
| **SA/SARL** | Net assets below half of capital |
| **SNC** | Death/incapacity of associate (unless continued) |
| **Single-member** | Death of sole member (unless continued) |

---

## 2. Voluntary Dissolution

### 2.1 Decision

**Art. 1100-2 L1915**: Shareholders may decide dissolution at EGM:

| Company | Majority | Quorum |
|---------|----------|--------|
| **SA** | 2/3 votes cast | 50% capital (first meeting) |
| **SARL** | 3/4 capital | N/A |
| **SNC** | Unanimous | N/A |

### 2.2 Formalities

| Step | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Decision** | EGM resolution |
| **Form** | Notarial deed (SA, SARL) |
| **Publication** | RCS filing and RESA publication |
| **Appointment** | Liquidator(s) designated |

---

## 3. Mandatory Dissolution (Capital Loss)

### 3.1 SA — Article 480-2 L1915

**Trigger**: Net assets fall below **half** of subscribed capital.

**Procedure**:
1. Board must convene EGM within **2 months**
2. Shareholders decide on dissolution or remedial measures
3. If net assets fall below **one-quarter**: dissolution may be demanded by any party

### 3.2 SARL — Article 500-17 L1915

**Trigger**: Net assets below **half** of stated capital.

**Procedure**: Same as SA — gérant must convene meeting for shareholders to decide.

### 3.3 Consequences of Non-Compliance

**Steichen**: If management fails to convene meeting:
- Directors/gérants personally liable
- Third parties may petition for dissolution
- Criminal sanctions possible

---

## 4. Judicial Dissolution

### 4.1 Grounds

**Art. 1100-4 L1915**: Court may order dissolution for:

| Ground | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Just cause** (*justes motifs*) | Serious reasons justifying dissolution |
| **Paralysis** | Deadlock in governance |
| **Loss of *affectio societatis*** | Irreparable breakdown between associates |
| **Abuse** | Serious misconduct by majority |

### 4.2 Procedure

| Step | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Application** | By associate, creditor, or public prosecutor |
| **Court** | Commercial chamber of district court |
| **Evidence** | Demonstrate grounds |
| **Outcome** | Dissolution order + liquidator appointment |

### 4.3 Just Cause Examples

**Steichen**: Courts have found just cause in:
- Persistent serious disputes between associates
- Systematic oppression of minority
- Complete loss of confidence
- Impossibility of normal functioning

---

## 5. Dissolution by Merger or Demerger

### 5.1 Merger

**Art. 1021-4 L1915**: Absorbed company dissolved:
- No liquidation (assets transferred universally)
- Shareholders receive acquiring company shares

### 5.2 Demerger (Scission)

**Art. 1022-3 L1915**: Similar to merger:
- Demerged company dissolved
- Assets split among receiving companies
- No liquidation procedure

---

## 6. Effects of Dissolution

### 6.1 Company Continues for Liquidation

**Art. 1100-5 L1915**: After dissolution:
- Company survives for liquidation purposes
- Legal personality continues
- Must add "en liquidation" to name
- New activities prohibited (except liquidation)

### 6.2 Powers of Organs

| Organ | Powers After Dissolution |
|-------|-------------------------|
| **Management** | Replaced by liquidator |
| **Shareholders** | Approve liquidation acts, accounts |
| **Supervisory** | Continue until end of liquidation |

### 6.3 Third-Party Effects

- Contracts may terminate (check clauses)
- Employment relationships affected
- Security interests continue during liquidation

---

## 7. Revocation of Dissolution

### 7.1 Possibility

**Art. 1100-3 L1915**: Shareholders may revoke dissolution decision:
- Before liquidation is substantially complete
- Same majority as for dissolution
- Requires EGM

### 7.2 Limitations

Revocation not possible if:
- Liquidation distributions already made
- Third-party rights would be prejudiced
- Dissolution was judicial

---

## 8. Single-Member Company

### 8.1 Death of Sole Member

**Art. 1100-1 L1915**: Death of sole member:
- Does not automatically dissolve company
- Heirs may continue
- Articles may provide otherwise

### 8.2 Continuation

Heirs have option to:
- Continue company
- Sell parts sociales/shares
- Dissolve and liquidate

## Banking & Finance Application

### Monitoring Covenants

| Indicator | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| **Net assets <50% capital** | Dissolution meeting required |
| **Audit qualification** | Review going concern |
| **Filing delays** | May indicate distress |

### Lender Response to Distress

| Situation | Consideration |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Pre-dissolution** | Covenant triggers, acceleration rights |
| **Dissolution pending** | File claims, protect security |
| **Liquidation commenced** | Prove claims, monitor distribution |

### Security Considerations

| Issue | Analysis |
|-------|----------|
| **Security continues** | During liquidation |
| **Enforcement** | May proceed against assets |
| **Priority** | Maintain ranking |
| **Liquidator cooperation** | May be required |

### Due Diligence Points

When lending to distressed company:
- Check net assets vs. capital
- Review management's remedial plans
- Assess litigation risk (judicial dissolution)
- Verify no dissolution resolution pending
