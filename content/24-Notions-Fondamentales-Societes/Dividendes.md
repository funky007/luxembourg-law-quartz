# Dividendes (Dividends)

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Chapitre 2; Titre 4, Chapitre 1
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 461-1 to 461-5 L1915 (SA); Art. 710-22 L1915 (SARL)

---

## Definition

> *Dividendes* (dividends) are distributions of profits to shareholders in proportion to their shareholdings, decided by the general meeting from distributable reserves after approval of annual accounts.

**Steichen**: Dividends represent the shareholder's right to participate in company profits — one of the fundamental rights attached to share ownership.

---

## 1. Right to Dividends

### 1.1 Fundamental Right

**Steichen**: Every shareholder has the right to:
- Share in profits (proportionally)
- Participate in distribution decisions
- Receive declared dividends

### 1.2 Not Automatic

**Steichen**: Dividend distribution requires:
- Profits exist (or distributable reserves)
- AGM decides to distribute
- Legal requirements satisfied

---

## 2. Conditions for Distribution

### 2.1 Distributable Amounts

**Art. 461-2 L1915** (SA): No distribution if:

> Net assets < subscribed capital + non-distributable reserves

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Net assets** | Total assets minus total liabilities |
| **Subscribed capital** | Registered share capital |
| **Non-distributable reserves** | Legal reserve + statutory reserves |

### 2.2 Legal Reserve

**Art. 461-1 L1915** (SA):
- 5% of annual net profit allocated to legal reserve
- Until reserve equals 10% of share capital
- Non-distributable

### 2.3 SARL Rules

**Art. 710-22 L1915**: Same principles:
- Distribution limited to profits + distributable reserves
- Net assets test applies
- Legal reserve requirement (same as SA)

---

## 3. Distribution Procedure

### 3.1 Ordinary Dividends

| Step | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Accounts approval** | AGM approves annual accounts |
| **Board proposal** | Directors recommend distribution |
| **AGM decision** | Shareholders vote on distribution |
| **Payment** | Within 9 months (unless extended) |

### 3.2 Interim Dividends

**Art. 461-3 L1915** (SA): Permitted if:
- Articles authorize
- Interim accounts prepared
- Legal reserve test satisfied
- Board/directoire decides

**Steichen**: Interim dividends anticipate year-end profits — risk of clawback if final profits insufficient.

### 3.3 Who Decides

| Company | Decision-Maker |
|---------|----------------|
| **SA** | AGM (ordinary) |
| **SARL** | Associates' meeting |
| **Interim** | Board (if authorized) |

---

## 4. Allocation Among Shareholders

### 4.1 Default Rule

**Steichen**: Distribution proportional to shareholding:
- One share = one dividend unit
- All shares of same class equal

### 4.2 Variations

| Arrangement | Validity |
|-------------|----------|
| **Preference shares** | Priority or enhanced dividend |
| **Founders' shares** | Special profit participation |
| **Unequal distribution** | Requires shareholder consent |

### 4.3 Treasury Shares

**Art. 430-16 L1915**: Company's own shares:
- No dividend rights while held by company
- Their portion accrues to other shareholders

---

## 5. Restrictions on Distribution

### 5.1 Legal Restrictions

| Restriction | Rule |
|-------------|------|
| **Net assets test** | No distribution below threshold |
| **Legal reserve** | Cannot distribute |
| **Unrealized gains** | Generally not distributable |
| **Share premium** | Distributable (unless articles restrict) |

### 5.2 Contractual Restrictions

Loan agreements may impose:
- Minimum retained earnings
- Dividend stopper
- Distribution baskets
- Coverage ratios

### 5.3 Consequences of Illegal Distribution

**Art. 461-5 L1915**: Shareholders who received distributions:
- In bad faith: must repay
- In good faith: no repayment obligation

Directors liable for authorizing illegal distributions.

---

## 6. Tax Treatment

### 6.1 At Company Level

**Steichen**: Dividends paid from after-tax profits:
- Not deductible for company
- Participation exemption may apply for received dividends

### 6.2 At Shareholder Level

| Shareholder | Tax Treatment |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Luxembourg corporate** | Participation exemption (if conditions met) |
| **Foreign corporate** | Withholding tax (15%, reduced by treaty) |
| **Individual** | Taxable income (50% exemption) |

---

## 7. Timing

### 7.1 When Payable

**Steichen**: Dividend payable when:
- AGM declares it
- Payment date arrives
- Shareholder identified (record date)

### 7.2 Record Date

**Art. 67-2 L1915**: For SA:
- Rights determined by shareholding on 14th day before AGM
- Unless articles provide otherwise

### 7.3 Prescription

**Steichen**: Unclaimed dividends:
- Prescribe after 5 years
- Revert to company

---

## 8. In Kind Distributions

### 8.1 Possibility

**Steichen**: Distribution may be:
- Cash (most common)
- In kind (assets)
- Shares (stock dividend)

### 8.2 Requirements

- Same approval process
- Valuation required for in kind
- Tax implications differ

---

## Banking & Finance Application

### Covenant Considerations

| Covenant | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Dividend stopper** | No distributions if default |
| **Restricted payments** | Cap on distributions |
| **Leverage test** | Distribution conditional on ratio |
| **Reserve requirement** | Maintain minimum equity |

### Due Diligence

| Check | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Distribution history** | Dividend policy |
| **Distributable reserves** | Capacity for distribution |
| **Restrictions** | Existing contractual limits |
| **Preferential rights** | Priority distributions |

### Cash Sweep Structures

In acquisition finance:
- Mandatory distribution provisions
- Upstreaming mechanisms
- Tax-efficient structures

### Warning Signs

- Distributions exceeding profits
- Failure to maintain legal reserve
- Distributions despite losses
- Related-party distributions at unusual terms
