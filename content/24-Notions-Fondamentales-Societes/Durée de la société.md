# Durée de la société

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 2, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 420-5, 710-3 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Durée de la société* (duration of the company) is the period for which a company is formed, as specified in the articles of association. It may be limited (fixed term) or unlimited.

---

## Types of Duration

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Durée illimitée** | No fixed term (most common) |
| **Durée déterminée** | Fixed term (e.g., 99 years) |
| **Object-linked** | Until completion of specific purpose |

---

## Mandatory Mention

**Arts. 420-5, 710-3 L1915**: Duration must be stated in articles:
- If not specified: unlimited (default interpretation)
- Maximum: no legal maximum
- Traditional practice: 99 years (now less common)

---

## Consequences of Expiry

**Steichen**: At term expiry:

| Effect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Automatic dissolution** | Company dissolves |
| **No further business** | Only winding up permitted |
| **Liquidation** | Must commence |
| **Unless extended** | Before term expires |

---

## Extension of Duration

**Steichen**: Before expiry, company may:

| Action | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| **EGM resolution** | Amend articles |
| **Before expiry** | Must act in time |
| **Publication** | RCS and RESA |
| **Notarial deed** | If SA/SARL |

---

## Reduction of Duration

**Steichen**: Company may also:
- Shorten duration by EGM
- Convert to unlimited
- With proper majorities

---

## Practical Considerations

| Aspect | Consideration |
|--------|---------------|
| **Long-term contracts** | May exceed company duration |
| **Investment vehicles** | May have fixed life |
| **Holding companies** | Usually unlimited |
| **Project companies** | May be project-linked |

---

## Calendar Monitoring

**Practice**: Important to monitor:
- Track duration in register
- Diary expiry dates
- Act well before expiry
- Consider conversion to unlimited

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Verify duration in articles
- Loan term should not exceed company duration
- Factor extension requirements
- Project finance: align with project life
