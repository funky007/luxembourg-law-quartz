# Réduction de capital

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Ch. 1
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 430-19 to 430-23 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Réduction de capital* (capital reduction) is the decrease of a company's share capital, subject to creditor protection rules given its impact on the capital maintenance principle.

---

## Types of Reduction

| Type | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Loss absorption** | Write off accumulated losses |
| **Return of capital** | Repay shareholders excess capital |
| **Share cancellation** | Reduce number of shares |
| **Nominal reduction** | Lower par value per share |

---

## Procedure (SA)

**Art. 430-19 L1915**:

| Step | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Board proposal** | With report on reasons |
| **EGM resolution** | 2/3 majority |
| **Notarial deed** | For articles amendment |
| **Creditor protection** | Opposition period |
| **RCS filing** | Publication |

---

## Creditor Protection

**Art. 430-22 L1915**:

| Mechanism | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Publication** | Twice in RESA |
| **Opposition right** | Creditors may oppose |
| **Waiting period** | 30 days after second publication |
| **Court involvement** | If opposition not settled |

---

## Equal Treatment

**Steichen**: Reduction must respect:
- Proportional treatment of shareholders
- No preferential return (unless specific class)
- Equal reduction within same class

---

## Minimum Capital

**Art. 430-20 L1915**: Capital cannot be reduced below:

| Company | Minimum |
|---------|---------|
| **SA** | €30,000 |
| **SARL** | €12,000 |
| **SE** | €120,000 |

---

## Special Cases

| Case | Rules |
|------|-------|
| **Treasury shares** | May be cancelled without payment |
| **Partial payment** | Release from future calls |
| **Loss absorption** | No creditor opposition needed |

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Verify compliance with loan covenants
- Capital reductions may trigger defaults
- Check minimum capital maintained
- Creditor opposition rights protect lenders
