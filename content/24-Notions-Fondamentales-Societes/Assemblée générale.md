# Assemblée générale (Shareholders' Meeting)

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Chapitre 2
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 67-67-4, 189-191 L1915

---

## Definition

> The *assemblée générale* (shareholders' or associates' meeting) is the supreme organ of the company where owners exercise their collective decision-making power on matters reserved to them by law or articles of association.

**Steichen, Titre 3, Ch. 2**: The assembly embodies the principle of shareholder sovereignty (*souveraineté des associés*) — ultimate authority over company affairs.

---

## 1. Types of Assemblies

### 1.1 Ordinary General Meeting (AGM)

**Art. 70 L1915** (SA): Annual meeting to:
- Approve annual accounts
- Discharge directors (*quitus*)
- Appoint/remove directors
- Appoint statutory auditor
- Decide on profit distribution

**Timing**: Within 6 months of financial year end.

### 1.2 Extraordinary General Meeting (EGM)

**Art. 67 L1915**: Required for:
- Amendment of articles
- Capital increases/reductions
- Mergers, demergers
- Dissolution
- Change of corporate purpose
- Transformation

**Quorum/Majority**: Higher thresholds apply.

### 1.3 Special Meetings

**Art. 68 L1915**: Class meetings for holders of specific share categories.

---

## 2. Convening the Assembly

### 2.1 Who May Convene

| Person | Circumstances |
|--------|---------------|
| **Board** | Normal course; mandatory for AGM |
| **Statutory auditor** | If board fails to convene |
| **Shareholders** (20%+) | Request to board; court order if refused |
| **Liquidator** | During liquidation |

### 2.2 Notice Requirements (SA)

**Art. 70 L1915**:
- Published in RESA (Recueil électronique)
- At least **8 days** before meeting (registered shares)
- At least **30 days** before (listed companies)

**Content**:
- Date, time, place
- Agenda
- Proposed resolutions
- Participation formalities

---

## 3. Participation and Voting

### 3.1 Right to Attend

**Steichen**: Every shareholder has right to:
- Attend in person
- Be represented by proxy
- Vote on all agenda items

### 3.2 Record Date

**Art. 67-2 L1915**: For SA, rights determined by shareholding on **14th day** before meeting (*date d'enregistrement*).

### 3.3 Voting Rights

**Default**: One share = one vote.

**Exceptions**:
- Shares with multiple votes (if permitted)
- Non-voting shares
- Treasury shares (no vote)

### 3.4 Proxy Voting

**Art. 67-3 L1915**: Shareholder may appoint proxy:
- In writing (including electronic)
- Proxy may be anyone (not just shareholder)
- Revocable until vote

---

## 4. Quorum and Majority

### 4.1 SA — Ordinary Matters

| Requirement | First Meeting | Reconvened |
|-------------|---------------|------------|
| **Quorum** | None (unless articles) | None |
| **Majority** | Simple majority of votes cast | Simple majority |

### 4.2 SA — Extraordinary Matters

**Art. 67-1 L1915**:

| Requirement | First Meeting | Reconvened |
|-------------|---------------|------------|
| **Quorum** | 50% of capital | No quorum |
| **Majority** | 2/3 of votes cast | 2/3 of votes cast |

### 4.3 SARL

**Art. 189 L1915**:
- **Ordinary**: Majority of capital (>50%)
- **Extraordinary**: 3/4 of capital
- **Unanimity**: Change of nationality, increase commitments

---

## 5. Powers of the Assembly

### 5.1 Exclusive Powers

**Steichen**: Only assembly may:
- Amend articles of association
- Approve annual accounts
- Appoint/remove directors
- Decide on dissolution
- Approve mergers/demergers
- Ratify certain board decisions

### 5.2 Residual Powers

Assembly may decide any matter not expressly reserved to other organs.

### 5.3 Limitations

Assembly cannot:
- Interfere in day-to-day management
- Bind company to third parties
- Override mandatory law

---

## 6. Decision-Making Procedures

### 6.1 Written Resolutions (SA)

**Art. 67-4 L1915**: Unanimous written consent may replace meeting:
- All shareholders must sign
- Not available for all matters

### 6.2 SARL Written Decisions

**Art. 191 L1915**: Written consultation permitted:
- Questions sent to associates
- Responses within 15 days
- Majority calculated on responses

### 6.3 Remote Participation

**Art. 67-3 L1915**: Articles may permit:
- Electronic voting
- Video/telephone participation
- Must ensure identification and security

---

## 7. Defective Resolutions

### 7.1 Grounds for Challenge

| Ground | Example |
|--------|---------|
| **Procedural defects** | Improper notice, no quorum |
| **Substantive defects** | Violation of law or articles |
| **Abuse of majority** | Decision in bad faith |

### 7.2 Nullity Action

**Steichen**: Resolution may be annulled by court:
- Action by shareholder, director, interested third party
- Prescription: varies by ground

## Banking & Finance Application

### Due Diligence on Corporate Approvals

| Transaction | Typically Requires |
|-------------|-------------------|
| **Guarantee** | Board or EGM (per articles) |
| **Security over assets** | Board; EGM if material |
| **Capital increase** | EGM |
| **Merger/acquisition** | EGM |
| **Amendment of purpose** | EGM |

### Documentation Checklist

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Notice of meeting** | Proper convocation |
| **Attendance sheet** | Quorum verification |
| **Proxy forms** | Representative authority |
| **Minutes** | Proof of resolution |
| **RCS filing** | Third-party effectiveness |

### Common Issues

1. **Defective notice**: May invalidate resolution
2. **Quorum not met**: Resolution voidable
3. **Ultra vires resolution**: Beyond assembly's powers
4. **Conflict of interest**: Voting restrictions may apply

### Shareholder Undertakings

In financing, banks may require:
- Undertaking to vote in favor of certain resolutions
- Advance approval of corporate actions
- Information rights on assembly outcomes
