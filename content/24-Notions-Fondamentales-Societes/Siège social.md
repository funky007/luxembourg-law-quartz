# Siège social

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Ch. 1
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 2 L1915; Art. 100-3 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: The *siège social* (registered office) is the official address of a company, determining its nationality, applicable law, and jurisdiction.

---

## Functions

| Function | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Nationality** | Luxembourg company if siège in Luxembourg |
| **Applicable law** | Luxembourg company law governs |
| **Jurisdiction** | Luxembourg courts competent |
| **Notifications** | Official address for legal service |
| **Publication** | RCS registration address |

---

## Requirements

**Steichen**: The registered office must be:

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Real** | Actual presence, not just postal |
| **In Luxembourg** | For Luxembourg nationality |
| **Determinate** | Specific address |
| **In articles** | Mandatory content |

---

## Siège Statutaire vs. Siège Réel

| Concept | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Siège statutaire** | Address in articles/RCS |
| **Siège réel** | Actual center of administration |
| **Luxembourg approach** | Siège statutaire determines nationality |
| **Consequence** | Substance requirements increasingly important |

---

## Transfer of Siège

| Type | Requirements |
|------|--------------|
| **Within Luxembourg** | Board/gérant decision (articles change) |
| **To another country** | Dissolution and reincorporation (generally) |
| **From another country** | Subject to origin country rules |

---

## Registered Agent

**Practice**: Many Luxembourg companies use:
- Domiciliation agents
- Professional registered office services
- Must comply with domiciliation law

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Verify siège from RCS extract
- Check for real substance vs. letterbox
- Domiciliation agreements may have restrictions
- Transfer of siège affects loan covenants
