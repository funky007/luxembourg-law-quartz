# Retrait d'associé

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Various L1915 provisions; Art. 1869 C.civ. (SC)

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Retrait d'associé* (withdrawal of associate) is the voluntary exit of an associate from a company, which may occur through share transfer, redemption by the company, or exercise of withdrawal rights where available.

---

## Methods of Exit

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Transfer** | Sale of shares/parts to third party |
| **Redemption** | Company buys back shares |
| **Contractual withdrawal** | If provided in articles |
| **Judicial dissolution** | Extreme remedy |

---

## By Company Type

### SA

| Mechanism | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **Share transfer** | Freely transferable (default) |
| **Approval clause** | If articles provide |
| **No withdrawal right** | Unless articles provide |
| **Redemption** | Share buyback procedures |

### SARL

**Art. 710-12 L1915**:

| Mechanism | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **To associates** | Free transfer |
| **To third parties** | Requires approval |
| **Refusal** | Company must buy or find buyer |
| **Judicial valuation** | If price disputed |

### Partnerships (SNC, SCS)

| Mechanism | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **Transfer** | Requires consent (all or per articles) |
| **Withdrawal** | May cause dissolution |
| **Judicial authorization** | Possible for just cause |

---

## SARL Exit Mechanism

**Art. 710-12 L1915**: If third-party transfer refused:

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Refusal** | Majority refuses approval |
| **3 months** | To find alternative |
| **Company purchase** | Or find another buyer |
| **Price dispute** | Expert valuation |
| **Approval deemed** | If no alternative found |

---

## Valuation

**Steichen**: Exit price determined by:

| Method | Situation |
|--------|-----------|
| **Agreement** | Parties agree price |
| **Articles formula** | If pre-agreed |
| **Expert** | Independent valuation |
| **Court** | If dispute persists |

---

## Payment

| Aspect | Consideration |
|--------|---------------|
| **Timing** | As agreed or determined |
| **Installments** | May be permitted |
| **Security** | For deferred payment |
| **Capital reduction** | If company redeems |

---

## Forced Exit (Exclusion)

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Just cause** | Serious breach by associate |
| **Procedure** | Court action or per articles |
| **Compensation** | Fair value of interest |
| **Common in** | Personal companies |

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Exit mechanisms affect share security
- Understand redemption procedures
- Valuation mechanisms critical
- May require lender consent
