# Augmentation de capital (Capital Increase)

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Chapitre 1
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 420-24 to 420-28 L1915 (SA); Art. 710-9 L1915 (SARL)

---

## Definition

> *Augmentation de capital* (capital increase) is the corporate operation that raises the company's registered share capital through new contributions (cash or in kind) or by incorporating existing reserves, resulting in issuance of new shares or increase in nominal value of existing shares.

**Steichen, Titre 4, Ch. 1**: Capital increase is the primary mechanism for raising equity financing and strengthening the company's capital base.

---

## 1. Methods of Capital Increase

### 1.1 By New Contributions

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Cash contribution** | New funds injected |
| **Contribution in kind** | Assets transferred to company |
| **Debt conversion** | Loans converted to equity |
| **Merger** | Capital increase by absorption |

### 1.2 By Incorporation of Reserves

| Source | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Retained earnings** | Profits converted to capital |
| **Share premium** | Premium reserve capitalized |
| **Revaluation reserves** | Asset revaluation incorporated |

### 1.3 Increase in Nominal Value vs. New Shares

| Mechanism | When Used |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Issue new shares** | Most common; dilutes existing stakes |
| **Increase nominal value** | Rare; requires unanimous consent if mandatory payment |

---

## 2. Procedure (SA)

### 2.1 Board Proposal

**Art. 420-24 L1915**: Board prepares:
- Proposal for capital increase
- Report explaining reasons
- Proposed terms (price, number of shares)

### 2.2 EGM Approval

**Art. 420-25 L1915**: Requires:

| Requirement | First Meeting | Second Meeting |
|-------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Quorum** | 50% of capital | No quorum |
| **Majority** | 2/3 of votes cast | 2/3 of votes cast |
| **Form** | Notarial deed | Notarial deed |

### 2.3 Subscription and Payment

| Step | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Subscription** | Shareholders exercise pre-emption |
| **Payment (cash)** | At least 25% at subscription |
| **Valuation (in kind)** | Independent réviseur report |

### 2.4 Filing and Publication

- Notarial deed filed with RCS
- Published in RESA
- New articles of association

---

## 3. Preemptive Rights

### 3.1 Principle

**Art. 420-26 L1915** (SA): Existing shareholders have preferential right to subscribe to new shares proportionally to their holdings (*droit préférentiel de souscription*).

### 3.2 Rationale

**Steichen**: Protects shareholders from:
- Dilution of voting power
- Dilution of economic value
- Entry of unwanted third parties

### 3.3 Exercise

| Period | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| **Minimum** | 14 days |
| **Notice** | Published + registered letters |
| **Transferability** | Rights may be transferred |

### 3.4 Exclusion of Preemptive Rights

**Art. 420-26 L1915**: May be excluded:
- By EGM (2/3 majority)
- Board must justify exclusion
- Common for: private placements, strategic investors, employee schemes

---

## 4. Authorized Capital

### 4.1 Concept

**Art. 420-27 L1915**: EGM may authorize board to increase capital:
- Within limits set by EGM
- For period not exceeding 5 years
- Up to amount of existing capital

### 4.2 Advantages

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Speed** | No need for EGM each time |
| **Flexibility** | Board decides timing |
| **Opportunity** | Seize market conditions |

### 4.3 Safeguards

- Must be used within authorized limits
- Preemptive rights apply (unless excluded)
- Report to next AGM

---

## 5. Contributions in Kind

### 5.1 Valuation Requirement

**Art. 420-6 L1915**: Independent réviseur must:
- Value contributed assets
- Prepare report
- Confirm valuation is fair

### 5.2 Common Contributions

| Asset | Considerations |
|-------|----------------|
| **Real estate** | Notarial deed required |
| **Shares** | Valuation methodology |
| **Receivables** | Collectability assessment |
| **Business** | Goodwill valuation |

### 5.3 Liability

**Steichen**: Contributors liable for:
- Overvaluation
- Defective assets
- Hidden defects

---

## 6. SARL Capital Increase

### 6.1 Procedure

**Art. 710-9 L1915**:

| Requirement | Rule |
|-------------|------|
| **Decision** | Associates (3/4 majority) |
| **Form** | Notarial deed |
| **Payment** | Full payment required |
| **Pre-emption** | If provided in articles |

### 6.2 Differences from SA

| Aspect | SA | SARL |
|--------|-----|------|
| **Partial payment** | 25% minimum | Full payment required |
| **Authorized capital** | Permitted | Not applicable |
| **Pre-emption** | Statutory right | Only if in articles |

---

## 7. Special Cases

### 7.1 Debt-to-Equity Conversion

**Steichen**: Converting loans to capital:
- Treated as contribution in kind
- Réviseur report required
- Often used in restructurings

### 7.2 Employee Share Schemes

May exclude pre-emption for:
- Stock options
- Employee share plans
- Management incentives

### 7.3 Anti-Dilution Protection

Investors may require:
- Weighted average adjustment
- Full ratchet protection
- Pay-to-play provisions

## Banking & Finance Application

### Equity Financing

| Context | Considerations |
|---------|----------------|
| **IPO** | Capital increase with public offering |
| **Private placement** | Pre-emption often excluded |
| **Rights issue** | Pre-emption maintained |
| **Rescue financing** | May dilute existing shareholders |

### Due Diligence

| Check | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **EGM resolution** | Proper authorization |
| **Pre-emption** | Rights respected or validly excluded |
| **Valuation report** | For contributions in kind |
| **RCS filing** | Completion of increase |

### Covenants

Loan agreements may require:
- Notice of capital increase
- Maintenance of minimum equity
- No dilution of security
- Permitted equity issuances

### Warning Signs

- Capital increase at below-market price
- Pre-emption excluded without justification
- Overvalued in-kind contributions
- Frequent authorized capital use
