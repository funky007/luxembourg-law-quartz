# Responsabilité limitée

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 1, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 100-2 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Responsabilité limitée* (limited liability) is the principle whereby associates' liability for company debts is limited to their contribution, shielding personal assets from company creditors.

---

## Core Principle

**Art. 100-2 L1915**: In limited liability companies:
- Associates risk only their contribution
- Personal assets protected from company creditors
- Loss limited to investment made

---

## Limited vs. Unlimited Liability

| Aspect | Limited | Unlimited |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **Associate's risk** | Contribution only | All personal assets |
| **Creditor recourse** | Company assets first | Associates directly |
| **Company types** | SA, SARL, SAS | SNC, SC, SCS (general partners) |
| **Capital rules** | More stringent | Less formal |

---

## Companies with Limited Liability

| Company | Liability of Associates |
|---------|------------------------|
| **SA** | Limited to subscription |
| **SARL** | Limited to contribution |
| **SAS** | Limited to contribution |
| **SCA** | Limited for commanditaires |
| **SCS** | Limited for commanditaires |

---

## Companies with Unlimited Liability

| Company | Liability |
|---------|-----------|
| **SNC** | Unlimited, joint and several |
| **SC** | Unlimited (may be joint or several) |
| **SCS (commandités)** | Unlimited |
| **SCA (commandités)** | Unlimited |

---

## Limits of Limited Liability

**Steichen**: Limited liability does NOT protect against:

| Situation | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Personal guarantees** | Associate personally liable |
| **Tortious acts** | Personal fault liability |
| **Piercing veil** | Exceptional cases |
| **Director liability** | For management faults |
| **Unpaid capital** | Must pay if called |

---

## Policy Rationale

**Steichen**: Limited liability serves:
- Encourage investment and risk-taking
- Capital formation
- Economic development
- Clear creditor expectations

**Counterbalance**: Capital maintenance rules protect creditors.

---

## Creditor Protection Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Minimum capital** | Initial cushion |
| **Distribution rules** | Protect existing capital |
| **Publication** | Creditors know company type |
| **Personal guarantees** | Voluntary extension of liability |

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Limited liability means recourse to company only
- Personal guarantees extend liability
- Corporate benefit analysis for upstream guarantees
- Understand which party liable for what
