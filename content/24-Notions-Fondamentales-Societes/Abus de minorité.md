# Abus de minorité

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Jurisprudential concept; Art. 1382 C.civ. (fault)

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Abus de minorité* (abuse of minority) occurs when minority shareholders exercise their rights (especially blocking rights) contrary to the corporate interest, solely to harm the majority or for personal advantage.

---

## Elements

**Steichen**: Abuse of minority requires:

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Blocking power** | Minority can prevent decision |
| **Essential decision** | Resolution necessary for company |
| **Contrary to corporate interest** | Blocks beneficial action |
| **Bad faith** | Personal interest or harm motive |

---

## Common Situations

| Situation | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| **Capital increase** | Blocking needed recapitalization |
| **Restructuring** | Preventing essential merger |
| **Articles amendment** | Blocking necessary modernization |
| **Director appointment** | Creating governance paralysis |

---

## Compared to Abus de Majorité

| Aspect | Abus de majorité | Abus de minorité |
|--------|------------------|------------------|
| **Who abuses** | Majority shareholders | Minority shareholders |
| **How** | Imposing harmful decisions | Blocking beneficial decisions |
| **Remedy** | Nullity of decision | Court-imposed decision |
| **More common** | Historically | Increasingly recognized |

---

## Remedies

**Steichen**: Courts may:

| Remedy | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Damages** | For harm caused |
| **Judicial mandate** | Court appoints mandataire |
| **Substitute decision** | Court authorizes act |
| **Exclusion** | In extreme cases |

---

## Defenses

**Steichen**: Minority may justify blocking if:
- Legitimate economic concerns
- Protecting real minority interests
- Proposal genuinely harmful
- Alternative solutions available

---

## Procedure

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Attempt resolution** | Try to reach agreement first |
| **Demonstrate necessity** | Show decision essential |
| **Prove bad faith** | Minority acting improperly |
| **Court application** | Seek judicial intervention |

---

## Prevention

**Steichen**: Structuring to avoid deadlock:
- Supermajority thresholds carefully set
- Exit mechanisms for minorities
- Dispute resolution in shareholders' agreement
- Mediator provisions

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Minority blocking rights affect restructurings
- Assess risk in lending decisions
- Shareholders' agreements address deadlock
- Due diligence on shareholder dynamics
