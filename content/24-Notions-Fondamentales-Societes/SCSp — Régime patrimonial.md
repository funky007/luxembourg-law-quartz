# SCSp — Régime patrimonial

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Sources**: P. Schleimer (Allen & Overy), Larcier 2013; D. Boone & A. Trotska, JurisNews 2012
> **Key Legislation**: L1915, Arts. 320-1, 320-2

---

## Definition

The **société en commandite spéciale** (SCSp), introduced by the law of 12 July 2013, presents a fundamental paradox in Luxembourg law: it is expressly deprived of legal personality yet possesses most of the attributes traditionally associated with legal entities.

> "La société en commandite spéciale ne constitue pas une individualité juridique distincte de ses associés" (Art. 320-1(2) L1915)

**Source**: P. Schleimer, "Réflexions sur le régime patrimonial de la société en commandite spéciale", *Les commandites en droit Luxembourgeois* (Larcier 2013), p. 10-11

---

## 1. The Legal Personality Paradox

### Traditional Theory vs. SCSp Reality

Under classical Belgian-inspired theory, a partnership without legal personality should lack:
- Patrimony (patrimoine)
- Registered office (siège)
- Nationality
- Capacity to contract
- Capacity to sue or be sued

However, the SCSp contradicts each of these principles through explicit statutory provisions.

### What the SCSp Has Despite No Personality

| Attribute | Provided By |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Patrimony** | Art. 320-2 L1915 |
| **Domicile** | Art. 320-1(7) L1915 |
| **Capacity to contract** | Art. 320-3 L1915 (via gérance) |
| **Capacity to sue/be sued** | Art. 320-3(4) L1915 |
| **Debt securities issuance** | Art. 320-1(4) L1915 |

**Source**: P. Schleimer, op. cit., p. 10-11

---

## 2. Patrimony Treatment (Art. 320-2 L1915)

### Property Inscription

**Art. 320-2(1) L1915**:
> "Les inscriptions et autres formalités relatives aux biens mis en commun au sein de la société en commandite spéciale ou sur lesquels elle a quelque droit sont faites au nom de la société en commandite spéciale."

This allows:
- Property registrations in the SCSp's name
- Pledge registrations for the SCSp's benefit
- All formalities related to pooled assets

### Creditor Protection (Asset Segregation)

**Art. 320-2(2) L1915**:
> "Les biens mis en commun au sein de la société en commandite spéciale répondent exclusivement des droits des créanciers nés à l'occasion de la constitution, du fonctionnement ou de la liquidation de la société."

**Consequences**:

| Creditor Type | Access to SCSp Assets |
|---------------|----------------------|
| **Partnership creditors** | Full recourse to pooled assets |
| **Personal creditors of partners** | NO direct recourse; can only pursue partner's parts d'intérêts |

**Source**: D. Boone & A. Trotska, "Le renouveau de la commandite", JurisNews Droit des sociétés Vol. 5 No 10/2012, p. 209-210

## 3. Gesamthand Theory

### German Comparative Law Reference

The SCSp regime is inspired by the German concept of **Gesamthand** (main commune/joint hand), applicable to:
- Offene Handelsgesellschaft (OHG) — equivalent to SNC
- Kommanditgesellschaft (KG) — equivalent to SCS

### How Gesamthand Works

Under Gesamthand:
- Partners contribute to a collective patrimony (*gemeinschaftliches Vermögen*)
- This becomes "Gesellschaftsvermögen" (partnership patrimony)
- Partners cannot individually dispose of pooled assets
- Only partnership creditors (not personal creditors) may pursue partnership assets

**Source**: P. Schleimer citing Palandt, *Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch*, 67th ed. 2008, pp. 22 et seq.

### Partial Legal Capacity (Teil-Rechtsfähigkeit)

German doctrine and the Bundesgerichtshof (since 2001) recognize that Gesamthand communities possess **partial legal capacity**:
- Can acquire rights and incur obligations in their own name
- Can acquire real property rights
- Can sue and be sued

This model directly influenced Art. 320-2 L1915.

**Source**: P. Schleimer, op. cit., p. 18-19

## 4. Property Ownership Mechanics

### Theoretical Debate

**Conseil d'État position**: The SCSp becomes "propriétaire" of contributed assets.

**Commission des Finances et du Budget** disagreed:
> "La société en commandite spéciale ne peut pas être propriétaire de biens et n'a donc pas de patrimoine social en tant que tel."

### Practical Resolution

Regardless of theoretical classification, the SCSp has all practical attributes of ownership:
- Inscriptions are made in the SCSp's name
- The gérance represents the SCSp in court
- The SCSp has dedicated creditors
- Governance mirrors limited partnerships with legal personality

**Source**: P. Schleimer, op. cit., p. 23-28

### Comparison with FCP (Fonds Commun de Placement)

| Feature | FCP | SCSp |
|---------|-----|------|
| Legal personality | No | No |
| Asset inscription | Via management company | In SCSp's own name |
| Representation | Management company acts for co-owners | Gérance acts for the partnership |
| Investor status | Co-owners (copropriétaires indivis) | Partners with parts d'intérêts |

## 5. Asset Registration in Practice

### Default Regime (Suppletive)

The Art. 320-2(1) inscription regime is **suppletive** (default), not mandatory:
> "Il est proposé de permettre une inscription de propriété..."

The partnership agreement may provide alternative arrangements:
- Fiduciary arrangements
- Trust structures (where applicable)
- Special treatment for in-kind contributions

**Source**: P. Schleimer, op. cit., p. 28-29; Doc. parl. no 6471, p. 203

### Assets Typically Registered in SCSp Name

- Securities and financial instruments
- Real estate (subject to foreign recognition)
- Intellectual property rights
- Contractual rights

## 6. International Recognition Issues

### Cross-Border Challenges

The SCSp's hybrid nature may create difficulties in foreign jurisdictions:

**Real Estate Registration**: Some jurisdictions (notably Germany regarding FCP) cannot accommodate foreign vehicles without legal personality in their property registries.

**Comparison with German KG**: The German KG, despite lacking personality, is recognized as "grundbuchfähig" (capable of property registration). This precedent may facilitate SCSp recognition.

**Source**: P. Schleimer, op. cit., p. 29

### Recognition Factors

For international recognition, the SCSp benefits from:
- Clear statutory framework
- Publication requirements (by extract)
- Identified management organ
- Defined legal domicile

## Banking & Finance Application

### Security Over SCSp Assets

| Consideration | Analysis |
|---------------|----------|
| **Pledgee identification** | Pledge granted to the SCSp itself |
| **Registration** | In SCSp's name per Art. 320-2(1) |
| **Enforcement** | Gérance acts for the partnership |
| **Cross-border** | Verify foreign registry acceptance |

### Lending to SCSp

- Loan made to the SCSp (represented by gérance)
- Security interest over pooled assets possible
- Personal creditors of partners have no priority over partnership assets

### Due Diligence

- [ ] Verify SCSp is properly registered at RCS
- [ ] Confirm partnership agreement provisions on asset holding
- [ ] Check any deviations from default Art. 320-2(1) regime
- [ ] Assess international recognition for cross-border assets
