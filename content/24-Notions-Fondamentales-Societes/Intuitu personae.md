# Intuitu personae

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 1, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: General principle; applied throughout L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Intuitu personae* (Latin: "in consideration of the person") describes companies where the personal qualities of associates are essential, making the company relationship personal rather than purely capital-based.

---

## Characteristic Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Personal consideration** | Associates chosen for who they are |
| **Restricted transfer** | Shares not freely transferable |
| **Death/incapacity** | May affect company existence |
| **Mutual trust** | Relations based on confidence |
| **Small number** | Typically few associates |

---

## Application by Company Type

| Company | Degree of Intuitu Personae |
|---------|---------------------------|
| **SNC** | Maximum (partnerships) |
| **SC** | High |
| **SCS** | High (for general partners) |
| **SARL** | Moderate (transfer restrictions) |
| **SA** | Minimal (unless restricted) |
| **SAS** | Variable (per articles) |

---

## Legal Consequences

### Transfer Restrictions

**Steichen**: In intuitu personae companies:
- Transfers require consent
- Pre-emption rights common
- Approval clauses standard

### Events Affecting Company

| Event | Effect in High Intuitu Personae |
|-------|--------------------------------|
| **Death** | May dissolve company (SNC) |
| **Incapacity** | May affect governance |
| **Withdrawal** | Subject to strict rules |
| **Bankruptcy** | May trigger dissolution |

### Exclusion

**Steichen**: May exclude associate for:
- Breach of duties
- Loss of required qualities
- Just cause (*juste motif*)

---

## Opposite: Sociétés de Capitaux

| Aspect | Intuitu Personae | Capital-Based |
|--------|------------------|---------------|
| **Focus** | Person of associate | Capital contribution |
| **Transfer** | Restricted | Free |
| **Death** | Significant impact | Shares pass to heirs |
| **Example** | SNC | SA |

---

## Practical Implications

**Steichen**: Intuitu personae affects:
- Drafting of articles (transfer clauses)
- Admission of new associates
- Exit mechanisms
- Governance structures

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Share security harder in intuitu personae companies
- Enforcement may require approvals
- Understand associate relationships
- Exit mechanisms critical for investments
