# Capital social (Share Capital)

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Chapitre 1
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 26-37, 69-72, 181-183 L1915

---

## Definition

> *Capital social* (share capital) is the sum of contributions made by associates, represented by shares (*actions*) or membership interests (*parts sociales*), which appears as a liability on the company's balance sheet and serves as the minimum guarantee for creditors.

**Steichen, Titre 4, Ch. 1**: Capital serves dual functions:
1. **Economic**: Resources for business activity
2. **Legal**: Creditor protection through capital maintenance rules

## 1. Minimum Capital Requirements

### 1.1 By Company Type

| Company | Minimum Capital | Reference |
|---------|----------------|-----------|
| **SA** | €30,000 | Art. 27 L1915 |
| **SCA** | €30,000 | Art. 102 L1915 |
| **SE** | €120,000 | Regulation 2157/2001 |
| **SARL** | €12,000 | Art. 182 L1915 |
| **SARL-S** | €1 to €12,000 | Art. 200-1 L1915 |
| **SAS** | €1 (no legal minimum) | Art. 101-2 L1915 |
| **SNC** | None | — |
| **SCS** | None | — |
| **SC** | None | — |

### 1.2 Subscription and Payment

**SA** (Art. 29 L1915):
- Capital must be fully subscribed
- At least **25%** paid up at formation
- Remainder callable within 5 years

**SARL** (Art. 183 L1915):
- Capital fully subscribed and **fully paid** at formation
- Contributions in kind valued by founders

## 2. Capital Principles

### 2.1 Principle of Fixity (*Fixité du capital*)

**Steichen**: Capital stated in articles cannot change without formal modification:
- Increase requires shareholder approval
- Decrease requires shareholder approval + creditor protection

### 2.2 Principle of Reality (*Réalité du capital*)

Capital must represent actual contributions:
- Contributions in kind must be valued
- No fictitious capital (sanction: nullity)

### 2.3 Principle of Intangibility (*Intangibilité*)

**Steichen**: Capital may not be distributed to shareholders:
- Only profits may be distributed
- Capital repayment only via formal reduction

**Art. 72-1 L1915**: Distribution may not reduce net assets below subscribed capital plus non-distributable reserves.

---

## 3. Capital Increase (*Augmentation de capital*)

### 3.1 Methods

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **New contributions** | Cash or in kind from new/existing shareholders |
| **Incorporation of reserves** | Convert profits/reserves to capital |
| **Debt conversion** | Convert loans to equity |
| **Merger** | Capital increase by absorption |

### 3.2 Procedure (SA)

**Arts. 32-32-4 L1915**:
1. Board proposal
2. EGM approval (2/3 majority)
3. Subscription by shareholders (preemptive right)
4. Payment
5. Notarial act
6. RCS filing

### 3.3 Preemptive Rights (*Droit préférentiel de souscription*)

**Art. 32-3 L1915**: Existing shareholders have priority to subscribe proportionally.

**Waiver**: May be excluded by EGM (2/3 majority) with board justification.

---

## 4. Capital Reduction (*Réduction de capital*)

### 4.1 Grounds

| Type | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Voluntary** | Return excess capital to shareholders |
| **Loss absorption** | Write off accumulated losses |
| **Mandatory** | Capital below legal minimum |

### 4.2 Creditor Protection

**Art. 69 L1915** (SA): Creditors may oppose reduction:
- 30-day opposition period after publication
- Court may require security or payment
- Reduction ineffective until creditors satisfied

### 4.3 Mandatory Reduction

**Art. 100 L1915** (SA): If net assets fall below **half** of subscribed capital:
- Board must convene EGM within 2 months
- Shareholders decide on dissolution or remedial measures

---

## 5. Capital and Creditor Protection

### 5.1 Distribution Limits

**Art. 72-1 L1915**: No distribution if:
- Net assets < subscribed capital + non-distributable reserves
- Net assets would fall below this threshold after distribution

### 5.2 Own Shares (*Actions propres*)

**Art. 49-2 L1915**: SA may acquire own shares only if:
- Funded from distributable reserves
- Not exceeding 10% of subscribed capital
- Authorized by EGM

**Steichen**: Acquisition of own shares is essentially a disguised capital reduction.

### 5.3 Financial Assistance

**Art. 49-6 L1915**: Company may not advance funds or provide security for acquisition of its own shares.

**Exceptions**: Employee share schemes, certain group transactions.

---

## 6. Authorized Capital (*Capital autorisé*)

### 6.1 Concept

**Arts. 32-1 to 32-2 L1915**: Board may be authorized to increase capital:
- Within limits fixed by articles
- For period not exceeding 5 years
- Up to amount of existing subscribed capital

### 6.2 Advantages

- Flexibility for rapid fundraising
- Board discretion on timing
- May exclude preemptive rights (if authorized)

## Banking & Finance Application

### Due Diligence on Capital

| Check | Source | Purpose |
|-------|--------|---------|
| **Subscribed capital** | Articles, RCS | Legal minimum met |
| **Paid-up capital** | Financial statements | Actual resources |
| **Capital increases** | EGM minutes | Dilution history |
| **Net assets** | Balance sheet | Art. 100 threshold |

### Capital Adequacy Concerns

**Warning signs**:
- Net assets below 50% of capital (Art. 100 trigger)
- Repeated losses eroding capital
- Distributions despite inadequate profits
- Unpaid capital calls

### Security Considerations

**Pledge over shares**:
- Value depends on underlying capital/net assets
- Dilution risk if capital increased
- Preemptive rights may require additional investment

**Corporate guarantees**:
- Distribution of capital to pay guarantee may be void
- Financial assistance rules may apply

### Acquisition Finance

**LBO structures**:
- Target company cannot finance acquisition of its shares (art. 49-6)
- Debt push-down requires merger, not upstream loans
- Capital reduction to fund returns requires creditor protection
