# Quorum et majorité

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 450-1 to 450-8 L1915 (SA); Arts. 710-21 to 710-22 L1915 (SARL)

---

## Definitions

**Steichen**:
- *Quorum*: Minimum attendance/representation required for valid meeting
- *Majorité*: Minimum votes required to pass resolution

---

## SA General Meetings

### Ordinary AGM (*Assemblée générale ordinaire*)

**Art. 450-3 L1915**:

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Quorum** | None required |
| **Majority** | Simple majority of votes cast |
| **Matters** | Annual accounts, appointments, dividends |

### Extraordinary GM (*Assemblée générale extraordinaire*)

**Art. 450-4 L1915**:

| Aspect | First Call | Second Call |
|--------|------------|-------------|
| **Quorum** | 50% of capital | None |
| **Majority** | 2/3 of votes cast | 2/3 of votes cast |
| **Matters** | Articles amendments |

---

## SARL General Meetings

**Arts. 710-21 to 710-22 L1915**:

| Type | Quorum | Majority |
|------|--------|----------|
| **Ordinary** | None | >50% of capital |
| **Extraordinary** | None | 2/3 of capital |
| **Articles amendment** | None | 3/4 of capital |

---

## Special Majorities

| Decision | Typical Requirement |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Articles amendment** | 2/3 or 3/4 |
| **Nationality change** | Unanimity |
| **Increase obligations** | Unanimity |
| **Transformation** | As required for target form |

---

## Calculation of Votes

**Steichen**: Votes calculated based on:

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Share/part** | Each carries votes per articles |
| **Default** | One vote per share/part |
| **Multiple voting** | May be permitted |
| **Non-voting shares** | Excluded from count |
| **Treasury shares** | Voting rights suspended |

---

## Second Convocation

**Art. 450-4 L1915** (SA EGM):
- If quorum not met at first meeting
- Second meeting may decide without quorum
- But same majority required

---

## Written Resolutions

| Company | Permitted? |
|---------|-----------|
| **SA** | If unanimous (Art. 450-8) |
| **SARL** | Yes, with requisite majority |
| **SAS** | As per articles |

## Banking & Finance Application
