# Immatriculation au RCS

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 2, Ch. 3
> **Key Legislation**: Loi du 19 décembre 2002 (LRCS); Art. 2 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Immatriculation au Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés* (RCS registration) is the formal registration of a company that confers legal personality and makes company information publicly available.

---

## Effect of Registration

**Art. 2 L1915**:

| Effect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Legal personality** | Acquired upon deposit at RCS |
| **Capacity** | Company can act legally |
| **Opposability** | Third parties bound by published information |
| **Publicity** | Information accessible to public |

---

## Required Filings

**Art. 3 LRCS**: Initial registration requires:

| Document | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Constitutive deed** | Notarial deed/articles |
| **Directors/gérants** | List of managers |
| **Beneficial owners** | RBE declaration |
| **Registered office** | Address |

---

## Ongoing Obligations

| Filing | Timing |
|--------|--------|
| **Annual accounts** | Within 7 months of year end |
| **Changes to articles** | Upon occurrence |
| **Director changes** | Upon occurrence |
| **Registered office changes** | Upon occurrence |

---

## Publication (RESA)

**Art. 9 LRCS**: Published at *Recueil Électronique des Sociétés et Associations*:
- Extracts of filings
- Appointments
- Annual accounts (if required)
- Legal notices

---

## RCS Number

**Practice**: Each company receives:
- Unique registration number
- Format: B followed by digits (e.g., B123456)
- Used in all official documents
- Required on letterhead, contracts

---

## Sanctions

| Issue | Consequence |
|-------|-------------|
| **Late filing** | Administrative fines |
| **Non-filing** | Dissolution risk (prolonged) |
| **False information** | Criminal liability |

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Always obtain recent RCS extract (extrait)
- Verify all filings current
- Check for any dissolution/liquidation mentions
- Obtain *certificat de non-inscription de décision judiciaire*
