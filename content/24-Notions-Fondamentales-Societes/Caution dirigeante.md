# Caution dirigeante

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Ch. 3 + Sûretés
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 2011-2043 C.civ. (cautionnement)

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: A *caution dirigeante* (director's guarantee) is a personal guarantee (*cautionnement*) given by a company director or manager to secure company obligations, extending personal liability beyond the corporate veil.

---

## Context

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Voluntary** | Director chooses to guarantee |
| **Additional security** | Beyond company assets |
| **Common practice** | Banks often require for SMEs |
| **Extends liability** | Personal assets at risk |

---

## Why Required

**Banking practice**: Directors' guarantees requested because:

| Reason | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Skin in game** | Aligns director's interests |
| **Additional recourse** | Beyond company assets |
| **Credit enhancement** | Supports weaker borrowers |
| **Risk sharing** | Director bears personal risk |

---

## Legal Nature

**Arts. 2011-2043 C.civ.**: As a cautionnement:

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Accessory** | Follows principal obligation |
| **Written** | Required for proof |
| **Amount** | May be limited |
| **Extent** | As agreed (simple or solidaire) |

---

## Formation Requirements

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Capacity** | Director must have capacity |
| **Consent** | Free and informed |
| **Written form** | Generally required |
| **No corporate authorization** | Personal act, not company act |

---

## Proportionality

**Case law**: Courts may consider:

| Factor | Relevance |
|--------|-----------|
| **Director's wealth** | Compared to guarantee amount |
| **Information received** | About risks |
| **Pressure/influence** | Creditor conduct |
| **Sophistication** | Business experience |

---

## Relationship to Director Duties

| Aspect | Consideration |
|--------|---------------|
| **Separate from role** | Personal obligation |
| **No mandate** | Not acting for company |
| **Own benefit** | May have shareholding too |
| **Survival** | Continues after leaving office |

---

## Limits and Defenses

| Defense | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Accessory nature** | If principal obligation void |
| **Disproportionality** | If manifestly excessive |
| **Creditor fault** | Loss of subrogation rights |
| **Prescription** | Time limits apply |

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Standard in SME lending
- Document proportionality analysis
- Update for director changes
- Enforce separately from company
