# Commissaire aux comptes

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Ch. 4
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 461-1 to 461-8 L1915; Art. 710-23 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: The *commissaire aux comptes* (statutory auditor) is an independent professional responsible for auditing the company's accounts and reporting to shareholders.

---

## Types of Control

| Type | Description | Required For |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| **Réviseur d'entreprises agréé** | Licensed auditor | Large companies |
| **Commissaire aux comptes** | Statutory auditor | Small SA (if no réviseur) |
| **External audit** | Full statutory audit | Above thresholds |

---

## Appointment

**Art. 461-1 L1915** (SA):

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Appointing body** | AGM |
| **Term** | 3 years (renewable) |
| **Number** | One or more |
| **Independence** | Must be independent |

---

## Duties

**Steichen**: The commissaire must:

| Duty | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Audit accounts** | Verify accuracy of financial statements |
| **Report to AGM** | Written report on accounts |
| **Verify compliance** | Articles, law compliance |
| **Alert function** | Report irregularities |

---

## Thresholds for Audit

**Art. 35 LCom**: Companies exceeding 2 of 3 criteria require statutory audit:

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Balance sheet total** | €4.4 million |
| **Net turnover** | €8.8 million |
| **Employees** | 50 average |

---

## Small Company Exemption

**Art. 69 LCom**: Small companies may:
- Have *commissaire aux comptes* instead of réviseur
- Or no statutory audit (below thresholds)
- Subject to articles requirements

---

## SARL Specifics

**Art. 710-23 L1915**:
- Required if above thresholds
- Otherwise at shareholders' option
- One or more commissaires

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Verify audit status from accounts
- Rely on audited accounts for due diligence
- Unaudited accounts require more verification
- Check auditor's report for qualifications
