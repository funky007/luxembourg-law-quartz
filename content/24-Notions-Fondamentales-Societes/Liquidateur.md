# Liquidateur

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Ch. 4
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 1100-1 to 1100-15 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: A *liquidateur* (liquidator) is the person appointed to wind up a company's affairs after dissolution, realizing assets, paying debts, and distributing any surplus to shareholders.

---

## Appointment

**Art. 1100-2 L1915**:

| Method | Situation |
|--------|-----------|
| **Shareholders** | Appointed by dissolving meeting |
| **Articles** | May designate liquidator |
| **Court** | If no appointment or dispute |
| **Former directors** | Act as liquidators until appointed |

---

## Eligibility

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Natural or legal** | Either permitted |
| **Multiple liquidators** | Permitted |
| **Shareholders** | May be appointed |
| **Former directors** | Commonly appointed |
| **Professional** | For complex liquidations |

---

## Powers

**Art. 1100-3 L1915**:

| Power | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Represent company** | In liquidation matters |
| **Collect receivables** | Pursue debtors |
| **Sell assets** | Realize property |
| **Pay debts** | Settle liabilities |
| **Sue and be sued** | On company's behalf |
| **Compromise** | Settle disputes |

---

## Limitations

**Steichen**: Liquidator may NOT:

| Restriction | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **New business** | No new commercial activities |
| **Outside winding up** | Only liquidation acts |
| **Without authority** | If specific authorization needed |

---

## Duties

| Duty | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Good faith** | Act in interest of stakeholders |
| **Diligence** | Proceed promptly |
| **Equal treatment** | Among creditors (by rank) |
| **Accounts** | Keep proper records |
| **Report** | To shareholders periodically |

---

## Remuneration

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **By shareholders** | Fixed at appointment |
| **Court** | If court-appointed |
| **Basis** | Fixed fee or percentage |
| **Priority** | Expense of liquidation |

---

## Liability

**Art. 1100-8 L1915**: Liquidator liable for:

| Fault | To Whom |
|-------|---------|
| **Mismanagement** | Company/creditors |
| **Breach of duty** | Stakeholders |
| **Preference** | Disadvantaged creditors |

---

## Term and Removal

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Duration** | Until liquidation complete |
| **Removal** | By shareholders or court |
| **Replacement** | If dies/resigns/removed |

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Know liquidator's identity and powers
- File claims in liquidation
- Verify distribution waterfall
- Monitor liquidation progress
