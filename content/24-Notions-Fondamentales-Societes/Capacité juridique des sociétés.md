# Capacité juridique des sociétés

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Ch. 1
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 100-3 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Capacité juridique des sociétés* (legal capacity of companies) is the ability of a company, as a legal person, to hold rights, incur obligations, and perform legal acts, bounded by its corporate purpose.

---

## Acquisition

**Art. 2 L1915**: Capacity acquired:
- Upon RCS registration
- With legal personality
- Full capacity from that moment

---

## Scope of Capacity

**Art. 100-3 L1915**: Company may:

| Power | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Own property** | All forms of assets |
| **Contract** | Enter agreements |
| **Sue and be sued** | Legal proceedings |
| **Incur obligations** | Debts and commitments |
| **Receive donations** | Accept gifts (with limits) |

---

## Limits on Capacity

### Corporate Purpose (*Objet social*)

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Within purpose** | Full capacity |
| **Outside purpose** | Ultra vires doctrine |
| **Third-party protection** | Ultra vires not opposable |

### Legal Restrictions

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Prohibited activities** | Without required license |
| **Regulated activities** | Need authorization |
| **Nationality restrictions** | Some sectors |

---

## Ultra Vires Acts

**Steichen**: Acts beyond corporate purpose:

| To Company | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| **Internal** | Directors may be liable |
| **External** | Company bound if third party in good faith |
| **Third-party protection** | Cannot invoke ultra vires |

**Art. 100-8 L1915**: Ultra vires not opposable to third parties unless they knew or should have known.

---

## Compared to Natural Persons

| Aspect | Company | Natural Person |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| **Source** | Law and registration | Birth |
| **Scope** | Limited by purpose | General |
| **Exercise** | Through organs | Directly |
| **Termination** | Dissolution/liquidation | Death |

---

## Special Capacities

| Capacity | Rule |
|----------|------|
| **Criminal** | Yes (since 2010) |
| **Tortious** | Yes (through organs) |
| **Constitutional rights** | Some applicable |

## Banking & Finance Application

- Verify corporate purpose permits transaction
- Third-party protection for good faith
- Check regulatory authorizations
- Review articles for capacity limits
