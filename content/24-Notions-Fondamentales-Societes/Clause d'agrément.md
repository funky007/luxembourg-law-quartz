# Clause d'agrément

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 430-4 to 430-7 L1915 (SA); Art. 710-12 L1915 (SARL)

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: The *clause d'agrément* (approval clause) is a provision in the articles requiring that share transfers to third parties be approved by a company organ, restricting free transferability.

---

## Purpose

| Purpose | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Control entry** | Vet incoming shareholders |
| **Maintain character** | Preserve company composition |
| **Protect interests** | Keep undesired parties out |
| **Intuitu personae** | Reinforce personal nature |

---

## Application by Company Type

| Company | Default Rule |
|---------|--------------|
| **SA** | Freely transferable (clause optional) |
| **SARL** | Statutory approval required for third parties |
| **SNC** | Unanimity required (very restrictive) |
| **SAS** | As per articles |

---

## SA Approval Clause

**Art. 430-4 L1915**: If articles provide:

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Scope** | May cover all or specific transfers |
| **Approving body** | Usually board |
| **Response period** | Articles must specify |
| **Silence** | Deemed approval (after period) |
| **Refusal** | Must provide alternative buyer |

---

## SARL Statutory Rules

**Art. 710-12 L1915**:

| Transfer To | Requirement |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Other associates** | Free (unless articles restrict) |
| **Spouse/heirs** | Free (unless articles restrict) |
| **Third parties** | Approval by majority of capital |

---

## Procedure for Approval

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Notification** | Seller notifies company of intended transfer |
| **Decision** | Approving body decides |
| **Approval** | Transfer proceeds |
| **Refusal** | Company must find alternative buyer (SA) |

---

## Consequences of Refusal

**SA (Art. 430-6 L1915)**:
- Company must propose alternative buyer
- At fair price
- Within specified period
- Otherwise approval deemed given

**SARL (Art. 710-12 L1915)**:
- Company may purchase shares
- Or propose another buyer
- Judicial valuation if no agreement

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Security over shares: check approval requirements
- Enforcement may require approval
- Built-in mechanisms protect enforcement
- Review articles before taking share pledge
