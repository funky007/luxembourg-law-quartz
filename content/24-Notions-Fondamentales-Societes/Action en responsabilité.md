# Action en responsabilité

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Ch. 3
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 441-9 L1915 (SA); Art. 710-16 L1915 (SARL)

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Action en responsabilité* (liability action) is a legal action to hold directors or managers liable for damages caused by breach of their duties, exercisable by the company, shareholders, or third parties.

---

## Types of Actions

| Type | Who Brings | Against | For |
|------|-----------|---------|-----|
| **Action sociale (ut universi)** | Company | Directors | Company damage |
| **Action sociale (ut singuli)** | Minority shareholders | Directors | Company damage |
| **Action individuelle** | Individual shareholder | Directors | Personal damage |
| **Third-party action** | Creditors/others | Directors | Direct damage |

---

## Action Sociale Ut Universi

**Art. 441-9 L1915**:

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Brought by** | Company (via AGM decision) |
| **For** | Damage to company |
| **Defendant** | Directors |
| **Recovery** | Goes to company |

---

## Action Sociale Ut Singuli

**Art. 441-9 L1915**:

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Brought by** | Minority shareholders |
| **Threshold** | 10% of capital (SA) |
| **For** | Damage to company |
| **Recovery** | Goes to company |
| **Purpose** | When majority won't sue |

---

## Action Individuelle

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Brought by** | Individual shareholder |
| **For** | Personal damage (not derived from company damage) |
| **Direct injury** | Must be distinct from company's |
| **Recovery** | Goes to shareholder personally |

---

## Grounds for Liability

**Art. 441-9 L1915**: Directors liable for:

| Ground | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Breach of duties** | Care, loyalty, diligence |
| **Law violations** | Statutory breaches |
| **Articles violations** | Company constitution |
| **Faute de gestion** | Management errors |

---

## Joint and Several Liability

**Steichen**: Where applicable:

| Situation | Liability |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Collective decision** | Joint and several |
| **Individual fault** | Personal only |
| **Dissenting director** | May escape (if recorded) |

---

## Defenses

| Defense | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Business judgment** | Honest informed decision |
| **No fault** | Duty not breached |
| **No causation** | Damage not caused by fault |
| **Prescription** | Time-barred |
| **Ratification** | AGM discharge (quitus) |

---

## Limitation Period

| Type | Period |
|------|--------|
| **General** | 5 years (prescription) |
| **Starting point** | Discovery of damage/fault |
| **Interruption** | By legal action |

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Directors' liability as enforcement route
- Personal liability beyond company assets
- D&O insurance considerations
- Document management decisions
