# Directoire et conseil de surveillance

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 2-2, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 442-1 to 442-11 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: The *directoire* (management board) and *conseil de surveillance* (supervisory board) form the dualistic governance model for the SA, separating management from supervision.

---

## Directoire (Management Board)

### Composition

**Art. 442-1 L1915**:

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Members** | 1 to 5 (may be extended by articles) |
| **Persons** | Natural persons only |
| **Term** | 2-6 years |
| **Chair** | *Président du directoire* |

### Functions

| Function | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Management** | Full powers within corporate purpose |
| **Representation** | Each member may bind company |
| **Reporting** | Quarterly reports to supervisory board |

---

## Conseil de surveillance (Supervisory Board)

### Composition

**Art. 442-3 L1915**:

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Members** | Minimum 3 |
| **Persons** | Natural or legal |
| **Term** | 6 years maximum |
| **Independence** | Cannot be directoire members |

### Functions

| Function | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Supervision** | Permanent control of directoire |
| **Appointment** | Names directoire members |
| **Authorization** | Major transactions requiring consent |
| **Reporting** | Comments on management to AGM |

---

## Separation of Functions

**Steichen**: Key principle is strict separation:
- Directoire manages, supervisory board supervises
- No overlap in membership
- Cannot be employee of company (directoire)
- Supervisory board not involved in management

---

## Compared to Monistic Model

| Aspect | Monistic | Dualistic |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Organs** | One board | Two boards |
| **Management** | Board of directors | Directoire |
| **Supervision** | Same board | Conseil de surveillance |
| **Common use** | Most companies | Larger/German-influenced |

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Less common but verify which model applies
- Check supervisory board approvals for large transactions
- Review directoire representation powers
- Quarterly reports provide insight into governance
