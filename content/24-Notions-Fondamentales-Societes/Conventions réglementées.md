# Conventions réglementées (Related-Party Transactions)

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Chapitre 3
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 441-12 to 441-14, 442-11 L1915 (SA); Art. 710-8 L1915 (SARL-U)

---

## Definition

> *Conventions réglementées* (regulated agreements / related-party transactions) are contracts or arrangements between a company and its directors, officers, or significant shareholders that are subject to special approval procedures to manage conflicts of interest.

**Steichen, Titre 3, Ch. 3**: These provisions protect the company and shareholders from self-dealing by requiring disclosure and approval of interested transactions.

---

## 1. Rationale

### 1.1 Conflict of Interest

**Steichen**: Directors/managers may have interests conflicting with company:
- Self-dealing at favorable terms
- Using company assets for personal benefit
- Competing with company
- Benefiting related parties

### 1.2 Protective Mechanism

Regulated agreement rules aim to:
- Ensure transparency
- Require informed approval
- Protect minority shareholders
- Maintain corporate integrity

---

## 2. SA Regime

### 2.1 Covered Transactions

**Art. 441-12 L1915**: Agreements between company and:

| Interested Party | Coverage |
|-----------------|----------|
| **Director/member of directoire** | All agreements |
| **Significant shareholder** (>20%) | Certain agreements |
| **Related companies** | Through common directors |

### 2.2 Procedure

**Art. 441-12 L1915**: Three-step process:

| Step | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **1. Disclosure** | Director notifies board of interest |
| **2. Board approval** | Board authorizes (director abstains) |
| **3. Shareholder ratification** | AGM approves; interested party abstains |

### 2.3 Auditor Report

**Art. 441-12 L1915**: For SA with commissaire:
- Auditor prepares special report
- Details transactions and their terms
- Presented to AGM

### 2.4 Consequences of Non-Compliance

**Steichen**: If procedure not followed:
- Transaction may be annulled
- Director liable for damages
- Does not affect third-party rights

---

## 3. Categories of Agreements

### 3.1 Regulated Agreements (*Conventions réglementées*)

Subject to approval procedure:
- Contracts with directors
- Loans to/from directors
- Guarantees for directors
- Service agreements with director-controlled entities
- Asset purchases from/to directors

### 3.2 Prohibited Agreements (*Conventions interdites*)

**Art. 441-13 L1915**: Absolutely prohibited:

| Prohibition | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Loans to directors** | Company may not lend to directors |
| **Guarantees for directors** | Company may not guarantee director debts |
| **Overdrafts** | On current account |

**Exception**: Banks may deal with director-customers in ordinary course.

### 3.3 Exempt Agreements (*Conventions libres*)

Not subject to procedure:
- Transactions at arm's length
- Ordinary course of business
- Standard terms

**Test**: Would the company do this with an unrelated party?

---

## 4. SARL Regime

### 4.1 General Position

**Steichen**: Standard SARL has NO regulated agreement regime:
- No mandatory approval procedure
- General duties of loyalty apply
- Abuse of corporate assets rules apply

### 4.2 SARL-U Exception

**Art. 710-8 L1915**: Single-member SARL:
- Contracts between company and sole member must be recorded
- Listed in special register
- Mentioned in annual report

---

## 5. Other Company Types

### 5.1 SNC

No statutory regulated agreement regime:
- Unanimous consent principle provides protection
- All partners typically aware of transactions

### 5.2 SAS

**Steichen**: Flexible regime:
- Articles determine procedure
- May adopt SA-style rules
- Or simpler approach

### 5.3 SCA

Similar to SA regime with adaptations for gérant.

---

## 6. Practical Application

### 6.1 What Triggers Procedure

| Transaction | Regulated? |
|-------------|-----------|
| Director's service contract | Yes |
| Sale of property to director | Yes |
| Loan from director | Yes |
| Director's remuneration | Often no (ordinary resolution) |
| Insurance premium | Context-dependent |

### 6.2 Director's Obligations

| Duty | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Disclose** | Must reveal interest before transaction |
| **Abstain** | Cannot vote on approval |
| **Document** | Ensure proper recording |

### 6.3 Board Minutes

Must record:
- Nature of interest disclosed
- Director abstention
- Board's analysis
- Terms of transaction

---

## 7. Sanctions

### 7.1 Nullity

**Steichen**: Transaction may be annulled if:
- Procedure not followed
- And transaction prejudicial to company
- Action brought within prescription period

### 7.2 Director Liability

Director may be liable for:
- Damages from transaction
- Even if transaction not annulled
- Based on breach of duty

### 7.3 Criminal Sanctions

Serious cases may constitute:
- Abuse of corporate assets (*abus de biens sociaux*)
- Breach of trust

## Banking & Finance Application

### Due Diligence

| Check | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Related party list** | Identify covered persons |
| **Transaction review** | Spot regulated agreements |
| **Board minutes** | Verify approvals |
| **AGM resolutions** | Confirm ratification |

### Common Related-Party Situations

| Situation | Analysis |
|-----------|----------|
| **Management fees** | May be regulated if paid to director-controlled entity |
| **Upstream guarantees** | Consider if director/shareholder benefits |
| **Group loans** | May involve common directors |
| **Lease from shareholder** | Typically regulated |

### Lending Considerations

When financing involves related parties:
- Verify approval procedures followed
- Obtain corporate benefit opinion
- Document in conditions precedent
- Ongoing covenant compliance

### Red Flags

- No board minutes for director transactions
- Missing auditor report at AGM
- Director voting on own transaction
- Transaction at non-market terms
