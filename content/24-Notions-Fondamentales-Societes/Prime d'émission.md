# Prime d'émission

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Ch. 1
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 430-8 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Prime d'émission* (share premium / issuance premium) is the amount paid by subscribers for new shares above the nominal (par) value, reflecting the company's increased value since formation.

---

## Components of Share Price

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Nominal value** | Par value of share |
| **Premium** | Amount above par |
| **Total subscription** | Nominal + Premium |

---

## Purpose

| Purpose | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Fair pricing** | New shares priced at actual value |
| **Anti-dilution** | Existing shareholders not diluted |
| **Value capture** | Company receives full value |
| **Capital vs. premium** | Accounting distinction |

---

## Example

| Element | Amount |
|---------|--------|
| **Par value** | €1 per share |
| **Current value** | €5 per share |
| **Premium** | €4 per share |
| **Subscription price** | €5 per share |

If 1,000 shares issued:
- Capital increase: €1,000
- Premium: €4,000
- Total received: €5,000

---

## Accounting Treatment

**Steichen**: Share premium recorded as:

| Account | Nature |
|---------|--------|
| **Prime d'émission** | Equity account |
| **Not share capital** | Separate from capital |
| **Distributable** | Subject to conditions |
| **Flexible use** | More than capital |

---

## Use of Premium

| Use | Permitted? |
|-----|-----------|
| **Loss absorption** | Yes |
| **Capital incorporation** | Yes |
| **Distribution** | Yes (subject to rules) |
| **Reserve transfer** | Yes |

---

## Compared to Capital

| Aspect | Share Capital | Share Premium |
|--------|--------------|---------------|
| **Legal minimum** | Yes | No |
| **Reduction** | Strict procedure | More flexible |
| **Creditor protection** | Maximum | Intermediate |
| **Distribution** | Very restricted | Less restricted |

---

## Determination of Premium

**Steichen**: Premium determined by:

| Factor | Consideration |
|--------|---------------|
| **Net asset value** | Company's equity |
| **Earnings** | Profitability |
| **Market value** | If listed |
| **Negotiation** | With investors |

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Premium is part of equity
- Affects capital adequacy analysis
- More distributable than capital
- Check use restrictions in articles
