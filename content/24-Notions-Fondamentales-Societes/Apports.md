# Apports (Contributions)

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 2, Chapitre 2, Section 1
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 1832-1833 Code civil; Arts. 26-1, 183 L1915

---

## Definition

> An *apport* (contribution) is the asset, right, or service that each associate undertakes to provide to the company in exchange for membership rights (shares or *parts sociales*).

**Steichen, Titre 2, Ch. 2, §1**: The term has dual meaning:
1. **The operation**: The act of contributing to the company
2. **The object**: The asset or service contributed

**Art. 1832 C.civ.**: Associates "conviennent de mettre en commun quelque chose" (agree to put something in common).

---

## 1. Types of Contributions

### 1.1 Apport en numéraire (Cash Contribution)

**Definition**: Contribution of money (legal tender).

**Steichen**: Most common and simplest form.

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Form** | Cash, bank transfer |
| **Timing** | May be immediate or deferred (*libération*) |
| **SA minimum** | At least 25% at formation (art. 29 L1915) |
| **SARL** | Full payment at formation |

### 1.2 Apport en nature (Contribution in Kind)

**Definition**: Contribution of assets other than cash — movable or immovable property, rights, receivables.

**Steichen**: Requires valuation to determine share allocation.

**Types**:

| Type | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Apport en propriété** | Full ownership transfers to company |
| **Apport en jouissance** | Right of use only; ownership retained |
| **Apport en usufruit** | Usufruct right transfers |

**Art. 26-1 L1915** (SA): Contributions in kind must be:
- Valued by an independent *réviseur d'entreprises agréé*
- Reported in a special report
- Filed with RCS

### 1.3 Apport en industrie (Contribution of Services/Know-how)

**Definition**: Contribution of work, services, technical knowledge, or expertise.

**Steichen**: Controversial and restricted in capital companies.

| Company Type | Apport en industrie |
|-------------|-------------------|
| **Société civile** | Permitted |
| **SNC** | Permitted |
| **SARL** | Prohibited for capital (art. 183 L1915) |
| **SA** | Prohibited for capital (art. 26-2 L1915) |

**Consequence in SARL/SA**: May receive *parts bénéficiaires* or profit-sharing rights, but not capital shares.

## 2. Characteristics of Contributions

### 2.1 Essential Requirements

**Steichen, §1**: All contributions must be:

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Real** (*réel*) | Genuinely exist and have value |
| **Serious** (*sérieux*) | Not fictitious or illusory |
| **Certain** (*certain*) | Determinable at formation |
| **Licit** (*licite*) | Legal assets/services |

### 2.2 Obligatory Character

**Art. 1832 C.civ.**: Every associate must contribute something.

**Steichen**: An associate without any contribution is not truly an associate — may be:
- A *prête-nom* (nominee)
- Recipient of disguised gift

---

## 3. Valuation of Contributions

### 3.1 Overvaluation Problem (*Surévaluation*)

**Risk**: Overvalued contributions dilute other shareholders and mislead creditors.

**Steichen**: Luxembourg law imposes controls:

| Company | Control Mechanism |
|---------|------------------|
| **SA** | Mandatory *réviseur* report (art. 26-1 L1915) |
| **SARL** | Founders' liability (art. 183 L1915) |
| **SNC** | Associates' joint liability |

### 3.2 Exemptions from Valuation Report

**Art. 26-1 L1915**: Report not required if:
- Securities valued at market price
- Prior independent valuation exists (within 3 months)
- Value derives from certified accounts

---

## 4. Transfer of Contributions

### 4.1 Apport en Propriété

**Steichen**: Ownership transfers according to nature of asset:
- **Movables**: Tradition (delivery) or registration
- **Immovables**: Notarial act and land registry
- **Receivables**: Notification to debtor (art. 1690 C.civ.)
- **Shares**: Per company's transfer rules

### 4.2 Apport en Jouissance

**Steichen**: Contributor retains ownership:
- Company has only use right
- Asset returns at dissolution
- Contributor bears risk of loss

---

## 5. Defective Contributions

### 5.1 Grounds for Challenge

| Defect | Consequence |
|--------|-------------|
| **Non-existence** | Nullity of subscription |
| **Eviction** | Warranty claims |
| **Hidden defects** | Warranty for vices cachés |
| **Overvaluation** | Founders' liability |

### 5.2 Sanctions

**Art. 26-2 L1915** (SA): Founders liable for:
- Defective contributions
- Failure to pay up
- Damage from overvaluation

## Banking & Finance Application

### Due Diligence on Contributions

| Check | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Paid-up capital** | Verify actual payment |
| **Valuation reports** | For contributions in kind |
| **Asset ownership** | Contributor had title to contribute |
| **Encumbrances** | Assets free of prior security |

### Common Issues

1. **Unpaid capital**: Directors may call up remaining capital
2. **Overvalued assets**: May indicate balance sheet manipulation
3. **Contributions in kind**: Verify independent valuation
4. **Related party contributions**: Check arm's length pricing

### Security Considerations

- **Pledge over shares**: Value depends on underlying contributions
- **Asset-backed lending**: Verify company actually owns contributed assets
- **Capital adequacy**: Unpaid contributions affect true net worth
