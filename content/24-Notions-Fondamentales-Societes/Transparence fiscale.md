# Transparence fiscale

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Various; Luxembourg tax law
> **Key Legislation**: LITL (Loi sur l'Impôt sur le Revenu); Partnership provisions

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Transparence fiscale* (fiscal transparency / tax transparency) refers to entities that are not subject to tax at entity level, with income instead taxed directly at the level of the members/partners—the entity is "looked through" for tax purposes.

---

## Transparent vs. Opaque

| Type | Tax Treatment |
|------|---------------|
| **Transparent** | Income taxed at member level |
| **Opaque** | Income taxed at entity level |

---

## Transparent Entities in Luxembourg

| Entity | Transparency |
|--------|-------------|
| **SNC** | Transparent |
| **SCS** | Transparent |
| **SC** | Transparent |
| **SA** | Opaque |
| **SARL** | Opaque |
| **SAS** | Opaque |

---

## Consequences of Transparency

**Steichen**: For transparent entities:

| Aspect | Consequence |
|--------|-------------|
| **Entity tax** | None at partnership level |
| **Partner tax** | Each partner taxed on share |
| **Character** | Income retains character |
| **Losses** | Pass through to partners |
| **Timing** | Taxed when earned |

---

## Allocation of Income

| Rule | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Pro rata** | According to partnership interests |
| **Partnership agreement** | May specify allocation |
| **Arm's length** | Special allocations must be justified |

---

## Practical Implications

### For Partners

| Aspect | Consideration |
|--------|---------------|
| **Tax return** | Report share of partnership income |
| **Cash flow** | Tax due even if not distributed |
| **Foreign partners** | Luxembourg source rules apply |

### For Planning

| Aspect | Consideration |
|--------|---------------|
| **Loss utilization** | Losses may offset other income |
| **Treaty access** | May depend on partner level |
| **Structuring** | Choose transparent vs. opaque |

---

## SCS/SCSp for Funds

**Practice**: Special limited partnerships are transparent:
- Used extensively for investment funds
- Investors taxed directly
- No entity-level Luxembourg tax (generally)
- Carried interest treatment

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Understand tax status of borrower
- Transparent vehicles: analyze partners
- Cash tax vs. reported profit may differ
- Distributions may not match taxable income
