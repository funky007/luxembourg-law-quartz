# Boni de liquidation

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Ch. 4
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 1100-11 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Boni de liquidation* (liquidation surplus / liquidation bonus) is the amount remaining after a company in liquidation has paid all debts and returned capital contributions, distributed to shareholders as their final participation.

---

## Calculation

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Assets realized** | All assets converted to cash |
| **Liabilities paid** | All debts satisfied |
| **Capital returned** | Nominal contributions repaid |
| **Boni** | Remaining surplus |

---

## Distribution

**Art. 1100-11 L1915**:

| Rule | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Pro rata** | According to shareholding |
| **Class rights** | Preferences respected |
| **Articles** | May modify allocation |
| **After creditors** | Only after full payment |

---

## Order of Distribution

| Priority | Recipient |
|----------|-----------|
| **1** | Liquidation expenses |
| **2** | Secured creditors |
| **3** | Unsecured creditors |
| **4** | Return of capital (shareholders) |
| **5** | Boni de liquidation |

---

## Share Classes

**Steichen**: If multiple share classes:

| Type | Treatment |
|------|-----------|
| **Ordinary** | Pro rata after preferences |
| **Preferred** | As per preference rights |
| **Founder shares** | Per articles |

---

## Compared to Mali

| Term | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Boni** | Surplus (positive) |
| **Mali** | Shortfall (negative) |
| **Mali effect** | No distribution to shareholders |
| **In unlimited** | Associates may have to contribute |

---

## Tax Treatment

| Aspect | Treatment |
|--------|-----------|
| **Nature** | Deemed distribution |
| **Withholding** | May apply |
| **Capital gains** | May apply to shareholders |
| **Participation exemption** | May shelter |

---

## Final Steps

After boni distributed:

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Final accounts** | Liquidation accounts closed |
| **Discharge** | Liquidator discharged |
| **Cancellation** | RCS registration cancelled |
| **End** | Legal personality extinguished |

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Boni goes to shareholders only
- Creditors paid first
- Security enforcement before liquidation preferable
- Subordinated debt: check ranking
