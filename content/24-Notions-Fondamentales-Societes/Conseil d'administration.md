# Conseil d'administration

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 2-2, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 441-1 to 441-14 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: The *conseil d'administration* (board of directors) is the collegial organ responsible for managing the SA under the monistic governance model.

---

## Composition

**Art. 441-1 L1915**:

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Minimum** | 3 directors (1 if single shareholder) |
| **Maximum** | 15 (unless articles permit more) |
| **Persons** | Natural or legal |
| **Shareholders** | Not required unless articles provide |

---

## Functions

### Management (*Gestion*)

**Steichen**: The board has full powers to manage the company:
- Strategic decisions
- Major contracts
- Policy setting
- Delegation to delegates

### Representation

**Art. 441-10 L1915**: The board represents the company:
- Collectively or through delegates
- Binds company to third parties
- Statutory limits not opposable externally

---

## Operation

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Meetings** | As needed per articles |
| **Quorum** | Majority present (unless stricter) |
| **Voting** | Majority of those present |
| **Minutes** | Must be kept |
| **Written resolutions** | Permitted if unanimous |

---

## Delegation

**Art. 441-10 L1915**: Board may delegate:

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Daily management** | *Gestion journalière* to delegate |
| **Special powers** | Specific matters to committees |
| **Representation** | To one or more directors |

---

## Compared to Directoire

| Aspect | Conseil d'administration | Directoire |
|--------|-------------------------|------------|
| **Model** | Monistic | Dualistic |
| **Supervision** | Self-supervising | Separate conseil de surveillance |
| **Members** | Directors | Members of directoire |
| **Common in** | Most SA | Larger companies |
