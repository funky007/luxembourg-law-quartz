# Administrateur

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 2-2, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 441-1 to 441-14 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: An *administrateur* (director) is a member of the board of directors (*conseil d'administration*) of an SA in the monistic governance structure, responsible for managing and representing the company.

---

## Eligibility

**Art. 441-1 L1915**:

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Natural persons** | Yes |
| **Legal persons** | Yes (through permanent representative) |
| **Shareholder** | Not required (unless articles) |
| **Nationality** | No restriction |
| **Residency** | No legal requirement |
| **Number** | Minimum 3 (or 1 if single shareholder) |

---

## Appointment

| Method | Situation |
|--------|-----------|
| **In articles** | First directors named at formation |
| **AGM election** | Normal appointment |
| **Co-optation** | Board fills vacancy (subject to AGM confirmation) |

---

## Term and Renewal

**Art. 441-2 L1915**:

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Maximum term** | 6 years |
| **Renewal** | Unlimited renewals permitted |
| **Staggered terms** | May be arranged |
| **Partial renewal** | Common practice |

---

## Removal

**Steichen**: Directors may be removed:

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Ad nutum** | At will, any time |
| **By AGM** | Simple majority |
| **No reason needed** | No justification required |
| **No compensation** | Unless abuse of right |

---

## Remuneration

**Art. 441-3 L1915**:

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Fixed fees** | Set by AGM |
| **Variable** | Performance-related |
| **Tantièmes** | Share of profits |
| **Special remuneration** | For specific tasks |

---

## Powers

| Power | Source |
|-------|--------|
| **Collective** | Board decisions |
| **Individual** | Only if delegated |
| **Representation** | Through board or delegation |
| **Daily management** | If delegated |

---

## Duties

| Duty | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Care** | Act prudently |
| **Loyalty** | Corporate interest |
| **Confidentiality** | Protect information |
| **Diligence** | Attend and participate |

See [[Responsabilité des dirigeants]].

---

## Liability

**Art. 441-9 L1915**:

| Type | To Whom |
|------|---------|
| **To company** | For breach of duties |
| **To shareholders** | For certain faults |
| **To third parties** | Personal fault |
