# Dénomination sociale

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Ch. 1
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 100-4 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: The *dénomination sociale* (corporate name) is the official name under which a company operates and is known, forming part of its legal identity.

---

## Requirements

**Art. 100-4 L1915**:

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Distinctiveness** | Different from other registered names |
| **Legal form** | Must indicate company type (SA, SARL, etc.) |
| **No deception** | Cannot mislead public |
| **Registration** | Filed at RCS |

---

## Form Indicators

| Company | Indicator Required |
|---------|-------------------|
| **SA** | "Société Anonyme" or "SA" |
| **SARL** | "Société à Responsabilité Limitée" or "SARL" |
| **SAS** | "Société par Actions Simplifiée" or "SAS" |
| **SNC** | "Société en Nom Collectif" or "SNC" |
| **SCS** | "Société en Commandite Simple" or "SCS" |
| **SE** | "Societas Europaea" or "SE" |

---

## Name vs. Raison Sociale

| Concept | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Dénomination sociale** | Free-form corporate name |
| **Raison sociale** | Includes partners' names (traditional for partnerships) |
| **Modern practice** | Most use dénomination sociale |

---

## Protection

**Steichen**: Corporate name is protected:
- Against identical or confusingly similar names
- Through RCS registration
- Civil liability for passing off
- May also be trademark protected

---

## Change of Name

| Aspect | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| **Decision** | EGM resolution (articles amendment) |
| **Form** | Notarial deed |
| **Publication** | RCS and RESA |
| **Effect** | Company identity continues |

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Verify exact legal name from RCS
- Use full name with form indicator in documents
- Name changes require documentation updates
- Check for similar names (confusion risk)
