# Nullité des sociétés

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 2, Ch. 4
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 420-8 to 420-9 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Nullité des sociétés* (nullity of companies) refers to the judicial declaration that a company is void, limited to specific grounds to protect third parties and legal certainty.

---

## Principle: Limited Grounds

**Art. 420-8 L1915**: Nullity of SA only for:

| Ground | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **No notarial deed** | Form requirement violated |
| **Illegal purpose** | Object contrary to law/public order |
| **No subscribers** | Capital not subscribed |
| **Incapacity** | All founders incapable |

---

## Restrictive Approach

**Steichen**: Nullity doctrine is restrictive because:
- Third parties relied on company existence
- Transactions have occurred
- Creditors have claims
- Better to cure than destroy

---

## Compared to Contract Nullity

| Aspect | Contract Nullity | Company Nullity |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Grounds** | Multiple (consent, capacity, etc.) | Very limited |
| **Effect** | Retroactive (ab initio) | Prospective |
| **Past acts** | May be void | Protected |
| **Third parties** | Less protection | Strong protection |

---

## Prospective Effect

**Art. 420-9 L1915**: When nullity pronounced:

| Effect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Not retroactive** | Does not undo past |
| **Leads to liquidation** | Company wound up |
| **Past acts valid** | Third-party transactions protected |
| **Creditor claims** | Preserved |

---

## Cure of Defects

**Steichen**: Before nullity declared:

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Rectify defect** | Nullity avoided |
| **EGM resolution** | May cure certain defects |
| **Court discretion** | May allow regularization |

---

## Who May Invoke

| Person | Right to Invoke |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Any interested party** | May bring action |
| **Associates** | Yes |
| **Creditors** | Yes |
| **Third parties** | If legitimate interest |

---

## Consequences of Nullity

| Consequence | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Liquidation** | Mandatory winding up |
| **Founder liability** | May be liable for defects |
| **Past transactions** | Generally protected |
| **No retroactive unwinding** | Of company acts |

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Limited nullity risk provides comfort
- Verify proper formation as due diligence
- Past transactions protected if nullity occurs
- Monitor for cure opportunities
