# Réserve légale

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Ch. 1
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 461-1 L1915 (SA); Art. 710-25 L1915 (SARL)

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: The *réserve légale* (legal reserve) is a mandatory reserve fund that companies must establish by allocating a portion of annual profits, providing a buffer to protect share capital and creditors.

---

## Requirement

### SA (Art. 461-1 L1915)

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Allocation** | 5% of net profits annually |
| **Minimum level** | Until reserve equals 10% of capital |
| **Mandatory** | Cannot be waived |
| **From net profit** | After loss carry-forward |

### SARL (Art. 710-25 L1915)

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Allocation** | 5% of net profits annually |
| **Minimum level** | Until reserve equals 10% of capital |
| **Same as SA** | Equivalent requirements |

---

## Purpose

| Purpose | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Capital protection** | Additional buffer beyond capital |
| **Creditor protection** | Funds not distributable |
| **Stability** | Absorbs minor fluctuations |
| **Legal requirement** | Mandatory compliance |

---

## Calculation

**Steichen**: Annual allocation:

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Net profit** | After taxes and loss carry-forward |
| **5% calculation** | 5% of net profit |
| **Check level** | Until 10% of capital reached |
| **Cease allocation** | When reserve equals 10% capital |

---

## Use of Reserve

**Steichen**: Legal reserve may only be used for:

| Use | Permitted? |
|-----|-----------|
| **Loss absorption** | Yes |
| **Distribution** | No |
| **Capital incorporation** | Debated |
| **Transfer to capital** | Possible with reconstitution |

---

## Compared to Other Reserves

| Type | Nature |
|------|--------|
| **Réserve légale** | Mandatory, non-distributable |
| **Réserve statutaire** | Articles may require |
| **Réserve libre** | Voluntary, distributable |
| **Report à nouveau** | Carried forward profit |

---

## Consequences of Non-Compliance

**Steichen**: Failure to allocate:
- Resolution may be challenged
- Directors potentially liable
- Tax implications

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Check legal reserve compliance
- Part of equity analysis
- Not available for distribution
- Affects distributable reserves calculation
