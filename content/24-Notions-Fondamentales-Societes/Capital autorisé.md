# Capital autorisé

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Ch. 1
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 430-14 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Capital autorisé* (authorized capital) is a mechanism allowing the board of directors to increase share capital within pre-approved limits, without requiring EGM approval for each issuance.

---

## Legal Basis

**Art. 430-14 L1915**: Articles may authorize board to:
- Issue new shares
- Up to specified maximum amount
- For specified period
- Without EGM for each issuance

---

## Requirements

| Requirement | Rule |
|-------------|------|
| **EGM approval** | Initial authorization in articles |
| **Maximum amount** | Must be specified |
| **Time limit** | Maximum 5 years (renewable) |
| **Publication** | Authorization published |

---

## Limits

**Art. 430-14 L1915**:

| Limit | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Amount** | Specified maximum |
| **Duration** | 5 years maximum |
| **DPS** | May suppress if authorized |
| **Board decision** | Each issuance needs board resolution |

---

## Procedure for Use

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Board meeting** | Decides to issue shares |
| **Terms fixed** | Price, number, conditions |
| **Subscription** | By investors |
| **Notarial deed** | Recording issuance |
| **RCS filing** | Capital increase recorded |

---

## Advantages

| Advantage | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Speed** | No EGM needed per issuance |
| **Flexibility** | React to market conditions |
| **Opportunistic** | Seize investment opportunities |
| **Simplified** | Reduced procedural burden |

---

## Safeguards

**Steichen**: Shareholders protected by:

| Safeguard | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Initial EGM** | Must approve scheme |
| **Caps** | Amount and time limited |
| **Renewals** | EGM must reauthorize |
| **Reporting** | Board reports on use |
| **DPS** | Unless expressly excluded |

---

## Comparison

| Aspect | Standard Increase | Authorized Capital |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **EGM needed** | Each time | Only for authorization |
| **Speed** | Slower | Faster |
| **Board role** | Proposes | Decides within limits |
| **Flexibility** | Lower | Higher |
