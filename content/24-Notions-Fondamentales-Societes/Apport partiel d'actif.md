# Apport partiel d'actif

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 1022-1 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Apport partiel d'actif* (partial asset contribution / hive-down) is a restructuring whereby a company transfers a branch of activity (*branche d'activité*) to another company in exchange for shares, without dissolving.

---

## Distinction from Other Operations

| Operation | Transferring Company | Result |
|-----------|---------------------|--------|
| **Apport partiel** | Continues to exist | Receives shares |
| **Scission** | Dissolves | Shareholders receive shares |
| **Sale of assets** | Continues | Receives cash |
| **Fusion** | May dissolve | Combined entity |

---

## Key Requirements

**Art. 1022-1 L1915**:

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Branch of activity** | Autonomous business unit |
| **Universal succession** | All assets/liabilities of branch |
| **Share consideration** | Transferor receives shares |
| **Continuity** | Transferring company continues |

---

## Branch of Activity (*Branche d'activité*)

**Steichen**: A branch requires:
- Autonomous economic activity
- Own assets and liabilities
- Capable of independent operation
- Identifiable business unit

---

## Procedure

Similar to merger/scission when merger rules apply:

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Contribution agreement** | Terms of transfer |
| **Valuation** | Of contributed branch |
| **Board approval** | Both companies |
| **EGM approval** | If required by articles |
| **Notarial deed** | If capital increase |
| **RCS filing** | Publication |

---

## Effects

| Effect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Asset transfer** | Branch transferred universally |
| **Shares received** | Transferor becomes shareholder |
| **Creditor protection** | May apply depending on structure |
| **Continuity** | Contracts generally continue |

---

## Tax Considerations

**Practice**: Often structured for tax neutrality:
- EU Merger Directive benefits
- Deferral of capital gains
- Requires business purpose

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Review for impact on loan security
- Branch assets may secure borrowings
- Lender consent often required
- Universal succession includes liabilities
