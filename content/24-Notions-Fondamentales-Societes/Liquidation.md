# Liquidation (Winding Up Process)

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Chapitre 4
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 1100-5 to 1100-13 L1915

---

## Definition

> *Liquidation* is the process of realizing company assets, paying creditors, and distributing any surplus to shareholders following dissolution. The company continues to exist for the sole purpose of liquidation until final cancellation from the RCS.

**Steichen, Titre 4, Ch. 4**: Liquidation (*liquidation*) is the final phase of corporate existence — winding up affairs, settling debts, and distributing residue (*boni de liquidation*) to members.

---

## 1. Purposes of Liquidation

### 1.1 Primary Functions

| Function | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Realize assets** | Convert company property to cash |
| **Pay debts** | Settle liabilities in order of priority |
| **Distribute surplus** | Pay remaining funds to shareholders |
| **Close affairs** | Complete pending business |

### 1.2 Company Continues

**Art. 1100-5 L1915**: After dissolution:
- Company retains legal personality
- Exclusively for liquidation purposes
- Must add "en liquidation" to name
- New activities prohibited

---

## 2. Opening of Liquidation

### 2.1 Commencement

**Steichen**: Liquidation opens automatically upon:
- EGM resolution of dissolution
- Expiry of company's term
- Court order
- Other statutory dissolution events

### 2.2 Appointment of Liquidator

**Art. 1100-6 L1915**:

| Appointing Body | When |
|-----------------|------|
| **EGM** | Voluntary liquidation |
| **Court** | Judicial dissolution |
| **Articles** | If pre-designated |
| **Court** | If others fail to appoint |

### 2.3 Who May Be Liquidator

**Steichen**: No specific qualifications required:
- Natural or legal person
- May be former director/manager
- May be shareholder
- May be third party (professional liquidator)

---

## 3. Powers and Duties of Liquidator

### 3.1 General Powers

**Art. 1100-7 L1915**: Unless otherwise provided:

| Power | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Represent company** | Externally for liquidation acts |
| **Realize assets** | Sell company property |
| **Collect debts** | Recover amounts owed to company |
| **Pay creditors** | In order of priority |
| **Settle litigation** | Conclude or settle pending cases |
| **Distribute surplus** | To shareholders after debts paid |

### 3.2 Limitations

**Steichen**: Liquidator may NOT:
- Continue trading (except to wind up)
- Enter new contracts (except for liquidation)
- Increase company's debts unnecessarily

### 3.3 Authorization Requirements

| Act | May Require |
|-----|-------------|
| **Sale of business** | EGM approval |
| **Sale of immovables** | Per articles or EGM |
| **Compromise claims** | May require approval |

---

## 4. Asset Realization

### 4.1 Methods

**Steichen**: Assets may be sold by:

| Method | Application |
|--------|-------------|
| **Private sale** | Negotiated with buyers |
| **Public auction** | If required or preferable |
| **En bloc sale** | Business as going concern |
| **Contribution** | To another company |

### 4.2 Preservation Duties

Liquidator must:
- Maintain company assets
- Collect receivables
- Preserve value where possible
- Act in company's interest

---

## 5. Payment of Creditors

### 5.1 Order of Priority

**Steichen**: Standard order applies:

| Rank | Creditor Type |
|------|---------------|
| 1 | Secured creditors (from collateral) |
| 2 | Super-privileged claims (wages) |
| 3 | General privileges |
| 4 | Ordinary unsecured creditors |
| 5 | Subordinated creditors |

### 5.2 Creditor Notification

**Art. 1100-8 L1915**: Liquidator must:
- Invite creditors to file claims
- Publish notice
- Review and verify claims

### 5.3 Insufficient Assets

If assets insufficient to pay all creditors:
- Pro rata distribution among same-rank creditors
- Shareholders receive nothing
- May trigger bankruptcy proceedings

---

## 6. Distribution to Shareholders

### 6.1 Boni de Liquidation

**Steichen**: Surplus after paying all debts (*boni de liquidation*):
- Distributed to shareholders
- In proportion to holdings (unless articles differ)
- After final accounts approved

### 6.2 Sequence

1. Repayment of capital contributions
2. Distribution of surplus proportionally
3. Subject to any preferences in articles

### 6.3 Interim Distributions

**Steichen**: Liquidator may make interim distributions if:
- Sufficient reserves retained for debts
- Approved by shareholders
- Creditors protected

---

## 7. Closure of Liquidation

### 7.1 Final Steps

| Step | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Final accounts** | Liquidator prepares |
| **Shareholder approval** | EGM approves accounts |
| **Discharge** | Shareholders discharge liquidator |
| **Final distribution** | Remaining funds distributed |
| **Cancellation** | Struck from RCS |

### 7.2 RCS Cancellation

**Art. 1100-12 L1915**: Upon completion:
- File closure notice with RCS
- Company ceases to exist
- Legal personality ends

### 7.3 Document Retention

**Art. 1100-13 L1915**: Company records must be:
- Preserved for 5 years after closure
- Deposited with designated person
- Available for inspection

---

## 8. Special Cases

### 8.1 Simplified Liquidation

Where company has:
- No liabilities
- No assets to realize
- Straightforward closure

Procedure may be expedited.

### 8.2 Judicial Liquidation

**Steichen**: Court may supervise if:
- Disputes among shareholders
- Creditor concerns
- Liquidator misconduct suspected

### 8.3 Bankruptcy

If company is insolvent:
- Liquidation transforms to bankruptcy
- Bankruptcy trustee replaces liquidator
- Bankruptcy rules apply

---

## 9. Liability of Liquidator

### 9.1 Standard of Care

**Steichen**: Liquidator liable for:

| Ground | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Breach of duty** | Failure to perform functions |
| **Negligence** | Careless management of liquidation |
| **Fraud** | Dishonest dealing |
| **Failure to distribute** | Wrongful retention of assets |

### 9.2 To Whom Liable

| Party | Basis |
|-------|-------|
| **Company** | Breach of mandate |
| **Shareholders** | For distributions errors |
| **Creditors** | For improper payments |

## Banking & Finance Application

### Lender Position in Liquidation

| Consideration | Action |
|---------------|--------|
| **Verify dissolution** | Obtain EGM minutes, RCS notice |
| **File claims** | Submit claim to liquidator |
| **Security interests** | Assert priority over collateral |
| **Monitor distributions** | Ensure proper ranking respected |

### Security Considerations

| Issue | Analysis |
|-------|----------|
| **Security continues** | During liquidation |
| **Enforcement** | May proceed against collateral |
| **Floating charges** | Crystallize on liquidation |
| **Guarantees** | Pursue guarantors separately |

### Due Diligence on Liquidation

| Check | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Liquidator identity** | Proper appointment |
| **Publication** | Correct notices |
| **Asset inventory** | Realization prospects |
| **Creditor list** | Priority ranking |

### Common Issues

1. **Late filing**: Claims may be subordinated
2. **Security gaps**: Unperfected security loses priority
3. **Preferential payments**: May be clawed back
4. **Shareholder loans**: Often subordinated
