# Actions (Shares)

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Chapitre 2; Titre 2-2, Chapitre 2
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 430-1 to 430-21 L1915

---

## Definition

> *Actions* (shares) are the securities representing ownership interests in a société anonyme (SA), société en commandite par actions (SCA), or société par actions simplifiée (SAS). They embody the shareholder's membership rights and obligations.

**Steichen**: Shares are *valeurs mobilières* (negotiable securities) that represent fractional ownership of the company's share capital.

---

## 1. Characteristics

### 1.1 Essential Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Negotiability** | Freely transferable (principle) |
| **Divisibility** | Capital divided into equal units |
| **Fungibility** | Shares of same class are interchangeable |
| **Nominal value** | Fixed par value per share |

### 1.2 Distinction from Parts Sociales

| Aspect | Actions (SA, SCA, SAS) | Parts sociales (SARL, SNC) |
|--------|------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Negotiability** | Freely negotiable | Transfer restricted |
| **Form** | Bearer or registered | Registered only |
| **Divisibility** | May be divided | Indivisible |
| **Transfer** | Simplified procedure | Formal assignment |

## 2. Forms of Shares

### 2.1 By Registration

**Art. 430-3 L1915**:

| Form | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Registered** (*nominatives*) | Owner recorded in share register |
| **Bearer** (*au porteur*) | Ownership by possession (abolished for most) |
| **Dematerialized** | Book-entry securities |

**Steichen**: Since 2014 reforms, bearer shares have been largely abolished. Shares must generally be:
- Registered, OR
- Dematerialized (book-entry)

### 2.2 By Rights Attached

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Ordinary shares** | Standard voting and dividend rights |
| **Preference shares** | Enhanced dividend or liquidation rights |
| **Non-voting shares** | Dividend preference but no/limited votes |
| **Founder shares** | Special rights for founders |

### 2.3 Multiple Voting Shares

**Art. 430-17 L1915**: Articles may provide for:
- Loyalty shares (voting bonus for long-term holders)
- Multiple voting rights (within limits)

---

## 3. Share Capital

### 3.1 Minimum Capital

**Art. 420-3 L1915** (SA):

| Company Type | Minimum Capital |
|--------------|-----------------|
| **SA** | EUR 30,000 |
| **SCA** | EUR 30,000 |
| **SAS** | No minimum (since 2016) |
| **SE** | EUR 120,000 |

### 3.2 Payment

**Art. 420-7 L1915** (SA):
- Capital must be **fully subscribed**
- At least **25%** paid up at formation
- Remainder callable within **5 years**

### 3.3 Nominal Value

**Steichen**: Each share has a nominal (par) value:
- Total nominal value × number of shares = share capital
- Shares issued above par: difference is *prime d'émission* (share premium)

## 4. Rights Attached to Shares

### 4.1 Financial Rights

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Dividends** | Share in distributable profits |
| **Liquidation proceeds** | Share in surplus on dissolution |
| **Preemptive rights** | Priority on new share issues |

### 4.2 Governance Rights

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Voting** | Participate in general meetings |
| **Information** | Access to accounts and reports |
| **Challenge resolutions** | Contest invalid decisions |

### 4.3 Patrimonial Rights

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Transfer** | Sell or gift shares |
| **Pledge** | Use as security |
| **Inheritance** | Transmit on death |

---

## 5. Transfer of Shares

### 5.1 Registered Shares

**Art. 430-9 L1915**: Transfer by:
1. Declaration of transfer in share register
2. Signed by transferor and transferee (or proxies)
3. Dated

**Effect**: Transfer effective against company and third parties from registration.

### 5.2 Dematerialized Shares

**Art. 430-12 L1915**: Transfer by:
- Book-entry transfer between securities accounts
- No physical delivery

### 5.3 Restrictions on Transfer

**Steichen**: Articles may provide:

| Restriction | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Approval clause** (*clause d'agrément*) | Board/shareholder approval required |
| **Pre-emption** (*droit de préemption*) | Existing shareholders have first refusal |
| **Inalienability** | Temporary transfer prohibition |
| **Tag-along/Drag-along** | Co-sale rights and obligations |

---

## 6. Special Categories

### 6.1 Treasury Shares (*Actions propres*)

**Art. 430-15 L1915**: Company may acquire own shares if:
- Funded from distributable reserves
- Not exceeding 10% of capital
- Authorized by EGM (5-year authorization)
- Voting rights suspended while held

### 6.2 Redeemable Shares (*Actions rachetables*)

**Art. 430-18 L1915**: Articles may provide for shares redeemable:
- At company's option, OR
- At shareholder's option
- Subject to conditions in articles

### 6.3 Tracking Shares

**Steichen**: Shares whose dividends are linked to performance of specific business unit or asset.

---

## 7. Share Register

### 7.1 Requirements

**Art. 430-5 L1915**: SA must maintain register of registered shares showing:

| Information | Content |
|-------------|---------|
| **Name and address** | Of each shareholder |
| **Number of shares** | Held by each |
| **Payment status** | Paid-up amount |
| **Transfers** | Date and parties |
| **Conversions** | If applicable |

### 7.2 Access

- Shareholders: right to inspect
- Third parties: limited access (no right to copy full register)

---

## 8. Shareholders' Agreements

### 8.1 Common Provisions

| Clause | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Lock-up** | Restrict transfers for period |
| **Tag-along** | Minority can join majority sale |
| **Drag-along** | Majority can force minority sale |
| **Pre-emption** | Right of first refusal |
| **Put/call options** | Options to buy or sell |
| **Voting arrangements** | Coordinated voting |

### 8.2 Enforceability

**Steichen**: Shareholders' agreements are:
- Binding between parties (contractual)
- Not binding on company unless incorporated in articles
- Subject to corporate law limits (e.g., voting pacts)

## Banking & Finance Application

### Pledge over Shares

**Key considerations**:

| Issue | Analysis |
|-------|----------|
| **Perfection** | Registration in share register or securities account |
| **Control** | Possession of certificates (if any) or account control |
| **Restrictions** | Check for approval clauses, pre-emption |
| **Enforcement** | Appropriation or sale procedures |

### Due Diligence Checklist

| Check | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Share register** | Verify ownership and encumbrances |
| **Articles** | Transfer restrictions, approval requirements |
| **Shareholders' agreements** | Pre-emption, tag/drag rights |
| **Capital structure** | Preference shares, dilution risk |
| **Paid-up status** | Outstanding calls on capital |

### Valuation Considerations

- Net asset value
- Earnings multiple
- Discounts for minority/illiquidity
- Control premium

### Common Issues

1. **Unregistered transfers**: Ineffective against company
2. **Approval not obtained**: Transfer may be void
3. **Pre-emption not respected**: Other shareholders may have claims
4. **Bearer shares**: Verify dematerialization compliance
