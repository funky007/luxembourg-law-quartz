# Société unipersonnelle

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 1, Ch. 1
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 1832 al. 2 C.civ.; Art. 710-2 L1915 (SARL); Art. 420-1 L1915 (SA)

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: A *société unipersonnelle* (single-member company) is a company with only one associate, permitted for certain legal forms by law, derogating from the traditional requirement of plurality of associates.

---

## Legal Basis

**Art. 1832 al. 2 C.civ.**: Companies may be formed:
- By two or more persons (traditional), OR
- By act of a single person (where law permits)

---

## Permitted Forms

| Company | Single Member Allowed? |
|---------|----------------------|
| **SA** | Yes (Art. 420-1 L1915) |
| **SARL** | Yes (Art. 710-2 L1915) |
| **SAS** | Yes (Art. 490-1 L1915) |
| **SARL-S** | Yes |
| **SNC** | No (requires 2+) |
| **SCS** | No (requires 2 types of partners) |
| **SC** | No |

---

## Characteristics

**Steichen**: Single-member company features:

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Legal personality** | Full separate personality |
| **Limited liability** | Same as multi-member |
| **Sole associate powers** | Exercises all shareholder powers |
| **No GM formalities** | Decisions recorded in minutes |
| **Governance** | Still needs director/gérant |

---

## Decision-Making

**Art. 710-13 L1915** (SARL unipersonnelle):

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Sole associate** | Exercises all AGM/EGM powers |
| **Written decisions** | Must be recorded |
| **Register** | Decisions kept in register |
| **No formal meeting** | No convocation needed |

---

## Transparency

**Steichen**: Additional disclosure:

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **RCS mention** | "Société unipersonnelle" may appear |
| **Contracts register** | Contracts with sole associate recorded |
| **Annual accounts** | Same filing requirements |

---

## Becoming Single-Member

A company becomes unipersonnelle when:
- All shares concentrated in one hand
- Other associates exit
- Does NOT cause dissolution (for permitted forms)

---

## Leaving Single-Member Status

**Steichen**: Company ceases to be unipersonnelle when:
- Shares transferred to second person
- New associate admitted
- Normal multi-member rules resume

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Full limited liability protection applies
- Check for single counterparty concentration risk
- Sole associate decisions still need documentation
- May be subsidiary in group structure
