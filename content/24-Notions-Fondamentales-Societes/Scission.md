# Scission

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Ch. 2
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 1021-1 to 1021-30 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Scission* (demerger/split-up) is a restructuring operation whereby a company transfers all its assets and liabilities to two or more recipient companies, with the transferring company being dissolved.

---

## Types of Scission

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Scission pure** | Transfer to newly created companies |
| **Scission par absorption** | Transfer to existing companies |
| **Scission partielle** | Partial asset transfer (*apport partiel d'actif*) |

---

## Procedure

**Art. 1021-1 et seq. L1915**:

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Merger/scission plan** | Draft plan published |
| **Board report** | Explain terms and allocation |
| **Independent expert** | Valuation report |
| **EGM approval** | All companies involved |
| **Creditor protection** | Opposition rights |
| **Notarial deed** | Formal execution |
| **RCS filing** | Registration and publication |

---

## Effects

**Art. 1021-23 L1915**:

| Effect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Universal succession** | Assets and liabilities transfer |
| **Share allocation** | Shareholders receive shares in recipients |
| **Dissolution** | Transferring company dissolved |
| **No liquidation** | Automatic without liquidation |

---

## Allocation Rules

**Steichen**: The scission plan must specify:
- Which assets go to which recipient
- Which liabilities follow which assets
- Share exchange ratio for each recipient
- Allocation of shareholders

---

## Joint Liability

**Art. 1021-24 L1915**: Recipient companies are:
- Jointly liable for transferred liabilities
- Up to net assets received (generally)
- Protecting creditors of demerging company

---

## Compared to Fusion

| Aspect | Fusion | Scission |
|--------|--------|----------|
| **Direction** | Combination | Split |
| **Result** | One company remains | Two+ recipients |
| **Shareholders** | All in one company | Split between recipients |
| **Assets** | Combined | Divided |

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- May require lender consent if material change
- Allocation of debt obligations critical
- Joint liability provides creditor protection
- Review scission plan for debt treatment
