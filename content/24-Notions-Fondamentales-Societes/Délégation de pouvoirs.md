# Délégation de pouvoirs

> **Type**: Notion Fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Ch. 3
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 441-10 L1915; Art. 710-15 L1915

---

## Definition

**Steichen**: *Délégation de pouvoirs* (delegation of powers) is the transfer of authority from a company organ to another person (director, employee, or third party) to act on behalf of the company.

---

## Types of Delegation

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Gestion journalière** | Daily management operations |
| **Special mandate** | Specific acts or transactions |
| **General representation** | Broad authority to bind company |
| **Limited delegation** | Specific matters only |

---

## SA Delegations

**Art. 441-10 L1915**: Board may delegate to:

| Delegatee | Scope |
|-----------|-------|
| **Administrator-delegate** | Daily management |
| **Director** | Specific powers |
| **Non-director delegate** | Daily management or specific acts |
| **Committee** | Certain board functions |

---

## SARL Delegations

**Art. 710-15 L1915**: Gérant may delegate:

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Authority** | Within gérant's own powers |
| **Delegatee** | Employees, agents |
| **Limits** | Cannot delegate entire function |
| **Responsibility** | Gérant remains responsible |

---

## Requirements for Valid Delegation

**Steichen**: Effective delegation requires:

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Competent delegator** | Must have power to delegate |
| **Written form** | Best practice (proof) |
| **Publication** | At RCS if external effect |
| **Acceptance** | By delegatee |
| **Scope clarity** | Clear boundaries |

---

## Publication

**Art. 441-10 L1915**:

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Daily management** | Published at RCS |
| **Effect on third parties** | Only from publication |
| **Internal limits** | Not opposable to third parties |

---

## Revocation

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **At will** | Generally revocable |
| **Effect** | Ceases delegatee's authority |
| **Publication** | Revocation must be published |
| **Third parties** | Protected until publication |

---

## Limits

**Steichen**: Delegation does NOT transfer:
- Overall responsibility (remains with delegator)
- Non-delegable functions (e.g., accounts approval)
- Strategic decisions (board level)

---

## Banking & Finance Application

- Verify delegation for signatory authority
- Check RCS for published delegations
- Limits in delegation may affect transaction
- Request delegation deed for verification
