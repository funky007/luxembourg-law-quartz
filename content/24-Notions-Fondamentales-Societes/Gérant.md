# Gérant (Manager)

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Chapitre 3
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 710-14 to 710-16 L1915 (SARL); Art. 200-1 L1915 (SNC)

---

## Definition

> The *gérant* (manager) is the executive organ of certain company types — primarily SARL, SNC, SCS, and société civile. The gérant exercises management powers and represents the company vis-à-vis third parties.

**Steichen, Titre 3, Ch. 3**: The gérant is appointed to manage the company's day-to-day affairs and to bind the company externally. Unlike directors of an SA, the gérant need not be plural.

---

## 1. Company Types with Gérant

| Company Type | Applicable Rules |
|--------------|------------------|
| **SARL** | Arts. 710-14 to 710-16 L1915 |
| **SNC** | Arts. 200-1 to 200-2 L1915 |
| **SCS** | Arts. 300-1 et seq. L1915 (commandités) |
| **Société civile** | Art. 1856 C.civ. |
| **SCA** | Arts. 600-5 et seq. L1915 (gérant commandité) |

## 2. Appointment

### 2.1 SARL Gérant

**Art. 710-14 L1915**:

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Who may appoint** | Associates (by majority of capital) |
| **Appointed in** | Articles or separate act |
| **Qualifications** | Natural person (or legal entity since 2016) |
| **Number** | One or more |
| **Associate requirement** | May be associate or third party |

### 2.2 Statutory vs. Non-Statutory Gérant

| Type | Appointed in | Removal |
|------|--------------|---------|
| **Statutory** (*gérant statutaire*) | Articles of association | Requires articles amendment |
| **Non-statutory** | Separate decision | Revocable by simple majority |

**Steichen**: The distinction is significant because:
- Statutory gérant removal requires EGM + notarial deed
- Non-statutory gérant revocable *ad nutum* (at will)

### 2.3 SNC Gérant

**Art. 200-1 L1915**:
- All associates are gérants unless articles provide otherwise
- May designate one or more gérants
- Statutory gérant removal requires unanimity

## 3. Powers

### 3.1 Internal Powers

**Steichen**: Within the company, the gérant may:

| Power | Scope |
|-------|-------|
| **Management** | Day-to-day operations |
| **Decision-making** | Matters not reserved to associates |
| **Implementation** | Execute associate decisions |

**Limits**: Subject to:
- Corporate purpose (*objet social*)
- Statutory restrictions
- Reserved matters (capital changes, accounts approval, etc.)

### 3.2 External Powers (Representation)

**Art. 710-15 L1915** (SARL):

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **General power** | Gérant binds company for all acts within corporate purpose |
| **Statutory limits** | Not opposable to third parties |
| **Ultra vires acts** | Company bound unless third party knew |

**Steichen**: Third parties are protected:
- Internal limitations on gérant's powers are ineffective against them
- Company cannot invoke internal restrictions

### 3.3 Multiple Gérants

**Art. 710-15 L1915** (SARL):

| Structure | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| **Joint management** | Each gérant may act alone (default) |
| **Joint signature** | Articles may require multiple signatures |
| **Opposition** | One gérant may oppose another's act |

---

## 4. Duties

### 4.1 Duty of Care

**Steichen**: Gérant must act as a *bonus pater familias*:
- Prudent management
- Informed decision-making
- Diligent supervision

### 4.2 Duty of Loyalty

| Obligation | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Avoid conflicts** | No self-dealing without disclosure |
| **Corporate interest** | Act in company's best interest |
| **Confidentiality** | Protect company information |
| **Non-compete** | May be implied or contractual |

### 4.3 Statutory Duties

| Duty | Reference |
|------|-----------|
| **Convene meetings** | When required |
| **Prepare accounts** | Annual accounts |
| **File documents** | RCS filings |
| **Report to associates** | Annual management report |

---

## 5. Liability

### 5.1 Liability to Company

**Art. 710-16 L1915** (SARL):

| Type | Ground |
|------|--------|
| **Breach of mandate** | Failure to perform duties |
| **Violation of law** | Breach of L1915 |
| **Violation of articles** | Breach of statutes |
| **Faute de gestion** | Management fault |

### 5.2 Liability to Third Parties

**Art. 710-16 L1915** (SARL):

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Direct damage** | Not merely derivative of company's loss |
| **Fault** | Wrongful act attributable to gérant |
| **Causation** | Link between fault and damage |

### 5.3 Joint Liability

**Art. 710-16 L1915**: Multiple gérants are:
- **Jointly liable** (*solidairement responsables*) for joint decisions
- **Individually liable** for personal faults
- May escape liability if opposed and recorded dissent

---

## 6. Removal

### 6.1 SARL Gérant

**Art. 710-14 L1915**:

| Type | Removal Procedure |
|------|-------------------|
| **Non-statutory** | Majority of capital |
| **Statutory** | Articles amendment (3/4 majority) |
| **Judicial** | For just cause (*juste motif*) |

**Steichen**: Removal is distinct from termination of employment:
- Gérant with employment contract may remain employee
- Damages possible if removal abusive

### 6.2 SNC Gérant

**Art. 200-1 L1915**:
- Statutory gérant: unanimity required
- May petition court for removal for just cause

---

## 7. Remuneration

### 7.1 Determination

**Steichen**: Remuneration may be:
- Fixed in articles
- Decided by associates
- Combination of salary + profit share

### 7.2 Social Security Status

**Steichen**: Gérant status depends on:

| Situation | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| **Majority gérant** | Self-employed (independent) |
| **Minority/equal gérant** | May be salaried (if subordination) |
| **With employment contract** | Dual status possible |

---

## 8. Termination of Functions

### 8.1 Causes

| Cause | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Resignation** | Must be notified; may require notice |
| **Removal** | By associates or court |
| **Death** | Functions end |
| **Incapacity** | Functions end |
| **Expiry of term** | If fixed duration |
| **Dissolution** | Company dissolved |

### 8.2 Handover

Outgoing gérant must:
- Hand over company property
- Provide access to records
- Cooperate with successor
- Not compete (if clause exists)

## Banking & Finance Application

### Due Diligence on Gérant

| Check | Source |
|-------|--------|
| **Identity** | RCS extract |
| **Authority** | Articles, appointment decision |
| **Signature powers** | Single or joint |
| **Limitations** | Internal restrictions (though not opposable) |

### Lending Considerations

| Issue | Analysis |
|-------|----------|
| **Signing authority** | Verify gérant can bind company |
| **Corporate benefit** | Transaction benefits company |
| **Conflict of interest** | Gérant not personally interested |
| **Personal guarantee** | Gérant may guarantee company debts |

### Personal Guarantee from Gérant

**See [[Caution dirigeante]]**:
- Common security in SME lending
- Assess gérant's personal assets
- Consider proportionality

### Red Flags

- Recent gérant change (verify authority)
- Multiple gérants with joint signature requirement
- Gérant also creditor or counterparty
- Gérant resignation pending
