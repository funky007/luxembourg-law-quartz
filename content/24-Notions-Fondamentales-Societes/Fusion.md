# Fusion (Merger)

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 4, Chapitre 2
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 1021-1 to 1021-47 L1915

---

## Definition

> *Fusion* (merger) is the corporate operation whereby one or more companies transfer their entire assets and liabilities to another company (existing or newly created) in exchange for shares issued to shareholders of the absorbed company(ies), resulting in dissolution without liquidation of the absorbed company(ies).

**Steichen, Titre 4, Ch. 2**: Merger is a *transmission universelle du patrimoine* (universal transfer of assets) — all rights and obligations pass by operation of law.

---

## 1. Types of Merger

### 1.1 Merger by Absorption (*Fusion-absorption*)

**Art. 1021-1 L1915**:

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Absorbing company** | Existing company receives assets |
| **Absorbed company(ies)** | Transfer all assets; dissolved |
| **Consideration** | Shares in absorbing company |

**Steichen**: Most common form — one company survives, other(s) disappear.

### 1.2 Merger by Formation of New Company (*Fusion par constitution*)

**Art. 1021-1 L1915**:

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **New company** | Created to receive all assets |
| **Merging companies** | All dissolved |
| **Consideration** | Shares in new company |

**Steichen**: Less common due to costs (new formation, transfer taxes).

### 1.3 Distinguished from Other Operations

| Operation | Difference from Merger |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Share acquisition** | Assets remain with target; no dissolution |
| **Asset purchase** | Individual asset transfer; no universal succession |
| **Scission** | Assets split among multiple recipients |
| **Apport partiel** | Only part of assets transferred |

---

## 2. Key Features

### 2.1 Universal Succession

**Steichen**: All assets and liabilities transfer automatically:
- No individual transfer formalities
- Contracts continue (subject to exceptions)
- Employees transfer with employment protection
- Permits and licenses (check specific rules)

### 2.2 Dissolution Without Liquidation

**Art. 1021-4 L1915**: Absorbed company:
- Ceases to exist
- No liquidation procedure
- No distribution to shareholders (they receive acquiring company shares)

### 2.3 Exchange of Shares

Shareholders of absorbed company receive:
- Shares in absorbing/new company
- Determined by exchange ratio
- Cash adjustment (*soulte*) permitted (max 10%)

## 3. Procedure

### 3.1 Merger Proposal (*Projet de fusion*)

**Art. 1021-5 L1915**: Board/management must prepare:

| Required Content | Description |
|------------------|-------------|
| **Company details** | Names, forms, registered offices |
| **Exchange ratio** | Shares to be issued |
| **Allocation** | Distribution of new shares |
| **Effective date** | When merger takes effect |
| **Rights** | Treatment of special shares, options |
| **Advantages** | Any special benefits to management |

### 3.2 Reports

**Art. 1021-7 L1915**: Required reports:

| Report | Prepared by |
|--------|-------------|
| **Management report** | Board of each company |
| **Independent expert report** | *Réviseur d'entreprises agréé* |

**Expert report** must verify:
- Exchange ratio is fair and reasonable
- Valuation methods appropriate
- Any difficulties encountered

### 3.3 Publication and Filing

**Art. 1021-6 L1915**:
- Merger proposal filed with RCS
- Published at least 1 month before EGM
- Available to shareholders, creditors, employees

### 3.4 Shareholder Approval

**Art. 1021-9 L1915**: Each company's EGM must approve:

| Requirement | SA | SARL |
|-------------|-----|------|
| **Quorum** | 50% capital | N/A |
| **Majority** | 2/3 votes cast | 3/4 capital |
| **Form** | Notarial deed | Notarial deed |

### 3.5 Creditor Protection

**Art. 1021-13 L1915**: Creditors may oppose merger:
- Within 30 days of publication
- Court may order security or payment
- Does not block merger (unless court orders)

### 3.6 Effective Date

**Art. 1021-12 L1915**:
- Between companies: as agreed in merger proposal
- Against third parties: from RCS publication

---

## 4. Simplified Procedures

### 4.1 Parent-Subsidiary Merger

**Art. 1021-27 L1915**: Simplified rules for 100% subsidiary:
- No exchange ratio needed (no shares issued)
- No independent expert report
- No shareholder approval at subsidiary
- Reduced documentation

### 4.2 90%+ Merger

**Art. 1021-28 L1915**: Where parent holds 90%+:
- No EGM required at parent
- Minority may demand purchase of shares
- Reduced procedural requirements

---

## 5. Cross-Border Merger

### 5.1 Legal Framework

**Arts. 1023-1 to 1023-21 L1915** (implementing EU Directive):

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Scope** | Between companies in different EU/EEA states |
| **Applicable law** | Each company follows its national law |
| **Coordination** | Common merger terms |
| **Pre-merger certificate** | Required from each company's authority |

### 5.2 Luxembourg Pre-Merger Certificate

**Art. 1023-12 L1915**: Notary issues certificate confirming:
- Legal requirements complied with
- Shareholder approval obtained
- Creditor rights respected

### 5.3 Post-Merger

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Company law** | Governed by absorbing company's state |
| **Employee participation** | Special rules may apply |
| **Registration** | Both states coordinate |

---

## 6. Effects of Merger

### 6.1 Asset Transfer

**Art. 1021-11 L1915**: All assets and liabilities transfer:

| Transfer | Mechanism |
|----------|-----------|
| **Real estate** | By operation of law (registration required) |
| **Contracts** | Continue (subject to change of control clauses) |
| **Employment** | Transfer with acquired rights protected |
| **Permits** | Check specific regulatory rules |
| **Litigation** | Absorbing company substituted |

### 6.2 Shareholders

- Shareholders of absorbed company become shareholders of absorbing company
- Minority protections apply (appraisal rights limited in Luxembourg)

### 6.3 Creditors

- Claims against absorbed company enforceable against absorbing company
- Security interests continue over transferred assets
- Priority among creditors preserved

---

## 7. Nullity

### 7.1 Grounds

**Art. 1021-20 L1915**: Merger may be annulled only for:
- No shareholder resolution
- Resolution not notarized
- Violation of essential procedural requirements

### 7.2 Limitations

**Steichen**: Very limited nullity regime:
- Third-party rights protected
- Prescription: 6 months from publication
- Ratification possible

## Banking & Finance Application

### Lender Perspective

| Issue | Consideration |
|-------|---------------|
| **Creditworthiness** | Assess combined entity |
| **Covenants** | Merger may trigger change of control |
| **Security** | Verify security transfers to new entity |
| **Guarantees** | Check if guarantor survives |

### Due Diligence

| Check | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Merger documents** | Valid approval, proper procedure |
| **Creditor opposition** | Rights respected |
| **Material contracts** | Change of control clauses |
| **Employment issues** | Transfer liabilities |

### Financing Documentation

Consider:
- Change of control provisions
- Merger approval requirements
- Continuation of security
- Guarantor accession requirements

### Common Issues

1. **Change of control trigger**: Merger may constitute default
2. **Security refresh**: May need new registration
3. **Guarantor dissolution**: Guarantee may fall away
4. **Novation questions**: Continuing obligations
