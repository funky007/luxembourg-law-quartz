# Part 07 — Extra-contractual Liability

> **Poelmans Reference**: Chapter X (La responsabilité extra-contractuelle)  
> **Navigation**: [[🧭 Droit des obligations — Index|← Back to Index]]

---

## 1. General Framework

### 1.1 Foundation (arts. 1382-1383)

> **Art. 1382**: "Tout fait quelconque de l'homme, qui cause à autrui un dommage, oblige celui par la faute duquel il est arrivé, à le réparer."

> **Art. 1383**: "Chacun est responsable du dommage qu'il a causé non seulement par son fait, mais encore par sa négligence ou par son imprudence."

### 1.2 Three Conditions

```
RESPONSABILITÉ DÉLICTUELLE
        │
        ├─→ 1. FAUTE (fault)
        │
        ├─→ 2. DOMMAGE (damage)
        │
        └─→ 3. LIEN DE CAUSALITÉ (causation)
```

---

## 2. Faute (Fault)

### 2.1 Definition

Any act or omission that violates a pre-existing duty.

### 2.2 Types

| Type | Article | Standard |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Faute intentionnelle** | 1382 | Deliberate wrongdoing |
| **Faute de négligence** | 1383 | Failure to act with due care |
| **Faute légère** | — | Slight carelessness |
| **Faute lourde** | — | Gross negligence |

### 2.3 Standard: Bonus Pater Familias

Fault assessed against conduct of reasonable person in same circumstances.

---

## 3. Dommage (Damage)

### 3.1 Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Dommage matériel** | Pecuniary loss (property, wealth) |
| **Dommage moral** | Non-pecuniary (pain, suffering, reputation) |
| **Dommage corporel** | Physical injury |

### 3.2 Requirements

- **Certain** (not hypothetical)
- **Direct** (not too remote)
- **Personal** to claimant
- **Legitimate** interest

### 3.3 Préjudice Futur

Future damage recoverable if sufficiently certain.

---

## 4. Lien de Causalité (Causation)

### 4.1 Theory: Equivalence des Conditions

Luxembourg applies **equivalence des conditions** — all necessary conditions are equivalent causes.

### 4.2 Test

*But for* the fault, would the damage have occurred?

### 4.3 Concurrent Causes

Multiple faults can each be a cause. Liability is *in solidum* between wrongdoers.

---

## 5. Responsabilité du Fait des Choses (art. 1384 al. 1)

**See full note**: [[Responsabilité du fait des choses (art. 1384)]]

### 5.1 Principle

> **Art. 1384 al. 1**: "On est responsable non seulement du dommage que l'on cause par son propre fait, mais encore de celui qui est causé par le fait des personnes dont on doit répondre, ou des choses que l'on a sous sa garde."

### 5.2 Conditions

1. **Chose** — any corporeal thing (movable or immovable)
2. **Garde** — use, direction, and control of the thing
3. **Fait de la chose** — thing played active role in causing damage

### 5.3 Presumption

Presumption of fault against guardian (*gardien*). Can only escape by proving:
- *Force majeure*
- Fault of victim
- Fault of third party

---

## 6. Responsabilité du Commettant (art. 1384 al. 3)

### 6.1 Principle

Master (*commettant*) liable for damage caused by servant (*préposé*) acting within functions.

### 6.2 Conditions

1. **Lien de préposition** — authority/subordination relationship
2. **Faute du préposé** — servant committed fault
3. **Dans l'exercice des fonctions** — during or in connection with duties

### 6.3 Effect

Strict liability — commettant cannot escape by proving lack of own fault.

---

## 7. Troubles de Voisinage (art. 544)

### 7.1 Principle

Owner causing abnormal neighborhood disturbances must compensate, even without fault.

### 7.2 Test

Whether disturbance exceeds **normal inconveniences of neighborhood**.

### 7.3 Remedy

Usually compensation (not necessarily cessation of activity).

---

## 8. Non-Cumul des Responsabilités

**See full note**: [[Non-cumul des responsabilités]]

### 8.1 Principle

> **Fundamental rule**: Contractual and extra-contractual liability cannot be combined. If contract exists between parties, liability is necessarily contractual.

### 8.2 Practical Consequences

| Aspect | Contractual | Delictual |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| **Basis** | Contract breach | Art. 1382 et s. |
| **Prescription** | Per contract/law | 30 years (commercial: 10) |
| **Proof** | Depends on obligation type | Claimant proves fault |
| **Damages** | Foreseeable only (art. 1150) | All direct damage |
| **Astreinte** | Available | Available |

### 8.3 Exceptions

- **Concurrent victims**: Contractual party + third party
- **Criminal fault**: May allow delictual action
- **Separate relationships**: Different legal basis

---

## 9. Banking & Finance Application

### Bank Liability

- **To clients**: Contractual (account relationship)
- **To third parties**: Delictual (no contract)
- **Duty of care**: Varies by service (moyens vs. résultat)

### Professional Liability

Directors, auditors — may face both:
- Contractual (to company)
- Delictual (to third parties/shareholders)
