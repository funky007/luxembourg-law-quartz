# Third-Party Effectiveness (Opposabilité aux tiers)

> **Type**: Fundamental concept / Publicity requirement
> **Source**: Westendorf, T.1, §38-42, §146-148, §475
> **Key Articles**: Arts. 2074, 2076 C.civ.; Art. 5(6) loi 5 août 2005
> **Key Cases**: Trib. Lux. 16/02/2011, no 62/2011
> **Related**: [[Dépossession]], [[Droit préférentiel]], [[12 - Gage sur créances]]

---

## Definition

*Opposabilité aux tiers* (third-party effectiveness) is the quality that makes a security interest enforceable against persons other than the contracting parties. A pledge that is valid inter partes may nonetheless be ineffective against third parties until publicity requirements are satisfied.

**Westendorf, T.1, §38**:
> The distinction between validity (*validité*) and opposability (*opposabilité*) is fundamental in Luxembourg security law.

---

## Distinction: Validity vs. Opposability

**Trib. Luxembourg, 16 février 2011, n° 62/2011**:
> "L'article 2076 du Code civil relatif à la remise de la chose en gage entre les mains du créancier ou d'un tiers convenu concerne l'opposabilité, et non la validité, du gage."

| Validity (*Validité*) | Opposability (*Opposabilité*) |
|-----------------------|-----------------------------|
| Inter partes effect | Erga omnes effect |
| Consent + object sufficient | Publicity required |
| Creates rights against grantor | Creates rights against third parties |
| Pledge exists but limited | Pledge fully effective |

---

## Mechanisms of Opposability

### For Corporeal Movables

**Art. 2076 C.civ.**:
> "Dans tous les cas, le privilège ne subsiste sur le gage qu'autant que ce gage a été mis et est resté en la possession du créancier, ou d'un tiers convenu entre les parties."

**Mechanism**: Dispossession provides publicity by demonstrating the grantor's reduced rights over the asset.

### For Claims (*Créances*)

**Art. 2074, al. 2, C.civ.**:
> "Le gage sur les meubles incorporels [...] n'est opposable aux tiers que par la notification [...] ou par l'acceptation [...]"

**Mechanism**: Notification to or acceptance by the debtor of the pledged claim.

**Exception — CGF regime**: Under loi 5 août 2005, pledge over claims is opposable from contract conclusion (Art. 5(4)).

### For Financial Collateral

**Art. 5(6) loi 5 août 2005**:
> "Le rang des gages est déterminé par rapport à la date où ils ont été rendus opposables aux tiers."

**Mechanism**: Possession or control, evidenced by appropriate formalities for the asset type.

---

## Consequences of Non-Opposability

If the pledge is not opposable to third parties:

1. **Other creditors** may seize the asset as if unpledged
2. **Subsequent pledgees** may take priority
3. **Acquirers** may take free of the security
4. **Bankruptcy trustee** may ignore the pledge in distribution

**Westendorf, T.1, §40**:
> An inopposable pledge remains valid between the parties but is "sterilized" (*inopérant*) as against third parties.

---

## Timing and Priority

**Prior tempore, potior jure**: First in time, first in right.

**Westendorf, T.1, §475**:
Priority among pledges is determined by the date of third-party effectiveness, not by contract date.

**Practical implication**: Complete perfection formalities immediately upon signing.
