# Financial Collateral Regime (Loi du 5 août 2005)

> **Type**: Special statutory regime for securities / Real security
> **Source**: Westendorf, T.1, §401-539
> **Key Legislation**: Loi du 5 août 2005 sur les contrats de garantie financière
> **Key Cases**: C.J.U.E., 10/11/2016, C-156/15; Trib. Lux. 20/05/2010
> **Related**: [[13 - Contrat de garantie financière]], [[Dépossession]], [[Appropriation (pacte commissoire)]]

---

## Definition

The **loi du 5 août 2005** implements Directive 2002/47/CE and establishes a special regime for pledges (*gages*) and title transfer arrangements over financial instruments and claims. It derogates from civil code formalities to enable rapid, efficient enforcement of financial collateral.

**Westendorf, T.1, §401**:
> The law's purpose is to provide market participants with reliable security mechanisms, particularly protection against counterparty default risks in financial transactions.

---

## Scope

### Personal Scope

No restrictions — any person (natural or legal, regulated or unregulated) may be grantor or beneficiary.

### Material Scope

**Assets (*avoirs*)** — Art. 1(4):
- Financial instruments (*instruments financiers*)
- Claims for money (*créances*)

**Secured obligations (*obligations financières couvertes*)** — Art. 1(3):
- Present, future, actual, conditional, or potential obligations
- Obligations giving rise to payment or delivery
- Obligations under master agreements (ISDA, GMRA, GMSLA)

---

## Key Features

### 1. Simplified Formation

**Art. 5**: Pledge is constituted by delivery, transfer, holding, registration, or any treatment giving the pledgee **possession or control**.

**For claims** (since 2011): Constitution occurs by contract conclusion alone — notification not required.

### 2. Self-Help Enforcement

**Art. 11**: Upon a triggering event, the pledgee may enforce without:
- Court authorization
- Prior demand (*mise en demeure*)
- Public auction

**Enforcement modes**:
- Appropriation (*pacte commissoire*)
- Private sale (at "normal commercial terms")
- Public auction
- Set-off (*compensation*)

### 3. Insolvency Protection

**Art. 20(4)**: Bankruptcy rules do not prevent enforcement. The pledge is immune from:
- Suspect period nullities
- Fraudulent preference actions
- Insolvency moratorium

### 4. Right of Utilization (*Droit d'utilisation*)

**Art. 10**: Parties may agree the pledgee can dispose of the collateral "as if owner" — including alienation and rehypothecation.

---

## Relationship to Droit Commun

**Westendorf, T.1, §408**:
> The loi de 2005 does not wholly replace the civil code regime; civil code rules apply residually where the law does not expressly derogate.

**Trib. Luxembourg, 29 janvier 2014**:
> "La loi modifiée de 2005 [...] ne constitue qu'une application spéciale des principes généraux édictés par les articles [...] du Code civil à la matière des contrats de garantie financière."
