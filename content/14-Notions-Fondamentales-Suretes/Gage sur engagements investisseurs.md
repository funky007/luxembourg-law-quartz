# Gage sur engagements investisseurs

> **Type**: Notion fondamentale
> **Source**: I. Dobric, "La mise en gage des engagements des investisseurs des fonds d'investissements à la lumière de la loi du 5 août 2005", JTL juin 2017, pp. 176-190
> **Key Legislation**: Loi 5 août 2005; L1915, Arts. 310-1, 320-1

---

## Definition

The pledge over investor commitments (*gage sur engagements des investisseurs*) is a security interest granted by investment funds to lenders, covering the fund's right to call capital from its investors under subscription agreements.

> "Les engagements des investisseurs vis-à-vis des fonds d'investissement forment le point essentiel des garanties fournies aux créanciers."

**Source**: I. Dobric, "La mise en gage des engagements des investisseurs", JTL 2017, p. 176

---

## 1. Context: Fund Financing

### Types of Fund Facilities

| Facility Type | Purpose | Security |
|---------------|---------|----------|
| **Subscription facility** (equity bridge) | Bridge financing pending capital calls | Uncalled commitments |
| **Leverage facility** | Investment leverage beyond committed capital | Various fund assets |
| **NAV facility** | Working capital based on net asset value | Portfolio assets |

### Why Commitment Pledges Matter

Investor commitments represent the primary source of repayment for subscription facilities:
- Lenders can demand or exercise capital calls directly
- Proceeds flow to repayment of bank credit
- Security over commitments enables rapid enforcement

**Source**: I. Dobric, op. cit., p. 176

---

## 2. Nature of the Obligation

### Payment Obligation vs. Obligation de Faire

The characterization of the investor's obligation is critical:

| Characterization | Features | Implications |
|------------------|----------|--------------|
| **Payment obligation** | Simple payment of money; no additional formalities | Can be enforced directly; falls within Loi 2005 scope |
| **Obligation de faire** | Requires investor to perform acts (sign documents, subscribe new units) | Traditional view: only resolves in damages (Art. 1142 C.civ.) |

### Modern Practice: SCS/SCSp Capital Accounts

Following the 2013 reforms, most SCS and SCSp funds use **capital account** structures:
- Investor receives parts d'intérêts at initial subscription
- Subsequent capital calls credited to capital account
- **No additional formalities required** for drawdowns
- This renders the obligation a **pure payment obligation**

**Source**: I. Dobric, op. cit., p. 177

### Drafting Consideration

> The contrat social and subscription documents should clearly characterize capital calls as requiring only payment, not additional formalities.

---

## 3. Applicability of Loi 2005

### Créances as Avoirs

**Art. 1 Loi 2005** defines "avoirs" to include "créances" (receivables).

Investor commitments constitute créances:
- Right to payment of specified amounts
- Arising from subscription agreements
- Subject to drawdown/capital call mechanics

### Debate on Non-Monetary Créances

Some debate exists whether non-monetary obligations (obligations de faire) qualify:

**Broad view**: Loi 2005 covers all créances per travaux préparatoires
**Narrow view**: Only monetary créances qualify

**Practical resolution**: Structure commitments as payment obligations to avoid controversy.

**Source**: I. Dobric, op. cit., p. 177; M. Lattard, "Typologie des créances pouvant être gagées sous un contrat de gage soumis à la loi du 5 août 2005", ACE 4/2015, p. 3

## 4. Pledged Rights

### Principal Rights

The pledge covers:
- Right to receive payment of committed amounts
- Right to capital calls already made but unpaid
- Right to future capital calls (up to commitment limit)

### Accessory Rights (Droits Accessoires)

**Art. 5 Loi 2005**: The pledge extends to accessory rights.

| Accessory Right | Description |
|-----------------|-------------|
| **Capital call right** | Right to demand payment from investors |
| **Enforcement right** | Right to pursue investors for non-payment |
| **Interest rights** | Default interest on late payments |
| **Information rights** | Access to commitment/drawdown records |

### The Critical Question: Who May Exercise Capital Calls?

Upon enforcement, the pledgee (lender) may:
1. Direct the fund to make capital calls
2. Exercise capital call rights directly (if assignment permits)
3. Collect proceeds into a pledged account

**Source**: I. Dobric, op. cit., p. 176

---

## 5. Perfection and Opposability

### Perfection Under Loi 2005

**Art. 5(3) Loi 2005**: Pledge over créances perfected by:
- Written agreement describing the créance, OR
- Entry in a register or account

### Notification to Investors

While not required for validity, notification to investors is recommended:
- Confirms investors' acknowledgment
- Establishes direct relationship with pledgee
- Facilitates enforcement

### Typical Documentation

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Pledge agreement** | Creates security interest |
| **Investor acknowledgment** | Confirms notice and waives defenses |
| **Account pledge** | Security over collection account |
| **Side letter** | Additional investor undertakings |

---

## 6. Enforcement Mechanics

### Step 1: Triggering Event

Upon occurrence of enforcement event under credit documentation:
- Lender accelerates facility
- Enforcement rights under pledge become exercisable

### Step 2: Capital Call Exercise

Pledgee exercises capital call rights:
- Direct call on investors, OR
- Instruction to fund to make calls

### Step 3: Collection

Investor payments flow to:
- Pledged account (if established)
- Applied to repay facility

### Step 4: Excess (Soulte)

Any excess after debt repayment:
- Returned to fund
- For benefit of other fund creditors/investors

## 7. Investor Defenses and Waivers

### Potential Investor Defenses

| Defense | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Compensation** | Set-off against amounts owed by fund to investor |
| **Prescription** | Time-barring of capital call right |
| **Excuses** | Non-performance excuses (force majeure, etc.) |
| **Conditions precedent** | Capital call conditions not satisfied |

### Waiver Under Loi 2005

**Art. 2(5) Loi 2005** permits investors to:
- Waive defenses and compensation rights
- Accept direct payment obligations to pledgee

**Typical waiver language in investor acknowledgments**:
- Irrevocable and unconditional payment obligation
- No set-off or counterclaim
- No defense based on fund's breach

**Source**: I. Dobric, op. cit., pp. 178-180

---

## 8. Future and Conditional Créances

### Future Commitments

**Art. 5 Loi 2005** expressly covers future créances:
- Commitments not yet called
- Future investors (if documented)

### Conditional Créances

Capital calls are conditional on:
- Fund's decision to call
- Investment opportunities
- Contractual prerequisites

The conditional nature does not preclude pledging:
- Pledge attaches upon satisfaction of conditions
- Enforcement depends on actual capital call or pledgee's right to call

**Source**: I. Dobric, op. cit., p. 179

---

## 9. Conflict of Laws (Rome I)

### Applicable Law

**Rome I Regulation (EC 593/2008), Art. 14(2)**:
- Law governing the assigned claim determines its assignability
- And conditions for opposability to debtor

### Practical Implication

For Luxembourg law commitments:
- Luxembourg law governs pledgeability
- Luxembourg law determines investor's obligations
- Foreign investors subject to Luxembourg law analysis of the commitment

**Source**: I. Dobric, op. cit., pp. 180-182

## Banking & Finance Application

### Due Diligence Checklist

- [ ] Review fund documentation (LPA, subscription agreements)
- [ ] Confirm commitments are payment obligations (not obligations de faire)
- [ ] Verify capital account structure (SCS/SCSp)
- [ ] Check remaining uncalled amounts
- [ ] Review any existing pledges or encumbrances
- [ ] Confirm no restrictions on pledge in fund docs

### Documentation Checklist

- [ ] Pledge agreement covering all investor commitments
- [ ] Investor acknowledgments with defense waivers
- [ ] Account pledge over collection account
- [ ] Notices to investors
- [ ] Step-in rights for capital call exercise

### Key Provisions to Negotiate

| Provision | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Direct call right** | Pledgee can call capital without fund's cooperation |
| **Investor waiver** | Investors waive defenses and set-off |
| **Minimum commitment ratio** | Coverage test for uncalled vs. outstanding debt |
| **Reporting** | Ongoing disclosure of commitment status |
| **Substitution** | Treatment of defaulting investors |

### Risk Factors

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Investor default** | Diversified investor base; default provisions |
| **Capital account structure** | Confirm payment obligation characterization |
| **Foreign investors** | Rome I analysis; investor acknowledgments |
| **Fund-level restrictions** | Negotiate carve-outs in fund docs |
