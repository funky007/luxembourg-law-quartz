# Right of Preference (Droit préférentiel / Droit de préférence)

> **Type**: Fundamental characteristic of real securities
> **Source**: Westendorf, T.1, §30 (note 83); T.3, §13-14
> **Key Articles**: Arts. 2073, 2102 C.civ.
> **Related**: [[10 - Principes généraux des sûretés]], [[Droit de suite]]

---

## Definition

The right of preference (*droit de préférence* or *droit préférentiel*) is the entitlement of a secured creditor to be paid from the proceeds of the charged asset **before** other creditors. It breaks the equality (*égalité*) between creditors that would otherwise apply under Art. 2093 C.civ.

**Westendorf, T.3, §14**:
> "Les sûretés réelles traditionnelles : il s'agit d'un simple droit de préférence sur un ou plusieurs biens. Ce droit de préférence permet au créancier de faire vendre le ou les biens grevés et d'être payé sur le prix de vente avant les créanciers ne bénéficiant pas d'un privilège."

*Translation*: "Traditional real securities: this is a simple right of preference over one or more assets. This right of preference allows the creditor to have the charged asset(s) sold and to be paid from the sale proceeds before creditors not benefiting from a privilege."

---

## Legal Basis

> **Art. 2073 C.civ.**: "Le gage confère au créancier le droit de se faire payer sur la chose qui en est l'objet, par privilège et préférence aux autres créanciers."
>
> *Translation*: "The pledge confers on the creditor the right to be paid from the thing which is its subject matter, by privilege and preference over other creditors."

> **Art. 2102, 2° C.civ.**: Lists among privileged claims "la créance sur le gage dont le créancier est saisi" (the claim on the pledge of which the creditor is seized).

**Westendorf, T.1, §30 (note 83)**:
> "Le seul privilège qui n'est pas attaché par la loi à la qualité de la créance, mais qui peut être attaché par la convention des parties à toutes créances."

*Translation*: "The only privilege not attached by law to the quality of the claim, but which can be attached by agreement of the parties to any claim."

---

## Nature of the Right

### Characteristic of Real Securities

The *droit de préférence* is the **defining characteristic** of traditional real securities (*sûretés réelles traditionnelles*). It distinguishes them from:

1. **Chirographic claims** — No preference; pari passu distribution
2. **Property-based securities** (*propriétés-sûretés*) — Creditor owns the asset, no need for preference

**Luxembourg jurisprudence**: Trib. Luxembourg, 6 avril 1990, n° 162/90:
> "La sûreté que constitue le gage n'est pas à proprement parler un privilège, puisqu'il ne s'attache pas à la qualité de la créance, mais résulte d'une convention qui peut être conclue pour la sûreté de n'importe quelle créance. Néanmoins le gage est techniquement traité par la loi comme un privilège."

*Translation*: "The security constituted by the pledge is not strictly speaking a privilege, since it is not attached to the quality of the claim, but results from an agreement which can be concluded for the security of any claim. Nevertheless, the pledge is technically treated by the law as a privilege."

---

## Conditions for Exercise

### For Pledge (Gage)

The right of preference only subsists if **dispossession** is maintained:

> **Art. 2076 C.civ.**: "Dans tous les cas, le privilège ne subsiste sur le gage qu'autant que ce gage a été mis et est resté en la possession du créancier, ou d'un tiers convenu entre les parties."

**Key principle**: Loss of possession = loss of preference (subject to exceptions for financial collateral).

### Ranking

When multiple secured creditors exist, preference is determined by:

1. **Prior tempore, potior jure** — First in time, first in right (for same type of security)
2. **Type of security** — Certain privileges rank above pledges
3. **Special statutory provisions** — E.g., employee wages, tax claims

---

## Scope of the Preference

### What is Covered

The right of preference covers:
- Principal amount of secured claim
- Interest (unless otherwise agreed)
- Enforcement costs
- Accessories of the claim (e.g., penalty clauses)

**Westendorf, T.1, §61 (note 188)**:
> "En l'absence de stipulation contraire des parties, le gage garantit aussi les accessoires de la créance, comme les intérêts, les coûts de réalisation, voire la clause pénale assortissant la créance garantie."

### Limitation: No Enrichment

The preference cannot exceed the secured claim. A security is never a source of profit beyond securing payment. (Westendorf, T.3, §5)

---

## Distinction: Preference vs. Exclusivity

| Droit de préférence | Propriété-sûreté |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Creditor has priority on proceeds | Creditor owns the asset |
| Subject to ranking with other secured creditors | Asset outside debtor's patrimony |
| Realized through sale and distribution | Realized through appropriation/retention |
| Residual value returns to debtor/other creditors | Creditor keeps asset (subject to return of surplus) |

**Westendorf, T.3, §14**: Property-based securities (*propriétés-sûretés*) provide an **exclusive** right rather than merely a preference — the asset belongs to the creditor and is not available to other creditors at all.

---

## Effect in Insolvency

In the debtor's bankruptcy (*faillite*):

1. **Secured creditors** exercise preference on their charged assets
2. **Unsecured creditors** share proportionally in remaining assets
3. **Super-priority claims** (employees, taxes) may rank above conventional security

The preference protects the secured creditor from the collective proceedings that bind chirographic creditors.

---

> **Common Law Comparison**
>
> English law distinguishes:
> - **Fixed charges**: Priority over the charged asset; similar to droit de préférence
> - **Floating charges**: Crystallize on enforcement; rank after fixed charges and preferential creditors
>
> Under the Enterprise Act 2002, a "prescribed part" of floating charge proceeds is reserved for unsecured creditors.
>
> UCC Article 9 determines priority primarily by **order of filing** (first-to-file rule), not possession or attachment.

## Banking Application

### Structuring Security Packages

When taking multiple forms of security:

1. **Maximize preference**: Ensure proper perfection to preserve ranking
2. **Consider competing claims**: Tax authorities, employees have statutory preferences
3. **Monitor possession**: For pledges, loss of possession destroys preference
4. **Financial collateral**: Loi 5 août 2005 provides enhanced enforcement rights

### Enforcement Considerations

The secured creditor with preference can:
- Sell the asset and be paid first from proceeds
- In financial collateral: appropriate the asset directly (pacte commissoire)
- Assert priority in bankruptcy distribution
