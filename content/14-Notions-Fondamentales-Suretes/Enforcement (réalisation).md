# Enforcement / Réalisation (Realization of Security)

> **Concept**: The methods by which a secured creditor converts collateral into payment
> **Source**: Westendorf, T.1, §§350-400, 560-590, 760-790
> **Key Legislation**: Loi 5 août 2005, Arts. 8-11 (financial collateral); Arts. 2078-2082 C.civ. (civil pledge)
> **Critical for**: Facility documentation, security enforcement, workout scenarios

---

## Definition

*Réalisation* (realization) is the process by which a secured creditor converts security assets into monetary value to satisfy the secured claim upon debtor default.

**Westendorf, T.1, §350**:
> "La réalisation du gage constitue la phase ultime de la vie de la sûreté, permettant au créancier d'obtenir paiement sur la valeur du bien affecté en garantie."

*Translation*: Realization of the pledge constitutes the ultimate phase of the security's life, permitting the creditor to obtain payment from the value of the asset dedicated as security.

## Enforcement Methods Overview

| Method | Applicable To | Court Involvement | Speed |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|-------|
| **Judicial sale** (*vente judiciaire*) | Civil pledge (default) | Required | Slow |
| **Appropriation** (*attribution*) | Financial collateral | None | Fast |
| **Private sale** | Financial collateral | None | Fast |
| **Set-off/Close-out netting** | Financial collateral | None | Immediate |
| **Direct collection** | Pledge over receivables | None | Fast |
| **Retention of ownership** | ROT, fiduciary, assignment | None | Immediate |

## Civil Pledge (*Gage civil*)

### A. Judicial Sale (Default Rule)

**Art. 2078 C.civ.**:
> "Le créancier ne peut, à défaut de paiement, disposer du gage; sauf à lui à faire ordonner en justice que le gage lui demeurera en paiement [...] ou que ce gage sera vendu aux enchères."

*Translation*: The creditor may not, absent payment, dispose of the pledge; save for having ordered by the court that the pledge shall remain to him in payment [...] or that the pledge shall be sold at auction.

**Procedure**:
1. Default by debtor
2. Application to court
3. Court-ordered auction or attribution
4. Distribution of proceeds

### B. Prohibition of *Pacte Commissoire*

**Art. 2078, al. 2 C.civ.**: Agreements permitting the creditor to appropriate the pledge without judicial control are **void** (*nul*) under traditional civil law.

**Trib. Luxembourg, 16 février 2011, n° 62/2011**:
> The prohibition protects the debtor from creditor abuse at the moment of vulnerability.

## Financial Collateral (*Garantie financière*)

### Derogation from Civil Code

**Loi 5 août 2005** fundamentally changes enforcement for financial collateral, permitting:

### A. Appropriation (*Attribution*)

**Art. 11(1) Loi 2005**:
> "[L]e preneur de garantie est en droit de réaliser [...] par voie d'appropriation [...] pourvu que cette faculté ait été convenue entre les parties."

*Translation*: The collateral taker is entitled to realize by way of appropriation, provided this option was agreed between the parties.

**Requirements**:
- Contractual agreement (*convention*)
- Evaluation at commercially reasonable terms
- Account for any surplus (*soulte*)

**Trib. Luxembourg, 7 décembre 2016, n° 161.169**:
> Appropriation under Loi 2005 is valid where parties agreed and evaluation is proper.

### B. Private Sale

**Art. 11(1) Loi 2005** also permits:
- Sale on recognized market
- Sale at prevailing market price
- Sale in commercially reasonable manner

**No court order required** - fundamental facilitation for banking practice.

### C. Close-out Netting

**Art. 11(2) Loi 2005**:
Financial collateral arrangements may provide for close-out netting, permitting immediate set-off of mutual obligations upon default.

### D. Proceeds Application

**Art. 11(1) in fine Loi 2005**:
> "[L]e preneur de garantie impute la valeur de réalisation [...] sur le montant des obligations financières couvertes. Le cas échéant, la soulte est versée au constituant de garantie."

*Translation*: The collateral taker applies the realization value to the covered financial obligations. Where applicable, the surplus is paid to the collateral provider.

## Property-Based Securities

### A. Retention of Title (*Réserve de propriété*)

**No "enforcement" required** - seller never transferred ownership.

**Art. 567(3) C.com.**: Upon buyer default/insolvency, seller may reclaim goods.

**Procedure**: Claim (*revendication*) filed with liquidator within relevant period.

### B. Fiduciary Security (*Fiducie-sûreté*)

**Art. 16(5) Loi 5 août 2005** (by reference):
The beneficiary may realize per agreement terms, similar to financial collateral.

**Key feature**: Assets held in separate patrimony, so no competition with other creditors.

### C. Assignment as Security (*Cession à titre de garantie*)

**Art. 14(3) Loi 5 août 2005**:
> "En cas d'inexécution [...] le cessionnaire est libéré de son obligation de retransfert."

*Translation*: In case of non-performance, the assignee is released from the retransfer obligation.

**Effect**: Creditor is already owner - simply retains ownership definitively. No formal enforcement procedure needed.

## Enforcement Triggers

### A. Events of Default

Typical enforcement triggers in facility documentation:
- Non-payment when due
- Breach of financial covenant
- Breach of representation
- Cross-default
- Insolvency event
- Material adverse change

### B. Acceleration

Before enforcing security, the creditor typically must:
1. Declare event of default
2. Accelerate the facility (make all amounts immediately due)
3. Issue enforcement notice

**Grace periods** may apply per facility terms.

---

## Insolvency Considerations

### A. Stay on Enforcement

**General rule**: Opening of bankruptcy does NOT prevent enforcement of:
- Possessory pledge (creditor already holds)
- Financial collateral (Art. 20 Loi 2005)
- Retention of title (ownership claim)

### B. Suspect Period (*Période suspecte*)

**Art. 445 C.com.**: Certain transactions in the suspect period before bankruptcy may be avoided.

**Carve-out** - Art. 20(1) Loi 2005:
> Financial collateral arrangements are protected from avoidance rules if concluded before insolvency.

## Banking Application

### Documentation Requirements

**Enforcement clause** (financial collateral):
> "Upon the occurrence of an Enforcement Event, the Security Agent shall be entitled, without prior notice or court order, to:
> (a) appropriate all or part of the Collateral;
> (b) sell all or part of the Collateral in any manner commercially reasonable; and
> (c) apply the proceeds in accordance with the Waterfall set out in Clause [X]."

### Evaluation Methodology

**Art. 11(3) Loi 2005**: Evaluation must be conducted in a "commercially reasonable manner."

**Best practice**:
- Reference to market price if traded
- Independent valuation if not traded
- Pre-agreed methodology in security document

### Notification to Debtor

**For receivables**: Notification to assigned debtor before collection recommended to prevent valid discharge to assignor.

### Due Diligence Points

1. **Verify enforcement rights** - are appropriation/private sale agreed?
2. **Check evaluation provisions** - is methodology clear?
3. **Confirm no restrictions** - intercreditor standstills, regulatory constraints?
4. **Review insolvency protection** - does Art. 20 Loi 2005 apply?
5. **Practical matters** - access to collateral, account control?
