# Cession de créance à titre de garantie (Assignment of Receivables as Security)

> **Type**: Property-based security / Ownership transfer as guarantee
> **Source**: Westendorf, T.1, §§1100-1145
> **Key Legislation**: Loi du 5 août 2005 sur les contrats de garantie financière (Art. 13-14); Loi du 21 décembre 1994
> **Nature**: TRUE sûreté - transfer of ownership of receivables to creditor

---

## Definition and Legal Framework (§1100)

The *cession de créance à titre de garantie* is a security mechanism whereby the debtor (or third party) transfers ownership of receivables to the creditor as security for an obligation. Unlike a pledge over receivables (*gage sur créances*), this involves actual transfer of ownership, not merely a security interest.

**Westendorf, T.1, §1100**:
> "Dans le domaine du droit financier, le transfert de propriété à titre de garantie était, à l'origine, pratiqué au Luxembourg par le recours à la fiducie."

*Translation*: In the field of financial law, the transfer of ownership as security was originally practiced in Luxembourg through the fiduciary mechanism.

### Dual Legal Basis

The assignment of receivables as security in Luxembourg is governed by two principal texts that "do not overlap" (*ne se recoupent pas*):

| Legislation | Scope | Key Feature |
|-------------|-------|-------------|
| **Loi 5 août 2005** | Financial collateral | Broad scope, simplified formalities |
| **Loi 27 juillet 2003** | Fiduciary contracts | Creates separate patrimony (*patrimoine d'affectation*) |

**Westendorf, T.1, §1100** (citing Ph. Hoss et P. Santer):
> "Les mécanismes résultant de ces deux lois présentent certaines différences, de telle manière que cette sûreté est régie, en droit luxembourgeois, par deux textes qui 'ne se recoupent pas'."

## Distinction from Pledge over Receivables

| Feature | Cession (Assignment) | Gage sur créances (Pledge) |
|---------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| Ownership | Transfers to creditor | Remains with debtor |
| Requalification risk | Protected by Art. 13 Loi 2005 | N/A |
| Derogation from Art. 1690 C.civ. | Yes | Yes |
| Enforcement | No procedure needed - creditor is owner | Appropriation or sale |
| Surplus | Must return (*soulte*) | Must account |

**Key Advantage**: The creditor becomes owner and need not follow enforcement procedures - ownership is already transferred. On default, the creditor is simply released from the obligation to retransfer (§1139).

## Formation (§§1105-1117)

### A. Personal Scope (§1105)

**Broad Application**: No requirement that either party be a financial professional.

**Art. 13, al. 1 Loi 2005**:
> "La présente loi s'applique aux opérations de transfert de propriété à titre de garantie d'avoirs, y compris par voie fiduciaire."

*Translation*: This law applies to operations of transfer of ownership as security over assets, including by fiduciary means.

**Restriction for Retail Clients** (§1101): Since 2018, credit institutions and investment firms are prohibited from concluding title transfer security arrangements with retail clients (*clients de détail*) under penalty of nullity (Art. 13, al. 4 Loi 2005).

### B. Object: Receivables (*Créances*)

The assignment may cover:
- Present receivables (*créances présentes*)
- Future receivables (*créances futures*) - without need to specify them individually
- Trade receivables, loan receivables, account balances

**Art. 13, al. 2 Loi 2005** (§1107):
> "[L]es opérations visées [...] sont celles qui consistent dans le transfert de la propriété d'avoirs appartenant ou venant à appartenir au cédant, sans qu'il soit besoin de les spécifier."

### C. No Dispossession Required (§1110)

**Westendorf, T.1, §1110**:
> "La dépossession effective n'est pas une condition de validité du transfert de propriété à titre de garantie."

*Translation*: Effective dispossession is not a validity condition for the transfer of ownership as security.

**Constitut possessoire** recognized: The receivables may remain on the assignor's books if designated as the assignee's property (§1110).

### D. No Special Formalities (§1109)

General contract law applies. No requirement for:
- Notarization
- Registration
- Special form

## Proof (§§1111-1117)

### Commercial Act Presumption (§1112)

**Art. 2(1) Loi 2005**: Financial collateral contracts are deemed commercial acts (*actes de commerce*) regardless of party status.

**Consequence**: Free proof under Art. 109 Code de commerce - no written requirement for amounts over €2,500.

### Written Attestation of Constitution (§1114)

**Art. 2(2) Loi 2005**:
> "La constitution de la garantie financière doit pouvoir être attestée par écrit."

*Translation*: The constitution of the financial collateral must be capable of attestation in writing.

**Purpose**: Identification of assets and traceability in insolvency (§1115).

**For Receivables** (§1117):
> "L'inscription sur une liste de créances transmise au preneur de garantie par écrit [...] suffit pour identifier les créances et pour prouver la constitution de la garantie financière sur ces créances à l'égard du débiteur et des tiers."

*Translation*: Entry on a list of receivables transmitted to the collateral taker in writing suffices to identify the receivables and prove the constitution of the financial collateral against the debtor and third parties.

## Opposability to Third Parties (§§1118-1120)

### Derogation from Art. 1690 C.civ. (§1119)

**Art. 14(2), al. 2 Loi 2005**:
For receivables, the transfer takes effect between the parties and becomes opposable to third parties **upon agreement** (*dès l'accord des parties*).

**Major Derogation**: No notification to debtor or acceptance required for third-party effectiveness. This derogates from Art. 1690 C.civ.

### Position of Assigned Debtor (§1120)

**Art. 14(2) in fine Loi 2005**:
> "Le débiteur se libère valablement entre les mains du cédant tant qu'il n'a pas connaissance du transfert de sa dette au cessionnaire."

*Translation*: The debtor validly discharges into the hands of the assignor until knowledge of the transfer to the assignee.

**Application of Art. 1691 C.civ.**: Notification remains important to prevent valid discharge to the assignor.

### Set-off Rights of Assigned Debtor

**Art. 1295 C.civ. applies** (§1120): The assigned debtor may invoke set-off against the assignee for claims against the assignor that arose before opposability and become due before or simultaneously with the assigned claim.

## Effects Before Maturity (§§1122-1130)

### A. Transfer of Ownership (§1123)

**Full ownership transfer**: The assignee becomes full owner, not merely holder of a security interest.

**Solo consensu principle** applies for receivables (§1123): Transfer occurs by mere agreement, subject to Art. 14(2) for book-entry instruments.

### B. Rights of the Assignee (§1125)

The assignee:
- May dispose of the receivables
- May re-pledge or re-assign them
- Exercises all rights attached to the receivables
- Receives accessories of the claim (Art. 1692 C.civ.)

**Trib. Luxembourg, 12 juillet 2013, n° 1463/2013** (§1125):
The assignment of a receivable carries assignment of its accessories including suretyship (*cautionnement*).

### C. Contractual Restrictions (§1128)

**Art. 14(1) Loi 2005**: Parties may restrict the assignee's exercise of ownership rights.

**Westendorf, T.1, §1128**: Such restrictions "n'affectent pas la nature du droit de propriété conféré au cessionnaire" - they do not affect the nature of the ownership right conferred.

**No requalification risk**: Even with severe restrictions, the arrangement cannot be requalified as a pledge.

### D. Accessory Character (§1131)

The assignment is **structurally accessory** (*structurellement accessoire*):
- Extinction of secured claim → disappearance of security
- Transfer of secured claim → transfer of security

---

## Effects on Default (§§1139-1144)

### A. Release from Retransfer Obligation (§1139)

**Art. 14(3) Loi 2005**:
> "En cas d'inexécution totale ou partielle des obligations financières couvertes, le cessionnaire est libéré de son obligation de retransfert à concurrence des obligations financières couvertes [...] et, sauf convention contraire, sans mise en demeure préalable."

*Translation*: In case of total or partial non-performance of covered financial obligations, the assignee is released from the retransfer obligation to the extent of the covered financial obligations, without prior notice unless otherwise agreed.

### B. No Formal Realization Procedure (§1140)

**Key distinction from pledge**: The creditor is already owner. There is no appropriation or sale procedure - the ownership-security simply becomes definitive ownership to the extent of the debt.

### C. Mandatory Evaluation (§1142)

**Westendorf, T.1, §1142**:
> "La constitution d'une sûreté n'ayant jamais pour finalité de produire un enrichissement du créancier, il est évidemment nécessaire de prévoir une évaluation."

*Translation*: As security can never have as its purpose enrichment of the creditor, evaluation is obviously necessary.

**Soulte obligation**: If receivables value exceeds debt, creditor must pay difference to debtor.

### D. Compensation Mechanism (§1144)

**Art. 14(2) Loi 2005**: Compensation between the creditor's claim and the value of transferred assets occurs per agreed modalities.

---

## Insolvency Protection (§§1129-1130)

### Protection of Retransfer Right (§1129)

**Risk**: As full owner, the assignee's creditors could seize the transferred receivables.

**Art. 20(4) Loi 2005** provides remedy:
Bankruptcy rules and seizure rules "ne font pas obstacle [...] à l'exécution par les parties de leurs obligations notamment de retransfert et de rétrocession."

*Translation*: Do not prevent the parties from performing their obligations, notably of retransfer and retrocession.

### Fiduciary Enhancement (§§1175-1192)

**Art. 14(4) Loi 2005**: Transfer may be effected "par voie fiduciaire" (by fiduciary means).

**Benefit**: Receivables enter a separate fiduciary patrimony (*patrimoine fiduciaire*), protected from:
- Creditors of the fiduciary
- Creditors of the assignor/fiduciant
- Bankruptcy of the fiduciary

## Common Law Comparison

| Feature | Cession (Lux.) | English Law Absolute Assignment | UCC Art. 9 (USA) |
|---------|---------------|--------------------------------|------------------|
| Nature | True ownership transfer | True ownership transfer | Security interest |
| Filing/registration | None required | Notice to debtor (s. 136 LPA 1925) | UCC-1 filing |
| Requalification risk | Excluded by statute | Equity may recharacterize | N/A |
| Future receivables | Covered automatically | May require novation | Covered |
| Notification | Not required for perfection | Required for legal assignment | Not required |
| Set-off protection | Art. 1295 C.civ. | Equities available | §9-404 |

### vs. English Law Security Assignment

| Feature | Luxembourg Cession | English Security Assignment |
|---------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Ownership | Full transfer | Full transfer (but equity of redemption) |
| Documentation | Simple writing | Usually deed |
| Notice | Optional (but recommended) | Required for legal assignment |
| Bankruptcy of assignor | Protected (Art. 20) | Protected if perfected |
| Recharacterization | Statutorily excluded | Risk exists |

## Banking Application

### Receivables Financing

The assignment of receivables as security is fundamental to:
- **Factoring arrangements**: Trade receivables assigned to factor
- **Securitization**: Receivables portfolios transferred to SPVs
- **Loan facilities**: Account receivables as collateral

### Due Diligence Checklist

1. **Verify assignor is owner** of receivables
2. **Check for existing assignments** - first in time prevails
3. **Review receivables contracts** for assignment restrictions
4. **Consider notification** to assigned debtors (for set-off protection)
5. **Draft clear evaluation methodology** for enforcement
6. **Consider fiduciary structure** for enhanced protection
7. **Exclude retail clients** if assignee is credit institution

### Security Document Considerations

**Identification clause**:
> "Le Cédant cède et transfère au Cessionnaire, à titre de garantie au sens de l'article 13 de la loi du 5 août 2005, la pleine propriété de toutes les créances présentes et futures résultant de [description]."

**Retransfer clause**:
> "À l'extinction intégrale et définitive des Obligations Garanties, le Cessionnaire retransférera au Cédant ou à son ordre la propriété des Créances Cédées non réalisées."

**Notification provision** (recommended):
> "Le Cédant autorise le Cessionnaire à notifier à tout moment aux Débiteurs Cédés le transfert des Créances Cédées."

### Intercreditor Issues

When multiple creditors take security over the same debtor's receivables:
- **Priority**: First assignment in time prevails (no registration)
- **Subordination agreements** commonly used
- **Security agent** may hold as fiduciary for syndicate
