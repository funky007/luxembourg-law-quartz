# SCSp — Fund Structures (AIFM/SICAR/FIS)

> **Type**: Main Part
> **Sources**: G. Dusemon & C. Pogorzelski, Larcier 2013; D. Boone & A. Trotska, JurisNews 2013; C. Duro, ACE 2013
> **Key Legislation**: L1915; Loi AIFM; Loi SICAR; Loi FIS

---

## Definition

The law of 12 July 2013 (Loi AIFM) simultaneously:
1. Transposed the AIFM Directive (2011/61/EU)
2. Modernized the SCS regime
3. Created the SCSp
4. Updated SICAR and FIS sectoral laws

This positioned Luxembourg to compete with Anglo-Saxon limited partnership jurisdictions (Jersey, Guernsey, Cayman, Delaware).

**Source**: G. Dusemon & C. Pogorzelski, "Les commandites dans le contexte des fonds d'investissement et gestionnaires réglementés", *Les commandites en droit Luxembourgeois* (Larcier 2013), p. 79-80

---

## 1. SCS vs SCSp Choice for Funds

### Key Distinguishing Features

| Feature | SCS | SCSp |
|---------|-----|------|
| **Legal personality** | Yes | No |
| **Tax transparency** | Case-by-case | Generally transparent |
| **Accounting obligations** | Standard obligations | Reduced/exempt |
| **Public filing of accounts** | Required (with exemptions) | Statistical filing only |
| **Merger/transformation** | Can merge | Cannot merge (no personality) |
| **International recognition** | Generally recognized | Depends on jurisdiction |

### When to Choose SCSp

The SCSp is particularly suitable when:
- Tax transparency is essential for investors
- Offshore structures are being redomiciled
- Minimal accounting disclosure is preferred
- The fund mirrors Anglo-Saxon LP structures

**Source**: P. Schleimer, "Réflexions sur le régime patrimonial", p. 10-11

### When to Choose SCS

The SCS may be preferable when:
- Legal personality facilitates transactions
- Merger capability is required
- Domestic investor recognition is important
- Accounting/audit infrastructure already exists

## 2. AIFM Directive Compliance

### Definition of FIA (Fonds d'investissement alternatif)

Under the AIFM Directive, an AIF:
- Raises capital from "a certain number of investors"
- Invests according to a defined investment policy
- In the interest of those investors

**Important**: Commandites always have at least one commandité and one commanditaire. The UK FCA position suggests exclusion only applies with a single commanditaire.

**Source**: G. Dusemon & C. Pogorzelski, op. cit., p. 88

### Manager Designation

Each FIA must designate an AIFM. For commandites:

**Internal Management** (Art. 4 Loi AIFM):
- The commandite itself becomes the AIFM
- Must be authorized under Chapter 2 of Loi AIFM
- **Note**: SCSp cannot be internally managed (lacks legal personality)

**External Management**:
- Separate legal entity designated as AIFM
- Must be authorized under Chapter 2 Loi AIFM (Luxembourg) or Chapter II AIFM Directive (other Member States/third countries)
- May also serve as formal gérant under L1915

**Source**: G. Dusemon & C. Pogorzelski, op. cit., pp. 91-92

## 3. SICAR Vehicle Structuring

### Available Forms (Art. 1 Loi SICAR)

Since 2013, SICAR may be established as:
1. Société anonyme (SA)
2. Société en commandite par actions (SCA)
3. Société à responsabilité limitée (Sàrl)
4. Société coopérative organisée comme SA
5. **Société en commandite simple (SCS)**
6. **Société en commandite spéciale (SCSp)** — new in 2013

### Key SICAR Modifications

| Modification | Effect |
|--------------|--------|
| **Variable Capital** | Loi SICAR Art. 4(2) expressly permits variable capital for SCS and SCSp |
| **Confidentiality** | Commanditaire identities not published (override of former Art. 6 L1915) |
| **Prospectus Updates** | Art. 26 Loi SICAR — updates only when new securities/parts d'intérêts issued to new investors |

**Source**: G. Dusemon & C. Pogorzelski, op. cit., pp. 82-85

### SICAR Governance Requirements (Art. 12 Loi SICAR)

Managers (dirigeants) must demonstrate:
- Honorability (honorabilité)
- Professional experience

This applies to:
- Commandités gérants (for SCA)
- Gérants, whether partners or not (for SCS/SCSp)

**Source**: G. Dusemon & C. Pogorzelski, op. cit., p. 84

## 4. FIS (Specialised Investment Fund) Use

### Structural Options

FIS may be organized as:

**Chapter 3 (SICAV - Variable Capital)**:
- SA, SCA, Sàrl, cooperative SA
- **SCS and SCSp** (added in 2013)

**Chapter 4 (Fixed Capital)**:
- Same forms, without variable capital provisions

**Practical effect**: SCS and SCSp always have variable capital under the reformed L1915, making Chapter 3/4 distinction less significant.

**Source**: G. Dusemon & C. Pogorzelski, op. cit., pp. 86-87

### FIS Accounting Obligations

| Form | Obligations |
|------|-------------|
| **SCS under FIS** | Standard accounting; annual report required (Art. 52 Loi FIS); audit if thresholds exceeded |
| **SCSp under FIS** | Exempt from standard accounting under LRCS; must prepare annual report per Loi FIS; for AIFM-managed: content per Art. 20 Loi AIFM |

**Source**: G. Dusemon & C. Pogorzelski, op. cit., pp. 89-90

## 5. Manager Independence

### Separation of Gérance and AIFM

The 2013 reform permits complete separation:

**Gérant** (formal management organ under L1915):
- May be commandité or third party
- Represents the partnership externally
- Powers determined by contrat social

**AIFM** (investment manager):
- Separate legal entity
- Performs portfolio management, risk management
- Subject to AIFM regulatory requirements

**Source**: D. Boone & A. Trotska, "Le renouveau de la commandite (3ème Partie)", JurisNews Droit des sociétés Vol. 6 No 1-2/2013, pp. 217-218

### Delegation Powers

**Art. 310-2 and 320-3 L1915** permit gérants to delegate powers:
> "Le contrat social peut autoriser les gérants à déléguer leurs pouvoirs à un ou plusieurs mandataires..."

This explicitly enables AIFM appointment without the AIFM being the formal gérant.

**Source**: G. Dusemon & C. Pogorzelski, op. cit., p. 91

## 6. Limited Partner Expanded Activities

### Safe Harbor Activities (Art. 310-3 and 320-4 L1915)

Commanditaires may engage in the following **without incurring unlimited liability**:
- Exercise of partner prerogatives
- Advice and counsel to the partnership or its affiliates
- Control and surveillance acts
- Granting loans, guarantees, or sureties
- Authorizations to gérants for acts exceeding their powers
- Acting as organ member or agent of a gérant (if representative capacity is indicated)

**Source**: D. Boone & A. Trotska, op. cit., pp. 214-215

### Creditor Status of Commanditaires

**Art. 310-3(7) and 320-4(7) L1915**:
> Commanditaires may enter into any transaction with the partnership without their creditor ranking being affected solely due to their partner status.

**Practical effect**: Commanditaires may:
- Grant loans and remain chirographic creditors
- Obtain security interests (sûretés) for their loans

**Source**: C. Duro, "La société en commandite simple et la société en commandite spéciale après la loi du 12 juillet 2013", ACE 2013/9, p. 18

## 7. Tax Transparency Considerations

### General Principle

The SCSp was designed primarily for tax transparency:
> "Leur reconnaissance fréquente en tant qu'entités fiscalement transparentes" is a fundamental attraction.

**Source**: P. Schleimer, "Réflexions sur le régime patrimonial", p. 10

### Jurisdictional Analysis

Tax treatment depends on:
- Investor domicile
- Investment location
- Applicable tax treaties

| Form | Tax Transparency |
|------|-----------------|
| **SCS** | Often given but must be verified case-by-case |
| **SCSp** | More likely due to absence of legal personality |

### Carried Interest

The Loi AIFM introduced a specific favorable tax regime for carried interest (intéressement aux plus-values), applicable to managers of alternative investment funds.

**Source**: C. Duro, ACE 2013/9, p. 10

## 8. Regulatory Interplay

### Hierarchy of Sources

For regulated commandites:
1. **L1915** (general company law) — as modified
2. **Loi SICAR or Loi FIS** (sectoral law) — Part I
3. **Loi AIFM** (manager regulation)
4. **Loi SICAR or Loi FIS Part II** (AIFM-specific product rules)
5. **Contrat social** (partnership agreement)

**Source**: G. Dusemon & C. Pogorzelski, op. cit., pp. 82-86

## Banking & Finance Application

### Fund Finance Considerations

| Issue | Analysis |
|-------|----------|
| **Borrower capacity** | SCSp borrows via gérance representation |
| **Security package** | Pledge over uncalled commitments; pledge of parts d'intérêts |
| **Tax structuring** | Confirm transparency treatment for lender |
| **Regulatory status** | Verify SICAR/FIS/RAIF classification |

### Subscription Line Facilities

- Security over investor commitments (capital calls)
- Pledge of parts d'intérêts as additional collateral
- Covenant package adapted to fund structure

### Due Diligence Checklist

- [ ] Confirm fund type (SICAR/FIS/RAIF/unregulated)
- [ ] Verify AIFM authorization and delegation arrangements
- [ ] Review gérant powers under contrat social
- [ ] Confirm SCS vs SCSp choice and implications
- [ ] Check variable capital provisions
