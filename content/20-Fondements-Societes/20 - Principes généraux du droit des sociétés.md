# 20 - Principes généraux du droit des sociétés

> **Type**: Main Part / Foundations
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 1, Chapitres 1-2
> **Key Legislation**: Art. 1832 Code civil; Loi du 10 août 1915

---

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Definition of Company

**Art. 1832 C.civ.**:
> "Une société peut être constituée par deux ou plusieurs personnes qui conviennent de mettre en commun quelque chose en vue de partager le bénéfice qui pourra en résulter ou, dans les cas prévus par la loi, par acte de volonté d'une personne qui affecte des biens à l'exercice d'une activité déterminée."

**Steichen, Titre 1, Ch. 1**: A company is formed when two or more persons agree to combine resources (*mise en commun*) for a shared purpose, typically to generate and share profits.

### 1.2 Interests of the Corporate Form

**Steichen**: The corporate form offers advantages to:

| Interest | Benefit |
|----------|---------|
| **Associates** | Pool resources, limited liability, perpetuity |
| **Directors** | Separation of ownership and management |
| **Creditors** | Dedicated assets, capital maintenance |
| **Third parties** | Clear counterparty, registered information |

## 2. Nature of the Company

### 2.1 Contract vs. Institution Debate

**Steichen, Titre 1, Ch. 1**: Classic debate on nature of company:

| Theory | View |
|--------|------|
| **Contractualist** | Company is a contract between associates |
| **Institutionalist** | Company is an institution with own interests |
| **Modern synthesis** | Company combines contractual and institutional elements |

### 2.2 Luxembourg Position

**Steichen**: Luxembourg law adopts a pragmatic approach:
- Company originates in contract (art. 1832)
- But has institutional characteristics
- Third-party protection paramount

## 3. Essential Elements

### 3.1 The Three Pillars

**Art. 1832 C.civ.** + **Steichen**: Every company requires:

| Element | French | Description |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| **Contributions** | *Apports* | Assets contributed by associates |
| **Profit sharing** | *Partage des bénéfices et pertes* | Distribution of results |
| **Common purpose** | *Affectio societatis* | Will to collaborate |

### 3.2 Contributions (*Apports*)

See [[Apports]] for details:
- Cash (*numéraire*)
- In kind (*nature*)
- Services (*industrie*) — limited in capital companies

### 3.3 Profit and Loss Sharing

**Art. 1832 C.civ.**: Associates share in:
- Profits (*bénéfices*)
- Losses (*pertes*)
- Savings (*économies*) in certain cases

**Steichen**: The *clause léonine* (lion's clause) excluding one associate from all profit or loss is void.

### 3.4 Affectio Societatis

See [[Affectio societatis]]:
- Intention to collaborate
- On equal footing
- For common purpose

## 4. Classification of Companies

### 4.1 By Liability

| Category | Liability | Examples |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| **Unlimited** | Associates personally liable | SNC, SC |
| **Limited** | Liability capped at contribution | SA, SARL |
| **Mixed** | Some unlimited, some limited | SCS, SCA |

### 4.2 By Character

| Category | Character | Examples |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| **Sociétés de personnes** | Personal (*intuitu personae*) | SNC, SC |
| **Sociétés de capitaux** | Capital-focused | SA, SCA |
| **Hybrid** | Mixed characteristics | SARL, SAS |

### 4.3 By Purpose

| Category | Purpose | Examples |
|----------|---------|----------|
| **Commercial** | Commercial activities | SA, SARL, SNC |
| **Civil** | Non-commercial activities | Société civile |

## 6. Fundamental Principles

### 6.1 Legal Personality

See [[Personnalité morale]]:
- Company is a separate legal person
- Has own rights and obligations
- Distinct from associates

### 6.2 Capital Maintenance

**Steichen**: Capital rules protect creditors:
- Minimum capital requirements
- Restrictions on distributions
- Capital reduction procedures

See [[Capital social]].

### 6.3 Limited Liability

For capital companies:
- Associates risk only their contribution
- Creditors have recourse only to company assets
- Exception: personal guarantees

### 6.4 Publicity

**Steichen**: Information available through:
- Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS)
- Publication at RESA (Recueil Électronique)
- Annual accounts filing
