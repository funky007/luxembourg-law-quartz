# 26 - Capital et financement

## Overview

This note covers capital structure and financing under Luxembourg company law. Capital (capital social) represents the contributions made by shareholders to the company and serves as the primary guarantee for creditors. The principle of capital fixity (fixite du capital) ensures that creditors can rely on the stated capital as security (Steichen, Chapter 2 - Le contrat de societe, pp. 69-106, and various provisions throughout).

---

## 1. Les apports (Contributions)

### A. Fundamental Principles

**Triple Role of Contributions (Steichen, p. 70):**
1. **Condition of Existence:** No contributions = no company contract ("pas d'apport, pas de societe")
2. **Means of Financing:** Contributions are subordinated to creditor claims; creditors generally have only the capital as guarantee
3. **Basis of Membership:** Contribution is a necessary condition to obtain shareholder status

**Key Principle (Art. 1832 C.civ.):**
"Une societe peut etre constituee par deux ou plusieurs personnes qui conviennent de mettre en commun quelque chose en vue de partager le benefice qui pourra en resulter" (Steichen, p. 69)

### B. Souscription (Subscription)

**Definition:** The engagement by which a person commits to contribute to the formation of capital in exchange for the company's commitment to issue shares (Steichen, pp. 70-71).

**Legal Nature:**
- Forms a **contrat d'apport** (contribution contract) - synallagmatic relationship between subscriber and company
- Art. 1843 C.civ.: "chaque associe est debiteur envers la societe de tout ce qu'il a promis de lui apporter en nature, en numeraire ou en industrie" (Steichen, p. 71)

**Characteristics (Steichen, p. 72):**
1. **Totale (Complete):** All capital must be subscribed - no issuance below par value
2. **Definitive:** Subscriptions must remain complete; conditional subscriptions have conditions deemed unwritten

**Effect:** Subscriber becomes shareholder upon subscription, even before liberation of contribution - entitled to dividends on unpaid shares (Steichen, p. 71)

### C. Liberation (Payment)

**Definition:** The execution of the subscription commitment - actual transfer of the promised contribution to the company (Steichen, p. 73).

**Key Distinction:**
- **Capital souscrit (Subscribed capital):** Amount committed by shareholders
- **Capital libere (Paid-up capital):** Amount actually transferred to the company

**Mandatory Liberation (Art. 420-13 L1915):**
"Les actionnaires ne peuvent pas etre exemptes de leur obligation de fournir leur apport" (Steichen, p. 73)

**SARL Requirement:** Full liberation required at constitution (Art. 710-6 no. 3 L1915) (Steichen, p. 73)

**SA Requirements:**
- Minimum 25% liberation at constitution
- Remainder callable by board of directors

**Unpaid Capital as Asset:**
- Uncalled capital appears as asset on balance sheet
- Constitutes a claim (creance) of the company against shareholders
- Liquidator or bankruptcy administrator may call unpaid capital (Art. 1100-6 L1915) (Steichen, p. 73)

## 2. Types of Contributions

### A. Apport en numeraire (Cash Contribution)

**Definition:** Contribution of a sum of money to the company (Steichen, p. 74).

**Most Common Form:** Simplest to value and verify.

**Requirements:**
- Deposit with credit institution (for SA at constitution)
- Certificate from bank confirming availability of funds
- Transfer to company account after constitution

### B. Apport en nature (Contribution in Kind)

**Definition:** Contribution of assets other than cash - movable or immovable property, receivables, securities, business assets, intellectual property (Steichen, p. 75).

**Valuation Requirements:**
- Independent expert valuation (reviseur d'entreprises agree)
- Report on value of contributions
- Shareholders may not overvalue contributions

**Types:**
1. **Apport en propriete:** Full ownership transferred to company
2. **Apport en jouissance:** Right of use only - contributor retains ownership

### C. Apport en industrie (Contribution of Services)

**Definition:** Contribution of labor, know-how, or services rather than assets (Steichen, p. 76).

**Restrictions:**
- Not permitted in SA (Art. 420-2 L1915)
- Not permitted in SARL (Art. 710-4 L1915)
- Permitted in partnerships (SNC, SEC)

**Characteristics:**
- Does not form part of capital social
- Confers rights to share in profits and losses
- Rights determined by statutes or, failing that, equal to smallest capital contributor

## 3. Capital social

### A. Minimum Capital Requirements

| Company Type | Minimum Capital | Liberation |
|--------------|-----------------|------------|
| **SA** | 30,000 EUR | 25% at constitution |
| **SCA** | 30,000 EUR | 25% at constitution |
| **SARL** | 12,000 EUR | 100% at constitution |
| **SARL-S** | 1 EUR to 12,000 EUR | 100% at constitution |
| **SNC** | No minimum | As per statutes |
| **SEC** | No minimum | As per statutes |

(Various articles L1915)

### B. Principe de fixite du capital (Capital Fixity)

**Fundamental Rule:** Capital, once constituted, may not be reduced without following prescribed procedures protecting creditors (Steichen, p. 75).

**Rationale:** Capital constitutes the primary security for creditors; they contract based on stated capital amount.

**Consequences:**
- Shareholders may not withdraw contributions during company life
- Capital reductions subject to strict procedures
- Prohibition on distributions reducing capital below minimum

### C. Capitaux propres (Shareholders' Equity)

**Components:**
1. Capital social (share capital)
2. Prime d'emission (share premium)
3. Reserves (legal, statutory, free)
4. Report a nouveau (retained earnings)
5. Resultat de l'exercice (current year profit/loss)

**Legal Reserve Requirement (Art. 461-1 L1915):**
- 5% of annual net profit to legal reserve
- Until reserve equals 10% of capital
- Not distributable

## 4. Augmentation de capital (Capital Increase)

### A. Decision-Making Authority

**Competence:** Extraordinary general assembly (assemblee generale extraordinaire) (Steichen, p. 404)

**Majority Requirements:**
- SA: Presence of 50% of capital; 2/3 majority of votes cast
- SARL: Majority representing 3/4 of capital

### B. Methods of Capital Increase

**1. By New Contributions:**
- New shares issued to existing or new shareholders
- Subscription price = nominal value + premium (if any)
- Preemptive rights (droit de souscription preferentiel) for existing shareholders

**2. By Incorporation of Reserves:**
- Conversion of reserves into capital
- No new contributions required
- Bonus shares distributed to existing shareholders

**3. By Conversion of Debt:**
- Conversion of shareholder loans (compte courant) into capital
- Strengthens balance sheet
- Apport en nature rules apply

### C. Droit preferentiel de souscription (Preemptive Rights)

**Principle (Art. 420-26 L1915):**
Existing shareholders have priority right to subscribe to new shares in proportion to their existing holdings.

**Purpose:** Protect existing shareholders from dilution of their proportionate ownership.

**Suppression:**
- May be suppressed by EGM decision
- Requires special report from board
- Reviseur d'entreprises agree report on issue price

## 5. Reduction de capital (Capital Reduction)

### A. Forms of Capital Reduction

**1. Reduction par remboursement aux associes:**
- Return of capital to shareholders
- Permitted only after creditor protection period

**2. Reduction par imputation des pertes:**
- Absorption of accumulated losses
- Restoration of balance between capital and net assets
- "Coup d'accordeon" - reduction followed by increase

**3. Reduction par annulation d'actions:**
- Cancellation of shares (e.g., following share buyback)
- Shares may be cancelled with or without reimbursement

### B. Creditor Protection

**Droit d'opposition (Art. 69-1 L1915):**
- Creditors may oppose capital reduction
- Opposition period: typically 30 days from publication
- Company must provide guarantees or reimburse opposing creditors

**Publication Requirements:**
- Decision must be published in RESA
- Effectiveness suspended during opposition period

### C. Perte du capital (Loss of Capital)

**Alert Thresholds (Art. 480-2 L1915 for SA):**
When losses reduce capitaux propres below:
- **50% of capital:** Board must convene EGM to decide on continuation or dissolution
- **Below legal minimum:** Company must be dissolved or capital increased/reduced

## 6. Other Financing Methods

### A. Obligations (Bonds)

**Definition:** Debt securities issued by the company (Steichen, p. 145).

**Characteristics:**
- Fixed interest rate
- Repayment at maturity
- May be convertible into shares

**Requirements:**
- Board authorization (or EGM for convertible bonds)
- Prospectus for public offerings

### B. Prets d'associes (Shareholder Loans)

**Compte courant d'associe:**
- Most flexible financing method
- No formalities required
- Interest deductible (subject to transfer pricing rules)

**Subordination:**
- May be subordinated to other creditors
- Quasi-equity treatment in insolvency

### C. Apports en compte courant vs. Apports en capital

| Feature | Compte courant | Capital |
|---------|---------------|---------|
| Return of funds | On demand (subject to agreement) | Only on liquidation or reduction |
| Interest | Deductible | Dividends not deductible |
| Insolvency ranking | Creditor (possibly subordinated) | Last in line |
| Formalities | Minimal | Notarial deed, publication |

## 7. Banking and Finance Application

### Capital Requirements for Financial Entities

**Credit Institutions:**
- Minimum own funds requirements under CRR
- Capital ratios (CET1, Tier 1, Total Capital)
- Capital buffers (conservation, countercyclical, systemic)

**Investment Firms:**
- Initial capital requirements per activity
- Own funds requirements under IFR/IFD

**Payment/E-Money Institutions:**
- Specific minimum capital per license type

### Capital Transactions

**Regulatory Approval:**
- Capital increases above certain thresholds may require CSSF notification
- Change of qualifying holdings requires prior authorization

**Documentation:**
- Board/shareholder resolutions
- Subscription agreements
- Notarial deed for statute amendments
- RCS filing and RESA publication

### Intragroup Financing

**Transfer Pricing:**
- Arm's length interest rates on intercompany loans
- Documentation requirements
- Advance pricing agreements available

**Thin Capitalization:**
- No specific Luxembourg rules
- Interest limitation under ATAD (30% EBITDA)
- Substance requirements for holding companies
