# 23 - Les associés

> **Type**: Main Part / Associates
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Chapitre 2
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 1832-1843 C.civ.; Various L1915 provisions

---

## 1. Definition and Acquisition of Status

### 1.1 Who is an Associate?

**Steichen, Titre 3, Ch. 2**: An *associé* (associate/shareholder) is a person who:
- Has made or promised a contribution
- Holds shares or parts sociales
- Has rights and obligations under company contract

### 1.2 Acquisition of Status

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Original** | Founding associate at formation |
| **Derivative** | Acquisition by transfer |
| **Subscription** | New shares in capital increase |
| **Inheritance** | Death of previous holder |

### 1.3 Proof of Status

| Company | Evidence |
|---------|----------|
| **SA** | Entry in share register; share certificate |
| **SARL** | Entry in register of parts |
| **SNC** | Articles of association |

## 2. Rights of Associates

### 2.1 Categories of Rights

**Steichen**: Associate rights fall into:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Financial** | Dividends, liquidation proceeds |
| **Political** | Voting, information |
| **Individual** | Protective rights |

### 2.2 Financial Rights

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Dividends** | Share in distributed profits |
| **Liquidation surplus** | Share on dissolution |
| **Pre-emption** | Priority on new issues (SA) |
| **Reimbursement** | Return of capital on reduction |

See [[Dividendes]], [[Droit préférentiel de souscription]].

### 2.3 Political Rights

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Voting** | Participate in decisions |
| **Attendance** | At general meetings |
| **Information** | Access to documents |
| **Question** | Ask questions at AGM |

See [[Assemblée générale]].

### 2.4 Individual Rights

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Exit** | Transfer shares (subject to restrictions) |
| **Challenge** | Contest invalid resolutions |
| **Minority protection** | Against majority abuse |

## 3. Obligations of Associates

### 3.1 Primary Obligations

**Art. 1843 C.civ.**:

| Obligation | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Contribute** | Make promised contribution |
| **Share losses** | Participate in losses |
| **Act in good faith** | Loyalty to company |

### 3.2 Contribution Obligation

See [[Apports]]:
- Must make promised contribution
- May not be released from obligation
- Enforceable by company

### 3.3 Liability for Debts

| Company | Liability |
|---------|-----------|
| **SA** | Limited to contribution |
| **SARL** | Limited to contribution |
| **SNC** | Unlimited and joint |
| **SCS (commandité)** | Unlimited |
| **SCS (commanditaire)** | Limited |

## 4. Classes of Shareholders

### 4.1 By Stake Size

| Category | Characteristics |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Majority** | >50% voting rights; controls company |
| **Minority** | <50%; protected by law |
| **Significant** | >20% (related party rules) |
| **Controlling** | De facto control |

### 4.2 By Share Type

| Type | Rights |
|------|--------|
| **Ordinary** | Standard rights |
| **Preferred** | Enhanced dividends |
| **Non-voting** | Dividends but no vote |
| **Founder** | Special rights |

### 4.3 Institutional vs. Individual

| Type | Characteristics |
|------|-----------------|
| **Institutional** | Investment funds, pension funds |
| **Individual** | Natural persons |
| **Strategic** | Industry players |
| **Financial** | Returns-focused |

## 5. Associate Relationships

### 5.1 With Company

| Aspect | Nature |
|--------|--------|
| **Contractual** | Through company contract |
| **Shareholder** | Rights against company |
| **Not employee** | Unless separate contract |

### 5.2 With Other Associates

| Aspect | Nature |
|--------|--------|
| **Co-contractors** | Party to same contract |
| **No direct liability** | To each other generally |
| **Shareholders' agreements** | May create direct rights |

### 5.3 With Third Parties

| Aspect | Nature |
|--------|--------|
| **No direct relationship** | Company is intermediary |
| **Limited liability** | Associates not liable for company debts |
| **Guarantees** | Personal commitment separate |

## 6. Information Rights

### 6.1 Permanent Rights

**Steichen**: Associates may at any time access:
- Articles of association
- Share register
- Minutes of general meetings (SA)

### 6.2 Pre-Meeting Rights

Before AGM, associates receive:
- Annual accounts
- Management report
- Auditor's report
- Proposed resolutions

### 6.3 Inspection Rights

| Company | Right |
|---------|-------|
| **SA** | Inspect register; question at AGM |
| **SARL** | Broader inspection rights |
| **SNC** | Full access |

---

## 7. Minority Protection

### 7.1 Statutory Protections

| Protection | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Quorum requirements** | EGM requires capital present |
| **Supermajorities** | Major decisions need 2/3 or 3/4 |
| **Pre-emption rights** | Against dilution |
| **Nullity actions** | Challenge invalid decisions |

### 7.2 Abuse of Majority

See [[Abus de majorité]]:
- Majority may not oppress minority
- Decisions must serve corporate interest
- Court remedies available

### 7.3 Exit Rights

| Mechanism | Availability |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Sale** | Subject to restrictions |
| **Redemption** | If refusal to approve transfer (SARL) |
| **Judicial dissolution** | Extreme remedy |

---

## 8. Exit from Company

### 8.1 Transfer of Shares

See [[Actions]], [[Parts sociales]]:

| Company | Transferability |
|---------|-----------------|
| **SA** | Freely negotiable (default) |
| **SARL** | Restricted to third parties |
| **SNC** | Requires unanimity |

### 8.2 Restrictions

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Approval clause** | Transfer needs consent |
| **Pre-emption** | Existing shareholders first |
| **Lock-up** | Prohibition for period |
| **Tag-along** | Join majority sale |

### 8.3 Death of Associate

| Company | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **SA** | Shares pass to heirs |
| **SARL** | Depends on articles |
| **SNC** | May dissolve company |
