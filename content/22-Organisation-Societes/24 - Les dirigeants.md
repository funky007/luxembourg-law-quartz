# 24 - Les dirigeants

> **Type**: Main Part / Directors & Managers
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Chapitre 3
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 441-1 to 441-14 L1915 (SA); Arts. 710-14 to 710-16 L1915 (SARL)

---

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Definition

**Steichen, Titre 3, Ch. 3**: *Dirigeants* (directors/managers) are the persons who:
- Manage the company (*gestion*)
- Represent it externally (*représentation*)
- Exercise powers conferred by law and articles

### 1.2 Terminology by Company Type

| Company | Term | Organ |
|---------|------|-------|
| **SA** | Administrateur | Conseil d'administration |
| **SA (dualist)** | Membre du directoire | Directoire |
| **SARL** | Gérant | Gérance |
| **SNC** | Gérant | Gérance |
| **SAS** | Président | As per articles |

## 2. SA Directors

### 2.1 Board Composition

**Art. 441-1 L1915**:

| Requirement | Rule |
|-------------|------|
| **Minimum** | 3 directors (1 for single shareholder) |
| **Maximum** | 15 (unless articles permit more) |
| **Persons** | Natural or legal |
| **Shareholders?** | Not required unless articles provide |

### 2.2 Appointment

| Appointing Body | Situation |
|-----------------|-----------|
| **Founders** | First directors in articles |
| **AGM** | Subsequent appointments |
| **Board** | Co-optation to fill vacancy |

### 2.3 Term and Removal

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Maximum term** | 6 years (renewable) |
| **Removal** | *Ad nutum* (at will) by AGM |
| **No compensation** | For removal unless abuse |
| **Resignation** | Effective immediately |

### 2.4 Remuneration

**Art. 441-3 L1915**:
- Fixed by AGM (or articles)
- May be fixed or variable
- *Tantièmes* (profit share) possible
- Special remuneration for specific tasks

## 3. SA Governance Structures

### 3.1 Monistic (Single Board)

**Traditional model**:
- Single *conseil d'administration*
- Board manages and represents
- May delegate to *délégué à la gestion journalière*

### 3.2 Dualistic (Two-Tier)

**Art. 442-1 L1915**: Alternative structure:

| Organ | Function |
|-------|----------|
| **Directoire** | Management and representation |
| **Conseil de surveillance** | Supervision |

### 3.3 Delegation

**Art. 441-10 L1915**: Board may delegate:
- Daily management (*gestion journalière*)
- To one or more directors
- Or to non-director delegates
- Published at RCS

## 4. SARL Gérant

See [[Gérant]] for details.

### 4.1 Appointment

**Art. 710-14 L1915**:
- One or more gérants
- Natural or legal person
- Associate or third party
- Majority of capital appoints

### 4.2 Removal

| Type | Removal |
|------|---------|
| **Statutory gérant** | Requires articles amendment |
| **Non-statutory** | Majority of capital |
| **For cause** | *Cause légitime* standard |

## 5. Powers

### 5.1 Internal Powers

**Steichen**: Within company, directors may:

| Power | Scope |
|-------|-------|
| **Management** | All acts within corporate purpose |
| **Policy** | Strategic decisions |
| **Delegation** | Appoint agents, employees |

**Limits**: Reserved matters require shareholder approval.

### 5.2 External Powers (Representation)

See [[Représentation de la société]]:

| Principle | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| **Full authority** | Directors bind company |
| **Statutory limits** | Not opposable to third parties |
| **Ultra vires** | Company bound unless third party knew |

### 5.3 Joint vs. Several Powers

| Structure | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **Board** | Collective action |
| **Individual director** | Only if delegated |
| **Gérant** | Each may act alone (unless joint signature) |

---

## 6. Duties

### 6.1 Duty of Care

**Steichen**: Directors must:
- Act prudently (*bon père de famille*)
- Make informed decisions
- Exercise supervision
- Maintain proper accounts

### 6.2 Duty of Loyalty

| Obligation | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Corporate interest** | Act for company's benefit |
| **Avoid conflicts** | Disclose personal interests |
| **No self-dealing** | Without proper approval |
| **Confidentiality** | Protect company information |

### 6.3 Statutory Duties

| Duty | Reference |
|------|-----------|
| **Convene AGM** | Annual accounts approval |
| **File accounts** | RCS filing |
| **Capital alert** | If net assets below 50% |
| **Bankruptcy filing** | If insolvent |

---

## 7. Liability

See [[Responsabilité des dirigeants]] for details.

### 7.1 To Company

**Art. 441-9 L1915** (SA):
- Breach of duties
- Violation of law or articles
- *Faute de gestion*

### 7.2 To Third Parties

**Art. 441-9 al. 3 L1915**:
- Personal fault causing direct damage
- Not derivative of company's fault

### 7.3 Joint Liability

| Situation | Liability |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Collective decision** | Joint and several |
| **Individual fault** | Personal only |
| **Dissent recorded** | May escape liability |

### 7.4 Business Judgment Rule

**Steichen**: Directors not liable for:
- Honest business decisions
- Even if wrong in hindsight
- If informed and no conflict

---

## 8. Termination of Functions

### 8.1 Causes

| Cause | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Term expiry** | Unless renewed |
| **Removal** | By competent organ |
| **Resignation** | Effective on notice |
| **Death** | Functions end |
| **Incapacity** | Functions end |
| **Disqualification** | Legal prohibition |

### 8.2 Handover

Outgoing directors must:
- Hand over documents
- Provide information to successor
- Not compete (if clause exists)

---

## 9. De Facto Directors

### 9.1 Concept

**Steichen**: Person who in fact manages without valid appointment:
- *Dirigeant de fait*
- May incur liability
- Same duties as de jure directors

### 9.2 Identification

| Factor | Indication |
|--------|------------|
| **Decision-making** | Takes management decisions |
| **Independence** | Acts autonomously |
| **Authority** | Exercises real power |
| **Appearance** | Held out as director |

## Banking & Finance Application

### Due Diligence on Directors

| Check | Source |
|-------|--------|
| **Identity** | RCS extract |
| **Authority** | Articles, board minutes |
| **Signing powers** | Single or joint |
| **Conflicts** | Related party analysis |

### Director Guarantees

See [[Caution dirigeante]]:
- Personal guarantees common
- Assess director's net worth
- Proportionality requirements

### Warning Signs

| Issue | Risk |
|-------|------|
| **Frequent changes** | Instability |
| **Nominee directors** | Lack of substance |
| **De facto directors** | Hidden control |
| **Conflicts undisclosed** | Governance failures |
