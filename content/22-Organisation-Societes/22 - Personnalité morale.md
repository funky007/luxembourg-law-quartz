# 22 - Personnalité morale

> **Type**: Main Part / Legal Personality
> **Source**: Steichen, *Précis de droit des sociétés*, Titre 3, Chapitre 1
> **Key Legislation**: Arts. 2, 100-3 L1915; Art. 1832 C.civ.

---

## 1. Acquisition of Legal Personality

### 1.1 When Acquired

**Steichen, Titre 3, Ch. 1**: Legal personality acquired upon:

| Event | Timing |
|-------|--------|
| **RCS deposit** | When deed deposited |
| **Not publication** | Publication is for opposability |

### 1.2 Effect

**Art. 2 L1915**: From registration:
- Company is a *personne morale*
- Separate from associates
- Has own legal existence

### 1.3 Before Registration

**Steichen**: Before registration:
- Company exists as contract
- No legal personality
- Promoters personally liable
- See pre-formation acts rules

---

## 2. Attributes of Legal Personality

### 2.1 Separate Legal Existence

| Attribute | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Name** | Own corporate name |
| **Domicile** | Registered office |
| **Nationality** | Luxembourg if registered there |
| **Capacity** | To hold rights and obligations |
| **Patrimony** | Own assets and liabilities |

### 2.2 Corporate Name (*Dénomination sociale*)

**Steichen**: Requirements:
- Distinct from other registered names
- May include founder names (but not required)
- Must indicate company form (SA, SARL, etc.)
- Registered at RCS

### 2.3 Registered Office (*Siège social*)

**Steichen**: Functions:
- Determines nationality
- Establishes jurisdiction
- Address for notifications
- Must be real (not just postal)

### 2.4 Nationality

**Steichen**: Luxembourg company if:
- Registered at Luxembourg RCS
- Registered office in Luxembourg
- Governs applicable company law

---

## 3. Capacity and Powers

### 3.1 Legal Capacity

**Art. 100-3 L1915**: Company may:
- Own property
- Enter contracts
- Sue and be sued
- Incur obligations

### 3.2 Limits on Capacity

| Limit | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Corporate purpose** | Acts must fall within *objet social* |
| **Ultra vires** | Acts outside purpose (limited effect) |
| **Specialty principle** | Company exists for stated purpose |

### 3.3 Representation

See [[Représentation de la société]]:
- Acts through organs (directors, managers)
- Organs bind company
- Third parties protected

---

## 4. Separation of Patrimony

### 4.1 Core Principle

**Steichen**: Company has own patrimony:
- Assets belong to company, not associates
- Debts are company's, not associates' (in limited companies)
- Clear separation

### 4.2 Consequences for Associates

| Aspect | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Assets** | Associates own shares, not company assets |
| **Debts** | Associates not personally liable (in limited companies) |
| **Creditors** | Associates' creditors cannot reach company assets |

### 4.3 Consequences for Company Creditors

| Aspect | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Recourse** | Against company assets first |
| **Associates** | Only if unlimited liability or guarantee |
| **Limited liability** | Maximum loss is contribution |

---

## 5. Piercing the Corporate Veil

### 5.1 General Principle

**Steichen**: Legal personality generally respected:
- Courts reluctant to pierce veil
- Requires exceptional circumstances
- Fraud, abuse, or sham

### 5.2 Grounds for Piercing

| Ground | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Simulation** | Company is a sham |
| **Fraud** | Used to defraud creditors |
| **Abuse** | Misuse of corporate form |
| **Undercapitalization** | Deliberate to defeat creditors |
| **Confusion of patrimony** | No separation maintained |

### 5.3 Consequences

If veil pierced:
- Associates personally liable
- No limited liability protection
- Applies in specific case only

---

## 6. Duration and Continuity

### 6.1 Duration

**Steichen**: Company may be:
- Limited duration (fixed term)
- Unlimited duration (default for most)
- Continues until dissolved

### 6.2 Perpetual Succession

Company continues despite:
- Death of associates
- Transfer of shares
- Change of directors
- Until dissolution

### 6.3 Change of Associates

Legal personality unaffected by:
- Sale of shares
- Inheritance
- New shareholders joining
- Company remains same legal person

---

## 7. Groups and Subsidiaries

### 7.1 Separate Personality of Subsidiaries

**Steichen**: Each group company is:
- Separate legal person
- Distinct patrimony
- Own rights and obligations

### 7.2 Parent-Subsidiary Relations

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Liability** | Parent not liable for subsidiary debts (principle) |
| **Assets** | Subsidiary assets belong to subsidiary |
| **Piercing** | Possible in exceptional cases |

See [[28 - Groupes de sociétés]].

---

## 8. End of Legal Personality

### 8.1 Dissolution

**Steichen**: Dissolution does NOT end legal personality:
- Company continues for liquidation
- Must add "en liquidation" to name
- Powers limited to winding up

### 8.2 End

Legal personality ends upon:
- Completion of liquidation
- Cancellation from RCS
- Final cancellation notice

See [[29 - Dissolution et liquidation]].

## Banking & Finance Application

### Due Diligence

| Check | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **RCS extract** | Verify legal existence |
| **Good standing** | Company not dissolved |
| **Registered office** | Real presence |
| **Name** | Correct legal name |

### Lending to Companies

- Contract with company, not associates
- Security over company assets
- Directors' personal guarantees separate
- Verify corporate authorization

### Group Structures

| Issue | Consideration |
|-------|---------------|
| **Upstream guarantees** | Corporate benefit analysis |
| **Cross-guarantees** | Each company separate obligor |
| **Cash pooling** | Intercompany claims |
| **Ring-fencing** | Structural subordination |

### Warning Signs

- Confusion between company and associate assets
- Undercapitalization
- Single-purpose entities with no substance
- No separation of accounts
