# 25 - Controle de la societe

## Overview

This note covers the control mechanisms for companies under Luxembourg law, focusing on the verification of accounts by auditors (commissaires aux comptes, reviseurs d'entreprises agrees) and judicial intervention in crisis situations. The auditor serves as the financial conscience of the company, ensuring that accounts reflect a faithful image (image fidele) of the company's patrimony and results (Steichen, Chapter 4 - Les Controleurs, pp. 336-375).

---

## 1. Object of Control: Les comptes sociaux (Financial Statements)

### A. Content of Financial Statements

**Legal Framework:** Articles 8 et seq. Code de commerce, as modified by L19.12.2002 and L18.12.2015 (transposing directive 2013/34/UE) (Steichen, p. 337).

**Components of Annual Accounts (Art. 8 C.com.):**
1. **Bilan (Balance Sheet):** Describes active and passive elements in order of increasing liquidity (Steichen, p. 338)
   - Actif immobilise (fixed assets): immobilisations incorporelles, corporelles, financieres
   - Actif circulant: stocks, creances, valeurs mobilieres, disponibilites
   - Passif: capitaux propres, provisions, dettes

2. **Compte de resultat (Profit and Loss Account):** Shows all income and expenses for the period, resulting in profit or loss (Steichen, p. 339)

3. **Annexe (Notes):** Completes and comments on information in balance sheet and P&L; must provide all "information of significant importance" on the financial and patrimonial situation (Steichen, p. 339)

**Image fidele Principle:**
The accounts must give a "faithful image" (image fidele / true and fair view) of the company's patrimony, financial situation, and results. This is the central objective of accounting, derived from the 4th EU Directive and the Anglo-Saxon concept of "true and fair view" (Steichen, p. 339).

**Key Principle:** If application of accounting principles does not permit a faithful image, the company may derogate from them to the extent necessary, though this must remain exceptional (Steichen, p. 339).

### B. Rapport de gestion (Management Report)

Required for all companies except small enterprises which may include the information in the annexe (Art. 68 par. 3 L19.12.2002) (Steichen, p. 340).

**Contents:**
- Evolution of business during the period
- Foreseeable development
- Significant events after closing date
- R&D activities
- Corporate governance statement (for listed companies)

**Non-Financial Reporting (Art. 68bis LRCS):**
Large public interest companies (500+ employees) must include non-financial declaration on environmental, social, employee, human rights, and anti-corruption matters (Steichen, p. 340).

### C. Size Categories and Accounting Obligations

| Category | Total Assets | Turnover | Employees | Accounting Relief |
|----------|--------------|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Micro** | < 350k EUR | < 700k EUR | < 10 | Simplified |
| **Small** | < 4.4m EUR | < 8.8m EUR | < 50 | Abbreviated balance sheet and P&L |
| **Medium** | < 17.5m EUR | < 35m EUR | < 250 | Abbreviated P&L |
| **Large** | Above thresholds | - | - | Full accounts |

(Art. 35, 47 LRCS) (Steichen, pp. 341-342)

**Exemptions:**
- SNC and SECS with annual turnover < 100,000 EUR exempt from formal accounting (Art. 13 C.com.) (Steichen, p. 338)
- SECsp exempt without turnover ceiling (Steichen, p. 338)

## 2. Approval and Publication of Accounts

### A. Assemblee generale ordinaire

**Requirement (Art. 450-8 L1915):**
General assembly must be held annually in Luxembourg within 6 months of financial year-end (first assembly may be within 18 months of constitution) (Steichen, p. 345).

**Same requirement for SARL (Art. 710-23 L1915)** (Steichen, p. 345).

**Functions:**
- Deliberate on accounts (shareholders may request explanations)
- Amend accounts if not conforming to legal requirements
- Approve or refuse accounts
- Vote on discharge (decharge) for directors and auditors

**Key Principle:** Assembly decision has force of res judicata - no shareholder may subsequently obtain judicial modification of accounts (except if contrary to statutes) (Steichen, p. 346).

### B. Publication Requirements

**Obligation:** Accounts must be filed within one month of approval (Art. 75 L19.12.2002) (Steichen, p. 346).

**Electronic Filing:** Mandatory since 1 January 2012 (Steichen, p. 346).

**Purpose:** Publication is the indispensable counterpart to limited liability - creditors and third parties must be able to assess financial situation (Steichen, p. 346).

### C. Sanctions for Non-Compliance

**Civil Liability:** Faute de regularite (breach of duty of compliance) (Steichen, p. 347)

**Criminal Sanctions (Art. 1500-2 L1915):**
- Fine of 500 to 25,000 EUR for failure to submit accounts to assembly or publish them (Steichen, p. 347)
- Applies to SA, SARL, and cooperatives
- Material offence - intent not required

**Judicial Dissolution (Art. 1200-1 L1915):**
- Company may be dissolved for serious violations of company law (Steichen, p. 348)
- Failure to publish accounts since constitution = grave infraction
- Late regularization does not necessarily prevent dissolution (Steichen, p. 349)

**Faux bilan (Fraudulent Accounts) - Art. 1500-8 L1915:**
- Imprisonment 5-10 years + fine 5,000-250,000 EUR (Steichen, p. 344)
- Requires: (1) intention frauduleuse/dessein de nuire (2) material falsification (3) potential prejudice
- False accounts generally involve overvaluation of assets or improper reduction of liabilities (Steichen, p. 344)

## 3. The Auditors (Les hommes du controle)

### A. Dual System

Luxembourg operates a **bicephalous system** (Steichen, p. 350):

| Type | Qualifications | Applies to |
|------|---------------|------------|
| **Reviseur d'entreprises agree** | Professional registration, examination, 3-year stage | Large/medium companies exceeding thresholds |
| **Commissaire aux comptes** | None required | Small companies (SA/SCA only) |

**Evolution:** The 1984 law professionalized the auditing function, creating the reviseur d'entreprises. The 2009 and 2016 laws (transposing EU directives) further strengthened requirements (Steichen, pp. 350-351).

### B. Reviseur d'entreprises agree

#### 1. Registration Requirements (L23.07.2016)

**Registration (Art. 13 L23.07.2016):**
Must be inscribed on register maintained by CSSF (Steichen, p. 352).

**Nationality:** EU member state national, or national of state accepting Luxembourg auditors without discrimination (Art. 5 par. 2 c L23.07.2016) (Steichen, p. 352)

**Professional Establishment:** Must maintain professional establishment in Luxembourg (Art. 3 par. 3 L23.07.2016) (Steichen, p. 352)

**Qualification Routes:**
1. **Luxembourg route:** Master's degree + aptitude examination + 3-year traineeship (Art. 9 L23.07.2016)
2. **EU route:** Legal auditor from another member state + aptitude test on Luxembourg law (Art. 5 par. 2 b L23.07.2016)
3. **Third country route:** Similar qualification + reciprocity (Art. 5 par. 2 c L23.07.2016)

**Practice through Company (Art. 3 par. 4 b L23.07.2016):**
- Permitted in any corporate form including SA
- Must remain under control of reviseurs (majority of directors and voting rights)
- Documents must bear personal signature of responsible reviseur (Art. 35 par. 4 L23.07.2016)

#### 2. Professional Organization

**Institut des reviseurs d'entreprises (IRE) - Art. 61 L23.07.2016:**
- All reviseurs d'entreprises (agree or not) are mandatory members
- Legal personality
- Functions: represent profession externally; ensure internal discipline
- May issue binding recommendations (normes) (Steichen, p. 354)

**CSSF Supervision (Art. 36 L23.07.2016):**
- External public oversight
- Quality assurance inspections at least every 6 years
- Covers: approval, audit standards, ethics, internal quality control, continuing education, investigation and sanctions (Steichen, p. 354)

**Unauthorized Practice:**
Using the title "reviseur d'entreprises agree" without registration is an offence (Art. 2 par. 1 L23.07.2016) (Steichen, p. 354)

### C. Commissaire aux comptes

**Scope:** Only required in small SA and SCA (Art. 443-1 L1915) (Steichen, p. 351)

**Qualifications:** None required - may be shareholder or non-shareholder (Steichen, p. 351)

**Key Point:** Commissaire institution is suppressed in any company where reviseur d'entreprises agree becomes mandatory or is voluntarily appointed (Art. 69 par. 3 L19.12.2002) (Steichen, p. 351)

## 4. Audit Mission and Powers

### A. Scope of Mission

**Surveillance Powers (Art. 443-2 L1915):**
"Les commissaires ont un droit illimite de surveillance et de controle sur toutes les operations de la societe" (Steichen, p. 354)

**Primary Functions:**
- Verify conformity of accounts with legal requirements
- Verify concordance of management report with accounts
- Issue attestation (audit opinion) on accounts

### B. Independence Requirements

**General Principle:** Auditor must be independent of the company audited (Steichen, p. 350)

**Enhanced Requirements for Public Interest Entities (L23.07.2016):**
- Stricter independence rules
- Mandatory audit firm rotation
- Limitations on non-audit services
- Enhanced reporting requirements

---

## 5. Judicial Control (Controle exceptionnel)

### A. Intervention in Crisis Situations

The second type of control is exceptional, exercised by tribunals in crisis situations (Steichen, p. 336).

**Circumstances:**
- Serious irregularities in management
- Deadlock between shareholders
- Threat to company interests

### B. Expertise de gestion

Shareholders holding minimum percentage may request court-appointed expert to examine management operations (Steichen, pp. 370-375).

---

## 6. Banking and Finance Application

### Regulated Entities

**Credit Institutions and PSF:**
- Subject to CSSF prudential supervision
- Mandatory audit by reviseur d'entreprises agree
- Enhanced reporting requirements (FINREP, COREP)
- Auditor must report certain matters to CSSF

**Investment Funds:**
- Specific audit requirements under fund legislation
- Depositary oversight functions

### Audit of Financial Statements

**Key Considerations:**
- Financial sector entities almost always exceed size thresholds
- Specialized knowledge required for financial sector audit
- Additional regulatory reporting obligations
- Auditor rotation requirements for EIP (Entites d'interet public)

### Non-Financial Reporting

Large financial sector entities subject to ESG reporting requirements under Art. 68bis LRCS.
