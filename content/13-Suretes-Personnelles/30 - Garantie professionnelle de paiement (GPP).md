# Garantie professionnelle de paiement (GPP)

> **Type**: Personal Security (*sûreté personnelle*)
> **Source**: Loi du 10 juillet 2020 relative aux garanties professionnelles de paiement
> **Entry into Force**: 17 July 2020
> **Related**: [[23 - Cautionnement — Formation]], [[26 - Garanties indépendantes]], [[Financial collateral (loi 5 août 2005)]]

---

## Definition

The **garantie professionnelle de paiement** (GPP) is a commitment whereby a guarantor undertakes to pay a beneficiary, at the request of the latter or an agreed third party, an indicated amount, in connection with certain claims or related risks.

> "Il s'agit d'une nouvelle sûreté personnelle qui est issue de la loi du 10 juillet 2020 relative aux garanties professionnelles de paiement. Le législateur a voulu créer une sorte de pendant aux contrats de garantie financière dans la matière de la sûreté personnelle."
>
> *Translation*: "This is a new personal security arising from the law of 10 July 2020 on professional payment guarantees. The legislature intended to create a counterpart to financial collateral agreements in the area of personal securities."

**Source**: Course notes, CCDL Droit des sûretés 2023/24, Me Armel Waisse

---

## Legislative Purpose

The GPP was designed as a **personal security parallel to the CGF** (Contrat de garantie financière):

| Feature | CGF (Loi 5 août 2005) | GPP (Loi 10 juillet 2020) |
|---------|----------------------|---------------------------|
| **Type** | Sûreté réelle | Sûreté personnelle |
| **Object** | Financial assets | Payment obligations |
| **Philosophy** | Creditor-friendly | Creditor-friendly |
| **Contractual freedom** | Maximum | Maximum |
| **Insolvency protection** | Yes | Yes |

Both laws aim to maximize creditor protection and reinforce Luxembourg's position as a "creditor-friendly" jurisdiction.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Opt-In Regime

The law applies **only if parties expressly agree** to subject the guarantee to it:

> The professional payment guarantee must be evidenced in writing, which can be in electronic form or any other durable medium.

**Practical implication**: Include express reference to the Loi du 10 juillet 2020 in guarantee documentation to trigger GPP protections.

### 2. Eligible Guarantors

Wide scope of eligible guarantors:
- Legal persons (companies)
- Entities without legal personality (SCSp, FCP)
- Natural persons
- Public or international institutions
- Other forms of joint ownership or co-ownership

### 3. Covered Obligations

The GPP can cover:
- Debts (*dettes*)
- Obligations (*obligations*)
- Risks (*risques*)

**Requirement**: Claims must be identified or identifiable, subject to public policy limits.

### 4. Maximum Contractual Freedom

The law provides extensive freedom to determine:
- Object of the guarantee (debts, obligations, risks)
- Conditions (amount, terms, duration)
- Call and payment procedures

---

## Autonomy vs. Accessory Character

### Insolvency Independence

A distinctive feature of the GPP:

> "The guarantor remains liable to the fullest extent under the professional payment guarantee, even if the debtor is subject to a reorganization, liquidation or other national or foreign measure."

This demonstrates **autonomy from the debtor's insolvency status** — unlike traditional cautionnement where the surety benefits from defenses based on the principal debt.

### Comparison with Other Guarantees

| Feature | Cautionnement | Garantie à première demande | GPP |
|---------|---------------|----------------------------|-----|
| **Accessory character** | Structural | Autonomous | Flexible |
| **Debtor defenses** | Available to surety | Limited | Contractually determined |
| **Insolvency impact** | May affect surety | Generally independent | Expressly protected |
| **Formality** | Art. 1326 mention | Bank practice | Written, express opt-in |

## Multi-Creditor Applications

### Security Agent Structures

The GPP is particularly useful in **syndicated lending**:
- Security agents or trustees can hold GPP for multiple beneficiaries
- Beneficiaries must be identifiable third parties
- Facilitates parallel debt and agency structures common in Luxembourg finance

### Practical Example

In a syndicated loan:
1. Borrower procures GPP from parent company or sponsor
2. GPP granted in favor of security agent
3. Agent holds for benefit of all lenders
4. Upon default, agent enforces on behalf of syndicate

---

## Documentation Requirements

### Written Form

Mandatory written evidence:
- Physical document
- Electronic form
- Any other durable medium

### Express Reference

To trigger GPP regime, the agreement must **expressly reference** the Loi du 10 juillet 2020. Without this:
- Document may be recharacterized as cautionnement or garantie indépendante
- GPP-specific protections may not apply

### Sample Clause

> "This guarantee is a garantie professionnelle de paiement governed by and subject to the Luxembourg law of 10 July 2020 on professional payment guarantees (Loi du 10 juillet 2020 relative aux garanties professionnelles de paiement)."

## Banking & Finance Application

### When to Use GPP

| Scenario | GPP Advantages |
|----------|----------------|
| **Syndicated lending** | Multi-creditor structure via agent |
| **Parent guarantees** | Clear autonomous character |
| **Acquisition finance** | Sponsor support |
| **Fund finance** | GP/sponsor guarantees for fund obligations |

### Due Diligence Checklist

- [ ] Verify express reference to Loi 10 juillet 2020
- [ ] Confirm written form requirement satisfied
- [ ] Check guarantor capacity and authorization
- [ ] Verify covered obligations are identifiable
- [ ] Review call and payment procedures

### Comparison with CGF

For comprehensive creditor protection in Luxembourg finance:
- **CGF** for real security over financial assets
- **GPP** for personal security from sponsors/parents

Together, they provide a "belt and braces" approach maximizing creditor protections.

---

> **Common Law Comparison**
>
> The GPP is comparable to:
> - **UK**: On-demand performance bond or bank guarantee
> - **US**: Independent guarantee (UCC context)
>
> Key advantage: Luxembourg law provides statutory framework with express insolvency protections, unlike common law guarantees which depend on drafting.
