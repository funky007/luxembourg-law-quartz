# Hypothèque conventionnelle (Conventional Mortgage)

> **Type**: Real Security (*sûreté réelle immobilière*)
> **Source**: Code civil, Arts. 2114-2203; H. Westendorf, *Les sûretés*, T.1
> **Key Articles**: Arts. 2114, 2124-2132, 2134-2145 C.civ.
> **Related**: [[10 - Principes généraux des sûretés]], [[Droit préférentiel]]

---

## Definition

The **hypothèque** (mortgage) is a real security over immovable property (*immeuble*) that confers on the creditor a right of preference (*droit de préférence*) and a right of pursuit (*droit de suite*), without transferring possession of the property to the creditor.

> **Art. 2114 C.civ.**: "L'hypothèque est un droit réel sur les immeubles affectés à l'acquittement d'une obligation."
>
> *Translation*: "A mortgage is a real right over immovable property allocated to the discharge of an obligation."

---

## Types of Mortgage

| Type | Source | Characteristics |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| **Hypothèque conventionnelle** | Agreement of parties | Most common; requires notarial act |
| **Hypothèque légale** | Operation of law | Statutory mortgages (spouse, minor, etc.) |
| **Hypothèque judiciaire** | Court judgment | Obtained by creditor with enforceable title |

This note focuses on the **hypothèque conventionnelle** (conventional mortgage), which results from agreement between the parties.

---

## Requirements

### 1. Valid Underlying Obligation

The mortgage is **accessory** to the secured obligation:
- The obligation must exist or be determinable
- Future obligations can be secured (subject to specification)
- The mortgage follows the fate of the secured claim

### 2. Mortgagor Capacity

The mortgagor must:
- Be owner of the immovable property
- Have capacity to alienate (*capacité de disposer*)
- For companies: verify corporate authorization

> A mortgage granted by a non-owner is void, subject to later acquisition of title (*validation*).

### 3. Mortgageable Property

Property capable of being mortgaged:
- Immovable property (*immeubles par nature*)
- Rights over immovables (*droits réels immobiliers*)
- Usufruct of immovables
- Emphyteutic rights
- Superficiary rights

**Excluded**: Movables (use gage or nantissement instead).

---

## Formalities

### Notarial Act Required

> **Art. 2127 C.civ.**: "L'hypothèque conventionnelle ne peut être consentie que par acte passé en forme authentique devant notaire."
>
> *Translation*: "A conventional mortgage can only be granted by an act in authentic form before a notary."

**Requirements**:
- Notarial deed (*acte authentique*)
- Precise identification of the property
- Amount secured (*montant garanti*)
- Description of the secured obligation

### Registration (*Inscription*)

The mortgage must be **registered** (*inscrite*) at the Conservation des hypothèques:

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| **Registry** | Conservation des hypothèques |
| **Location** | Office for district where property located |
| **Effect** | Creates opposability to third parties |
| **Duration** | 10 years (renewable) |

## Ranking (*Rang*)

### Priority by Registration Date

> "Among creditors, a mortgage—whether legal, judicial, or conventional—ranks only from the day of registration taken by the creditor at the mortgage conservation office."

**Key principle**: First to register has priority (*prior tempore potior jure*).

### Duration and Renewal

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Initial duration** | 10 years from registration |
| **Renewal** | Must be renewed before expiration to preserve rank |
| **Lapsed registration** | New registration ranks only from new date |

### Multiple Mortgages

| Situation | Treatment |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Same day registrations** | Rank concurrently (*rang égal*) |
| **Sequential registrations** | Earlier registration has priority |
| **Conventional subordination** | Permitted by agreement |

## Rights of the Mortgage Creditor

### 1. Right of Preference (*Droit de préférence*)

The mortgage creditor is paid before unsecured creditors from the proceeds of the mortgaged property.

### 2. Right of Pursuit (*Droit de suite*)

The creditor can pursue the property into the hands of any subsequent acquirer:
- Property alienation does not affect the mortgage
- New owner takes subject to registered mortgage
- Enforcement possible against third-party holder

### 3. Accessory Nature

The mortgage follows the secured claim:
- Transfer of claim includes mortgage
- Payment extinguishes mortgage
- Partial payment reduces mortgage proportionally

---

## Enforcement (*Réalisation*)

### Procedure

1. **Default**: Debtor fails to perform secured obligation
2. **Commandement**: Formal demand for payment
3. **Seizure** (*saisie immobilière*): Court-supervised sale process
4. **Adjudication**: Judicial sale to highest bidder
5. **Distribution**: Proceeds distributed by rank

### Pacte Commissoire

> **Art. 2078 al. 2 C.civ.** prohibition: The creditor cannot become owner of the property directly upon default.

**Exception**: Post-default agreement to transfer property at fair value is permitted.

---

## Release (*Mainlevée*)

### Voluntary Release

Upon satisfaction of the secured obligation:
- Creditor provides release (*mainlevée*)
- Notarial act of release
- Registration of release at Conservation

### Judicial Release

If creditor refuses voluntary release:
- Debtor applies to court
- Court orders release if obligation satisfied
- Court-ordered release registered

## Banking & Finance Application

### Real Estate Financing

Hypothèque is the standard security for:
- Acquisition finance
- Development loans
- Commercial mortgages
- Refinancing facilities

### Due Diligence Checklist

- [ ] Title verification (*état hypothécaire*)
- [ ] Existing encumbrances and mortgages
- [ ] Corporate authorization (for company mortgagor)
- [ ] Property valuation
- [ ] Insurance requirements
- [ ] Rank confirmation

### Documentation

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Acte de prêt** | Loan agreement |
| **Acte d'hypothèque** | Mortgage deed (notarial) |
| **Bordereaux d'inscription** | Registration forms |
| **État hypothécaire** | Title search |

### Costs

| Cost | Basis |
|------|-------|
| **Notary fees** | Scaled by amount |
| **Registration fees** | Fixed and proportional |
| **Registration tax** | 1% of secured amount (subject to change) |

---

> **Common Law Comparison**
>
> **UK**: Legal charge (most common) or mortgage by demise; Land Registry registration.
>
> **US**: Deed of trust or mortgage; varies by state; recording required.
>
> **Key difference**: Luxembourg requires notarial act; common law permits private instruments with registration.
