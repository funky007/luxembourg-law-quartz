# Loi du 7 août 2023 — Préservation des entreprises

> **Type**: Legislative Reform
> **Source**: Loi du 7 août 2023 relative à la préservation des entreprises et portant modernisation du droit de la faillite
> **Entry into Force**: 1 November 2023
> **EU Directive**: Directive (UE) 2019/1023 on preventive restructuring frameworks
> **Related**: [[Faillite et période suspecte]], [[Procédures d'insolvabilité — Vue d'ensemble]]

---

## Overview

The Law of 7 August 2023 represents the most significant reform of Luxembourg insolvency law in over a century. After more than ten years of legislative procedure, Luxembourg finally modernized its framework for business preservation and bankruptcy.

> "On a voulu moderniser la faillite qui auparavant ne pouvait être déclarée qu'à la mort du débiteur, c.à.d. qu'à la mort de l'entreprise."
>
> *Translation*: "The aim was to modernize bankruptcy, which previously could only be declared at the death of the debtor, i.e., at the death of the company."

**Source**: Course notes, CCDL Droit des sûretés 2023/24, Me Armel Waisse

---

## Key Objectives

1. **Transpose EU Directive 2019/1023** on preventive restructuring frameworks
2. **Modernize outdated procedures** that were rarely used due to complexity
3. **Provide rescue mechanisms** to preserve economic value and employment
4. **Align Luxembourg** with international restructuring standards

---

## New Reorganisation Procedures

### 1. Out-of-Court Reorganisation (*Réorganisation par accord amiable*)

Informal reorganization through negotiated agreements with creditors, outside formal court proceedings.

### 2. Judicial Reorganisation (*Réorganisation judiciaire*)

Three new judicial reorganisation procedures designed to preserve business continuity:

| Procedure | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Stay procedure** | Obtain suspension of enforcement to enable amicable settlement |
| **Reorganisation plan** | Obtain creditor agreement on restructuring plan |
| **Court-ordered transfer** | Allow transfer of all or part of assets/activities to third parties |

### Purpose (Art. 1)

> "The purpose of judicial reorganisation proceedings is to preserve, under the supervision of the judge, the continuity of all or part of the debtor's assets or activities."

---

## Abolished Procedures

The Law abolishes three rarely-used procedures:

| Former Procedure | Reason for Abolition |
|------------------|----------------------|
| **Concordat préventif de faillite** | Complexity and limited use |
| **Sursis de paiement** (moratorium) | Superseded by new mechanisms |
| **Gestion contrôlée** | Considered outdated |

---

## Scope of Application

**Covered Entities:**
- All traders (*commerçants*)
- Craftsmen (*artisans*)
- Commercial companies (including **SCSp**)
- Civil companies (*sociétés civiles*)

**Excluded Entities:**
- Investment firms
- Insurance and reinsurance companies
- UCIs (Undertakings for Collective Investment)
- SIFs (Specialised Investment Funds)
- SICARs (Venture capital companies)
- Pension funds
- RAIFs (Reserved Alternative Investment Funds)

---

## Key Provisions

### Company Conciliator (*Conciliateur d'entreprise*)

The conciliator's mission is to:
- Prepare and promote conclusion of an amicable agreement
- Obtain creditor agreement on a reorganisation plan
- Facilitate court-ordered transfer of assets

### Bankruptcy Procedure Amendments

1. **Public prosecutor intervention**: Bankruptcy can be initiated by the prosecutor without company or creditor intervention
2. **Director suspension**: Directors are suspended from the obligation to declare cessation of payments from the date of filing for judicial reorganisation
3. **Second chance for entrepreneurs**: Bankrupt natural persons may obtain discharge from pre-bankruptcy debts

## Impact on Security Interests (Sûretés)

### Article 30: Protection During Reorganisation

> **Art. 30**: The opening of judicial reorganisation proceedings cannot serve as a legal basis for early termination or modification of an agreement, notwithstanding any contractual provision to the contrary.

**Critical implication**: Ipso facto clauses triggered solely by reorganisation proceedings are unenforceable.

### Financial Collateral (Loi 5 août 2005)

**Good news for secured creditors**: The Financial Collateral Law's protections remain largely intact:

| Aspect | Status Under New Law |
|--------|----------------------|
| **Bankruptcy-proof character** | Maintained |
| **Enforcement rights** | Preserved |
| **Immunity from collective proceedings** | Confirmed |

### Critical Limitation: Acceleration-Based Triggers

**Warning**: If enforcement triggers depend on debt acceleration, complications arise:

- Acceleration may be restricted during reorganisation periods
- Security enforcement could be blocked if trigger requires prior acceleration
- **Practical solution**: Decouple enforcement triggers from acceleration

**Banking recommendation**: Include independent enforcement events in security documentation (e.g., reorganisation proceedings themselves as a trigger).

## Banking & Finance Application

### Due Diligence Updates

Since 1 November 2023, verify:
- [ ] Enforcement triggers in collateral agreements
- [ ] Whether triggers depend on acceleration (risk)
- [ ] Independent enforcement events included
- [ ] Applicable law carve-outs for financial collateral

### Documentation Considerations

| Issue | Recommendation |
|-------|----------------|
| **Acceleration dependency** | Decouple enforcement from acceleration |
| **Trigger events** | Add reorganisation proceedings as independent trigger |
| **CGF provisions** | Verify "bankruptcy-proof" language |
| **Cross-default clauses** | Review interaction with Art. 30 restrictions |

### Excluded Transactions

For transactions with **regulated vehicles** (UCIs, SIFs, SICARs, RAIFs), the new reorganisation procedures do not apply, providing additional creditor certainty.

---

> **Common Law Comparison**
>
> Luxembourg's new framework is broadly comparable to:
> - **UK**: Restructuring Plan (Part 26A Companies Act 2006)
> - **US**: Chapter 11 bankruptcy reorganisation
> - **France**: Procédure de sauvegarde
>
> Key difference: Luxembourg's Financial Collateral Law provides stronger "creditor-friendly" protections than many comparable jurisdictions.

## Legislative Context

### Why This Reform Matters

> "Le Luxembourg a décidé d'aller au-delà de la directive pour protéger le créancier dans un soucis de protection de la place financière luxembourgeoise."
>
> *Translation*: "Luxembourg decided to go beyond the directive to protect creditors, out of concern for protecting the Luxembourg financial center."

**Source**: Course notes, CCDL Droit des sûretés 2023/24

The reform balances:
1. **Debtor protection**: Mechanisms to preserve viable businesses
2. **Creditor protection**: Financial collateral remains enforceable
3. **Financial center interests**: Maintaining Luxembourg's "creditor-friendly" reputation
