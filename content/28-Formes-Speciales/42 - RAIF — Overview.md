# RAIF — Reserved Alternative Investment Fund

> **Type**: Alternative Investment Fund Vehicle
> **Source**: Loi du 23 juillet 2016 relative aux fonds d'investissement alternatifs réservés
> **Entry into Force**: 1 August 2016
> **Regulatory Status**: Semi-regulated (no direct CSSF supervision)
> **Related**: [[SCSp — Fund Structures (AIFM-SICAR-FIS)]], [[40 - Société Européenne (SE)]]

---

## Definition

The **RAIF** (Reserved Alternative Investment Fund / Fonds d'investissement alternatif réservé) is a Luxembourg investment vehicle that combines:
- **Operational flexibility** similar to unregulated vehicles
- **Investor protection** through mandatory authorized AIFM management
- **Speed to market** without prior CSSF authorization

> The RAIF was designed to offer fund promoters a regulated product framework without the delays of CSSF product approval, while maintaining the substance and governance standards expected by institutional investors.

---

## Key Characteristics

### Semi-Regulated Status

| Aspect | RAIF Position |
|--------|---------------|
| **CSSF product approval** | Not required |
| **AIFM requirement** | Mandatory (authorized under Loi AIFM) |
| **Depositary requirement** | Yes (Luxembourg domiciled) |
| **Auditor requirement** | Yes (approved independent auditor) |
| **Central administrator** | Yes (Luxembourg head office) |

### Comparison with Other Vehicles

| Feature | RAIF | SIF | SICAR | Part II UCI |
|---------|------|-----|-------|-------------|
| **CSSF approval** | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **AIFM required** | Yes | Optional | Optional | Optional |
| **Eligible investors** | Well-informed | Well-informed | Well-informed | Unrestricted |
| **Investment restrictions** | Flexible | Flexible | Risk capital | Diversified |
| **Time to launch** | Fast | Slower | Slower | Slower |

## Legal Forms

RAIF can be structured as:

### Contractual Form
- **FCP** (Fonds commun de placement) — Common contractual fund without legal personality

### Corporate Forms
- **SA** (Société anonyme)
- **SCA** (Société en commandite par actions)
- **SàRL** (Société à responsabilité limitée)
- **SCS** (Société en commandite simple)
- **SCSp** (Société en commandite spéciale)
- **Cooperative organized as SA**

**Most common for fund finance**: SCS and SCSp forms, providing partnership flexibility and tax transparency.

---

## Structural Flexibility

### Umbrella Structure

RAIF can operate as:
- **Single fund** with one investment policy
- **Umbrella fund** with multiple ring-fenced sub-funds

Each sub-fund can have:
- Separate investment policy
- Distinct asset classes
- Different investors
- Separate financing arrangements

### Variable/Fixed Capital

RAIF can be established with:
- **Variable capital** (SICAV) — most common
- **Fixed capital** (SICAF)

## Regulatory Requirements

### Minimum Net Assets

| Requirement | Amount |
|-------------|--------|
| **Minimum NAV** | EUR 1,250,000 |
| **Timeline** | Must be reached within 24 months of formation |

### Service Provider Requirements

| Provider | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| **AIFM** | Authorized under AIFM Law (can be EU-passported) |
| **Depositary** | Luxembourg registered office or EU branch |
| **Auditor** | Approved independent auditor |
| **Central administrator** | Luxembourg head office |

### Eligible Investors

RAIF is reserved for **well-informed investors**:
- Institutional investors
- Professional investors
- Investors investing minimum EUR 125,000
- Investors with written confirmation of investor status from credit institution, investment firm, or management company

---

## Tax Treatment

### Exemptions

RAIF is **not subject to**:
- Corporate income tax (*impôt sur le revenu des collectivités*)
- Municipal business tax (*impôt commercial communal*)
- Net wealth tax (*impôt sur la fortune*)

### Subscription Tax

| Rate | Basis |
|------|-------|
| **0.01%** per annum | Net asset value |
| **Calculation** | Quarterly |
| **Exemption** | Certain institutional shares, money market sub-funds |

### Tax Transparency

For SCS/SCSp-structured RAIFs:
- Generally treated as fiscally transparent
- Income taxed at investor level
- Facilitates investment by tax-exempt or tax-sensitive investors

## Banking & Finance Application

### Fund Finance Considerations

| Issue | Analysis |
|-------|----------|
| **Borrower capacity** | RAIF (if corporate) or AIFM/GP (if FCP/SCSp) |
| **Security package** | Pledge over uncalled commitments, units/shares |
| **Regulatory status** | Confirm RAIF vs. sub-AIFM threshold |
| **Restructuring law exclusion** | RAIF excluded from Loi 7 août 2023 |

### Subscription Line Facilities

For RAIF-structured funds:
- Security over investor commitments
- Pledge of partnership units (parts d'intérêts for SCS/SCSp)
- Account pledges over collection accounts
- Capital call mechanics similar to unregulated funds

### NAV Facilities

For asset-backed lending to RAIF:
- Security over portfolio investments
- Pledge of shares in SPVs
- Receivables assignment
- Consider sub-fund segregation

### Due Diligence Checklist

- [ ] Verify RAIF registration at RCS
- [ ] Confirm authorized AIFM appointment
- [ ] Review depositary arrangements
- [ ] Check sub-fund ring-fencing (if umbrella)
- [ ] Verify well-informed investor status of LPs
- [ ] Confirm borrowing capacity in constitutional docs
- [ ] Review AIFM delegation arrangements

---

## Insolvency Considerations

### Exclusion from Restructuring Law

RAIFs are **excluded** from the scope of the Loi du 7 août 2023 (préservation des entreprises). This provides:
- Greater certainty for creditors
- No risk of reorganization stay affecting enforcement
- Traditional bankruptcy/liquidation regime applies

See [[42 - Loi 7 août 2023 — Préservation des entreprises]] for details.

---

> **Common Law Comparison**
>
> The RAIF is comparable to:
> - **Cayman**: Registered Fund (no regulatory approval required)
> - **Delaware**: LP with registered investment adviser
> - **Ireland**: QIAIF (Qualifying Investor AIF)
>
> Key advantage: EU passport potential through authorized AIFM, combined with Luxembourg's robust legal infrastructure.
