# UCI — Undertakings for Collective Investment

> **Type**: Regulated Investment Fund
> **Source**: Loi du 17 décembre 2010 relative aux organismes de placement collectif
> **Regulatory Status**: CSSF-supervised
> **EU Framework**: UCITS Directive (Part I); AIFMD (Part II)
> **Related**: [[42 - RAIF — Overview]], [[SCSp — Fund Structures (AIFM-SICAR-FIS)]]

---

## Overview

The **Loi du 17 décembre 2010** (UCI Law) establishes Luxembourg's framework for collective investment schemes, creating two main categories:

| Part | Name | EU Framework | Investor Access |
|------|------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Part I** | UCITS | UCITS Directive | Retail (EU passport) |
| **Part II** | UCI | AIFMD | All investors |

> Luxembourg is the world's second-largest fund domicile after the United States, with over €5 trillion in assets under management (2022).

---

## Part I: UCITS

### Definition

**UCITS** (Undertakings for Collective Investment in Transferable Securities) are retail investment funds complying with EU harmonized rules, enabling distribution across the EU via passport.

### Investment Restrictions

UCITS must invest in:
- Transferable securities admitted to regulated markets
- Other liquid assets (money market instruments, cash)
- Investment funds (within limits)
- Financial derivative instruments (for hedging/efficient portfolio management)

**Prohibited**:
- Physical commodities
- Real estate (direct)
- Non-transferable securities (beyond limits)

### Key Requirements

| Requirement | UCITS Standard |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Diversification** | Maximum 10% per issuer (5/10/40 rule) |
| **Liquidity** | Daily NAV calculation |
| **Leverage** | Limited (100% of NAV via derivatives) |
| **Depositary** | Luxembourg-based |
| **CSSF approval** | Required |

### Legal Forms

UCITS can be structured as:
- **FCP** (Fonds commun de placement) — contractual
- **SICAV** (Société d'investissement à capital variable) — corporate

---

## Part II: UCI (Non-UCITS)

### Definition

**Part II UCIs** are alternative investment funds that can invest in all asset types, with greater flexibility than UCITS but still subject to CSSF supervision.

### Investment Flexibility

Part II UCIs may invest in:
- All transferable securities
- Real estate
- Commodities
- Private equity
- Infrastructure
- Any other assets

### Legal Forms

Since recent reforms, Part II UCI can be structured as:
- **SA** (Société anonyme)
- **SCA** (Société en commandite par actions)
- **SCS** (Société en commandite simple)
- **SCSp** (Société en commandite spéciale)
- **SàRL** (Société à responsabilité limitée)
- **SCoSA** (Coopérative organisée en SA)
- **FCP** (Fonds commun de placement)

### AIFM Requirements

Part II UCIs must appoint an AIFM unless benefiting from exemptions:

| Management Type | Description |
|-----------------|-------------|
| **External AIFM** | Separate entity manages fund |
| **Internal management** | Fund itself is authorized AIFM |
| **Sub-threshold** | Small funds may be exempt |

## Comparison: UCI Vehicles

| Feature | UCITS (Part I) | Part II UCI | SIF | SICAR | RAIF |
|---------|----------------|-------------|-----|-------|------|
| **CSSF approval** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Investor access** | Retail | All | Well-informed | Well-informed | Well-informed |
| **Investment restrictions** | Strict | Flexible | Flexible | Risk capital | Flexible |
| **EU passport** | Product passport | Via AIFM | Via AIFM | Via AIFM | Via AIFM |
| **Diversification** | Required | Flexible | 30% max single asset | None | Flexible |

## Regulatory Framework

### CSSF Supervision

Both Part I and Part II UCIs are supervised by the CSSF:

| Aspect | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| **Authorization** | Prior CSSF approval required |
| **Prospectus** | CSSF-approved offering document |
| **Annual reporting** | Audited accounts to CSSF |
| **NAV publication** | Regular (daily for UCITS) |
| **Depositary** | Luxembourg-based, CSSF-supervised |

### Service Providers

| Provider | Part I | Part II |
|----------|--------|---------|
| **Depositary** | Required (Luxembourg) | Required (Luxembourg) |
| **AIFM** | Management company | Required (with exemptions) |
| **Auditor** | Required | Required |
| **Central admin** | Required | Required |

## Banking & Finance Application

### Fund Finance Considerations

UCIs are **less common** in fund finance than unregulated or RAIF structures due to:
- Investment restrictions (UCITS)
- Regulatory constraints on borrowing
- Daily liquidity requirements (UCITS)
- Retail investor protections

### When UCI Financing Applies

| Scenario | Relevance |
|----------|-----------|
| **Bridge financing** | Short-term liquidity for settlements |
| **Leverage** | Within UCITS limits (temporary borrowing) |
| **Part II leverage** | More flexibility for alternative strategies |
| **Feeder structures** | UCI investing in master fund |

### Borrowing Restrictions

**UCITS (Part I)**:
- Temporary borrowing only (10% of NAV max)
- No structural leverage via borrowing
- Derivatives leverage within limits

**Part II UCI**:
- More flexible borrowing
- Subject to prospectus/fund docs
- AIFM leverage requirements apply

## Tax Treatment

### Luxembourg Taxation

| Tax | Treatment |
|-----|-----------|
| **Corporate income tax** | Exempt |
| **Municipal business tax** | Exempt |
| **Net wealth tax** | Exempt |
| **Subscription tax** | 0.05% p.a. (Part I) / 0.01-0.05% (Part II) |

### Withholding Tax

- No Luxembourg withholding on distributions to non-residents
- Treaty access depends on investor jurisdiction

## Insolvency Considerations

### Restructuring Law Exclusion

UCIs are **excluded** from the scope of the Loi du 7 août 2023 (préservation des entreprises):

> "Undertakings for collective investment" expressly excluded from reorganization procedures.

This provides certainty for:
- Depositary arrangements
- Creditor claims
- Liquidation procedures

See [[42 - Loi 7 août 2023 — Préservation des entreprises]].

>
> - **UK**: Similar structure with OEIC/ICVC (UCITS equivalent) and NURS/QIS
> - **US**: No UCITS equivalent; mutual funds under Investment Company Act 1940
> - **Ireland**: Competing UCITS domicile with similar framework
>
> Key Luxembourg advantage: Largest European fund domicile with established infrastructure and expertise.
