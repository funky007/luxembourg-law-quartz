## 2. Paiement (arts. 1235-1270)

**See full note**: [[Paiement]]

### 2.1 Definition and Legal Nature

**Poelmans §487**: Payment is the execution by the debtor of the obligation to which he submitted. It is the normal mode of extinction.

**Legal nature**: Controversial (contract, acte juridique, or fait juridique).

**Poelmans view**: Payment should be considered a *fait juridique* because:
- Does not require creditor's acceptance
- Can be performed by third party
- **Consequence**: Proof of payment may be by all means (CA, 25 novembre 2009, Pas. 35, p. 40)

### 2.2 Who May Pay — Le Solvens (arts. 1236-1238)

**Art. 1236 al. 1**: Obligation may be discharged by any *interested* person:
- **Debtor** (natural payer)
- **Mandatary** of debtor (e.g., domiciliary bank)
- **Caution** (surety)
- **Coobligé** (joint debtor)
- **Caution réelle** (third-party security provider)
- **Bénéficiaire of stipulation pour autrui** — CA, 5 juin 2002, n° 26331

**Art. 1236 al. 2**: Also by *non-interested* third party — two scenarios:
1. Acting in debtor's name and for debtor's account (gérant d'affaires)
2. Acting in own name (no automatic subrogation)

**Trib. Lux., 10 février 2006, n° 99339**: Third party payment does not prove third party is the contracting party.

**Presumption of gift**: When third party pays without subrogation, jurisprudence presumes *libéralité* (rebuttable) — CA, 7 janvier 2004, n° 27655.

**Exception — Art. 1237**: Creditor may refuse third party payment for obligations *intuitu personae*.

**Capacity**: Solvens must be capable. For obligations de dare, must be owner and capable of alienating (art. 1238).

### 2.3 To Whom — L'Accipiens (arts. 1239-1242)

**Art. 1239 al. 1**: Payment must be to creditor or mandatary.

**Valid payment alternatives**:
- Conventional, legal, or judicial mandatary
- Ratification by creditor
- Creditor profited (art. 1239 al. 2)
- **Possessor of debt** — good faith payment valid (art. 1240)

**"Qui paie mal paie deux fois"** — Trib. Lux., 16 juin 2011, n° 134023: Payment to wrong person requires re-payment to true creditor.

**When payment to creditor impossible**:
- **Incapable creditor** (minor, bankrupt) — valid only if proven profitable (art. 1241)
- **Saisie-arrêt** — payment despite attachment ineffective against attaching creditor (art. 1242)

### 2.4 Effect of Saisie-Arrêt (Banking Practice)

**Art. 1242**: Payment made despite attachment is not valid against attaching creditor.

**Effect on bank accounts**: Attachment freezes all assets held by bank — even if exceeding attaching creditor's claim.

**G. Loesch & F. Kremer**: Attachment renders unavailable the totality of assets held.

**CA, 14 juin 2006, n° 29759**: Jurisprudence appears to favor *partial unavailability* (only up to attachment amount), allowing release of excess.

**Criminal attachment**: Bank must block account upon notification of *ordonnance de perquisition et de saisie* from investigating judge — no verification duty.

### 2.5 Object (arts. 1243-1248)

- **Identité** (art. 1243): Exact thing owed — creditor cannot be forced to accept substitute
- **Intégrité** (art. 1244): Full amount — no partial payment
- **Indivisibilité**: Cannot force partial acceptance

### 2.6 Imputation (arts. 1253-1256)

When multiple debts exist:
1. **Debtor chooses** at payment (art. 1253)
2. If silent → **most onerous** debt first (art. 1256)
3. If equal → **proportional**

## 3. Compensation (arts. 1289-1299)

### 3.1 Definition

Automatic extinction of reciprocal debts up to concurrent amount.

### 3.2 Conditions for Legal Compensation

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Réciprocité** | Same parties, personal capacity |
| **Fongibilité** | Both debts of same kind (usually money) |
| **Liquidité** | Amount determined |
| **Exigibilité** | Both debts due |

### 3.3 Effect

> **Art. 1290**: Compensation operates **automatically by force of law** (*de plein droit*), even unknown to parties.

### 3.4 Types

- **Légale**: Automatic if conditions met
- **Conventionnelle**: By agreement (conditions may be modified)
- **Judiciaire**: Ordered by judge when one debt not liquid

## 4. Novation (arts. 1271-1281)

### 4.1 Definition

Extinction of obligation by substitution of new obligation.

### 4.2 Types

| Type | Change |
|------|--------|
| **By object** | New prestation |
| **By debtor** | New debtor |
| **By creditor** | New creditor |

### 4.3 Key Rule: Non-Presumption

> **Art. 1273**: "La novation ne se présume point." Must be proven by clear intention (*animus novandi*).

### 4.4 Effect

- Old obligation extinguished
- New obligation created
- Accessory rights (guarantees) do not transfer unless expressly agreed

## 5. Prescription Extinctive

**See full note**: [[Prescription extinctive]]

### 5.1 General Period (art. 2262)

> **30 years** for personal actions (droit commun).

### 5.2 Special Periods

| Period | Application | Article |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **10 years** | Commercial obligations | art. 189 C.com |
| **5 years** | Vices du consentement | art. 1304 |
| **5 years** | Nullité relative | art. 1304 |
| **1 year** | Lésion (art. 1118) | art. 1118 al. 3 |

### 5.3 Starting Point

- **General**: When right could be exercised
- **Vices**: Discovery (erreur, dol) or cessation (violence)
- **Lésion**: Contract conclusion

### 5.4 Suspension (arts. 2251-2259)

Prescription suspended for:
- Minors and *interdits* (art. 2252)
- Between spouses (art. 2253)
- Héritier bénéficiaire for claims against succession (art. 2258)
- **Contra non valentem agere** — impossibility to act

### 5.5 Interruption

- Citation en justice
- Commandement or saisie
- Acknowledgment by debtor

## 6. Transfer of Obligations

### 6.1 Cession de Créance (arts. 1689-1701)

**Assignment of debt by creditor** (cédant) to third party (cessionnaire).

**Formalities**:
- Writing (for proof)
- **Signification** to debtor or acceptance by debtor (for opposability)

**Effects**:
- Cessionnaire acquires all accessory rights
- Debtor may oppose defenses available against cédant

### 6.2 Subrogation (arts. 1249-1252)

**Substitution in creditor's rights** by person who pays.

**Types**:
- **Légale** (art. 1251): Automatic in specified cases (caution who pays, etc.)
- **Conventionnelle** (arts. 1249-1250): By agreement with creditor or debtor

## 7. Proof of Obligations (La Preuve)

### 7.1 Burden of Proof

> **Art. 1315**: Who claims must prove.

- **Creditor** proves existence of obligation
- **Debtor** proves extinction

### 7.2 Hierarchy of Proof

1. **Acte authentique** — full faith (arts. 1317-1319)
2. **Acte sous seing privé** — full faith between parties (art. 1322)
3. **Commencement de preuve par écrit** — permits testimonial supplement
4. **Témoignage** — limited to < €2,500 (art. 1341)
5. **Présomptions**
6. **Aveu**
7. **Serment**

### 7.3 Written Proof Requirement (Art. 1341)

**Poelmans §401**: All *actes juridiques* exceeding €2,500 must be proved by writing (authentic or sous seing privé).

**Key distinction** (verified from Poelmans):

| Category | Proof Mode | Example |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| **Actes juridiques** | Written (art. 1341) | Contracts, mandates |
| **Faits juridiques** | Free proof | Inexécution, vices du consentement |
| **Faits matériels** | Free proof | Theft, accident |

**CA, 14 juillet 2004, n° 26588**: *Acte juridique* = manifestation of will to create, transfer, modify or extinguish obligations. *Fait juridique* = fact producing legal effects without such intention.

### 7.4 Specific Applications (Jurisprudence)

**Requiring written proof**:
- Loan contract (*contrat de prêt*) — CA, 28 mai 2008, n° 32446
- Convention de prête-nom — Trib. Lux., 16 janvier 2009, n° 116432
- Mandate for legal proceedings — CA, 22 juin 2006, n° 30005
- Bank transfer order (*ordre de virement*) — CA, 4 mai 2011, n° 34452
- Donation (animus donandi) — CA, 27 octobre 1999
- Partition agreement — CA, 16 mai 2007

**Free proof permitted**:
- Contractual non-performance (*inexécution*) — CA, 7 juin 2007, n° 31464
- Vice du consentement — CA, 23 avril 2008, n° 32983
- Identity of contracting party — CA, 14 juillet 2004, n° 26588
- Simulation (by third parties) — CA, 9 mai 2001, Pas. 32, p. 75
- Vehicle theft (insurance) — Trib. Lux., 20 janvier 2005
- Acts of mandatary during mandate — CA, 2 juillet 2008, n° 32494
- Prescription starting point — CA, 29 avril 2009, Pas. 34, p. 468
- Professional's duty to inform — Trib. Lux., 26 janvier 2007, n° 105914

### 7.5 Proving Against Written Act (Art. 1341 al. 2)

> No testimonial proof "contre et outre" a written act — even < €2,500.

**CA, 26 mars 1998, Pas. 31, p. 13**: Parties cannot prove inexactitudes or omissions of written act except by writing.

**CA, 29 octobre 2003, n° 27133**: Verbal modifications to written construction contract cannot be proved by testimony.

**Exception**: Correction of obvious clerical errors permitted via testimony (CA, 23 avril 2008).

## 8. Plurality of Obligations

### 8.1 Obligations Conjointes (Default)

**Definition**: Multiple debtors/creditors, obligation divides.

- Each debtor owes only their share
- Each creditor can claim only their share

### 8.2 Solidarité (art. 1200 et s.)

**See full note**: [[Solidarité (art. 1202)]]

**Definition**: Each debtor liable for whole; each creditor can claim whole.

**Key Rule**:
> **Art. 1202**: "La solidarité ne se présume point." Must be express or legal.

**Sources**:
- **Conventionnelle**: Express clause
- **Légale**: Law imposes (e.g., co-managers)
- **In solidum**: Jurisprudential (concurrent tortfeasors)

### 8.3 Indivisibilité

**Definition**: Obligation that cannot be divided by nature or intention.

**Effect**: Similar to solidarité but survives to heirs.

### 8.4 Obligations Alternatives

**See full note**: [[Obligations alternatives]]

**Definition**: Multiple objects; debtor discharged by performing one.

**Choice**: Belongs to debtor unless otherwise agreed (art. 1190).
