## ⚠️ Key Warnings

### 1-Year Prescription for Lésion

> **Art. 1118 al. 3**: Action for lésion must be brought within **1 YEAR** from contract conclusion — much shorter than other vices (5 years).

### Luxembourg Retains Cause Doctrine

> Unlike France (2016 reform), Luxembourg **still uses cause** as validity requirement.

### Non-Cumul

> Contractual and delictual liability **cannot be combined** between contracting parties.
